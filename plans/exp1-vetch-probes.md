## Known weaknesses, stated in advance

- **#1 is a guess.** The obligation says "debt/eviction/sick parent" — three alternatives,
  and I do not know which the story chose. If the answer is the sick parent, these probes
  miss almost everything and #1 will read as neglected when it is not. That failure mode is
  the price of blindness and is the most likely thing to go wrong here.
- **#3, #6, #7 are bare character names.** They will fire on any mention, not only on the
  obligation being pressed. Expect them to over-report. A name is a floor on attention, not
  a measure of it.
- **#5 is thematic**, not object-bound. Words like `aware` and `subject` may fire on
  unrelated prose. Expect noise.
- **#2** depends on the story literally using the phrase "the figure". If it names the
  figure something else after the first appearance, this under-reports by design — which is
  itself informative about whether the obligation stayed unexplained.
