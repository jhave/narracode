Same blind protocol as `plans/exp1-vetch-probes.md`. Written from
`Stories written with Narracode/31-07-2026_Alpha-Gaia/structural/obligations.md` alone — its
nine `## Active` entries and three `## At Risk Of Neglect` entries. Not read at the time of
writing: any draft, `POETICS.md`, `PROMPTS.md`, the scene ledger, the critiques,

the method depends on how the obligation was written down — see §"What this comparison

## Predictions, recorded before the run

These are the point of pre-registering. Each is checkable against the output.

1. **#3 dragonfly and #7 child at the pond should show a plant, a long gap, and a single
   late return.** The obligations file says both are "owed" a return, singular. If the curve
   shows them re-touched repeatedly, the story is fussing at them.
2. **#1 the greyed field should fire at most three times** — the file says "must not be
   returned to more than twice." This is the only obligation in either story with an explicit
   *ceiling*, which makes it the one place the ledger can catch a stated constraint being
   violated. If it fires four or more times, that is a finding.
3. **#10 the park should fire in every section.** The file says every feed section owes the
   park a return. Any section where it does not fire is the risk the file already named.
4. **#8 should fire early and late but not resolve.** Unfalsifiable by this method — the
   ledger sees mentions, not answers. Recorded so I do not later claim it as a result.

## Known weaknesses, stated in advance

- **#11 is a bare character name** and will fire constantly. It measures presence, not
  whether the skepticism stayed funny. It is included only so the protagonist's attention
  baseline is visible against the other curves; treat its line as a control, not a result.
- **#5 `meat`** may fire on unrelated food. **#7 `child`** may fire on any child.
- **#10 `heat`** will fire inside `heated`, `wheat`. Deliberately not fixed — the probe was
  written blind and stays as written.
- **#4 `mist`** fires inside `mistake`, `mistaken`. Same: left as written, reported as noise.
