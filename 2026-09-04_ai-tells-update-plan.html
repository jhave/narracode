In late 2025 and 2026, frontier models (Claude 3.5/3.7/Opus 5, GPT-4o/o1/o3, and Gemini 1.5/2.0/3.x) evolved past early, crude "tells" like delve or raw em-dash floods. In their place arose far more insidious structural conventions: the Contrast Reframe ("not X, but Y"), Somatic Interiority Clichés, Sensory Dyads, Uniform Information Density (UID) smoothing, and Thematic Closing Wrap-Ups. Left unaddressed, these conventions homogenize generative literature into a polite, predictable, middle-brow drone.

The AI-tells pass in the Narracode harness was originally built as a span-level audit to catch machine markers. Over August 2026, empirical measurement across 1,234 human edits (What the Edits Know) proved that simple lexical subtraction accounts for only 9% of what human revision does; the remaining 91% is non-trivial textural substitution: breaking metric symmetry, colliding estranged registers, and introducing radical linguistic friction.

This report documents the completed update to the Narracode harness: expanding the tell registry to 29 classes, conditioning remedies on story-specific poetics, wiring live exemplar retrieval into generation, and implementing closed-loop arc perturbation.

Implemented Architectural Upgrades

  • Registry 2.0 (Classes 21–29): Added to master_ai_tells.md. Targets the 2025–2026 model conventions, including contrast reframes, somatic reflex shortcuts, sensory twin-packs, smoothing transitions, and the newly added Class 29: Numeric Fixation & Arbitrary Specificity (e.g. obsessive recurrence of eleven, seventeen, unanchored round primes).
  • The Poetics Filter: Weeding and retrofit remedies in master_ai_tells.md and narracode.md now actively read the project's local POETICS.md to bend, blend, and subtly shift substitutions into the active story's native attentional dialect.
  • The Arc-Perturbation Engine: A new generative revision mode in narracode.md that rewrites flat sentences by perturbing them with active pressures from structural/character-interiority.md, structural/obligations.md, and structural/motifs.md.
  • Closed-Loop Clearance Check: Every perturbed rewrite is immediately audited back against master_ai_tells.md in a closed loop before committing, ensuring the perturbation does not smuggle in melodrama or unanchored symbols.
  • Live Exemplar Retrieval in pre_draft: Grounding scene generation in 6–8 verified before → after pairs from the 1,234-pair corpus.

1. Contemporary AI Conventions (The 2025–2026 Model Fingerprint)

Modern models are trained to avoid obvious mistakes. In doing so, they gravitate toward the statistical mode of language, generating prose that is blameless, symmetrical, and devoid of tactile surprise.

# Convention Structural Mechanism Model Motivation
C1 The Contrast Reframe
(Not X, but Y)		 \b(not (a|an|the)? \w+, but (a|an|the)? \w+)\b
"It was not malice, but exhaustion."		 Simulates philosophical nuance by rejecting a strawman premise before offering a tidy resolution.
C2 Somatic Reflex Clichés
(The Visceral Shortcut)		 Involuntary bodily reactions: "breath he didn't know he was holding", "something shifted behind her eyes", "stomach dropped". Over-indexes on "show, don't tell" by converting complex private states into autonomic nervous system reflexes.
C3 Adjectival Sensory Dyads
(The Twin-Pack)		 Coordinated pairs of sensory modifiers: "cold, metallic air", "hollow, brittle certainty", "pale, trembling hands". Acoustic balancing: ensuring rhythmic symmetry across noun phrases.
C4 Semantic Smoothing
(Connective Over-glaze)		 Clause-leading transitional glues: "And yet," "Still," "In that moment," "Even so," "Perhaps that was why." Eliminates the disorienting, jagged leaps of thought native to human consciousness.
C5 Uniform Information Density (UID)
(The Monotone Ceiling)		 Evenly distributed token surprisal. Sentences adhere to a 12–22 word metronome with balanced periodic clauses. Mathematical minimization of perplexity variance; avoids both bizarre solecisms and bone-dry monosyllabic slogs.
C6 Epistemic Varnish
(Performed Hesitancy)		 Softening hedges: "a kind of," "in some quiet way," "seemed almost to," "perhaps." Simulates human doubt without committing to real narrative risk.
C7 Thematic Wrap-Up
(The Moralizing Coda)		 Ending scenes or paragraphs with a neat, universalizing philosophical takeaway. Assistance bias: the urge to leave the prompter with a polished, morally rounded artifact.
C8 Numeric Fixation
(Arbitrary Specificity)		 Overuse of specific recurrent odd numbers: eleven, seventeen, forty-one, or unanchored counts ("eleven minutes", "seventeen similar entries"). Simulates precision by grabbing low-probability integers from a low-loss latent pocket, creating false authenticity without system mechanics.

2. Registry 2.0: Classes 21–29 in master_ai_tells.md

The registry has been updated from 20 to 29 classes. These additions provide machine-auditable regexes, concrete remedies, and strict target rates:

Class Name Diagnostic Pattern / Detector Remedy & Target Rate
21 The Contrast Reframe \b(not (a|an|the)? \w+ (of|in) \w+, but (a|an|the)? \w+)\b or \b(not \w+, but \w+)\b where both terms share semantic domain. CUT negation; state the second term flatly, or cut entirely. Target rate: 0 per draft.
22 Somatic Reflex Shortcut Autonomic clichés: breath [he/she] didn't know, knot in [his/her] stomach, hair on [his/her] neck, something shifted in. REPLACE with an external, concrete physical action or tool telemetry. Target rate: 0 in narration.
23 Sensory Dyad (Twin Adjectives) Two coordinate adjectives modifying a noun where one is literal and one is metaphorical: "cold, bitter air", "hollow, pale light". CUT to the single stranger adjective, or convert to a bare noun. Target rate: ≤ 1 per 2,000 words.
24 Semantic Smoothing Glues Conjunctive adverbs opening sentences: "And yet," "Still," "Moreover," "Even so," "Perhaps that was why." CUT the conjunction. Let the sentences collide paratactically. Target rate: ≤ 1 per section.
25 Epistemic Varnish (The Politeness Hedge) Intrusions of "in some quiet way", "a sort of", "almost as if", "seemed to" where narration has focal authority. CUT the hedge. State the event as cold fact. Target rate: 0 in austere narration.
26 Unearned Thematic Coda Paragraph-final sentence summarizing the emotional or intellectual significance of the paragraph's actions. CUT the final sentence. Terminate on the preceding physical action. Target rate: 0 at beat endings.
27 Symmetrical Periodic Sentence Compound sentences joined by while or as where both independent clauses have equal word counts (±2 words) and parallel syntax. PUNCTUATE into two asymmetric sentences; convert one into a verbless noun fragment.
28 Ecclesiastical Diction Sneaking church vocabulary into secular scenes: litany, baptism, catechism, sacred, reverent, testament, confessional. REPLACE with administrative, industrial, biological, or vernacular terms. Target rate: 0 unless church-world.
29 Numeric Fixation / Arbitrary Specificity Unanchored recurrent integers (especially eleven, seventeen, forty-one, three minutes) doing aesthetic work without operational mechanics. REPLACE with true engineering/logistical telemetry (e.g. 320ms, shard NOR-14, 504 error) or CUT the number to let the noun stand alone. Target rate: ≤ 1 unanchored numeral per story.

3. The Poetics Filter for Remedies

A mechanical audit that replaces machine spans with generic stock words merely substitutes one form of synthetic flatness for another.

Under the updated specification in master_ai_tells.md, every proposed remedy or retrofit substitution MUST consult the active project's POETICS.md:

                    │   - Reference Authors (Knausgaard, Butler)   │
                    └──────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                           │
                                           ▼

If POETICS.md specifies an austere post-industrial register, a Class 22 somatic reflex ("knot in his stomach") is replaced not by generic stoicism ("he was calm"), but by an objective physical transaction native to the world ("His thumbs caught in the copper turnings"). The substitution is dyed in the project's unique attentional substrate.

4. The Arc-Perturbation Engine (Closed-Loop Structural Revision)

To move beyond line-level trimming, narracode.md now specifies The Arc-Perturbation Pass (invoked with "perturb this", "retrofit this", or "break the cadence"). When a sentence is flagged as synthetic or flat, the engine leverages the story's structural memory to perturb the sentence with dramatic and thematic friction:

4.1 The Three Structural Invocations

  • Character Interiority (structural/character-interiority.md): Perturb the sentence by surfacing an unresolved shame, an unacknowledged desire, or a private contradiction.
  • Active Obligations (structural/obligations.md): Perturb the sentence by tangling it with a planted object, an unpaid emotional debt, or an overdue question withheld from the reader.
  • Motif Recurrence (structural/motifs.md): Perturb the sentence by re-introducing an accumulated physical texture, an odor, or an atmospheric pressure in a transformed state.

4.2 The Closed-Loop Clearance Check

Because structural perturbation involves creative re-writing, it carries an immediate risk: smuggling in melodrama, unanchored symbols, or new AI tells.

To ensure these revisions smooth away statistical conformity without introducing new model habits, the engine enforces a mandatory Closed Loop:

1. Original Draft (Synthetic / Flat):
"She waited by the window as the rain started to fall, feeling a sudden pang of regret."

2. Arc Perturbation (Consulting character-interiority.md & obligations.md):
"She stood by the sill, her fingers testing the salt stain on the envelope she had sworn to burn before Marcus reached the ferry."

3. Closed-Loop Tell Audit (Scanned against Classes 1–29):
- Contrast reframe? None.
- Somatic shortcut? None (fingers testing salt stain is active manual task).
- Sensory dyad? None.
- Ceremonial predicate? None.
- Thematic wrap-up? None.
Result: CLEAR. Committed to draft.

6. Conclusion

Literary prose is not generated by suppressing errors; it is generated by motivated asymmetry:

  • Replacing rhetorical contrast reframes with unvarnished factual assertions.
  • Replacing somatic interiority clichés with cold, tactile objects and domain telemetry.
  • Replacing smooth periodic sentences with ragged parataxis and local perplexity spikes.
  • Bending replacements through local story poetics and perturbing flat cadences with active character arcs.

By pairing a 29-class audit with poetics-conditioned remedies, live exemplar retrieval, and closed-loop arc perturbation, the Narracode harness ensures that generative literature remains what art has always been: not a smoothed consensus of the linguistic past, but an unpredictable encounter with the real.
