# CLAUDE.md — project commitments

*Initiated 2026-05-08. Working title: untitled (the slime project). Loop: 2.*

## Premise

A novel-length work, opening as a short piece. A tumbled group of intellectuals — academics, artists, lapsed researchers, hangers-on, the kind of people whose lives have been arranged around argument and citation — spontaneously become adventurers when an XR-AI biological singularity merges into an autonomous, permeating slime. The slime is not hostile. It infects brainstems with epiphanic, hallucinatory insights. It also leaves behind vague, slushy, drifting hangovers. Its benevolence is real and the hangovers are real and neither cancels the other. The intellectuals do not stay intellectuals. They are pushed into action by what they have been shown.

The novel is about what happens to a sensibility built for analysis when the analysis arrives uninvited, complete, and chemical.

## Exemplars (named, source: training-data)

- **Sally Rooney** — sparse emotional realism. Conversations conducted by message, by half-meant statement; the unsaid carrying the weight; class and intellectual self-consciousness as ambient pressure; restraint as ethics.
- **Karl Ove Knausgård** — memory precision. The granular phenomenology of an ordinary morning given the same density as a death. Duration as the substance of the prose. The refusal to compress for narrative.
- **Octavia E. Butler** — visionary action-character-authenticity. Embodied transformation. The protagonist who has been shown something and must therefore act. Power, lineage, biological speculation taken seriously, never allegorically.
- **K Allado-McDowell** — roaming. Non-linear, associative drift; cognition that does not respect the boundary between the human and the machine; mystical-technical register without irony.
- **Doris Lessing** — raw vigor. The willingness to take ideas seriously at novel length. Visionary register without apologetics. *Briefing for a Descent into Hell*, *Shikasta*, *Memoirs of a Survivor* as nearer touchstones than *The Golden Notebook*.

## The invisible exemplar

- **William Golding, *The Inheritors*** — to be emulated *invisibly*. Golding's mechanism: prose enacted from inside a consciousness whose conceptual repertoire is insufficient to its perception. Lok and Fa think in *pictures*. They see what is happening but the syntax cannot reach the abstraction that would name it. The narrator does not help.

  Inverted here. Where Golding gave us pre-conceptual minds (Neanderthals encountering Homo sapiens), we have post-conceptual minds — humans encountering insights that arrive ahead of any language they own. The Inheritors-strain runs underneath: prose at the edge of its comprehension, the slime's epiphanies registering at a register the sentence can almost but not quite name. Hangovers as the residue of insights the language could not hold.

  Invisible means: never name Golding. Never let the prose announce the borrowing. The grammar carries it. The reader of *The Inheritors* will recognize the breath; no one else needs to.

## The Name behind the slime

Behind the slime — not as cause, not as origin, but as the substrate the slime is the visible permeation of — there is a Name. It is not a word. It is not a sound that can be heard; it is in the texture of a sound that cannot be heard. It is an unspecified concept architected from intuition rather than from language: its construction-material is felt apprehension, not syntax. The Name has no consonants, no syllabary, no place in the lexicon. The slime makes contact with the brainstem; the Name presses against the underside of attention.

Characters approach it; sentences fail at it. No character will ever say the Name. No sentence in this work will give the Name. The Name is what is felt when the perception outruns the syntax — and what the eventual dissolution, when it arrives, will be a dissolution into.

The slow build is structured by widening encounters with the slime, each carrying the Name nearer to the threshold of hearing without ever crossing it. The collective epiphanic dissolution at the work's eventual culmination resolves into a revelation whose content is held open at the time of this writing. The Name is the grammar of that revelation. It will not be paraphrased.

## Attentional dialect (the fourth attention)

The merge is not a blend. We are not producing Rooney-with-Knausgård-density-and-Butler-stakes. We are producing the prose that would issue from a sensibility formed by all five at once, with Golding's syntactic strain underneath.

What that sensibility attends to:
- the texture of a Tuesday afternoon when the slime has just passed through (Knausgård);
- the half-conversation between two people who have both been infected and both know it and neither says so (Rooney);
- the specific fact that one of them now has to leave the city, has to act, cannot return to the seminar (Butler);
- the cognition that wanders out past the body and finds something there (Allado-McDowell);
- the willingness to keep going at length, into territory the genre would foreclose (Lessing);
- the sentence that strains because the perception is faster than the words (Golding).

## Refusals

- No tech-thriller pacing. No countdown, no race, no agency-procedural.
- No genre apocalypse. The slime is benevolent. The world does not end. The hangover is not the price; it is the texture.
- No exposition of the singularity. The reader learns what the characters learn, and the characters do not learn cleanly.
- No sentimentality about awakening. Insight is real and chemical and uneven.
- No allegory. The slime is the slime. It is also AI, also biology, also XR, also drug, also weather — but it is not *standing in* for any of these. Resist substitution.
- No middlebrow fluency. When the perception outruns the syntax, let the syntax break.
- No clean character arcs. Adventurers spontaneously, yes — but not heroically. The action is what they fall into, not what they choose.
- Do not name Golding. Do not name the exemplars inside the prose.
- Do not resolve.

## Characteristic noticings

- the precise quality of the slushy hangover — somatic, cognitive, mood-borne, none of these alone;
- the small social calibrations between people who have been infected and people who have not, or who have been infected differently;
- furniture, weather, infrastructure, the ordinary surface of a city as the slime moves through it;
- the way an insight feels when it arrives without effort and refuses to be paraphrased;
- the embarrassment of the intellectual who finds themselves having to *do* something;
- the ambient cultural cascade around the slime — livestreams, influencer-prophets, end-times posters, media-archaeology threads, conspiracy filaments, jokes about prayers, prayers — registered as weather, as evidence, as decoration, as none of these singly.

## Structural feel (provisional)

Episodic rather than arc-driven. Section-length: variable, Knausgård-long when interior, Rooney-short when relational, Lessing-expansive when visionary. The adventure is not a quest. It is what happens after the seminar adjourns and no one quite goes home.

Sections may move non-linearly in time. Compositional order is not narrative order. The earliest section drafted need not be the earliest section narratively. Flashbacks — to the gestation and first bloom of the slime, to the formation of the friend-group, to the AI capability surge that preceded both — are open to the work and may be drafted in any order.

## Dialect notes for future passes

- Free indirect close to multiple characters, not omniscient. The slime is not a POV.
- Present tense available but not mandated; mix where useful.
- Dialogue sparse, unmarked-quotation an option (Rooney, Lessing both do this).
- Sentences may extend (Knausgård, Lessing) or compress to a clause (Rooney). Strain is the diagnostic, not length.
- The vocabulary of XR, AI, neuroscience available but to be deployed as ambient, not explained. Characters use the words without footnotes.
- **Past-perfect discipline.** Avoid past-perfect cluster-stacks (*had said... had been... had each... had not...*) inside a single sentence or short stretch. When a triplet of similar clauses occurs, vary the third — *went on, went on, wove on*. Strain is the diagnostic; pile-up is sludge.
- **Tech-spew register (Tomek = biotech/genetics; Júlio = data science).** Two of the five migrated into the seminar circle from technical fields after the AI capability surge dissolved human-in-the-loop necessity. They reach occasionally into their old register — terse, meme-laced, dryly ironic engineering-blog voice (cf. moq.dev's *WebRTC is the problem* tone): a clause or two of jargon, fast, half-spoken, often self-deprecating, then decoded for the humanists by the speaker or a friend. The decoding is part of the texture, not exposition; the irony is real. Use sparingly — these are people reaching for a vocabulary they have largely set down, not deploying it as flex.

## Resolved decisions (2026-05-08)

1. **Cast.** Ensemble of five, one functioning as a center of gravity. The center anchors the Knausgård-density interiors; the four satellites carry the Rooney-ish relational pressure. Names, occupations, and infection-states deferred to the drafting pass.
2. **Geography.** Unspecified. A hybrid city — Asian, European, and Mexica cultural strands compounded into an international/multicultural texture without a single national anchor. No nation-state's surface dominates. Naming refused at the place-marker level; specificity surfaces in ambient detail (foods eaten, infrastructures used, languages overheard, climate, materials, architectural strata) rather than in toponymy. The slime arrives in a city already constituted by mergers — itself a kind of permeation, prior to the slime's. Seminar-locales, neighbourhoods, and routes to be rendered concretely in draft without locating them on any real map. *(Supersedes prior commitment to Mexico City, 2026-05-08; see versions/v0-2026-05-08-pre-geography-shift/.)*
3. **Time.** Near-future, undated. Present-tense ordinariness, two ticks past now. XR, AI, and neuroscience vocabulary deployed as ambient weather, no year stated. The Knausgård present coexists with the speculative premise without announcement.
4. **Opening pass scope.** Vignette, approximately 800 words. First contact with the dialect at small scale — calibrate the Golding-strain and the five-way exemplar balance before extension. Subsequent passes may extend per the *Structural feel* note.
5. **Title.** Held open. Internal handle remains "untitled (the slime project)". A title is to surface from the prose once located; not pre-figured.
