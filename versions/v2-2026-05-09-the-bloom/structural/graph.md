## The slime

- Permeating, non-hostile, brainstem-contact. Leaves a hangover-state: somatic, cognitive, mood-borne, none of these alone.
- Re-infections are possible (Ines).
- Movements announced by buildings like tram delays, in multiple languages.
- Never visibly rendered. Always registered through air, residue, weight in the body, calibration between people, change in the way dust hangs.
- **First contact at the bloom (six months back) registers as a transient DMT-glimpse — three seconds, blurred distortion, non-obliterating.** Public realization arrives with a panic-then-acclimation wave; the body learns it is survivable.

## The cultural cascade (six months back)

- Livestreamers, vlog-prophets, ring-light commentators saying *brothers, sisters.*
- Comments scrolling in seven languages — prayers, jokes about prayers, *it's just DMT, calm the fuck down,* speculations about labs / companies / agencies / gods.
- Academic threads on *latency*, on *the substrate that is always already.*
- Conspiracy filaments. Memes. Influencers mining the lore.
- Yet life going on: buses, roasted-seed vendors, broken streetlights.

## The Name

- Behind the slime; not its cause, its substrate.
- In the texture of a sound that cannot be heard.
- Architected from intuition, not language.
- Approached but never said. The syntax fails at it.
- Distantly: the grammar of the eventual collective dissolution.
