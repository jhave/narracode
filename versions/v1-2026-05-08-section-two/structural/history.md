## Established

- The five are named: Ines (center), Anwen, Tomek, Júlio, Kasimi.
- Ines was infected once before, last winter, at the lacquer market. The hangover is recurring for her.
- The seminar this afternoon discussed Hovena's paper on enactive grief.
- Tomek argued; Anwen did not speak; Júlio was late; Kasimi was half-elsewhere.
- The slime had passed through the institute's upper floors approximately one hour before section one ends. The building services woman announced it in three languages, in the register of a tram delay.
- The institute has an ambient overlay system. Ines has stopped acknowledging it.
- Anwen sent Ines a text: *are you coming for the thing.* Lower-case *a*, no question mark.
- Ines walked seven flights of stairs and out into the street, heading toward the bar where the others would be.
- The bar is basement-level, old wood floors. A house tea arrives uncalled for at the start of every visit.
- The slime passed through the bar at 6:49, during section two. Ines's wrists registered it first; the others each registered it differently. Anwen's first infection occurred during this passage — registered through Ines as the lifted hairs on Anwen's wrist and an *oh* that was not a word.
- The Name behind the slime is a working commitment of the project. It pressed against Ines's attention during the bar-passage but was not paraphrased. It will not be.
