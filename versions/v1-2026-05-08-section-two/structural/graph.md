## Characters

- **Ines** — center of gravity. Lapsed researcher; institute-adjacent. Previously infected (last winter, lacquer market). The hangover is a recurring, half-acclimated state. Has stopped trying to describe what stands behind her after a slime-passage. Will not write the article she is meant to write. Free indirect close in section one.
- **Anwen** — artist. Did not speak at the seminar. Sent Ines the text *are you coming for the thing.* Lower-case, no question mark — her register. Status of infection: unclear at end of section one; to be tested in section two.
- **Tomek** — academic, contentious. Argued in seminar. Avoiding something at the coffee table afterward; Ines could hear the timbre. Talks too much when uneasy. Possibly infected, possibly resisting; not yet calibrated.
- **Júlio** — researcher / writer (lapsed). Was late to seminar. Beard grown out, face thinned in writing-mode. Greets in slow gladness rather than words. Likely infected; status to deepen.
- **Kasimi** — hanger-on. Settled mode of being half-elsewhere. Phone-watcher who is not quite watching. Ambiguously present.

## Spaces

- **The institute** — multi-floor building, overlay system ambient/usually ignored, eastern boulevard outside, weeks of construction dust. The slime passed through the upper floors approximately one hour before section one ends.
- **The lacquer market** — off-stage. Site of Ines's earlier infection. Reachable in memory.
- **The bar / the thing** — basement-level, old-building wood floors, a kitchen frying with dark oil and a herb whose name has not been learned in any language at home. Site of section two.
- **The street between institute and bar** — trams, vendors, kiosk calling specials in two-and-a-half languages. Allspice, exhaust, the sweet-not-sweet of slime residue.

## The slime

- Permeating, non-hostile, brainstem-contact. Leaves a hangover-state: somatic, cognitive, mood-borne, none of these alone.
- Re-infections are possible (Ines).
- Movements are announced by buildings like tram delays, in multiple languages.
- Never visibly rendered. Always registered through air, residue, weight in the body, calibration between people, change in the way dust hangs.

## The Name

- Behind the slime; not its cause, its substrate.
- In the texture of a sound that cannot be heard.
- Architected from intuition, not language.
- Approached but never said. The syntax fails at it.
- Distantly: the grammar of the eventual collective dissolution.

## Calibrations carried forward

- Infected vs. otherwise-infected vs. uninfected — a silent triangulation that goes round any room with more than two of the five.
- Ines registers others' infection-states through small bodily signals (hairs lifted on a wrist, a breath let out, a glass-hand gone still).
