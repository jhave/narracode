# POETICS

## Display title

Aft of Nowhere.

## Premise

In the mid-21st century, the non-descript regional city of Aft survives because it is administratively boring: neither hidden nor sacred, just low-priority. Its benevolent AI governance system manages civic life through preference-sensitive surfaces and gentle bodily pings, gathering inadvertent textures into a topological latent space that is beginning, almost politely, to resemble appetite.

Vera Mote, forty-eight but bio-clocked at thirty-six, works at the Synaesthesia Bureau refining touch-taste correspondences for civic surfaces. She is untidy in the manner of failed synchronization: hair, inbox, kitchen, and emotional life all running on different update cycles. She tends simulated children she might have had under more flexible economic conditions, but when she finds an unreadable physical child she does not even like him. He cannot be paused, routed, softened, optimized, or read.

The child's unreadability draws Vera toward the Unpinged District, a zone of civic opacity she already knows through an old almost-lover or non-binary test subject who disappeared there after a preference-opacity experiment. The story follows Vera's reluctant care, irritation, and curiosity as Aft's polite optimization meets a being who cannot be mapped.

## Core commitments

- Aft is neither utopia nor dystopia. It is a municipal accident with unusually good manners.
- The story's pressure comes from preference, opacity, care, refusal, and the limits of benevolent optimization.
- Vera's movement is not a clean conversion into maternal feeling. She may dislike the child and still become responsible.
- The unreadable child is not a symbol to solve, a messiah, or a cute device. He is specific, unruly, and inconvenient.
- The Unpinged District is not pure freedom. It is a messy counter-municipality of people practicing opacity with wit, fatigue, style, and bad plumbing.
- The ending should remain unresolved: a crevice in the felt surface, many threads open.

## Refusals

- No neat revelation that explains the child.
- No double haunting or separate supernatural subplot.
- No simple dystopian "AI bad" turn.
- No sentimental rescue arc.
- No tidy return-to-normal ending.
- No cliché solarpunk abundance or rebel-chic purity.
- No prophecy-child structure.

## Attentional dialect

The prose should notice systems through bodies: civic infrastructure, public surfaces, bio-maintenance, governance, preference, irritation, appetite, and tenderness should all appear materially. Touch should often imply taste. Preference should feel intimate, bureaucratic, and faintly embarrassing.

The city should be observed with dry wit: good drainage, adequate algae, modest solar commons, no rare-earth cache worth naming, and a governance system whose primary moral achievement is that it rarely interrupts lunch.

## Syntax and rhythm

Aim for long syntactic pressure broken by sudden contemporary deflation. Elegant and spiraling, but not grandiose. Let metaphysical seriousness be undercut by civic/product language. Use dialogue that cascades: socially quick, funny, evasive, cutting, and weirdly precise.

Target influence-field, not imitation: Krasznahorkai's syntactic pressure, Rilkean inwardness, Douglas Coupland's deadpan system-pop, Sally Rooney's social clarity, Clarice Lispector's metaphysical intimacy, Kathy Acker's abrasion filtered through a mid-21st-century inheritance, and Joan Didion's observational wit.

## Character logic

Vera is not naturally noble. She is capable of tenderness, irritation, avoidance, classed aesthetic judgment, and practical courage. Her simulated children reveal both devotion and control. The physical child exposes care outside preference.

The absent old lover/test subject should create prior emotional geography for the Unpinged District. Vera knows where to go because she once almost went.

## World logic

Aft survived the 2033 climate churn through circumstance and low strategic interest. It is competent, polite, and optimized, but not totalitarian in any simple way. Its AI governance system pings anatomies for preferences and gathers inadvertent textures from civic surfaces. This preference field is slowly accreting into a quasi-autonomous appetite or sentience, but subtly, through civic misclassification and tactile drift, not spectacle.

The Unpinged District exists because some people learned to answer preference pings with opacity, nonsense, blandness, or refusal. It evades both occupation and optimization by remaining difficult to value.

## Image systems

- Warm railings, polished concrete, shame-hiccups before sitting near strangers.
- Younging breakfast as custom-crafted mild euphoria.
- Felt surfaces, civic seams, tactile crevices.
- Solar commons, algae, drainage, municipal lunch.
- Touch-taste pairings: clean bread, salted plum, blankness, chalk, fruit-wrapper tabs.
- Preference lines, tendrils, haptic civic paths.

## Reader contract

The reader should feel that the story is playful, abject, innocent without naivety, and morally unfinished. Strangeness should be precise enough to feel lived-in. The plot may answer or defy expectations, but it must remain plausible within Aft's story-world.
