# Character Interiority

## Characters

### Vera Mote

- Public state: Synaesthesia Bureau worker; competent but untidy and socially mis-synchronized.
- Private state: attached to controlled simulations of children; uneasy around forms of care that persist beyond her preference.
- Avoidance: avoids the old path toward the Unpinged District and the person who disappeared there.
- Contradiction: refines subtle civic preference but may not know what she wants when preference fails.
- Irritation: she does not initially like the unreadable child; this dislike should remain active.

### The unreadable child

- Public state: unregistered or unclassifiable physical child.
- Private state: unknown.
- Behavior: disorderly, gummy, too loud at wrong times, weirdly formal at others.
- Narrative pressure: cannot be paused, routed, optimized, or softened into a scheduled encounter.

### Absent almost-lover / non-binary test subject

- Public state: former participant in preference-opacity testing.
- Private state: unknown; may be remembered differently by the Unpinged District.
- Narrative pressure: Vera almost followed them and did not.

## Arc Potentials

- Vera may move from preference-managed tenderness toward reluctant responsibility without becoming purified by it.
- The unreadable child may force Aft and Vera into non-optimized encounter.
- The absent lover/test subject may become less a lost romance than a measure of Vera's old refusal.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points

- Vera chooses not to hand the child over to a system that would protect him by making him legible.
- Vera admits dislike without using it as permission to abandon.
- Vera enters the Unpinged District not as romance or rebellion, but as a practical errand she has avoided for years.
