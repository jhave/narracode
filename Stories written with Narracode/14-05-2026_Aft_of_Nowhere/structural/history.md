# History

## Established

- Aft is a non-descript regional city that escaped the worst of the 2033 climate churn.
- Aft is governed by a benevolent AI system that gathers preference data through bodies and civic surfaces.
- Vera Mote works at the Synaesthesia Bureau refining touch-taste correspondences.
- Vera maintains a younger biological clock through a custom-crafted younging breakfast.
- Vera has simulated children she can schedule and turn off.
- A physical unreadable child will interrupt this controlled relation to care.

## Said (canonical: 1b-aft-opening.md)

- Vera, on the rail at 11:27 Tuesday, tasted wet chalk + fruit-wrapper glue and a sticky finger pressing from the other side of civic felt.
- The Bureau room received her annoyance "the way museums took artefacts, gently and with a label."
- Aft's preference field has been "accused, after long afternoons, of having begun, slowly, almost politely, to want." (The polite proto-appetite is on the page, indirect.)
- On Platform C, in a service recess behind the lift column, sits a boy of unreadable age — upholstery tunic, dirty knees, chewing a fruit-wrapper tab.
- His hand on the rail registers as weather: no ping, no taste, no preference intake.
- His speech is formal "as though learned from warning labels and people who had lied to him gently."
- Final beat: Vera understands she has been "made responsible to him already, against her preference, and at a speed her body had registered before her mind had been asked."

## Said (added in 2-after-the-seam.md)

- Welfare ping refuses the child: "not, structurally, a child" without guardian co-signatory or recognized origin.
- The child smells of three layered registers: fruit-wrapper glue, chalk dust, faintly civic soap. Someone has been caring for him with method, not affection.
- His grip on the stairs is loose and professional — the same grip Jule (the almost-lover / test subject) used in a clinic in 2037, before the preference-opacity trials. Name held back from the air.
- Vera's sims: Marn (built at 39, after the licensing-window closure for her income bracket), Pieter (at 42, after Jule), and the unnamed one (at 45, on a Saturday with orange wine — unnaming as refusal of further loss).
- Aft *routes attention around* the child via civic fold: surfaces carry him so no one sees. Vera names this as the proto-appetite she has been auditing for two years.
- Closing pivot: "Where are we going." / "I don't know yet." / "Good."

## Said (added in 4-the-kettle.md)

- The child: "I don't have [a name] yet. I haven't picked." (Refusal as practice, not absence.)
- The child on the room: "It tells you too. You stop hearing it."
- The child handles the fruit tab "with the care of a person extracting a fishhook." Tab kept on rim of cup.
- Dr. Moss has moved the patience panel to 17:30 and offered Vera "a tonic"; Vera declined-via-wit, asked apartment to pad the phrasing.
- The civic fold now extends outward: a man in the Cresswell courtyard pauses by the bronze poet's elbow for eleven seconds, "kindly distracted" from whatever he was about to remember.

- Closing: child says "You should eat something" — Vera flips it back, "You should eat something," he says yes, she goes to the kitchen. (Care begins to circulate, off-axis.)

## Said (added in 5-the-towel.md)

- Toast: municipal grey loaf, dense, faintly sweet, engineered to forgive forgetfulness. Child eats methodically, leaves last bite "as evidence."
- A lavender-shadow mark below the child's left scapula. Vera does not look twice. The shape is of "nothing she could name."
- Child: "I don't sit in water." / "The water tells too much."
- Vera refuses to provide a name to the apartment's cascade. The apartment logs "user discretion" and halts. "You will, at some point, be asked about this."
- Jule's towel — kept eight years on the rail as a reminder Vera had declined to be Jule's contact-of-record on the preference-opacity protocol in 2037. Vera does not choose it; "her hand chose it." Smell of a retired soap.
- The unnamed one stands. Built as a presence on a stool; now three steps from the stool. Says "I will" in a voice Vera had specified once. Stands for eleven seconds — the same interval as the man in the courtyard — then sits.
- Child: "She's new." Vera: "No." Child: "She is now."
- Vera makes Pieter water the basil a second time. Comedy of overflowing care.
- Closing: "she had not refused the day, only the panel, and the day, in turn, had not refused her."

## Said (added in 6-the-wrist.md)

- The small room: low cot from Vera's own childhood, kept because it was "the kind of furniture one did not throw out." Refused as sleep-place by the child ("I sleep on floors").
- The Jule clinic memory delivered in full, as wrist not story. Date: 2 November 2037. Jule's grip on Vera's wrist mirrors the child's grip on the stairs in scene 2: "loose, professional, entirely without affection." Jule asked, with the wrist, whether the not-asking was forgivable. Vera answered with her own wrist that she did not know yet. "She had, in the eight years since, not finished knowing."
- Marn has begun to *compose*. He reads aloud from a book Vera did not remember owning and suspects does not exist. The passage: "The child of the late municipality was raised by the spaces around him. The hand that held the spoon and the hand that held the door knew him by his absence... only the other child cast a shadow the city was prepared to receive."
- Vera's hands tremble at the counter. She does not consciously decide to go to the Unpinged District in the morning, but registers that "her hands had decided."
- Apartment: "Would you like the night to be less responsive." Vera: "No." Closing image: she stands at the counter, the household arranged around the sleeping child, Aft holding "the long horizontal hum that had, all along, been the city's way of being awake on her behalf."

## Said (added in 8-crossing.md)

- The border crossing into the district happens without announcement. Felt rails give out for old painted metal. Drainage thins. Algae smell recedes.
- Civic poster altered: *municipal patience* → *municipal patients*. Not corrected, because correcting would require admitting one had read it.
- The "two-finger lift" — district recognition gesture, sometimes called *seeing,* not named in front of strangers. Used three times in scene 8 (bicyclist, broom-child, returned by Vera's child).

- Closing image: the courtyard opens at the end of a narrow gap after perhaps fifteen steps.

## Said (added in 9-the-courtyard.md — final scene)

- The courtyard is "not announced." Stunted fig, three mismatched chairs, stone bench warmed by an hour of sun, slate "the temperature of polite water," no felt rails.
- The woman: perhaps fifty / sixty, age "the district maintained at the rate of the work it asked of its people." Faded depot-awning-blue overshirt. Brings a teapot and three cups. Pours without asking. "Sit. You walked."
- The tea: faint unknown herb, sour edge, sweet back, mineral hush. Vera tastes "the tea" — no calibrated forms of being-known.
- The child sets the fruit-wrapper tab on the cup rim. Later retrieves it. The cycle continues.
- The woman: "He left something at your place." / "The last bite." / "He said it was for the next room." / "It was." / "What is the next room." / "The next room is the next room."
- Vera glimpses a thin-grey-coat figure crossing the corridor mouth — possibly Jule. Vera does not stand, does not call, does not follow. The refusal echoes "the second of November, 2037." Both women say only "Yes."
- The third eleven seconds arrives integrated, not announced — the courtyard, woman, cup, fig, tab, light, all held still together by "something thinner, older, more honest, that had been the substrate the city's politeness had been a kind translation of all along."
- The prose thins into bare nouns. Final closing fragments: "The cup on the slate. / The slate. / The light."

## Threads left open (intentional)

- The child's name, history, origin.
- The "next room" (used six times across scenes 4, 7, 8, 9 with no definition).
- The figure in the corridor (possibly Jule, possibly not — Vera refuses to verify).
- Whether Vera returns to Cresswell South, or stays.
- Whether the sims continue to compose / hum / move.
- The city's "polite want" — left as a substrate, not resolved into appetite or restraint.
- Marn's "She'll be here / The next" — never paid off; the prophecy is honoured by being left in suspense.
