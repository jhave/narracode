# Reader State

## Current Understanding

- The reader should understand Aft as competent, polite, lucky, and morally incomplete.
- The reader should understand Vera as intelligent, funny, embodied, professionally sensitive, and emotionally avoidant.
- The unreadable child should feel like an interruption to both civic systems and Vera's curated tenderness.

## Expectations

- The reader may expect the unreadable child to expose the limits of preference governance.
- The reader may expect the Unpinged District to answer questions, but it should complicate them instead.
- The reader may expect Vera to learn to love the child; the story should resist making that simple.

## Plausible Defiance Paths

- The child remains unexplained and still changes Vera's practical obligations.
- The Unpinged District is neither solution nor sanctuary, only another social field.
- Vera does not become good; she becomes less able to excuse non-care.

## Productive Mystery

- Why the child cannot be read.
- What the preference field is becoming.
- What happened to the absent almost-lover/test subject.

## Accidental Confusion Risk

- Too many speculative concepts before the child appears may dilute the story.
- The governance system must be legible as benevolent but insufficient.
