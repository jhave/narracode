# Motifs

## Active

- Civic surfaces: railings, benches, felt, polished concrete, doors.
- Touch-taste correspondences: clean bread, salted plum, nothingness, chalk, algae, euphoric younging.
- Inadvertent textures: withdrawal, hesitation, shame, contact, refusal.
- Administrative boringness: lunch, drainage, algae, low-priority survival.
- Readability and unreadability: pings, opacity, preference, civic legibility.
- Simulated children versus persistent physical child.

## Transformations

- Preference should transform from comfort into intrusion, then into an inadequate category for care.
- Civic kindness should transform from admirable competence into lonely over-asking.

## Avoid Overuse

- Do not overstate the AI's appetite.
- Do not make every object symbolic.
- Avoid repeating "unreadable" as a label without new concrete consequences.
