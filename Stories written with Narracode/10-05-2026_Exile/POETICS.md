# POETICS.md — project commitments

*Initiated 2026-05-10. Working title: Exile (May10). Loop: 0.*

## Premise

A human has been cast out from an enclave for using AI after it has been banned. An anecdote of exile in the style of W.G. Sebald.

## Uploads (named, source: training-data)

- **W. G. Sebald (late works)** — texture of melancholy, geographical wandering, slow accretion of historical and personal memory, the haunting presence of the past in physical objects and landscapes. The prose should feel like a slow walk through ruins.
- **Yiyun Li ("A Thousand Years of Good Prayers")** — relational density. The unsaid space between individuals. Pain and history masked by quiet, polite, or seemingly ordinary interactions. Deep restraint in expressing grief.
- **László Krasznahorkai** — sentence-architecture. A recursive, looping, obsessive return to clauses that build a claustrophobic or inescapable mental state. **Crucial constraint**: without Krasznahorkai's extreme sentence-length. The architecture is present in the rhythm and the subordinate clauses, but punctuated by shorter breaths.

## Structural feel

- **Pacing**: Slow, accumulative, meditative.
- **Perspective**: Deep internal focalization, colored by the withdrawal from AI symbiosis.

## Refusals

- **Refuse cyberpunk/dystopian tropes**: No neon, no high-octane techno-thriller elements, no focus on the "action" of the banishment or the rebellion. The technology is a ghost, an absence.
- **Refuse sentimentality**: The loss of the AI should not be mourned like a dead pet, but like a lost lobe of the brain. The exile should not be heroic.
- **Refuse expository world-building**: Do not explain how the enclave works or the political history of the AI ban. Reveal the world only through the specific, narrow constraints of the protagonist's immediate perception and memory.

## Dialect notes

- **Syntactic strain**: Sentences should fold back on themselves, examining their own propositions, but end before they exhaust the reader.
- **Characteristic noticings**: The protagonist should notice the *silence* of objects, the lack of algorithmic mediation, the raw uninterpreted nature of the physical world.
