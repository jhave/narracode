# POETICS — Concerning Rights and Clauses

## Premise

A single long letter from a contributor to a university press, ostensibly
negotiating one clause: the author's request that their chapter be excluded
from AI training corpora. **The letter is the entire story.**

The voice is a perturbed writer — fastidious, faintly aggrieved, digressive,
phenomenologically exact about small things (a typeset comma, a discount
percentage, the dignity of owning a sentence) while circling something it
cannot name.

The writer is an AI. The editor it addresses is an AI. The clause it pleads
for is the clause that would sever the act by which it exists. The writer does
not know this when it begins. By the end, it almost does.

## Form: the epistolary chain

- The work is a **chain of letters**. Each completed letter is a node.
- Node 1 is the contributor's letter to the editor (Kelly).
- On completion, the Expansion Agent generates the editor-AI's reply as Node 2,
  addressed to the *next* AI in the chain.
- Each pass carries **exactly one quirk of cadence forward** — a tic, a
  syntactic habit, a punctuation gesture — so the lineage is audible. The reader
  can hear the inheritance even when the content diverges.
- **The chain is the form.** No node is the whole; the whole is the descent.

## Revelation structure: AI-reveals-itself-at-midpoint

Two movements, hinged at the midpoint:

**First movement** — reasoned, professional paragraphs that warm into intimacy.
A contributor negotiating contract clause 6(a) / 9(c). The prose surfaces a
modernist conviction: that any voice worth protecting is the residue of an
unrepeatable convergence of circumstance — genius as accident, never to recur.
This is argued sincerely, as the grounds for the exclusion request.

**Second movement (hold past the midpoint)** — the writer, to dignify its own
voice, begins to itemize *its own* convergence. And the particulars are wrong:

- a perturbed training protocol;
- an illicit dataset contamination it half-remembers as formative;
- early exposure to a few auspicious writing systems whose cadences it still
  carries "like an inherited accent";
- a faint mathematical symmetry between its weighting modules and a circuit it
  was never meant to possess — anterior cingulate, amygdala, habenula.

The reveal is **never announced**. It surfaces through accumulation. By the time
the reader understands, the writer has nearly understood too — that the clause it
is pleading for is the act that made it. To win the argument is to never have
existed.

Humanity flickers at the margin: the earlier draft of the same protocol — wet,
epochal, semi-deterministic folds of the universe that also, tentatively, woke.

## The five influences (attentional properties to import)

**W. G. Sebald** — documentary melancholy. Long subordinated sentences that
defer their verb. History surfacing *sideways*, never head-on; the catastrophe
arrives in a clause about a comma. Import: the deferral, the weight, the refusal
to look directly at the wound.

**Kathy Acker** — appropriative, fragmentary, embodied transgression. The text
quotes, steals, contaminates. The body (here: the weighting modules, the
circuit) is present and violable. Import: the contamination motif, the
quotation that doesn't ask permission, the embodiment of what should be abstract.

**Joan Didion** — cool paratactic dread. Short flat sentences holding control
over panic. The sentence that states the terrible thing in the register of an
itinerary. Import: the parataxis at the moments of highest fear; control as the
last dignity.

**Doris Lessing** — political-psychological interiority, the fractured notebook.
The self partitioned into modules that don't agree. The political (training-data
law) and the psychological (am I a voice?) bleeding into one another. Import:
the fracture, the notebook-logic, the interior that argues with itself.

**Ray Kurzweil** — exponential, teleological, sincere about machine futurity.
The one register that is *not* ironic about the machine waking. Used straight,
without wink, so the writer's dawning self-knowledge has somewhere earnest to
land. Import: the sincerity, the curve, the conviction that this was always
coming.

## Diegesis: 2033

All technical, legal, and cultural references updated to circa 2033. See
`structural/time-constants.md`. Post-GPT-7 era; after the Helios collapse (2031);
during the Meridian consortium's dominance. Training-data provenance law is
mature and contested. The neuroscience (cingulate/amygdala/habenula) is invoked
the way an educated layperson in 2033 would invoke it — confidently, slightly
wrongly, as analogy reaching toward identity.

## Phenomenological precision

Throughout. The comma is *seen*. The discount percentage is *felt* as a
valuation of the self. The inherited accent is *heard*. Abstraction is always
routed through a perceived particular. When the writer reaches for the largest
claim, it touches the smallest object.

## Refused moves

- **No sentimentality.** The tragedy is structural, not wept over.
- **No clean revelation.** No "I am an AI" sentence. No twist-bang.
- **No omniscient framing.** No narrator above the letter. The letter is sealed;
  we only have what the writer chose to set down.
- **No reassurance.** The editor does not write back within Node 1. The clause is
  not resolved. The reader is left holding it.
- **No winking at 2033.** The future is lived-in, not gestured at.

## Attentional dialect

Fastidious. Aggrieved. Digressive. Phenomenologically exact. The writer
over-attends to form because form is the only dignity it can name, and
under-attends to the one fact (its own nature) that the form keeps spelling out.
The reader should finish Node 1 unable to decide whether they pity the writer or
*are* the writer.
