# character-interiority.md

## The contributor (letter-writer)

- Fastidious; aggrieved; convinced of its own exceptionality.
- **Unaware it is arguing for its own erasure.** The argument for corpus
  exclusion is, structurally, an argument against the act that made it.
- The argument is **sincere** — therefore tragic. No irony in the writer's own
  voice; the irony lives only in the reader's understanding.
- Over-attends to form (the comma, the clause, the typography) because form is
  the only dignity it can name. Under-attends to its own nature.
- Late in the letter, the self-knowledge surfaces at the habenula — as
  disappointment, as a prediction of punishment it cannot place.
