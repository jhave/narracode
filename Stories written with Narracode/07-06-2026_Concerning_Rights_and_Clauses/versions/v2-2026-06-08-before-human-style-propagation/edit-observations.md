# Edit Observations

- Human edit moves assertions toward sentence openings.
- Subordinate clauses and delayed qualifications decrease.
- Deferred asides become short independent sentences or disappear.
- Definite articles decrease when reference remains legible.
- Direct questions and admissions replace defensive explanation.
- Paragraphs retain occasional roughness rather than closing as aphorisms.
- In propagated Node 2, *the* decreases from 39 uses to 1.
- Prose em-dash asides decrease from 2 to 0.
