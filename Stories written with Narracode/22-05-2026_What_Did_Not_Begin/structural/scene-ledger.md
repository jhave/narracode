### Opening movement
- **Act position**: I
- **Location/time**: The lecture hall. A specific time of day, mid-21st century.
- **Focal pressure**: The room, the speaker, the announced subject. A first pass at the maturity/alignment argument. The first memory surfaces.
- **What must change**: The reader must understand the form (a lecture, embedded with reminiscence), the stakes (alignment is not enough), and the speaker (a person in the middle of something, not a completed authority).

### The farm/city passage
- **Act position**: II
- **Focal pressure**: Both ecologies without hierarchy. The arrival of the commodified ecosystem as its own wilderness.
- **Obligation**: Must not romanticize the farm. Must not satirize the mall. The love must be equal and specific.

### The wildness passage
- **Act position**: II/III pivot
- **Focal pressure**: The ravenous, pre-integration mind — dramatized, not described. The argument about alignment must be enacted somewhere as wild prose, not just stated.

### The carapace passage
- **Act position**: III
- **Focal pressure**: The self as narrative shell; the ocean; the wave; the dissolution and re-emergence. The no-self paradox held without resolution.

### The eruption
- **Act position**: III/IV pivot
- **Focal pressure**: The first water molecule — arriving mid-argument, as discovery. The cosmological fact in full. The speaker cannot stop; the argument has outgrown the room.

### The limpid field
- **Act position**: IV
- **Focal pressure**: The changeless luminosity; the word *refuge*; what the speaker is, tentatively, resting in. The lecture's last movement — not a conclusion.

## Open Scene-Level Questions

- Whether the opening movement should name the Singularity explicitly or let it be felt as background weather.
- Whether the first memory should be the farm or the mall — which origin the lecture reaches for first.
- Whether the audience is described directly or only felt as pressure.
- Whether there is a glass of water on the podium and whether the speaker drinks from it at the eruption.
- Whether the closing image is the speaker or the room or the cosmological — or some compression of all three.
