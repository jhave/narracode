# History

## Established

- The academic job market has been gutted by chatbots distributing expert knowledge. The lecturer's appointment is a miracle.
- The AI Singularity is near-fact; researchers are still arguing about ethical alignment.
- The lecturer grew up on a farm and loves it: moss, old oak, fern, wild grass, the rhythm of seasons.
- The lecturer also loves the city and the mall: glazed floors, air conditioning, perfume, dense signifiers, the commodified ecosystem.
- He calls the mall a "commodified ecosystem of culture, teeming with dense signifiers."
- His research subject: the necessity for maturity.
- His argument: alignment = external ethical constraint, necessary but insufficient; maturity = internal integration of ethics; until ethics become cellular there is a ravenous wildness to the mind.
- The first water molecule: arose ~12 billion years ago from energy potentials; did not have a beginning, it just arose; cosmic water found over 12 billion light years away holds 140 trillion times the mass of Earth's oceans.
