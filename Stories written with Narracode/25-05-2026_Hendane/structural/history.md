# HISTORY — Pre-story facts

Facts established before page one. The narrator may surface any of these; the Compositional agent should treat them as load-bearing background, not all of which will appear in text.

## Hallvard's biography (compact)

- Born 1986 in Bergen. Mother a librarian (retired, lives in Landås, mentioned once). Father a hospital orderly (deceased, 2011, mentioned obliquely).
- Bergen Katedralskole, graduated 2005. Mediocre grades in everything except Norwegian (excellent) and math (terrible).
- UiB, BA in administration, 2008. He liked the building more than the subject.
- 2009: Six months in Trondheim, an attempted move that he aborted. He refers to it as "the experiment." He does not elaborate.
- 2010–2014: Worked in Bergen kommune administration. Boring, in a non-productive way.
- 2014: Joined NERV. Boring, in a *productive* way, which he found tolerable.
- 2017: Promoted to administrasjonskoordinator.
- 2019: Met Marit. Moved into the Åsane unit.
- 2021: Marit moved out. They were not married. There is no acrimony. They send each other a birthday SMS.
- 2024: Bought the book *A Philosophy of Boredom* by Lars Svendsen at Norli Galleriet. Read it twice. Recognized himself, was not displeased.
- 2025 (spring): First experimented with generative AI image tools — Midjourney-shape, Stable-Diffusion-shape — to make a cover for a NERV annual report. The director rejected the cover ("a bit too much, Hallvard"). He kept the tools.
- 2025 (summer): Began noticing the mold on his bathroom grout. Tried Jif. Tried bleach. Tried Zalo and a sponge. Zalo worked. He filmed it on his phone, alone, to send to no one. He watched the clip back. He felt something.

## NERV's history (compact)

- Founded 2003 as a cross-institutional research initiative. The exact mandate has been revised four times. Current mandate text is on a laminated card in the lobby; no one has read it in five years.
- ~40 staff. Funding stable, project-based. Reports annually to a board that meets in Oslo and is therefore mildly resented.
- Hallvard arrived in 2014, has outlasted three directors. Solveig Tveit is the fourth.
- The institute occupies the sixth floor of a glass building in the Marineholmen / Nygårdshøyden area. Rents are subsidized.
- Coffee machine replaced in 2022. The previous one is a folk memory.

## Bergen's role (compact, as fact-set)

- Population ~290,000. Norway's second city. Considers itself the first.
- Annual rainfall ~2,250mm. Days with measurable precipitation: 240+.
- Bergensbanen runs to Oslo in ~6.5 hours. Hallvard has taken it four times in his life.
- Bryggen is a UNESCO site. Fløibanen is a funicular. Ulriken is a mountain with a cable car. Fisketorget is the fish market that locals avoid in summer because it is for tourists.
- Bybanen, the light rail, opened 2010. Hallvard's commute since 2014.
- Local newspaper: Bergens Tidende ("BT"). Local dialect: Bergensk, a variant of Vestlandsk.
- The Bergen-Oslo tension is real, structural, and weather-dependent.

## The channel's birth conditions

These are the specific pre-conditions that made *Hendane* possible. The Compositional agent should treat them as fated, not arbitrary:

1. **The mold.** A real, persistent strip of black mildew along the grout between the third and fourth tile from the bathroom floor. Hallvard had tried four products. The fifth, Zalo + soft sponge + heat, worked.
2. **The phone.** A two-year-old phone with a good enough camera and a kitchen counter that is the right height for a low-angle shot of hands.
3. **The AI tools.** Already on his laptop from the NERV report incident. Already paid for, monthly subscription auto-renewing.
4. **The boredom.** Years of accumulated, technically-administered boredom. Svendsen's book named it for him.
5. **The dialect.** Hallvard thinks in Bergensk. The voice that writes the text overlays is his own inner monologue, lightly edited.
6. **The yard.** Small, flat, photographically tractable. Mostly grey sky. Almost zero foot traffic on a Sunday.
7. **The solitude.** No one to ask what he is doing. No one to disapprove. No one to encourage him into something else.

## What Hallvard does not know at story-start

- That he will become *Hendane*.
- That a recommender algorithm will surface his work in waves.
- That a classifier exists which will, eventually, correctly identify what he is doing.
- That the act of being unmasked will, when it happens, be administered by him voluntarily and feel like joy.
- That the philosophy of boredom has a lateral exit.

## What the reader has not been told yet at story-start

- That Hallvard has the AI tools already. (Revealed in Act II.)
- That Marit existed. (Mentioned briefly in Act I or II.)
- That NERV's research will never be named. (The reader will gradually realize this; the narrator may eventually wink at it.)
- That the platform will ban the channel. (Foreshadowed; the *how* is the suspense.)
- That Hallvard will reveal himself voluntarily. (Held until Act V.)
