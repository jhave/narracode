# TIME CONSTANTS — *Hendane*

The story takes place across roughly one calendar year, weighted to autumn-through-spring. The Bergen weather year is the substrate.

## Story-time spine

- **Story opens:** Late September. Hallvard is 39. NERV's autumn cycle has just begun. Rain count for the year so far: 168 days, per Hallvard's mental tally.
- **First video (private, unposted):** Sunday, mid-October, ~22:14.
- **First video (posted):** Sunday, late October, ~22:30.
- **Channel viral in Bergen:** mid-November (week 4).
- **First Bergens Tidende mention:** early December (week 7).
- **NERV colleague shows Hallvard a *Hendane* video:** mid-December (week 9), in the lift, the director Solveig with her phone.
- **Reddit r/bergen "is this AI" thread:** late December (week 10), holiday lull.
- **Bergens Tidende feature, "Kven er Hendane?":** mid-January (week 13).
- **Rain-gutter video (peak):** late January (week 14).
- **Platform classifier flag / ban:** first week of February (week 15–16).
- **Appeal denied:** 72 hours after flag. Mid-February.
- **Hallvard's empty kitchen evening:** the same week. One full evening, ~four hours, no filming.
- **First *Avkledd* video, mid-upload:** late February. Story ends here.

Total story-time: approximately 21 weeks. Late September to late February.

## Hallvard's workweek

- Monday–Friday at NERV, 08:30–16:00, walking distance from Bybanen.
- Lunch at 11:30 with Ingvild on Thursdays.
- Weekends are *Hendane* time. Saturday: scripting, shopping (Kiwi in Åsane Senter, occasionally Obs! for bulk, Bunnpris for the impulse buys). Sunday: filming, editing, posting around 22:00 for the Monday-morning Bergen commute scroll.

## Channel lifespan (exact)

- **Day 0:** First posted video.
- **Day 7:** ~600 followers.
- **Day 42:** ~12,000.
- **Day 70:** ~140,000.
- **Day 98:** ~480,000.
- **Day 105–112:** Classifier flag, appeal, denial, ban.
- **Day ~120:** *Avkledd* channel opens.

## Key durations

- **A single video shoot:** ~2 hours including setup, ~30 minutes filming, ~90 minutes editing/AI-background generation. Hallvard works through the AI pipeline with a fluency that grows visibly across Act II.
- **The grout-cleaning shot:** 12 seconds of footage, looped and ramped.
- **The lift ride with Solveig:** 9 seconds. Hallvard considers it the longest lift ride of his life.
- **The empty kitchen evening, Act IV:** 22:14 to 02:11, his own time-stamped recollection.
- **The first *Avkledd* upload bar:** 0% to 67% complete in the final paragraph of the novella.

## The platform ban as event horizon

The ban is the structural climax. Everything before tilts toward it; everything after refracts away from it. The reader knows it is coming from Act I (the poetics may foreshadow, the narrator may foreshadow — Vonnegut's permission). The dramatic tension is not *if* but *how it lands on Hallvard*.

Before the ban, time accelerates (week-counts, view-counts). After the ban, time decelerates (an evening becomes hours, an upload becomes minutes, a final paragraph becomes a percentage bar).

## Weather chronology

- Sept–Oct: rain, mild, dim.
- Nov: rain, cold, dark by 16:00.
- Dec: rain, occasional sleet, lights up on Bryggen and at Festplassen for jul.
- Jan: rain, occasional snow that does not stick, the year's lowest light.
- Feb: rain, the first hint that the days are no longer shortening.
- The ban occurs in the week that the days first lengthen, measurably. This is not pointed out by the narrator. Only the reader who is paying attention notices.

## NERV's institutional calendar (atmospheric)

- Mid-September: autumn semester start.
- Mid-October: budget revision.
- Late November: external review (Hallvard prepares the binder).
- Mid-December: julebord (Christmas dinner) at a restaurant in Sandviken. Hallvard attends, leaves at 21:00.
- Mid-January: spring semester. Hallvard updates the conference room booking system.
- February: a grant deadline. Hallvard is in the office until 18:00 three nights in a row. The ban arrives during this stretch.
