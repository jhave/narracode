# GRAPH — Characters, Entities, Relations

## A. Primary character

### Hallvard Nygaard, 39
- **Role at NERV:** Administrator-coordinator. Title roughly: *administrasjonskoordinator*. He is the person who knows the budget line items, the cleaning rota, the badge-system password, and who has the conference room booked when. He is not a researcher.
- **Origin:** Born Bergen, grew up in Landås, schooled at Bergen Katedralskole, BA in administration from UiB (University of Bergen). Has never lived outside Vestland for more than four months (a 2009 stint in Trondheim he refers to as "the experiment").
- **Lives:** Detached row-house unit in Åsane, the northern Bergen suburb that is mostly a roundabout and an IKEA. Front yard is small, partly gravel, with a green plastic chair. View: a neighbor's hedge, a corner of grey sky.
- **Civil status:** Single. Lived with someone (Marit, never named in the text) until 2021. No children. No dog. A plant called *Berit* that survives on neglect.
- **Body:** 1.79m, beige, the precise build of a man who walks to Bybanen and back and does nothing else.
- **Surface:** Polite, dry, mildly funny in meetings if you listen for it. Wears the same five shirts in rotation. Never raises his voice. Has been described in his performance review as "remarkably reliable."
- **Interior:** A philosopher of boredom in the Lars-Svendsen sense (Hallvard owns the book, has read it twice). Not depressed; *bored* in a specific, almost technical way.
- **Hands:** Long-fingered, slightly chapped from Bergen winters. The protagonist of the channel.

## B. NERV — Nordic Environmental Research Vestland

- **Location:** A repurposed building in the Marineholmen / Nygårdshøyden corridor, near UiB. Glass entrance, beige carpet, sixth-floor view of rain.
- **Staff:** ~40 people. Researchers (~25), admin (~6, Hallvard among them), technicians (~6), a director (Solveig Tveit), a comms person (Aksel), a cleaner (Mona, who Hallvard considers the only honest worker in the building).
- **Research:** Deliberately unspecified. Some combination of environment, oceanography, climate modeling, ecological policy. Funded by a mix of the Norwegian Research Council, EU Horizon, and a private foundation no one quite remembers the name of.
- **Tone:** Competent, sub-vibrant. Coffee machine works. Printer mostly works. Lunch is at 11:30.

### NERV staff (named, minor)
- **Solveig Tveit, ~55** — Director. Likes Hallvard because Hallvard makes problems go away. Will, in Act III, show him a *Hendane* video on her phone in the lift, asking if he's seen it.
- **Aksel Berg, ~31** — Communications. Sleuths the channel in his off-hours. Posts on Vestlendingen.no under a pseudonym Hallvard could guess but doesn't.
- **Mona, ~60s** — Cleaner. Hallvard greets her by name every morning. She greets him by name. Neither knows the other's last name.
- **Ingvild Rasmussen, ~42** — Senior researcher. Eats lunch with Hallvard on Thursdays. Mentions, in Act III, that her teenager has tried the mold-removal recipe and that it worked.

## C. *Hendane* — the channel (Act II–IV)

- **Platform:** A short-form video platform — TikTok-shaped, though the text may keep it slightly generic. Cross-posted reels to Instagram-shaped platform.
- **Tagline:** *Ingen dagar utan tips* [no days without tips, the obvious echo of Bergen's *no days without rain*]
- **Format:** ~45-second videos. Only Hallvard's hands. No face, no voice (text overlays in Bergensk with bracketed English glosses for the international viewers who arrive in Act III).
- **Aesthetic:** AI-anonymized backgrounds (Norse folklore tableaux, medieval Bryggen, fjordscapes), sometimes AI-replaced hands (a child's hands, an old woman's hands, a fisherman's hands). Foreground products are real. Soundtrack: a single hardanger-fiddle loop the creator did not pay for.
- **Cadence:** First video posted on a Sunday in late autumn (Act I close). Three videos per week. Total run: ~16 weeks before ban.
- **Growth:** 0 → ~600 followers in week 2. ~12,000 by week 6. ~140,000 by week 10. ~480,000 by week 14, the week of the ban.
- **Top videos:** (1) Mold-on-grout with Zalo (the founding video, ~3M views), (2) Stabburet kaviar concentric circle installation (~6M views), (3) Rain-gutter deconstruction at golden hour (~8M views, the one that gets him into Bergens Tidende).

## D. *Hendane: Avkledd* — the reveal channel (Act V)

- **Platform:** Same platform (he is not banned as a person, only the channel handle).
- **First video:** Hallvard shot in mid-frame, face partly visible, narrating the Zalo recipe in plain Bergensk dialect, on-screen text in English. Behind him: the actual Åsane kitchen. Foreground: the actual mold, the actual sponge.
- **Tagline:** None yet. He has not decided. The story ends mid-upload.

## E. Bergen community entities

- **Bergens Tidende (BT):** Local newspaper. Runs the Act III feature: *"Kven er Hendane?"* Reporter is **Linn Hauge, ~34**, a name that may appear once.
- **Vestlendingen.no:** A regional forum (semi-fictional, in the tradition of real Norwegian regional forums). Long threads of community sleuthing. Aksel posts there.
- **Bergen Facebook groups:** "Bergen for alle," "Du veit du er frå Bergen når...," etc. Memes propagate. Someone makes a knitted pattern of Hallvard's hands. Someone else makes a Halloween costume.
- **Reddit r/bergen:** Smaller, English-tilting, more skeptical. The first to suspect AI involvement, week 8.
- **Local podcasters:** A two-episode arc on a Bergen culture podcast (*Byluft* — "city air"). Hallvard listens to it on Bybanen and does not breathe for one stop.

## F. Platform / algorithmic entities

- **The recommender:** Surfaces *Hendane* to Bergen-tagged users in week 2, to all of Norway by week 5, internationally by week 8.
- **The content classifier:** A model that scores videos for "predominantly AI-generated content." Threshold appears to be around 0.7. *Hendane's* videos score, on Hallvard's later calculation, between 0.81 and 0.94.
- **The ToS clause:** Section 4.3.b, "synthetic or substantially synthetic media without clear labeling." Hallvard reads it twice in Act IV.
- **The appeal queue:** Form submitted, auto-response received, human review denied within 72 hours with a four-sentence template reply. The story names this mechanism precisely.

## G. Domestic / object entities (these recur enough to graph)

- **The Åsane kitchen** — site of most filming. North-facing window. Fluorescent under-cabinet light Hallvard hates but uses because the colour temperature is right for the camera.
- **The front yard** — site of the rain-gutter video. Gravel, plastic chair, hedge, sky.
- **The Bybanen** — Hallvard's commute. Site of much thinking.
- **Berit, the plant** — a pothos. Acknowledged in passing.
- **The Zalo bottle** — green, recognizable, load-bearing.
- **The Stabburet kaviar tube** — pink, recognizable, load-bearing.
