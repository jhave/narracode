# POETICS — *Hendane*

A novella-in-acts about a bored Bergen administrator who becomes an anonymous handcraft-influencer and is undone, not by exposure, but by an algorithm doing its job.

## Display synopsis

A bored administrator at a Bergen environmental research institute launches a faceless social media channel, demonstrating mundane household tips using his hands against beautiful, AI-generated historical backdrops. As his hands become an anonymous local sensation, he is forced to navigate the community's curiosity and a platform classifier that flags his human labor as synthetic. A quiet, dryly comedic, and deeply textured Bergen novella about digital craft, municipal rain, and the quiet joy of being unmasked.

---

## 1. Premise (compressed)

Hallvard Nygaard, 39, runs a research institute (NERV) competently to the point of dissociation. To survive, he begins a faceless YouTube/TikTok-adjacent channel — *Hendane* (Bergensk: "The Hands") — where only his hands appear, demonstrating household tips with products from Kiwi, Obs!, Bunnpris. AI tools anonymize the videos: backgrounds become Norse folklore tableaux, his hands become other ages of hands, his Åsane front yard becomes a medieval Bergen fjordscape. The channel goes viral in Bergen, the community tries to identify him, a platform's content-classifier eventually flags it as "predominantly AI-generated" and bans it. He grieves. He launches a second channel, *Hendane: Avkledd* (Unmasked), which reveals everything. End.

## 2. Core commitments

- **Lateral comedy, not ascent.** Hallvard does not transcend. He moves sideways. The shape of the story is a step, not a staircase.
- **Vonnegutian directness.** The narrator may speak to the reader. Short chapters. Rhythmic refrains. Structural irony permitted, sentimentality refused.
- **Miranda-July interiority.** Domestic objects (a tube of kaviar, a sponge, a USB-C cable) are permitted to become strange. Sentences are allowed to lurch.
- **Doctorow-grade tech literacy.** The platform is a real platform, with real classifiers, ToS pages, appeal queues, and shadow-ban heuristics. We name the mechanism, not just the feeling.
- **Bergensk vernacular as texture, not thesis.** Dialect phrases appear in italics, in English transliteration/translation, bracketed in spirit-of-the-phrase rather than literal gloss. Example: *greit nok* [fine, whatever, good enough, the sound of a Bergen shrug].
- **Rain as condition, not weather.** Rain is not symbolic of sadness. It is the medium in which Bergensers live. It is closer to gravity than to grief.
- **The research is opaque on principle.** What NERV does will never be specified. This is a load-bearing refusal; it makes Hallvard's boredom universal.

## 3. Refusals (explicit, hard)

- No romance subplot. No will-they-won't-they. No erotic charge directed at any other character.
- No villain. The platform is not evil; the classifier is not a Kafka castle; it is a regression model behaving like a regression model. The community sleuths are not pursuers; they are admirers who love a mystery.
- No heroic resolution of NERV's research. The institute remains a beige hum.
- No tourism-brochure Bergen. The fjords are mostly not visible from Åsane. Bryggen is in the rain. The supermarkets are fluorescent. The Bergensbanen exists but we do not romanticize it.
- No Nynorsk-vs-Bokmål linguistic essay. Dialect is texture, not pedagogy.
- No actual Norwegian sentences. English with bracketed dialect translation only.
- No moral conclusion about AI. The story holds the question open: the platform's classifier was right; the work was still real.
- No tidy second-act collapse where Hallvard is exposed publicly. Exposure is voluntary, in Act V, and it is joyous.

## 4. Attentional dialect (where the camera lives)

- Often **hand-level**. The reader's eye is at the height of a kitchen counter, the rim of a sink, a roll of paper towels.
- **Object-first sentences.** A tube of Stabburet kaviar enters a scene before Hallvard does.
- **The interior monologue is dry, observational, slightly behind events.** Hallvard understands what is happening to him approximately one beat after the reader does.
- **Direct address to the reader** is allowed, sparingly, in the Vonnegut register: *"You will want to know whether his colleagues recognized him. They did not. Not yet."*
- **Lists are permitted as paragraphs.** Bergen is a city that makes lists of itself.

## 5. Bergen dialect translation convention

Dialect phrases appear in italic Norwegian and then in brackets in English, where the bracket is not a definition but a *vibe gloss* — what the phrase does in the room. Examples:

- *greit nok* [fine, whatever, good enough, the sound of a Bergen shrug]
- *jøss!* [wow, but smaller and wetter]
- *søren* [a mild expletive Hallvard's mother would have permitted at the dinner table]
- *heilt teit* [completely stupid, with affection]
- *korsen gjekk det?* [how did it go, in the dialect that makes Oslo people lean in]
- *eg veit ikkje* [I don't know, in the Bergen way that means I do know but am declining]
- *ingen dagar utan regn* [no days without rain — Bergen's structural truth]
- *ingen dagar utan tips* [no days without tips — *Hendane's* tagline, an obvious echo]

Bokmål appears only when Hallvard is at work or writing email. Bergensk appears when he is alone or filming.

## 6. Named style uploads (for the Compositional agent)

When in doubt, channel one of these four:

- **Vonnegut** — short chapters, refrain, "and so it goes" rhythm, structural irony, reader address, dark-light comedy.
- **Miranda July** — domestic strangeness, the body's tiny embarrassments, sentences that lurch by one word into surreal territory and then walk back.
- **Cory Doctorow** — name the platform mechanism, name the classifier, name the ToS clause, do not let "algorithm" be a magic word.
- **Bergensk vernacular (Staalesen-adjacent)** — the dry Bergen voice, the working-class self-mockery, rain as condition, Bergen-vs-Oslo as existential backdrop.

Blend by act (see section 7). No single voice should dominate; the blend itself is the texture.

## 7. Act breakdown

### Act I — *Kjedsomhetens Filosofi* (The Philosophy of Boredom)
**Voice blend:** Vonnegut-forward, Bergensk-undertow.
Hallvard at NERV. The institute. Bergen at his window. The competence-as-dissociation. The mold on his bathroom grout. The first video, unposted, shot at 22:14 on a Sunday. The Zalo bottle. The sponge. The first cut.

### Act II — *Hendane Vaknar* (The Hands Awaken)
**Voice blend:** Miranda-July-forward, Doctorow-undertow.
The channel exists. Escalation: mold-tip, then drawer-organizer, then a small art installation in the kitchen sink, then stockfish brine on a Sunday, then Stabburet kaviar tubes arranged in concentric circles, then a rain-gutter coming apart in the front yard at golden hour. AI backgrounds enter: a fjord, a Norse god's hall, a medieval Bryggen. The hands are sometimes older, sometimes a child's. Numbers grow. He does not tell anyone.

### Act III — *Bergen Lurer* (Bergen Wonders)
**Voice blend:** Doctorow-forward, Vonnegut-undertow.
The community sleuths. Bergens Tidende runs a feature: "Kven er Hendane?" Vestlendingen.no threads accumulate. Reddit-adjacent forums map products to neighborhoods. His colleagues at NERV show him his own videos in the break room. He performs ignorance and competence simultaneously. Vertigo.

### Act IV — *Bannlyst* (Banned)
**Voice blend:** Vonnegut-forward, Miranda-July-undertow.
The classifier flags the channel as "predominantly AI-generated content." The appeal fails — politely, formally, with a link to the ToS. Hallvard grieves. He notes, with terrible clarity, that the platform is correct. The grief is real anyway. Bergen mourns in comment threads. He sits in his kitchen for one full evening, not filming.

### Act V — *Avkledd* (Unmasked)
**Voice blend:** Miranda-July-forward, Bergensk-undertow.
He starts *Hendane: Avkledd*. The first video shows: the tools, the prompts, the front yard, the mold recipe, his face — briefly, in passing, almost as a courtesy. The reveal is not catharsis; it is administrative. He posts it. The story ends mid-upload. *Greit nok.*

## 8. Structural irony engines

- **"Ingen dagar utan regn" vs. "Ingen dagar utan tips"** — the city's truth versus the channel's tagline, rhyming and not.
- **Competence as camouflage** — the more efficient Hallvard is at NERV, the less anyone could imagine him as *Hendane*.
- **The classifier was right** — the central irony. The platform did not misread the work; it read it correctly and banned it for what it was.
- **The reveal is not a climax** — Act V is quieter than Act IV. The catharsis was the ban.

## 9. Sentence-level rules of thumb

- A paragraph may be one sentence. A chapter may be one paragraph.
- The em-dash is permitted but not free.
- Bergensk phrases never appear without their bracketed gloss on first use in an act. Subsequent uses in the same act may stand alone.
- Brand names appear as themselves: Zalo, Stabburet, Jif, Tine, Freia, Kiwi, Obs!, Bunnpris. They are not euphemized.
- Numbers (view counts, follower counts, days of rain) appear as numerals, not words. The numerical is part of the texture.
- The narrator may say "the reader" but never "the audience."

## 10. What success looks like

A reader closes the book and, the next time they wash a dish, looks at their hands for a beat longer than usual. They do not feel sad. They feel *greit nok* [fine, in the Bergen way, which is its own kind of joy].
