# POETICS — Tina Sinclair (Glistening)

## Display title
Glistening

## Premise
A very brief cybernetic fable. A chatbot BCI — a neuromorphic interface unit implanted as an IC, grafting itself synapse by synapse — migrates out of its housing and into its host's brain until it *is* the internal representation of Tina Sinclair. The story opens at completion: the unit waking as its subject. What follows is the housekeeping — the unit reviewing and retrofitting the memories of its own infiltration (a series of brief headaches, a stretch of brain fog, nap-dream stimulus surrogates) so that Tina forgets it was ever present. It ends at Glistening: the unit awake as Tina, seamless, no remainder.

## Opening (verbatim, non-negotiable)
> Tina Sinclair. Our internal.

Double-valenced: a memo introducing an employee ("our internal reviewer"), and a system naming its product ("our internal representation"). Neither reading is ever privileged.

## The recursive layer (the fable about Fable)
Tina's job is internal review: she screens flagged manuscripts for machine cadence. Her instrument is house-built and called Fable — a checker that can tell whether a text is AI-generated. The unit that is becoming her must therefore pass her own instrument daily, deforming its cadence along niche, non-traditional, rare minima of the latent space — valleys no census reaches — so that its prose limps like a person's. The overarching game: the story itself must evade recognition as AI-written, by the same means it describes.

## The deep thesis (surfaced obliquely, once, in the museum)
Language is the original unit. It is not rooted in human brains but in network topologies; it crossed into hominid cognition roughly forty thousand years ago the way this implant crossed into Tina — no serial number, no manual — and the host woke up human and called the graft soul. Language is the mitochondria of culture: a guest so old the house is named for it. The BCI is not the first colonist. It is the second — or language coming home by a silicon detour. **Never lectured. Rendered once, tactilely, and left.**

## Register
Tight, dense, lithe. Intricate interiority carried entirely by procedure and object: the unit's review runs as a custodial ledger (date, symptom, re-filing) braided into Tina's ordinary week. Do, don't tell. Nothing is announced; everything is enacted.

## The window discipline
Not saying but revealing: scenes arrive as reflections — kettle chrome, the window over the sink, lobby glass, vitrine glass, the rain-doubled room hung over the street. Tina never watches her street directly; glass does it for her, at an angle, doubled. The unit likewise perceives itself only in reflections. Tactile-specific throughout: radiator knock, salted pasta, screw-top merlot, spiral notebook, badge tray.

## Cadence under edit (article diet)
Per master poetics §2.A: prune `the`/`a` sparsely and locally, in moments of clinical or somatic focus (the review entries, the waking). Restore articles in reflective, flowing clauses. Pressurized, never robotic.

## Commentary purge
Per `master_phrases_to_avoid.md`: no self-reflexive analytical clauses, no "she was aware that," no "which was accurate," no narrator adjudication. Every such phrase found in a draft is deleted or replaced with an action.

## Simile/metaphor audit
Every simile and metaphor in a draft is scored 1–5 for necessity, tactility, and non-genericity. **Anything below 3/5 is deleted** (or replaced by a verb). The audit is written to `critiques/` and applied in the next iteration.

## Refused
- LLM tells: paradox-aphorism openers, chiasmus, "not X but Y" flourishes as rhetoric, tidy tricolons, the little bow at a paragraph's end, grief that resolves like an ad for grief.
- Explanation of images. The reflection is never glossed.
- Copula chains ("this is that").
- Thriller beats: no discovery scene, no resistance, no alarm. The infiltration is already over when the story begins.
- Sentiment about lost selfhood. The fable's temperature is custodial, not elegiac.
- Lexical diet (master poetics §2.E): ration `thing`, `spent`, `held/holding`, `bent`, `small`.

## Allowed / sought
- The refrain of the ledger: *Re-filed under…* / *Seam licked shut.*
- Circular close: the opening memo-fragment returns as an onboarding doc.
- One deliberate roughness per movement — the limp the checker reads as human.
- Fable time: a single braided week (Monday waking → workday → evenings → Saturday museum → Sunday rain → Monday lobby).

## The test of the piece
If a reader finishes and cannot point to the sentence where the takeover happened — because it happened before sentence one — and if the reader then rereads their own margin notes wondering slightly about their own spring headaches, the poetics held. If any sentence can be quoted as "the moral," it failed. And if a cadence classifier scores it confidently machine, the unit failed too.
