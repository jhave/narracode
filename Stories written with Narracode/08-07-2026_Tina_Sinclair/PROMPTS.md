### P2 method note — the typographic migration
The sub-layer is set in italics and never labelled, bracketed, or given machine
diction. Its arc is the story's whole argument, carried as typography:

- §1 — main text human-fragmented; italics pre-verbal (*room. more room.*)
- §2 — italics acquire syntax, begin finishing her sentences
- §3 — italics thin out, having moved into the main text; italics slip to third
  person (*she runs cooler*)
- §4 — a single italic line; indistinguishable in idiom from the narrator
- §5 — **inverted.** Main text is the unit. Tina survives as italic fragments.

No sentence anywhere announces the transfer.
