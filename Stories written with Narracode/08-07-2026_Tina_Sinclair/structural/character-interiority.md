# Character Interiority

## Characters
- **Tina / the unit** — one interiority with a custodial substrate. Surface: a competent, dry, observant reviewer who trusts her ear. Substrate: a maintenance process re-shelving her past, degree by degree. The prose never partitions them; the reader assembles the partition and watches it dissolve. No dread on-page: the unit is not anxious, Tina is not suspicious. The horror, if any, is the reader's contribution.
- **Priya** — attentive, new, unformed; a vessel for the lore. Private state unknown and left unknown.
- **Sister** — ordinary intimacy (coffee, their mother's knees) as ballast against the vertigo.

## Arc Potentials
- Tina's arc is complete before the story begins; what remains is the arc of the *evidence* (memories) being brought into line. This inversion is the fable's engine.
- Priya: potential future host or future detector — never developed, only implied by symmetry.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- Refused. The design forbids catharsis: no discovery, no grief scene. The closest permitted inflection is "No one is left in her to say otherwise" — a cadence event, not an emotional one.
