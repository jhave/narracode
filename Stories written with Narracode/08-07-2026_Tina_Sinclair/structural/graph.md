## Characters and Entities
- **Tina Sinclair** — internal reviewer, fourth floor; screens flagged manuscripts for machine cadence. Six years in her flat. Rust coat, spiral blue notebook.
- **The unit** — neuromorphic BCI, formerly an IC implant; migration into cortex complete before sentence one. Now runs Tina. Never named in-text as "the unit" after the opening ambiguity; present only as procedure (the review, the re-filing).
- **Fable** — house-built cadence classifier at Tina's workplace; her instrument. Scores texts human/machine.
- **Priya** — new hire; receives Tina's craft lore ("machines average, people limp"); her welcome doc closes the story.
- **Tina's sister** — Saturday museum companion; talks about their mother's knees.
- **Language** — the elder colonist; syntax as the original implant, forty thousand years installed. Appears once, in the museum.
- **Mitochondrion** — the biological precedent; guest so old the house is named for it.
