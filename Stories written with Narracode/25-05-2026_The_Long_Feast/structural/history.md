# History

## Established

- **Setting**: mid-21st century, near-post-singularity. Longevity treatments (cellular resets), AI wellness monitoring, gig economy as neo-feudalism, private climate-controlled enclaves, water markets.
- **The Green Ring**: a planted corridor separating premium residential zones from management zones.
- **The Deep Grid**: the city in its other register — lower-income zones, logistics infrastructure, the part of the city routed around by the elevated pedestrian network.
- **Cerulean Strategic**: a sovereign intelligence firm. Provides Complexity Horizon Positioning — helping ultra-wealthy clients navigate and benefit from systemic instability. Holds a Resilience Portfolio of natural resources and displacement infrastructure assets.
- **Cellular reset treatments**: longevity medicine. Tier 1 = full treatments, phenotypic age decoupled from chronological age. Cost-stratified. Mads Torren has them; Solen Torren does not by choice.
- **The Meridian Club**: exclusive members' venue above Fennen Square transit hub. Water sourced from Cerulean's Alpine Vault.
- **The Alpine Vault**: Cerulean's water holdings in geologically stable, low-volatility alpine regions. Part of the Resilience Portfolio.
- **The Torren family**: Aldric (patriarch, age 78, reset to ~55), Mads (22, phenotypically 18, Tier 1 treatment), Solen (25, no treatments, moral discomfort about this). Second-generation wealth. Portfolio: infrastructure, biomedical data licensing, Complexity Horizon Positioning including Sahel water rights.
- **The Sahel water rights position**: Torren family holds extraction and distribution rights in a hydrostress-amplifying region. Value mechanism: scarcity and displacement. Solen says "there are people dying." Dex explains the mechanism. He believes the explanation.
- **The regional stabilization event with elevated kinetic activity**: a war in the northern periphery. Referred to euphemistically throughout. Connected to Defense Solutions subsidiary in Cerulean's portfolio.
- **The Defense Solutions subsidiary**: held at arm's length through intermediate structures. Component manufacturing facilities in the conflict zone. Liability exposure: documentation gap relating to labor conditions and worker ages in one facility.
- **WV**: initials of the consortium VP managing Defense Solutions. Named by initials only, by convention and design.
- **The elevated pedestrian network**: enclosed, conditioned at 21°C, scented with Nordic Conifer Blend. Connects towers above Kessler Green. Transparent floor panels showing the service level below.
- **The service level**: ground-level logistics corridor. Delivery workers in pale grey kit, moving in routing-algorithm patterns.
- **Kel**: wellness practitioner visiting the office Tuesdays and Thursdays. Works through movement, breathwork, somatic regulation. Tipped via platform that takes 34%.
- **The Meridian Covenant Gala**: annual event, Atrium at Aldwich House. 22°C inside. The evening of the water distribution failure.
- **The water distribution failure**: three Deep Grid zones lose municipal supply for up to 48 hours during heat advisory (44°C, operative temperature 49°C). Mobile stations deployed. Lines extending past camera range.
- **The civil infrastructure feed**: public-access camera footage from the mobile water stations. Isla is watching it when Dex comes home.
- **Isla**: Dex's daughter. Age 11. Watches things directly. Asks: "Are we going to do anything?"
- **Presca**: Dex's wife. Has her own suite on the south-facing wall of the apartment. The arrangement suits them.
- **Kessler Green**: premium residential zone containing Dex's tower. Named, possibly ironically, for a person or a policy.
