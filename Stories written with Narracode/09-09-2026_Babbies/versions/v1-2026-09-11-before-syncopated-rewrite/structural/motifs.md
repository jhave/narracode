# Motifs

## Active
- **The Clamshell Snap (*THWAP-CLACK*)**: Clack's punctuation for indignation, closure, excitement, or dramatic finality.
- **The Mooey-Stewy Simmer**: Warm, rich, savory fat bubbling like a domestic hearth; cumin, cracked pepper, and toasted sunflower-paste.
- **Antenna Gestures**: Clack's wire antenna tilting, drooping when sad, or vibrating when detecting static or bureaucratic nonsense.
- **The Pocket Refuge**: Ollie's cavernous oilcloth coat pocket, lined with dry lint and velvet where Clack sleeps.

## Transformations
- Clack's hinge sound shifting from sharp self-defense to gentle, quiet companionship.
- The cutlets shifting from anonymous metric sustenance in Grod's eyes to living culinary children in Ollie's care.

## Avoid Overuse
- Don't click the flip phone on every sentence; ration the *clack* for true structural turns.
- Keep the food smells comforting and appetizing, not greasy or sickening.
