# History

## Established
- The Slumping Shelf produces thirty crates of graded protein squares per cycle for the Upper Concourse.
- Ollie inherited Vat Four after the old tender retired to the Salt Caves.
- Clack was found in an abandoned cable trench three seasons ago, caked in lavender grease; Ollie cleaned its contacts with a toothbrush.
- The cutlets in Vat Four were nicknamed "the Babbies" because they floated in clusters like resting ducklings.
- Scene 0: Morning simmer established; Clack charged on candied lemon peel; babbies flipped; Grod's monowheel approached.
- Scene 1: Inspector Grod arrived with seventeen zinc clipboards and spring-loaded Caliper of Compression; demanded Shore Durometer 84 and 4cm square geometry; moved to clamp the babbies.
- Scene 2: Ollie blocked caliper with drift-alder paddle; Clack bluffed with full antenna extension, dial-up screech, and fictional 40,000-credit pension fine.
- Scene 3: Grod's empty stomach groaned; Ollie offered warm, salted broth-crust on an alder saucer; Grod tasted it and his bureaucratic posture melted.
- Scene 4: Grod stamped Clipboard Seven with purple ink under Exemption 9-B (In-situ Tenderness Protocol); departed into lavender mist; Ollie tucked the babbies and wrapped Clack in rabbit-flannel for the night.

## Said
- Clack repeatedly claims to have once received a call from the Moon's switchboard, though it admits the reception was crackly.
- Ollie: "Clouds don't taste like thyme. And food shouldn't fight you back while you're eating it."
- Grod: "A soft cutlet is treason against the jawbone!"
- Grod: "The thyme is... within municipal tolerances. The salt is adequate."
- Clack: "Barely enough juice to dream about telephone poles, Ollie."

## Refused
- Ollie refused to use commercial coagulant pellets or let Grod clamp the soft unhardened babbies.
- Grod refused to condemn the vat after tasting the broth-crust.
