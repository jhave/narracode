# History

## Established
- The Slumping Shelf produces thirty crates of graded protein squares per cycle for the Upper Concourse.
- Ollie inherited Vat Four after the old tender retired to the Salt Caves.
- Clack was found in an abandoned cable trench three seasons ago, caked in lavender grease; Ollie cleaned its contacts with a toothbrush.
- The cutlets in Vat Four were nicknamed "the Babbies" because they floated in clusters like resting ducklings.
- Scene 0 established the morning routine: the vents opened four minutes early; Clack received candied lemon peel to charge its second battery bar; Ollie flipped two babbies using the butter-ash paddle before the monowheel screech sounded on the east spur.

## Said
- Clack repeatedly claims to have once received a call from the Moon's switchboard, though it admits the reception was crackly.
- Ollie: "Clouds don't taste like thyme. And food shouldn't fight you back while you're eating it."

## Refused
- Ollie refuses to use commercial coagulant pellets, insisting that slow-simmered nut-lipids and gentle stirring produce superior crumb.
