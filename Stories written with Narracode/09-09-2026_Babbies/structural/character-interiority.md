# Character Interiority

## Characters

### Ollie
- *Private State*: Quietly terrified that the world outside the Shelf has no room for things that take time to become soft. Loves the broth not because it is food, but because watching something grow in a warm bath feels like proof that the universe isn't entirely made of iron.
- *Contradiction*: Earnest and obedient about routine, but completely rebellious against any rule that treats living texture as a mere number.
- *Arc Potential*: Moving from a shy tender hiding behind vats to an assertive advocate who demonstrates that kindness is the only thing that makes texture palatable.

### Clack (The Flip Phone)
- *Private State*: Terrified of the "Silent Sleep" (running out of charge permanently or having its memory cleared). Compensates with flamboyant vanity, pretending it is doing Ollie a favor by staying awake.
- *Contradiction*: Claims to be a sophisticated piece of telecommunication hardware, but has zero desire to connect to any network other than Ollie's coat pocket.
- *Arc Potential*: Enduring a sacrificial battery drain to generate a distracting alarm or light beam when Ollie needs protection.

### Inspector Grod
- *Private State*: Exhausted, heartburn-ridden, perpetually chewing cold compressed vitamin cubes because he believes eating warm meals is an inefficient waste of billable seconds.
- *Contradiction*: Deeply proud of his strict objectivity, yet secretly haunted by a childhood memory of a homemade potato dumpling that was warm and soft.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- Grod tasting a freshly skimmed piece of broth-crust and having his rigid bureaucratic demeanor momentarily shatter into weeping wonder.
- Clack using its final bar of battery to ring an outgoing call to Grod's own childhood telephone number.
