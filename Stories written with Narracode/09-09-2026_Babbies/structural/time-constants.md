# Time Constants

## Chronology
- **The Shift**: Tending occurs between the First Steam Whistle (morning vent-release) and the Third Dimming (when the sodium overhead lamps flicker into night-ember).
- **The Simmer Cycle**: A batch of cutlets requires exactly four turnings of the skim-spade per bell-hour.
- **Battery Life**: Clack loses one bar of charge every two hours unless stroked with a wool mitten or exposed to rising clove steam.

## Durations
- Vat Four is on its third day of simmer: the critical transition window where soft slurry becomes formed cutlet.
- Inspector Grod's monowheel inspection window is strictly eighteen minutes.

## Constraints
- If a cutlet is prodded with metal before it seals, it collapses into broth-mud.
- Clack cannot get wet; moisture makes its keypad type nonsense syllables (*#99*9#*).
