# Graph

## Characters and Entities
- **OLLIE**: Apprentice vat-tender on the Slumping Shelf. Wears an oversized oilcloth smock with deep pockets and mismatched rubber boots. Candid, earnest, observant, speaks with unadorned literalism.
- **CLACK**: A talkative pastel-lilac clamshell flip phone (model: Tele-Spindle 90). Possesses an extendable brass wire antenna, a sharp mechanical hinge that snaps (*THWAP-CLACK*), and a tiny green-lit bitmap screen that displays pixelated expressions. Charged by wool friction, lemon zest, and spoken compliments. Melodramatic, vain, but fiercely bonded to Ollie.
- **THE BABBIES**: The tender, floating vegan protein cutlets in Vat Four. Unformed, buoyant, simmering in seasoned synth-fats, requiring patient skimming and gentle hums to prevent gristle.
- **INSPECTOR GROD**: The District Chewing & Caloric Auditor. Rides a squeaking brass monowheel; carries spring-loaded chew-calipers and seventeen carbon-paper ledgers. Obsessed with chew-resistance quotas and commercial density.
- **THE SLUMPING SHELF**: A warm, cantilevered terrace of corrugated iron and violet moss jutting over the steam vents of the Lower Sump. Houses seven simmering fat-vats.

## Relations
- **Ollie & Clack**: Domestic codependence. Ollie keeps Clack dry, polished, and charged; Clack provides dramatic running commentary, tells stories of the Old Dial Tone, and keeps watch over the boiler dials.
- **Ollie & The Babbies**: Devoted stewardship. Ollie treats the protein culture as living, sensitive beings who respond to mood, salt balance, and voice.
- **Inspector Grod & Ollie**: Ideological collision: cold industrial quantification vs. warm, quirky culinary care.
