# Scene Ledger

## Scenes
- **Scene 0 (`0-the-simmer-and-the-clack.md`)**:
  - *Location*: The Slumping Shelf, Vat Four.
  - *Focus*: Ollie tending the warm plant-lipid broth; Clack's theatrical morning waking and lemon-peel recharge; the delicate skimming and flipping of the young, buoyant Babbies.
  - *Turn*: The metallic screech of a dry monowheel announces Inspector Grod on the east spur.
  - *Aftermath*: Clack snaps shut in panic; Ollie is caught mid-simmer with unhardened cutlets.

- **Scene 1 (`1-the-caliper-of-grod.md`)**:
  - *Location*: The catwalk beside Vat Four.
  - *Focus*: Inspector Grod dismounts his brass monowheel with his seventeen zinc clipboards and spring-loaded Caliper of Compression to audit the babbies' chew-density.
  - *Turn*: Grod prepares to clamp a floating babbie; Clack hisses a panicked warning from the heart pocket.
  - *Aftermath*: Cold bureaucratic quantification directly threatens the unhardened cutlets.

- **Scene 2 (`2-the-clack-interference.md`)**:
  - *Location*: Rim of Vat Four.
  - *Focus*: Ollie blocks the descending caliper with the drift-alder paddle; Clack bursts open (*THWAP-CLACK*) with full antenna extension, unleashing a ferocious sonic blast of dial-up static and mimicking an emergency dispatch from the Central Exchange.
  - *Turn*: Clack threatens Grod with a fictional forty-thousand-credit fine against his pension ledger.
  - *Aftermath*: Grod is stunned and backed against his monowheel; the immediate physical strike is averted.

- **Scene 3 (`3-the-broth-crust.md`)**:
  - *Location*: The grating beside the monowheel.
  - *Focus*: Grod clutches his fluttering clipboards as his empty stomach groans; Ollie uses the horn spoon to lift a warm, amber-gold shard of crisp broth-crust onto an alder saucer with three grains of sea-salt.
  - *Turn*: Grod tastes the warm, savory crust; his rigid posture thaws into weeping sensory nostalgia.
  - *Aftermath*: The ideological barrier dissolves in the mouth; real nourishment displaces cold quantification.

- **Scene 4 (`4-the-twilight-stamp.md`)**:
  - *Location*: Vat Four in deepening twilight.
  - *Focus*: Grod stamps Clipboard Seven with purple ink under Emergency Exemption 9-B ("In-situ Tenderness Protocol"), forbidding mechanical calipers for seventy-two hours; departs into the lavender mist.
  - *Turn*: Evening arrives with the Third Whistle; Ollie flips the remaining babbies to rest shoulder-to-shoulder.
  - *Aftermath*: Clack is wrapped in static-charged rabbit-flannel and goes to sleep with a quiet, contented *clack*; domestic peace restored on the Slumping Shelf.
