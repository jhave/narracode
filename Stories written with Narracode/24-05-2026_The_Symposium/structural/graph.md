# Graph

## Characters and Entities

- **Mara Selene**: musicologist, 11 years studying Kessler Recordings. Middle worlds — neither empire center nor periphery.
- **Dov Anast**: Bureau archivist for cancelled musical traditions. True believer, increasingly lost.
- **Priya Ulm**: theorist. Author of "The Complicity of Presence." Applying for voluntary dissolution.
- **The old man**: unnamed, no badge. Has studied the music of the First Lush. A long watcher.
- **Cossel**: registration desk, Veld Station symposium.
- **Helda Kessler**: dead archivist. Made the Recordings. Found complicit (meat, reproduction, atmospheric travel).
- **The Kessler Recordings**: 312 hours of Meridian-4 polyphonic folk music. Under review.
- **The Contrition Bureau**: regulatory body determining complicity.
- **The Empire**: unnamed. Oscillates between Lush (excess) and Contrition (cancellation).

## Relations

- Mara / Kessler Recordings: 11 years of scholarly devotion; the recordings are her life's work.
- Dov / archive: he maintains what he cannot entirely justify maintaining.
- The old man / Mara: strangers who find the same limit.
- Priya / Mara: ideological mirror; Priya follows the cancellation logic to its end.

- Mara arrives with a paper to present; departs with no paper presented and no resolution, but something received.
