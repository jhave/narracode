# Motifs

## Active

- **Violet static / cancellation weather**: the beauty of the process of erasure. Present in I, V, VII.
- **The next note**: the story's closing image. Process without center. Continuation without securing.
- **The gesture**: the old man's recurring phrase for the empire's single repeated movement, whether toward or away.
- **Under review**: the liminal status of everything in the Contrition — not yet cancelled, not yet safe.
- **The bread**: a small object the old man holds and does not eat. An unremarkable object carrying the weight of everything.

## Transformations

- The violet static begins as weather (I), becomes backdrop to Priya's argument (III), clears (V), clears further (VII). Its disappearance is the sky filling with other suns.

## Avoid Overuse

- The voice-as-position thesis is stated once (II) and thereafter only gestured at.
