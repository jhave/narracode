# History

## Established

- Veld Station orbits Ulthara-7, at the periphery of a dominant empire.
- The empire has oscillated between Hedonist Epochs (the Lushes) and Contrition Epochs.
- The First Lush: ~200 years before story present. Now largely demolished/cancelled.
- The Second Lush: ended some decades before story present. The Late Lush was its final phase.
- Helda Kessler made 312 hours of folk-music recordings on Meridian-4 ninety years ago.
- The Kessler Recordings are currently under review by the Contrition Bureau.
- Mara Selene has studied the Kessler Recordings for eleven years.
- Mara's paper was not presented; she was quietly disinvited.
- Priya Ulm intends voluntary dissolution after her paper.
- The empire has no word for what is neither possessed nor renounced.

## Said

- "The Complicity of Presence" — Priya Ulm's thesis: selfhood itself is the root of complicity.
- "The gesture is the belief that Paradise can be secured." — the old man.
- "It is difficult to explain why this feels more important than it is." — Kessler's note.

## Refused

- The recognition is not named directly by any character.
- The music is not resolved or saved; it simply continues.
