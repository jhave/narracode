# Drift — 2026-07-20, post-composition

## What the piece is becoming

POETICS declared entwined provocations that never resolve yet evoke. The composed piece is that, but it has drifted toward something POETICS did not fully anticipate: a **single-night nocturne**. The seed's new moon quietly became the clock — low at the strike, higher and thinner at the close — and the whole braid now hangs inside one moonrise, with the backward strand excavating beneath the night and the forward strand extending it. This tightening was not declared. It is fertile, not regressive: it converts the braid from an alternation device into a chamber.

## Declared vs. composed

- **Declared**: "enigmatic ambiguous subtle nuanced tentative." **Composed**: more architecturally decisive than tentative — the motif-lattice (click/click, whistle/name, sand/water, door/door) is tightly joined. The tentativeness survives at the level of meaning (ferry direction, word's future, Qais's fate), not of construction. Acceptable reading of the instruction; the prompter may want more genuine looseness — one movement that refuses the lattice.
- **Declared**: raised temperature, reduced function-words. **Composed**: temperature raised more by image-speed than by syntax-breakage; the pruning is conservative relative to, e.g., Project A-0. Room to push harder in a revision pass if wanted.
- **Declared**: both figures dead, one metaphorically. **Composed**: fulfilled, but the piece added a third figure of death not in the contract — the listening brother, dead-still, counting-adjacent, the story's residue. This is drift as bonus; it now carries the final line.

## Genre-regression check

- War-fiction defaults mostly refused: no rescue, no recognition scene, no perpetrator-redemption, no named-battle journalism. The rave and the propaganda-torrent risk topical-fiction cliché; both are saved by function (failed anesthesia) rather than spectacle.
- The largest standing risk is earnestness — the material's gravity invites solemnity everywhere. The piece's few jokes (the misers counting, "herons") are load-bearing; a human pass might add one more permission for lightness in the −2 room.

## Verdict

Drift is toward density and closure-of-form around openness-of-wound. Fertile. No correction required before the human editing pass.
