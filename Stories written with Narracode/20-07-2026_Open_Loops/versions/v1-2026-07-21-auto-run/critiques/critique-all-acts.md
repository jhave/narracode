- **Aphorism pressure.** "Keeping it was the door" (−1), "The whistle was the door of the room that had no door" (−2), "binding the whole dark together" (+3): three sentences that know they are good. The door pair is load-bearing motif and can stand; the prompter should adjudicate the third.
- **Symmetry temptation.** The final paragraph's "one draining, one drained" is near-chiasmus; the piece elsewhere resists ornament, and this is its most visible flourish. It is also the contract line. Flagged, not condemned.
- **Named-lyric peak.** "Two kites testing one string" (−3) — one lyric peak per strand is the ration; this is the strand's one. At quota, not over.
