## Display title

Open Loops

---

## Display synopsis

Two stories entwined around one click. One runs backwards: a seven-year-old in Gaza, orphaned, sole keeper of a bed-bound brother, retreating from the instant of an autonomous drone strike toward the unbearable ordinary that preceded it. One runs forwards: the staff lieutenant who approved the strike, carried through confirmation image, propaganda torrent, ecstasy rave, and a night of grief-rage, toward a metaphoric death of his own. The strands never meet again after the first paragraph. Neither resolves. Both remain open.

---

## Form

- **The braid.** Alternating movements. Minus-strand (the child) moves in reverse chronology: each movement earlier than the last, ending at the point of greatest life. Plus-strand (the lieutenant) moves forward: each movement later, ending at thoughtless dark. Movement 0 — the seed paragraph — is fixed, verbatim, untouchable.
- **Open loops.** Engagement hooks folded in, subtle, tenacious, non-obvious, integral to plot and character arcs. Every planted object (key on twine, father's phone, promised sea, unopened photo archive, ferry with no flag) stays planted. Hooks propel; they do not close. Resolution is refused as a formal principle.
- **Ending contract.** Both figures dead at the end — one literal, splayed in sand; one metaphoric, after orgasm, thoughtless, subconsciously dreaming escape from Israel and the hatred of genocide. The word has taken root, unavoidably, from the child's dead eyes.

## Linguistic pressure

- Raise the temperature. Reduce prepositions, possessives, articles — sparse, localized pruning under clinical or somatic pressure (per master poetics §2.A); restore flow in reflective clauses. Never robotic.
- Evade the subtle imperatives of linguistic normalcy: syntax may fracture under grief, run-on under drug, contract under procedure.
- Enigmatic, ambiguous, subtle, nuanced, tentative, stylistic provocation. Evoke; never resolve.
- No self-reflexive commentary, no analytical clauses, no editorializing (per master_phrases_to_avoid.md). No moralizing — the material moralizes itself or not at all.

## Refused

- Redemption arcs. Symmetry of suffering as equivalence claim. Journalism cosplay. Named-battle specificity. Sentimentality. The consolations of closure. Explicit anatomical pornography — the sex scene is violence of grief, rendered somatic and hot, not graphic catalogue.
- Lexical diet (master poetics §2.E): audit `thing`, `spent`, `held/holding`, `bent`, `small`, `wage` on sight.
