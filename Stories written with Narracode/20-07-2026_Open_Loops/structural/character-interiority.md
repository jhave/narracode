# Character Interiority

## Characters
- **Sama**: no interiority monologue granted — rendered through logistics, promises, rehearsals. Her love is procedural (routes, rations, reports); her childhood leaks out sideways (skipping on safe stretches). Fear is managed, never named.
- **The lieutenant**: interiority as queue-management. Grief arrives only in unauthorized channels: under eyelids, under MDMA, under orgasm, under sleep. He cannot mourn in the first person; the story mourns around him. His hands become "monstrous to him" only mid-force — recognition without reform.
- **Qais**: interiority displaced into world-rebuilding (the serial city). Rage in the mornings, gentleness by noon — cycle noted, unexplained.
- **Noa**: withheld. One gesture (wiper-blade thumb, "clearing nothing") stands for a full unwritten story — she has her own queue, her own thumbnails.

## Arc Potentials (potentials, not mandates — none forced to resolve)
- Lieutenant: the rooted word could grow toward refusal, exile, testimony, or nothing. Text ends before germination shows above soil. Do not choose.
- Qais: the listening could become the next story's aperture. Do not open it here.
- Noa: mirror-arc available (she saw the same image from another angle). Latent only.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- Deliberately bypassed: the orgasm is anti-catharsis (execution's mercy, thoughtless); the rave is anti-catharsis (burial that fails). The piece routes all catharsis into the reader's unrelieved expectation of it.
