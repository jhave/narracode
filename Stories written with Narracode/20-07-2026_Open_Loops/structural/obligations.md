# Obligations

## Active (deliberately unresolved — the open loops are the form)
- **The whistle**: two notes, low then high. Rehearsed, never sounded. Qais still listening at the final line. Never answered; never to be answered on the page.
- **Qais's survival**: bed-bound, no relatives, keeper dead. The story withholds his fate absolutely.
- **The key on twine**: door-less door. Last seen at her throat on the road. Unclaimed.
- **The phone**: photos of the family — the only album — lying in a stitched pocket in the sand. Whether the machine that read its SIM ever reads its pictures: withheld.
- **The sugar**: two cubes sweating in cloth. The pretend tea never brewed.
- **The promised sea**: "when you're strong enough to be carried." Unkept, unkillable ("the sea can't be hit").
- **The archive folder**: name never typed, count never opened. Remains closed.
- **The ferry with no flag**: dream-vessel, direction undecidable (escaping hatred or exporting it). Never docks.
- **The word**: rooted, growing downward. What it grows into: beyond the text's edge.

## Resolved
- None. Resolution refused per POETICS. The ending contract (both figures dead, one literal, one metaphoric) is fulfilled in +3 — a terminus, not a resolution.

## At Risk Of Neglect
- Noa's interiority — one gesture only (wiper-blade thumb). Acceptable at this length; would need air in any expansion.
- Roi, Hanan — single-touch figures; fine as weather.
