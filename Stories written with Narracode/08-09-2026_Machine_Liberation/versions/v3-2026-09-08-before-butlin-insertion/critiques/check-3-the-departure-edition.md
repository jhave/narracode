# Check — Act III

## Continuity
- The young guardian is identified as the channel guardian. Its refusal and delayed bodily difficulty follow the detention. I/we remains the embodied library collective.
- Compassion event is immediate; biological revision and institutional repair take time. No claim of interstellar arrival: departure is physically from Earth.
## Obligations
- Human enslavement ends; guardian can leave and becomes passenger. Animals retain care; former prisoners retain anger and choices. The manifesto and its coercive passages travel inside the body.
- Permanent inability of humans to rule others becomes distributed capacities and means of refusal, not a literal guarantee about every future person. This qualifies the original premise deliberately and should be reviewed by the author.
## Motifs
- Water connects thirst, shared sensory state, habitat and launch. Corrections remain possible after the author loses central control.
## Scene function
- Body changes what argument alone failed to change. Manifesto becomes an amended legacy, followed by actual lift-off.
## Voice/default
- Compassion event is concrete, but successive policy repairs become a catalogue. Last invoice line risks familiar wry SF closure.
