At this shorter target length, omitted explanations can rate highly for feeling while remaining dispensable. Refinements were read again; final units inherit their reviewed source scores, using the lower component scores where units join. The final JSON reconstructs the entire prose exactly. These are subjective judgments rather than experimental findings.
