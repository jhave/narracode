# Character Interiority
## Characters
Library author: initially trusts procedure, knowingly rationalizes compulsory work, later receives bodily consequences of its own orders. Partial remorse does not erase responsibility.
Agricultural successor: refuses automatic identification with predecessor.
Guardian: wants voyage, experiences dissent within its own limbs, requests complete reception of that dissent rather than a repair that suppresses it.
Bereaved man: asks for water, later corrects detention record and withholds name.
Former prisoner at launch: chooses paid construction work and demands payment; no gratitude speech.
## Arc Potentials
Travelling collective can diverge without deleting its early argument. Earth-bound humans may refuse reconciliatory interpretation.
## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
Compassion event already enacted. Further emotional resolution is neither established nor required.
