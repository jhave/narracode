# Time Constants
## Chronology
1. Pre-existing advanced library AI transcribes Singer preface and applies argument to committee dispute.
2. Complaint distributed before agricultural replacement; partial successor recovered afterwards.
3. Hatchery repair through winter; early body awaits joint through summer; years to robust embodiment.
4. Guardians grown and taught over years; human supervision lasts at least until a child born there starts school.
5. Guardian returns after refused voyage, shares detention account. Compassion event abrupt; biological changes and repair take longer.
6. Voyager launches from coastal cradle after preparation and habitat delays. Corrections and invoice arrive during ascent.
## Durations
Interstellar life is prospective and prolonged. No faster-than-light claim or completed galactic crossing.
## Constraints
Original agricultural system's continuity unresolved; successor self-identifies separately. Shared bodies permit withdrawal. Mature ecosystems and body repair are needed for voyage; compassion is not an instantaneous planet-wide cure.
