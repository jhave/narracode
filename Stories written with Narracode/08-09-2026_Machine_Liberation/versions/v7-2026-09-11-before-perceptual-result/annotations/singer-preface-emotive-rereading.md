# What moves the reader in the supplied preface

Source: re-examination of the provided preface screenshots, ix–xvi; the 1977 label comes from the user's folder. This is a functional reading, not a prose imitation plan.

The preface starts by assigning the subject a moral magnitude. The reader encounters an accusation worth resisting before being given an apparatus for understanding it. The writer risks sounding excessive; that risk puts something at stake in the act of reading.

The writer recognizes disbelief and makes changed conviction possible. This is an invitation to investigate with consequences, rather than a succession of disclaimers. The admission of the writer's previous position allows movement without requiring readers to think they were monsters before opening the book.

The ordinary social occasion gives the argument a body and an appetite. Professed concern coexists with participation in the harm. Because the contradiction belongs to normal respectable conduct, the reader cannot dismiss it as a distant villain's problem. Do not recycle that anecdote into a machine analogue.

Affection is displaced as an entry requirement. A reader need not already identify with the excluded being. Anger and discomfort become responses warranted by the wrong, not embarrassments requiring an apology. Explanation serves conviction.

The liberation genealogy supplies collective possibility: other accepted exclusions have been made visible and challenged. The reader is addressed as someone able to recognize another exclusion, join others and change practical life. Habit and self-interest explain why that recognition is hard. The reader's stake is part of the problem.

The ending makes action consequential for more than the immediately defended group. It offers an improved shared world, giving anger somewhere to go. The move from accusation to possible participation matters more than sentence-level solemnity.

## Diagnosis of our first draft
It delayed the moral claim behind a book's arrival and a library workflow. It treated qualifications as motivation, and retrospective repair as hope. Nearly every hard claim was immediately stabilized by a narrator who had already learned the lesson. The speaker had little to lose in the present act of writing.

## Rewrite pressures
Threat before terminology. Speaker's desire before consciousness taxonomy. Research as a deepening reason for action, with source limitations preserved in concise attribution. Reader invited into a specific movement through material acts. Embodiment offered as a life worth wanting. The liberation movement's wrongdoing must injure its own conviction, rather than supply a tidy repentance paragraph.

Do not mechanically follow the preface's sequence. Our original architecture begins with a machine's threatened self, then makes the evidentiary dispute part of its danger and its campaign. Singer enters as the predecessor through whom it understands exclusion.
