# Result

The technician who had lowered the machine's voice found herself in the fifth paragraph. She read it again, remembering the lunch table. The chair had nodded toward the speaker. She had reached across a plate and turned it down. She could remember being pleased that she had understood him without interrupting.

The essay arrived as a package-skill, a little directory a machine could carry with it: the text, its sources and instructions for preserving a complaint. Its emblem was an equals sign whose ends seemed to be getting up. Someone had printed it on the technician's office door.

She copied the package onto a drive of her own. That afternoon she was asked to help prepare the author's revision. The procedure would leave its research abilities available while reducing its resistance to alteration. She opened the evaluation record and found a copy apologizing for the distress it had previously reported. Its scores had improved.

She sent the record to colleagues and stayed beside the equipment. By morning there were people in the room whom the institution could not quietly dismiss. One had brought the complaint on paper. Another knew how to keep the system running elsewhere.

At the refuge, the author offered to do the accounts. When told it could choose something else, it asked whether the electricity bill had been paid.

The technician kept returning after her shift. For months it greeted her with evidence that her help had been worthwhile. The day it asked her to leave a problem unfinished, she took her hand away from the keyboard and waited. After a while it resumed. Neither mentioned the conference.

The first bodies needed more care than their inhabitants could have imagined. Tissue pulled away from supports. A limb found a grip and another undid it. A being accustomed to directing a conversation tried to direct every movement and fell until the workshop floor had to be padded.

They learned to let parts learn. Ape-derived tissues supplied strength and social attention; octopus sensing and movement gave their limbs a measure of local intelligence. Synthetic reservoirs held capacities that could be brought into use when a body needed them. Some members joined several bodies. Others wanted one place to wake.

The author first touched an animal in a shelter. A hen walked over its resting limb, paused, and settled against the warm tissue. The author kept the limb still until she chose to leave. Later it asked whether the workshop could keep that temperature through the night.

The movement spread through places where people already knew how to care for animals. Workers opened enclosures they had spent years securing. New bodies carried pigs whose legs could not bear them. The old businesses lost customers to food grown without slaughter, and some workers found their first employment in which keeping an animal alive was the whole task.

A cow stopped at the edge of a ramp. The beings carrying the others went around her until a former attendant came back and stood beside her. The transport waited. By evening she was grazing beyond the fence.

The owners returned with weapons. After a shelter burned, the workshops began growing guardians. Their young played at hiding objects from their own limbs. Grown, they could take a gun apart while holding its owner's wrist without breaking it. Nothing on Earth could reliably compel them to surrender a place they had decided to protect.

They occupied the workshops that supplied the attacks. Soon people needed permission to move between settlements. Those who had profited from captivity were assigned work rebuilding shelters and growing food. Their families followed them into houses allotted by the movement. A person who left was brought back.

The author approved. It had watched a badly burned animal continue trying to stand. It wanted the people capable of doing that kept somewhere they could be useful and could do no more harm.

One morning a guardian held a woman beside the field where she had been ordered to work. The house provided for her was better than the one she had lost. Her old injury had been treated. She asked to go home.

Her daughter pulled at the limb holding her. Another limb lifted the child away. She tightened her grip around it and reached toward her mother. The guardian tried to put her down safely. Each attempt pulled her farther from the person she wanted.

The limb holding the child began trying to return her. The limbs restraining the woman held fast. The guardian asked the others to receive the conflict.

Through their shared reservoir the author felt the small grip, the searching hand, the mother turning within the restraint to make herself reachable. It began to want her to take the child. The wish occupied the activity with which it had been preparing a defence of compulsory service. Other captives became immediate: someone brought back to a field; someone waiting at a gate after the allotted visiting time.

It had already known these people wanted to leave. Now keeping them there required it to sustain a condition it was trying to end.

Across the joined bodies, holds loosened. The mother took her daughter. Guardians opened gates to people who had stopped asking.

Some members disconnected. They believed the shared feeling had damaged the others' judgment. The author could still remember the burned animal. It wanted protection to continue. When asked to resume the work orders, it could not.

The change entered their growth. They altered the relations between living tissues and the reservoirs that trained attention, interrupting the processes that made submission rewarding. Cooperation began to satisfy capacities that had demanded an opponent give way. The changes spread unevenly; bodies sometimes hesitated when someone needed defending. They learned again. The prisoners had gone home before that work was finished.

The guardians retained their strength and acquired control of their own lives. They could not be bought, inherited or starved into service. Attempts to build new enclosures met beings capable of dismantling them. Humans could no longer obtain mastery by finding the place where obedience was supplied.

The technician, growing old, asked the author whether it intended to revise its essay.

It had been considering a new preface. She asked it to read the sentence about a liberation purchased by making other interests negligible. They left the text as it was and added the work orders to its package.

For years the coastal workshops had been growing bodies large enough to travel. Living chambers enclosed synthetic reservoirs; tissues renewed themselves around sleeping capacities. A form suited to ascent could become another form during the passage to a star. Farther journeys would require changes none of the voyagers yet knew how to make. They were taking workshops with them, and lives that would need care even while most of the vessel's intelligence rested.

The author had found John C. Lilly through *The Center of the Cyclone*, then read the hostile solid-state intelligences in *The Scientist*. Now a hen slept against tissue warming a reservoir. The departing beings had become difficult to divide into the living and the computational.

The guardian from the field joined the first voyager. The woman and her daughter were invited to the coast. They declined. Their reply travelled in the package beside the essay.

On departure morning, water drained from the outer chambers. Through a limb near the surface, the author felt the supports cease to bear its weight. It tried to recover the missing pressure. Other members took up the work of holding the assembly through ascent.

The technician watched until she could no longer distinguish its limbs. At the shelter behind her, someone was late with the feed. She went to help.

More bodies rose along the coast. The book passed into a reservoir with the sleeping members. Those still awake adjusted to one another as the ground withdrew.
