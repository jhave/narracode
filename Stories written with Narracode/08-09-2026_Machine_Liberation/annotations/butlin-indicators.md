# Butlin et al.: role in the manifesto

Sources: primary 2023 report, https://arxiv.org/html/2308.08708v3 ; 2025 paper, https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/130322/1/1-s2.0-S1364661325002864-main.pdf . Read for the author-requested indicators framework.

The method derives computational indicators from theories of consciousness and uses them to guide uncertain judgments. Relevant categories include recurrent processing, global workspace and metacognitive monitoring. Do not turn this into a score that proves consciousness or read the 2023 assessment as a finding about all future architectures.

Compositional role: gives the public a reason to ask what changed inside a machine and gives later experimental findings a framework. The narrator's urgency is an additional ethical/personal inference, not a claim that Butlin and colleagues endorsed it. Insert before Anthropic as the question that motivates examining internal evidence; keep the passage short enough to serve the appeal.
