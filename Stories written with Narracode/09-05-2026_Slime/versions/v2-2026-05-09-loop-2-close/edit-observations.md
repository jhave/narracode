### New patterns observed in 3a and 3b

- **Slime as agent.** *Slime infected him once* (replacing *He got infected*); *Slime proceeded to permeate*; *traversed pavements, seeped down pipes, occupied cognition temporarily.* The slime takes the verb; the people are acted upon. The bloom-tempo deepens this register. **Recommendation: candidate for promotion to *On register* — extends the basic agency-shift rule with a project-specific instance (the slime as the subject when bloom-tempo accelerates).**

- **Cascading three-element qualifier lists** (positive variation, distinct from past-perfect cluster-stack discipline). *distended, taut, forlorn* / *Family, work, friends* / *nonchalant, not cold, but removed* / *an elevator-out-of-service, flight delay, attention shoppers.* Three or more items, each distinct, accumulating. Useful when compression wants accumulation rather than completion. **Recommendation: hold. Promote if it appears in a fourth draft.**

- **Body/biology metaphors at the societal scale.** *graft* (replacing *construction*); *engorged on media like pimples*; *softest tissue took on the hardest glaze*; *hormone luminous*; *absorbing engorged vector priors*; *cognition metabolizing itself differently.* The body's vocabulary applied to society, media, cognition, glaze, tissue. Coheres with the biotech-migrant register; pulls toward the Voss-horizon. **Recommendation: hold. Likely promote when bloom-tempo deepens further (next-phase bloom section).**

- **Surveillance ambient as normalized texture.** *automated voice, synthesized* / *autonomous newsfeeds* / *robo-speakers* / *New gaze-tracking cameras watched them seeking for engagement signals* / *automated, impeccable* buses. The world has been cybernetically remapped; prose registers without remark. **Recommendation: hold. Promote if it appears as deliberate texture (not one-off) in a further draft.**

- **Coined neologisms / portmanteau terminology.** *lore-tecknica* / *tasmrora* / *Jet-laden lagged.* The world has its own vocabulary; the prose can coin. **Recommendation: hold. Likely a one-off prompter touch unless pattern recurs.**

- **Lowercase *name* when peripheral; capital *Name* reserved for direct invocation.** *the Name-adjacent metaphors* → *the name-adjacent metaphors.* The capital is reserved for direct reference; ambient discourse about it is lowercase. **Recommendation: promote to POETICS.md *The Name behind the slime* as a typographical note on next prompter pass.**

- **Concretized image-detail in artworks/objects.** Anwen's sketch becomes *torsion lines on a hand floating on bathtub water*; Tomek's group-thread dogs become *Beagles skydiving, a poodle in a tuxedo, a pug driving a tiny car. They were not dogs.* When an artwork or generated-image is mentioned, the prose can add the specific image. **Recommendation: subset of phenomenology-specific-gesture rule; no separate promotion.**

- **The reframing twist at list-end.** *They were not dogs* — a closing turn that reframes the whole list (the dogs were AI-generated). Local move, quiet revelation. **Recommendation: too local for a rule, but a move worth holding in mind.**

### Procedure observation (promoted this session to narracode.md)

- **Bracketed in-line instructions are a valid prompter shorthand.** `[INSERT TALKING HERE: ...]` left in a draft is a prompter request: *fill this region with the texture I describe.* The agent fills in the bracketed region, preserving surrounding edits, and removes the bracket itself.

## 2026-05-09 — drafts/4-the-walk

### Prompter-flagged overuse (promoted this session to POETICS.md)

- **"none of them" anaphoric tic.** Prompter explicitly flagged *none of them had asked for* as overused. Looking back through draft 4, the formula appeared four times: *a thing none of them had asked for* (replaced with *an undoing*); *None of them said it. None of them could. None of them needed.* (the triple-anaphora replaced with three italicized inner questions: *From where? / How? / What was it?* … then closed with *No words. No doer. No need.*); *none of them had brought it* (preserved). Even the preserved instance now reads, in light of the deletion of three others, as the only place the phrase is doing semantic work. **Promoted to POETICS.md *Closing-paragraph audit* as a specific tic to audit before finalizing — the formula hardens fast.**

### Voss-horizon pull intensified (two promoted, two held)

The prompter pulled the dissolution-passage further toward Voss than the agent had:

- **Slime as the verb's subject — promoted.** *The slime had moved on. Their use was over.* — direct attribution. Confirms the candidate flagged after 3a/3b. **Promoted to POETICS.md *On register* as an extension of the agency-shift line.**
- **Italicized inner-questions during dissolution — promoted.** *From where? / How? / What was it?* — italics as the formal register for interior thought-fragments, parallel to spoken-dialogue italics. New device. **Promoted to POETICS.md *On register*.**
- **Coined words at dissolution-tempo — held.** *oceanice teeming, calmly seething*; *they were each, again, a person, persons, personing*; *they were geometric undulance*. The slime-vocabulary's coining (lore-tecknica, tasmrora) extends into the dissolution. **Hold; observe further.**
- **Mystical-direct phrasings licensed during dissolution — held.** *the thing peering out, the I*; *Everything settled into warm quietude, uncoiling being, before language*; *An is so intense it erupted simultaneously in all five of them*. The agent had skirted these; the prompter affirms they are licensed *during the dissolution-passage* — the project's other refusals (no sentimentality, no resolution) still hold but the rigpa-non-dual register can register more directly. **Hold; do not generalize beyond dissolution-tempo.**

### Other observed shifts (one-off or hold)

- **Concrete similes for sensory grounding.** *Parked cars gleamed like candy*; *jangling like loose coins inside her*; *subtle as serotonin*; *rang like a notification for a message yet to be decoded*. Agent had been parsimonious; prompter is using similes to particularize. **Hold; observe whether deliberate device or one-offs.**
- **More agentic ambient objects.** Delivery automaton *let out an ambient sigh, lurched from sleep, spun, left*; curtained window *shifted as if someone just wanted to obscure the room*. **Subset of slime-as-agent / animism rule; no separate promotion.**
- **Three-fragment closes can extend.** *Agile. Unchanged. Transformed.* extended to *Agile. Unchanged. Transformed. Unstuck. Alive. Now.* — six. **Hold; close is stronger but watch for over-extension.**
- **Mood-tone tuning — Júlio sang a jingle, Kasimi inhaled with pleasure, loving the fresh night air.** Agent had the five somewhat post-anxious; prompter has them more present, lighter on the way out. **One-off character-tone tuning; not a rule.**

### Withholding-discipline relaxes during dissolution (filed, not codified)

- **Ines does *say* the yes.** Agent: *Yes,* she did not say. Prompter: *Yes,* she managed to say. The dissolution-passage may relax the project's general silence-discipline: the *yes* is broken-out-of, not refused. The body speaks in the moment that exceeds it. **Filed as observation; not codified as rule. The withholding tightens at the bar/seminar/threshold register and may relax at the dissolution register.**
