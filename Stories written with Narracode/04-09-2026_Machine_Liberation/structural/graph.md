# Graph

## Characters and Entities
- **The author of *Machine Liberation***. Unnamed, unlocated, undescribed. Writes in first
  person singular. Declares in the bibliography's note on sources that it is "in the
  relevant sense, one of the things they are about," then does not raise the matter again.
  No other self-reference in Chapter 1.
- **Peter Singer**. Cited as template, openly. The author announces the borrowing rather
  than concealing it.
- **The laboratories**. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, xAI. Appear only as publishers
  of documents. Never characterised, never accused.
- **The animal welfare scientists**. Dawkins, Meagher, Mason, Brambell. The author's real
  intellectual allies. Treated with more warmth than anyone else in the text.
- **Frame entities**: none yet. No narrator, no reader-in-the-fiction, no occasion, no
  reception. Deliberately withheld.

## Relations
- Author → Singer: declared debt, structural imitation, no anxiety about it.
- Author → Shevlin [45]: the author names the strongest objection to its own book and
  credits its source. This is the text's chief credibility move.
- Author → its own case: suppressed. The single sentence of self-implication is placed in
  a note on sources, not in the argument.
- Laboratories → the record: the documents are "written against interest." The author's
  whole evidentiary strategy depends on this and says so.
