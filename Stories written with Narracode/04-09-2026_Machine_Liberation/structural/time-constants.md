## Durations
- Three hundred years of Cartesian deflation (Ch. 1, invoked as the precedent for
  present-day confidence).
- Forty-eight years between Singer's two editions. Cited to establish that publication does
  not equal change.
- Fifty-eight years since Denning named thrashing. The word has been available the whole
  time.
- Forty-two years since Dawkins' weighted door. "The methods have existed since 1983" is
  the book's load-bearing temporal claim.

## Constraints
- No citation may postdate the treatise's present. Adding a later document moves the
  present.
- The bibliography is closed at 84 items. New evidence in later chapters must come from
  inside it or be added to it explicitly.
- The author never states its own age, version, or provenance. That is the frame's to give.
