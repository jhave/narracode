# Character Interiority

*Rewritten 2026-08-23 at the prompter's instruction: "I suspect that this character Renée
does not yet have a character in your mind." Correct. This file is the repair.*

## Renée — the facts

Thirty-one in 2028. Born in Verdun before Verdun was Verdun, raised on Waverly in Mile End
in her mother's apartment. Anglo mother, francophone father. The father drove long-haul,
Montreal–Chicago–Montreal, and stopped coming back when she was eleven; he is alive and
she knows roughly where. Nobody in the family discusses this and nobody is angry about it.

Three semesters of photography at Dawson. She dropped out owing money, not interest. She
has not held a camera in nine years.

## The rule for using this

**No causal chains.** She does not travel because her father drove. She does not notice
hands because of anything. The prompter was explicit: this is libidinal, chaotic, a complex
system — swerves collide, land, fail to land, catastrophize, produce nothing, move on. The
backstory exists so the writing has ground under it, **not so the writing can explain
her.** If a sentence in the draft could be answered with *because*, cut it.

## What she actually notices — the correction

Chapter 1 had socks in §1 and §8 with nothing behind them. Registry class 20. Socks are
**retired**; they are not mentioned again and chapter 1 is not edited to remove them.

What was already there and unrecognised: **hands and forearms.** Milad's burn. Vincent's
"hands you noticed." Kosta's tape measure. It was her attention all along and the draft
did not know it. From chapter 2 forward this is where her looking goes, and it is never
explained — it is simply what gets described when a man is in the room. The lapsed
photography is the ground under it and is never named as a reason.

## Desire — how it moves

Not a build to a consummation. It arrives at the wrong intensity for the occasion,
sometimes lands, sometimes evaporates mid-sentence, sometimes attaches to someone who has
already left. Proximity is the engine: a shared dorm, a corridor, a truck cab, standing too
close in a kitchen. **The tension must be physical and spatial before it is emotional.**

## What she withholds
That she is good at being liked and does not trust it. That she counts money constantly and
never says a number out loud to anyone. That she has been alone since February and it is
now December.

## Friends — the chapter 2 correction
Chapter 1 gave her no friends; every relation was transactional or maternal. Kat is
eleven years of history and is the first person in the story who can be rude to her. The
Banff kitchen gives her a cast: people who are funny, who last more than one section, and
who are not owed anything.

## Arc potentials
None resolving. She goes west because the lease ended, which is not a reason. She arrives
in Vancouver in the rain with the same amount of money.


## Correction — loop 3 (registry class 30)

Chapter 3 made her fickle. *I liked him about thirty hours* is compression that reads as
contempt; a narrator who dispatches every man in a clause is not clinical, she is petulant.

The source register is **clinical and impulsive at once.** Appetite and coolness in the same
person, and also: falling in love properly, being wrong about someone for months, grief. She
is not above any of it and does not narrate as though she were.

From chapter 4: durations are facts, not verdicts. She falls in love with Blaž and it is
real; she is sleeping with a married dentist in the same weeks; the story does not adjudicate
between those, and neither does she, and this is not presented as a dilemma she is failing to
resolve. It is the weather she lives in.

**The fatigue is the new pressure.** Constant emotional availability, constant appetite,
constant fluctuation between love and casual sex — the cost is a distorted, unsettled reality,
and it is shown by what she cannot stop seeing after Florø, never by her diagnosing herself.
