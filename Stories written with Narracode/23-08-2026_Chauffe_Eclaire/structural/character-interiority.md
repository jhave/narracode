# Character Interiority

## Characters
**Renée.** Wants warmth, states it once (§6), and the statement is about a thermostat. Loyal instantly and without deliberation (§2). Takes money against advice and does not defend it. Refuses shame where shame is expected and offers no substitute. Her interiority is conveyed almost entirely by what she declines to say after a fact.

**The men.** Each is given one competence and one wound and then removed. None is judged.

**The mother.** The only person permitted a confession of her own, and she delivers it in the register the whole story has been training the reader to hear — flat, sexual, unashamed, immediately followed by soup.

## Arc Potentials
Deliberately unrealized. She does not learn, ascend, or settle. The rent ladder rises; she does not.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
§6 is the closest the story comes and it is a refusal of catharsis: "I want to say I was ashamed. I was not ashamed." The reader supplies the feeling the narrator declines.
