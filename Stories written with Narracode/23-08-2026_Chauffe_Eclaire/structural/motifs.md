# Motifs

## Active
- **chauffé éclairé** — title, §1 opening (false), §6 (stolen, true), §8 closing (offered again). Three appearances, exactly.
- **Numbers as the only adjective** — 640, 590, 19.50, 41, 90, 1,190, 610, 2,000, 23, 300, 940, 1,655, 900.
- **Heat and its absence** — cold radiators, humidex, cooling centre, central air, 23 degrees stolen, smoke.
- **Socks** — Kosta keeps his on §1; Ahmed leaves his §8. Bookend, unremarked.
- **Food as the only tenderness** — Fadwa's foil plate, the mother's soup. Both accepted, neither discussed.
- **Located abstraction** — three uses, at cap: hate in the teeth (§2), pity in the wrists (§4), comfort behind the ribs (§6).

## Transformations
Heat moves from withheld (§1) to purchased by proxy (§3) to stolen (§6) to unbreathable (§8).

## Avoid Overuse
`chauffé éclairé` at three is at ceiling. A fourth would make it a refrain and the story would start explaining itself. Socks must never be mentioned a third time.


## Chapter 2 — added
- **Hands and forearms** — promoted to the primary motif and made retroactive: Dez's forearm (§9), Emerson's floured knuckles and short sleeves in a cold house (§11), Kieran's wrists (§13). Continuous with Milad's burn and Vincent's hands in chapter 1 without chapter 1 being altered.
- **Socks** — RETIRED. Registry class 20. Zero occurrences in chapter 2.
- **Room-and-price openers** — RETIRED. Registry class 21.
- **The gnomic place-line** — new. §9 Toronto, §11 Winnipeg. Deliberately absent from four sections so it does not become the replacement formula.
- **The chest** — used twice as the site of feeling (§10 Loreen's belt, §11 the struck pipe). Flagged in the tell-scan. At ceiling; a third would be a tic.
- **Smoke** — returns from §8 transformed: June to October, Quebec to BC, eleven days to a season that "never used to be October".
