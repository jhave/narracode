# Motifs

## Active
- **chauffé éclairé** — title, §1 opening (false), §6 (stolen, true), §8 closing (offered again). Three appearances, exactly.
- **Numbers as the only adjective** — 640, 590, 19.50, 41, 90, 1,190, 610, 2,000, 23, 300, 940, 1,655, 900.
- **Heat and its absence** — cold radiators, humidex, cooling centre, central air, 23 degrees stolen, smoke.
- **Socks** — Kosta keeps his on §1; Ahmed leaves his §8. Bookend, unremarked.
- **Food as the only tenderness** — Fadwa's foil plate, the mother's soup. Both accepted, neither discussed.
- **Located abstraction** — three uses, at cap: hate in the teeth (§2), pity in the wrists (§4), comfort behind the ribs (§6).

## Transformations
Heat moves from withheld (§1) to purchased by proxy (§3) to stolen (§6) to unbreathable (§8).

## Avoid Overuse
`chauffé éclairé` at three is at ceiling. A fourth would make it a refrain and the story would start explaining itself. Socks must never be mentioned a third time.
