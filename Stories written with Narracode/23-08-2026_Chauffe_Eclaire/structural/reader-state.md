# Reader State

## Current Understanding
A woman is being moved through a city by prices. Men are incidents. The sections do not connect and are not meant to.

## Expectations
By §3 the reader expects each section to end in a turn, and starts reading for it. By §5 the reader expects her to take the money and she does. This is the story keeping faith, not the story being predictable.

## Plausible Defiance Paths
Not taken, and deliberately: the gun returning; Ahmed getting a ninth section; the tribunal date arriving; her getting the Rosemont apartment.

## Productive Mystery
The gun. Whether the letter worked. Whether she is all right, which the story never raises as a question and therefore makes unanswerable.

## Accidental Confusion Risk
The rent ladder is the only chronological anchor between sections. A reader skimming may lose the sixteen months. Judged acceptable — the disorientation is proportionate to the subject.
