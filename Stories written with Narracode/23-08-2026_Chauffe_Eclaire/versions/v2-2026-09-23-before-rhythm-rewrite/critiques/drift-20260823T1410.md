# Drift — Chauffé Éclairé

*Reflexive agent, drift mode. Cumulative material against `POETICS.md`.*

## What the piece declared it would be
Episodic sexual autobiography in a flat short-declarative register, Montreal 2028,
socioeconomics carried by neighbourhood, each section turning on an unprepared confession.

## What it became
That, with one unplanned addition: **a housing story that uses men as its unit of
measurement.** POETICS framed the apartments as setting and the encounters as subject. In
the finished draft the ratio inverted. Six of eight sections are organised by a lease, a
rent, or a displacement; the men arrive inside those structures and leave when the lease
does. The title turned out to be the subject rather than the frame.

## Is the difference fertile or genre regression?
**Fertile.** It is also the more honest version of the prompter's brief — he asked for
socioeconomic valences modulated by neighbourhood, and the draft took that instruction more
seriously than the POETICS document did. The inversion should be written back into POETICS
rather than corrected out of the draft.

## Second drift, smaller
The register began as flat-observational and acquired, unbidden, a **dryness** — Milad's
"that is what finished means", the one fork, "Don't be, it was the good room". Comedy was
not declared in POETICS. It arrived anyway and it is carrying the sections where the
material is bleakest. Recommend declaring it: *humour in the same flat register as the
grief, never signalled*. It is already in the Voice section as an aside; it deserves to be a
commitment.

## Drift not present
No slide toward lyricism. No section acquired an explanatory paragraph. Weather never
became a participant. The rent ladder never turned into a moral.

## Recommendation
Amend `POETICS.md` at loop 1 on two points — housing as subject rather than frame, and
comedy as a declared commitment. Do not amend the draft to match the old document.
