# Critique — Chauffé Éclairé, all sections

*Reflexive agent, critique mode, against `POETICS.md`. Recommends; does not revise.*

## The challenge, restated

The prompter set a specific test: write in a raw, succinct, observational register without
producing a rule-regulated clone, and prove that a machine can reach the embodied
directness of a phrase like the one he quoted. He was explicit that he expected to be
proved wrong.

**He was half proved wrong.** The prosody is reproducible and was reproduced. The
embodiment is partial. The gap between those two results is the honest finding of this run.

## What holds

**The numbers did the work adjectives would have done.** Every rent, wage, temperature and
payout stands bare, with no clause telling the reader how to feel about it. This was the
constraint most likely to fail and it did not fail once in 1,430 words. The socioeconomic
brief was executed through arithmetic and postal codes rather than through commentary, as
`POETICS.md` required.

**The turns land.** §5 (advice given, advice disobeyed in one sentence) and §6 (trespass
confessed, shame refused) are the two places where the flatness produces the intended
convulsion. In both, the turn is a fact in the same register as the furniture, and the
reader supplies the feeling. That is the mechanism the prompter described as punk music in
words, and in those two sections it works.

**Nobody is developed.** The refusal to let a liked character return is the structural
discipline the register depends on, and it held for all fourteen named people. Milad,
Fadwa, Vincent and the woman on Bourget are each strong enough that a conventional draft
would have brought them back. None returns.

**Zero em-dashes. Zero registry tells.** The house's most persistent machine signatures are
absent, verified line by line rather than asserted.

## Where it falls short of the brief

**1. The located abstraction is the weakest imitation of the strongest thing.**
The prompter's quoted phrase does something the three instances here do not: it places an
abstract organ inside a specific sexual body-part and refuses to soften either noun. Our
three — teeth, wrists, ribs — are anatomically safe. They locate feeling in *neutral*
body-parts. That is the operation performed at reduced voltage. The engine correctly
identified the procedure and then executed it timidly. This is the run's clearest failure
against the target and it should be said plainly rather than dressed up.

**2. Grammatical monotony under a satisfied metric.**
The validator confirms varied sentence *length*. It cannot see that the dominant *shape* is
subject-verb-object declarative. Read aloud, §2 and §6 develop a metronome. Three or four
sentences per section should be broken toward fragment, question, or bare noun phrase — for
shape alone, at no cost to the numbers.

**3. The compensation aphorism.**
Three section-endings reach for a quiet universal. Individually defensible; as a set they
are the naming clause in shorter trousers. Proposed as a new registry class in the
tell-scan. §3's "Both of those were true and I have not worked out how" is the one to cut
first if the prompter cuts one.

**4. Sex is candid but thin.**
Named plainly, once per encounter, undecorated — which satisfies the letter of §4 of the
voice engine. But the source register the prompter named is *specific* about bodies in a way
this draft is not. The draft achieves unsentimentality partly by achieving vagueness, and
those are not the same accomplishment. A human pass should push two of the encounters
toward physical specificity without adding a single adjective of feeling.

**5. Length.**
1,430 words across eight sections gives roughly 180 words per section. The turns arrive
because the sections are short enough that they must. A longer draft would test whether the
register can sustain itself or whether it depends on brevity for its effect. Untested here.

## Judgement

The measurable properties of the register were extracted, specified, instrumented and met.
The unmeasurable property — the specific nerve of the quoted phrase — was approached and
not reached. The instrument in `tools/tillman_check.py` is the durable artefact of this run:
it will fail any future draft that drifts back toward fluent literary default, and it
cannot tell you whether the draft is alive. Both halves of that sentence are the result.

## Recommended next pass

Human seamless edit, in this order: the three located abstractions (§1 of the failures),
then the two encounters that need bodies, then §3's closing sentence. Do not touch the
prosody — it is at target and the numbers are recoverable if an edit breaks them.
