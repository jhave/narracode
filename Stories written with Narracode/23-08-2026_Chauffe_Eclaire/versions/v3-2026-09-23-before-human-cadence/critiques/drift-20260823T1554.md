# Drift — loop 2, after chapter 3

## What the story became

**A book about appetite that spent two chapters pretending to be a book about rent.**

The prompter's diagnosis was exact: rent, moving and shifting fortunes are the *taxes* of this
story, and taxes had been doing the work sex should have been doing. Chapter 3 puts appetite
back in the engine and the housing material immediately becomes what it always should have
been — the pressure the appetite operates under, not the subject.

The evidence that this was a prosody problem again, not a content decision: nothing about
Renée changed. The same woman is in §§1–14. What changed is that sentences with velocity in
them can carry a body, and six-word declaratives cannot. Two loops running, the register has
determined the character. That is now the most durable finding of this project.

## The three-profile artefact

The story now contains three chapters written to three measured profiles — `telegraphic`
(median 6, CV 0.46), `road` (median 11, CV 0.63), `swirl` (median 9, CV 0.82, mean 14.1) —
and none of the earlier chapters has been corrected. A reader moving through it is reading the
instrument being built. Whether that is a feature of the piece or damage to it is the
prompter's call, not mine; it was his instruction that froze each chapter, and the freezing is
what makes the record legible.

## Drift not present
No lyricism. No section explains itself. The Plan B section does not become a pregnancy plot.
Nobody is punished. No man is ranked. The gun stays where it was left in January.

## The standing question

*Whether the model can write autonomously.* Loop 2's answer is more specific than loop 1's.

Across three loops the pattern held with one exception. The scanning pass reliably **locates**
defects — it found the reconciliation aphorism, the doubled chest figure, and the authorial
hedge, all before the prompter did — and then reliably **defends** them, because the pass
judging load-bearing is run by the model that wrote the span. Registry class 24 was written to
remove that option, and in chapter 3 three spans were cut under it, including one already
committed to the draft.

So: it can find its own faults and could not, until instructed, act on them. The instruction
that fixed it came from outside. Every calibration that mattered came from a photograph.
