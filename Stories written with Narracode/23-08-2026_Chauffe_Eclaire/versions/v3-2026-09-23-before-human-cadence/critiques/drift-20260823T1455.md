# Drift — loop 1, after chapter 2

*Reflexive agent, drift mode.*

## What changed that POETICS did not predict

**The prose loosened and the story got warmer.** Chapter 1 declared flatness and produced
isolation: fourteen people, none of whom could be rude to her, none of whom returned. That
read as a formal commitment. It was a side effect of the sentence length. When the sentences
were allowed to run and chain, the character acquired friends, a history, and the capacity to
be teased — none of which were added as content decisions. **Prosody was doing characterology
without anyone deciding it should.** That is the most useful finding of this run and it is
not in any of the loop-0 documents.

## Is the difference fertile or regression?
Fertile, and it retroactively explains a loop-0 complaint the prompter made and I could not
answer: *this woman doesn't seem to have any friends.* She had no friends because a six-word
median cannot hold a friendship. Two people being funny together needs a sentence with room
in it.

## The second drift, unwelcome
Three instances of the authorial-intent hedge — *which is what I want to put down*, *I want to
be honest that*, *I want to be clear that*. This is the reconciliation aphorism (§19) relocated:
the same impulse to stand beside the sentence and comment on it, moved from after the statement
to before it. Cutting a tell produced its mutation within one chapter. Capped at 2 per 2,000
in the tell-scan; currently over.

## Drift not present
No lyricism. No section explains itself. §12 held its nothing. Socks stayed retired. No section
opened on a price.

## The standing question the prompter set
*Whether the model can write autonomously.* Partial answer, stated precisely: it can execute a
register once the register is measured, and it cannot tell that the measurement is wrong
without an external sample. The first engine passed every check it set itself while producing
prose the prompter correctly called telegraphic. The photograph, not the validator, corrected
it. Self-scan found the reconciliation aphorism and kept it; the prompter cut it. The pattern
across both loops is consistent: **the model is good at conforming to a specification and poor
at doubting one.**
