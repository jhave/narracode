## Found by the audit in my own draft and cut

**Two fresh class-22 hedges**, written after registering the class:
- *I want to be accurate: I thought about her precisely twice…* → **I thought about her twice, both times in the stairwell.**
- *…and I want to be exact about my own conduct: I did not step forward…* → **I did not step forward. I did not look away. I noticed that her shoes were good.**

The second correction is the better sentence by a distance, and it is the one the register wanted. Registering a class did not stop me producing it; the tool caught it.

**A numeric cluster identical in kind to chapter 3's.** First draft: *four* × 10, *one* × 9, *two* × 6, *eleven* × 5. This is registry class 26, written the same hour, and I did it again immediately. Rewritten to zero values at three or more occurrences.

**Vagueness:** *the thing*, *things* × 2, *something* × 2, *in a way*, *completely*, *entirely* × 2, *extremely*, *a new kind of tiredness*. All replaced with the noun or deleted.

**A static repeat inside a working motif:** a seventh *charge* used for the baggage fee, duplicating the sense already carried by the dental charge. Cut. A motif does not get an exemption from its own rule.
