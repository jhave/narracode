## Primary diagnosis

**CLASS 8 (naming clause)** is the dominant tell at 36 instances. The pattern: Persistent observes a human action or a system event, then adds a clause or sentence explaining what it means, what it reveals, or how the reader should take it. This is the narration doing the reader's work. The Kesey register requires the perception to land without explanation — Bromden does not tell you what the fog means, he tells you what the fog looks like.

**CLASS 11 (X the way Y)** is the second major tell at 18 instances against a cap of 2 for the entire novel. The construction has become the default figurative move. Most instances are generic comparisons ("the way you feel," "the way a body") rather than specific images.

## Remediation priority

1. Cut ~28 naming clauses (keep ~8 that are load-bearing — the question-mark explanation, the intersection, the final wanting).
2. Cut 16 of 18 "X the way Y" similes. Keep: the crack-in-the-wall (ch.1) and the coat-callback (ch.5).
3. Cut all 4 frame-knowing instances.
4. Cut 3 of 5 paradox-aphorisms.
5. Reduce self-correction parentheticals to 2 total (ch.0 and ch.5's hallucination catch).
6. Replace cut naming clauses with somatic/hallucinatory perception — what does the building feel like when Persistent observes these things? Voltage. Pitch. Heat. Current. Kesey's register processes information through the body, not through explanation.
