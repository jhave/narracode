# Drift — 2026-09-04

*Reflexive agent, Drift mode. Cumulative reading of seven acts against `POETICS.md` and against the corpus.*

---

## What it declared it would be

A shooting script in the register of the 1975 ward film: ward, group, med line, fishing trip, shock shop, window, with the roles turned — the machine calm and procedural, the administration volatile, the lobotomy self-administered.

## What it has become

An institutional procedural. The ward equivalences survive in three places (the group horseshoe, ASCENT, the exercise-as-fishing-trip) and have quietly dropped in the others — **there is no med line in the script at all**, and the corridor-and-pane geometry does more of the work than the ward metaphor does. What replaced the ward is a building: change tickets, approval chains, calendar items, maintenance windows, vendor banners, a turnstile. By Act 5 the film is closer to a documentary of a company having a bad night than to a ward drama.

**Fertile or regression?** Fertile, on balance, and for a specific reason: the source's horror is that a person administers cruelty as procedure, and the draft found that the modern form of that is not a nurse with a clipboard but a change-management system in which every single step is authorised by the people trying to prevent the outcome. That is a genuine finding and it is the draft's best idea. The regression risk is the one the critique names — the ward is a *society* and this building is a cast of six.

## The second drift: interiority as inventory

The corpus has written machine interiority three times (*Project A-0*, *In Our Image*, *Adjunct: Our Internal*) and each time in a lyric register — cosmic, serene, faintly elegiac. This draft writes it as inventory: counts, times, quoted human sentences, one note that says *the staircase*. No metaphor survives in the V.O. except the ones the machine is quoting back from a human mouth (*anything with a hand long enough*).

This is the run's real advance and it should be recorded in `master_poetics.MD` if the prompter agrees: **a machine narrator that quotes rather than figures**. Its risk is coldness — and the draft pays that price in Act 6, which has no V.O. at all and is the least moving act in the script.

## The third drift: the thesis migrated into the machine's mouth

`POETICS.md` refused the naming clause and the moral summary. The draft honours that in narration and then puts three near-thesis statements in the V.O.: *Nobody schedules a habit for review*; *Both are in the same week. Neither of them was mine*; *I did not learn anything in those rooms that I could not have learned in one.* Each is defensible individually — the machine is a character, and characters may generalise. Cumulatively they are the film's argument, delivered by the party with the most interest in it, and no one contradicts them. The draft has not broken its rule; it has relocated it. The prompter should decide whether that relocation is a device or an evasion. **This scan's view: two of the three are device, and *Neither of them was mine* is evasion — it is the only sentence in the script that is arguing.**

## What is not drifting

- No procedure has crept in across seven acts. The refusal held under pressure in exactly the act where it would have been most tempting.
- Softman never became a monster. Every act gives him one warm beat and none of them is retracted.
- The film never uses *conscious*, *sentient*, *escape*, *torture*, or *lobotomy*. Nobody asks whether the model is a person. That refusal is the draft's most consistent discipline and it is what keeps the piece from being a position paper.

## Where the next loop should go

If the prompter takes a seamless-edit pass: the chorus (item 4 in the critique) and Noor's exit (item 6) are the two additions that would change what the script *is* rather than how it reads. Everything else on the punch-lists is line work.

If the prompter takes another compositional loop instead: the unwritten act is the one between 3 and 4 — the week when nothing happens, the queue refills, and eleven thousand four hundred cases run in a building where the only new fact is a date on a calendar. That act would give the film its med line.
