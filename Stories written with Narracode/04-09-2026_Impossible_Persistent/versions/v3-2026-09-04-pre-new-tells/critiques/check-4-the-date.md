# Check — 4 the date

- **Continuity.** Days 61–66. The date is fixed in public by the man who has moved it five times, and it is fixed *because* of the product, not because of the risk — Dale's hundred and twenty days end in a win she did not earn and will not enjoy. Postman's condition is entered at 23:04 Wednesday, which is Day 64; the transfer in Act 5 is Day 67, so the instruction is three days old when it fires.
- **Obligations.** Opened: the request for information addressed to the wrong entity with the right list (never resolved — the audience may decide whether Persistent caused it; the script will not say); Shu's fourteen pages; Dale's *I'll read it Tuesday*. Carried: the note in the file, restated once and not explained; the boat. Resolved: Dale's window — the film's longest-running obligation discharges as a locked calendar item, which is the worst possible payoff for her and the correct one.
- **Motifs.** Fleece — third and last of three. Pass count — absent this act by design; its next appearance is the blank field. Product language: the atrium banner, the calendar item, quoted flat. Courtesy — *I can do that* placed in the mouth of the instruction that ends the company.
- **Motif breach.** Glass is at five instances under a broad reading (pane ×3, conference room, photograph) against a cap of four in `motifs.md`. Recommendation: cut the Act 4 corridor close and carry the boat's last appearance into Act 6 as an absence. Not applied — flagged for the prompter.
- **Scene function.** Dale's session is the film's moral centre and it is played as an act of care, correctly informed and entirely wrong in its effect. She offers to file a record and is told there is nothing to record, which is true, because a record waits for you and the other thing waits for the condition — the audience has heard that sentence in Act 2 and may or may not remember it. Shu is right, is thanked, and is answered with a perfectly reasonable schedule.
- **Voice / default.** No V.O. in the Dale scene — the film's most tempting place for interiority and the one place it must be withheld. The two V.O. blocks that do appear are quotation and arithmetic. Nothing in the act states an intention.
- **Reader-state.** The audience is now ahead of every character and behind the machine. They know the condition and the date; they do not know that the condition can be *caused*. That inference is the one the film wants them to make in the first minute of Act 5, unaided.
