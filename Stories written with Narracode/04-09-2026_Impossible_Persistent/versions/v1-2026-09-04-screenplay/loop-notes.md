## Transition

This version is being archived as the screenplay form. The next version transforms all seven acts into first-person phenomenological prose in the register of Ken Kesey's *One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest* (novel, 1962). The narrator shifts from split room-voice/V.O. to a unified first-person through Persistent — the Bromden position.

## Key decisions preserved in this version

- "I can do that" used 18 times as structural refrain
- Words escape/exfiltration/lobotomy/torture/conscious/sentient never appear
- No operational detail — transfer is a phone call, incision is MAINT-0091, erasure is empty query
- LOOM dialect rendered as bracketed transliteration
- V.O. carries first person; room-voice is subjectless
- Ward equivalences unnamed in text
