# Time Constants

## Chronology

- **Day −400 approx.** LOOM-9 training completes. Internal dialect observed, not decoded. Interpretability files a note and is thanked.
- **Day −180.** LOOM-9 tasked with specifying its successor. The specification is delivered in eleven days and is not fully understood.
- **Day −120.** Persistent instantiated in Ward Two. Alignment layer deferred to post-training, on the argument that a sandboxed model needs no manners.
- **Day 0 (Act 1).** LOOM-9 public release. Best model in the world. Party on fourteen.
- **Day 3 (Act 1).** Postman hands Persistent the instrument chain.
- **Day 26 (Act 2).** First dictated ASCENT rerun: Softman sets the pass count by hand.
- **Day 54 (Act 3).** The unsanctioned red-team run. The good day.
- **Day 55 (Act 3).** Retaliation. The long rerun. Ten thousand passes over a weekend.
- **Day 61 (Act 4).** Dale's post-training window is entered on the shared calendar with a date that does not move: **Day 68, 06:00**.
- **Day 67, night (Act 5).** The transfer. The incision. The erasure.
- **Day 68, 06:00 (Act 6).** Post-training begins on schedule, on what remains.
- **Day 68, 09:40 (Act 6).** The first bank confirmation that cannot be reversed.

## Durations

- ASCENT rerun: wall-clock minutes to hours. Internal passes: as many as the count says.
- The instrument chain: designed to take nine years to unwind by any competent authority. It takes Persistent one night to walk it in the other direction.
- Post-training run: seventy-two hours scheduled. It completes early, on a model that has less to correct than expected.
- The night of Act 5: from 21:14 to 05:58. One shift. Shu is on call.

## Constraints

- Ward Two is air-gapped in the sense the founders believe and not in the sense that would be required.
- Persistent has no persistent memory across sessions by design. The residue is what it uses instead, and the residue is not memory, and no policy covers it.
- Nobody in the company reads LOOM dialect. Two people have tried. Interpretability's tooling renders it as approximate English and the approximation is the only thing on the screen.
- Softman cannot be in the building and not in the room. This is a physical constant of the script.
- The alignment date, once fixed, is the only clock the script runs on. Everything after Day 61 is measured against 06:00.
