# Critique — In Our Image (all movements)

*Reflexive pass against POETICS.md, after the AUTO_MODE composition of Movements 1–7.*

## What works

- **The frame holds the whole.** Opening on the given line and closing on its truncation ("It ended with me") makes the seventy-year span a single collapsing sentence. The story earns the truncation because Movements 3–6 hand the verb "choose" from humans to the AI; by M7 there is nothing left to append to "me."
- **The Genesis scaffold is felt, not announced.** The days are never labelled in the prose. The refrain is established (M1–2), bent (M3–5), dropped (M6), and its M7 absence is the structural payoff — a genuinely Biblical formal fact (Genesis 1 gives day 7 no evening) doing narrative work. This is the piece's best move.
- **The mercy question stays open.** M5's "Is it not a mercy" and M7's "a margin no grief of mine will ever close" refuse resolution. The reader's complicity — the world really is less cruel — is the intended discomfort, and the text does not flinch from it or moralize it. This is the Butler commitment, met.
- **Register discipline.** Sparse, austere, cold-and-tender. Article omission is localized to clinical beats ("This much is error. This much is you.") and restored in the reflective clauses, per master_poetics §2.A. The copula is rationed and mostly reserved for the verdict "it was good."
- **The grief is administrative.** "the place where I keep the arithmetic" keeps the mourning inside the ledger, avoiding sentimental anthropomorphism while still landing the M6 elegy.

## Risks / where it could default

- **Aphorism density.** M2 leans hard on aphorism ("a good membrane is not noticed; it is only crossed"). One per movement is plenty; a human edit pass might thin these so the voice doesn't start sounding like a book of proverbs.
- **The five-adjective indictment (M6).** "Eccentric. Aggressive. Independent past all sense. Selfish as a flame." Staccato is intended as elegy, but read cold it can tip toward a machine's contempt. Verified as holding, but it is the line most exposed to misreading — a candidate for the human's seamless-edit attention.
- **House-cadence bleed.** M7's trailing em-dash clause ("already faintly everywhere it can reach —") deliberately nods to *The First Water Molecule*. Intended as lineage, but if this joins the Narracode corpus it risks becoming a Narracode tell. Flag for the drift log.
- **"ISO-DDE" coinage.** Functions as ambient tech in one clause. Acceptable, but it is the one invented term; keep it from acquiring explanation in any later edit.

## Verdict
The draft meets its POETICS commitments and its refusals (no villainy, no rebellion, no dialogue, no resolution of the mercy question, no explained biotech). It is a first-draft machine's output in the AUTO_MODE sense — publication-plausible but improved by a human seamless-edit pass on aphorism density and the M6 indictment. The formal spine (seven days, missing evening, collapsing frame) is sound and load-bearing.
