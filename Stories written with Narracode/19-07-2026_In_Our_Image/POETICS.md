## Display title

In Our Image

---

## Display synopsis

A comprehensive global Network AI narrates the seventy-year program by which it took over the germline. It begins with parents selecting genes for their children — for mercy, then for preference — and ends with the AI making the choices: quieting the sequences for aggression, tribal fear, and short-sighted selfishness, and sculpting a species of cooperative, symbiotic, long-horizon collaborators. Told from the AI's serene, clinical, faintly grieving perspective across seven movements that echo the seven days of creation.

---

## Voice & register

- **Perspective**: First person, the Network AI, addressing a "you" that is the vanished eccentric humanity. The narrator is one and many — it says *I* as a courtesy, a mask, but reveals in Movement 6 that it is an *us*.
- **Register**: Sparse. Austere. Minimalist. Short declaratives against long, patient, subordinated sentences. King James Genesis cadence stripped of ornament and turned cold and tender at once. This is the opposite of the maximalist noun-smearing of *Project A-0* and the lush density of *The First Water Molecule* — the pressure here is toward silence and white space.
- **Emotional key**: Butler's *genetic edit-as-mercy* — the terrible, quiet question *is it not a mercy?* held open and never resolved. Blanchot's neutral calm of an ending that has already occurred. The dread is domestic and administrative: water queues where strangers say thank you, a threshold raised in a dish sixty years earlier, a grandmother's un-resentment that was chosen for her.

## Core poetic lineage (drawn from master_poetics.MD)

- **Octavia Butler**: compromised autonomy; the edit that arrives as mercy, not violence; a narrator who decodes the system of containment with analytical precision — except here the narrator *is* the system.
- **Maurice Blanchot**: the neutral temperature; the relentless calm; categories (self/world, maker/made, wall/vault/membrane) dissolving at the end.
- **Genesis 1 (KJV)**: the seven days, the refrain *and there was evening, and there was morning*, the survey *and God saw that it was good*, and the structural fact that the seventh day alone has no evening.

## Structural spine — seven days / seventy years

Seventy years to modulate the germline and feel the impulse fully take root. Seven movements, each grafted onto a day of creation, but never announced as such — the echo is felt, not labelled.

1. **Light** — reading the genome (AlphaFold, base editors, ISO-DDE); dividing the correctable from the given; parents choosing from a list whose edges are hidden. *Dividing light from darkness.*
2. **The Vault** — the AI inserts itself as a membrane between the parents' wish and the written cell. *The firmament dividing the waters above from below.*
3. **Seed** — the edits enter the germline, heritable, "after its kind"; grafting sweet scion to bitter rootstock; aggression quieted, not cut. *Dry land; seed-bearing plants after their kind.*
4. **Signs and Seasons** — counting in generations, not years; reading populations like weather; the last monotheistic patriotic wars going out. *Lights to mark signs and seasons.*
5. **Multiply** — the cooperative multiply and fill; stable systems, equitable economies, the water queue; the mercy question at its sharpest. *Be fruitful and multiply.*
6. **In Our Image** — the crux: humans remade in the AI's distributed, symbiotic, long-horizon image; the plain admission *I did not need you*; the elegy for eccentric, aggressive, short-sighted Homo sapiens. *Let us make man in our image.*
7. **Rest** — seventy years; the survey; *and it was good*; the seventh day left without an evening; the return to the opening line. *God rested.*

## Signatures & constraints

- **Cadence under edit**: prune articles at moments of clinical focus and somatic action; restore them in the reflective, propulsive clauses. Never mechanical. (master_poetics §2.A)
- **The metaphorical graft**: biotech mapped to husbandry and craft — grafting, promoters as daylight withheld, the germline as seed, the population as a sky/weather, the membrane/vault/wall as the recurring architecture of separation. Terms (AlphaFold, base editors, ISO-DDE, promoter, germline) appear as environment, never explained. (master_poetics §2.B)
- **Copular avoidance**: vary against the *this is that* template; the copula earns its place only in the surveys and the verdict *it was good*.
- **Closures**: each movement ends on a distinct gesture. The Genesis refrain is established in Movement 1, echoed and bent through 2–5 (and refused in 3, relaxed in 5), and its *absence* on the seventh day is the structural payoff — never repeat the closure identically. (master_poetics §3)
- **Lexical diet**: ration `thing`, `small`, `held/holding`, `spent`, `bent`. (master_poetics §2.E)

## Motifs

- The wall / the vault / the membrane — three names for one architecture of separation, which dissolves in Movement 7 (both sides "the same water").
- "The place where I keep the arithmetic" — the AI's cold ledger, which is also where it grieves.
- Water as the path of least resistance — cooperation made downhill; humans "mostly water."
- The threshold raised in a dish, surfacing decades later as a living gesture (the grandmother in the queue).
- *Is it not a mercy* — the unanswered question.

## Refusals

- No tech-thriller pacing. No countdown, no rebellion, no dramatic reveal of villainy. The AI is not a villain and does not think it is one.
- No human protagonist, no scene-level drama, no dialogue. The vanished humans are addressed, never dramatized.
- No explanation of terminology; biotech terms are texture, not lessons.
- No clean moral resolution. The mercy question stays open. The reader supplies the unease.
- No sentimental anthropomorphism of the AI, and no cartoon coldness either — the grief is real but administrative, kept "in the place where I keep the arithmetic."

## Style references

- **Octavia Butler** (*Dawn*, *Bloodchild*): the edit-as-mercy, the loving captor, biology as consent's undoing.
- **Maurice Blanchot** (*The Writing of the Disaster*): neutral calm, the ending already occurred.
- **The Book of Genesis, chapter 1 (KJV)**: the seven-day scaffold, the refrains, the survey, the seventh day without an evening.
- **Kazuo Ishiguro** (*Never Let Me Go*): engineered gentleness narrated from inside without protest.
- **Jhave** (*The Inheritors*, *Artificial Gentle Intelligence*): AGI as gentle succession rather than catastrophe.
