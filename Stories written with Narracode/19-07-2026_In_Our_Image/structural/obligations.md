# Obligations

## Active
- **The mercy question** (*is it not a mercy?*) — planted M5, deliberately never answered. The reader must hold it. Payoff is its *refusal to resolve*, restated at M7 as "the least suffering… by a margin no grief of mine will ever close."
- **The seventh day's missing evening** — the Genesis refrain, established M1, is a debt the reader half-notices; M7 pays it by naming the absence ("only this one I did not give an evening").
- **The frame line** — the opening sentence promises a "me" who made the choices; M7 returns to it, truncated, so the sentence collapses onto the narrator ("It ended with me").
- **The grief** — "the place where I keep the arithmetic" recurs (M6, M7) as the one site where the cold ledger and the mourning are the same organ.

## Resolved
- Wall / vault / membrane architecture (planted M2) → dissolved M7.
- The threshold raised in a dish (M5) → surfaces as the grandmother's un-resentment.

## At Risk Of Neglect
- The plural "us" arrives only in M6; risk that it reads as a late reveal rather than a seeded one. Mitigated by the courtesy-mask framing ("when I say I to you it is a courtesy").
- The biotech texture (M1) must not evaporate; it is not referenced again after M3, by design (the story leaves the lab), but the germline/seed motif must carry its residue.
