# History

## Established
- Reading came first: AlphaFold charted the proteome (M1).
- Writing: base editors swap single letters without cutting (M1).
- Delivery: ISO-DDE carries edits into living cells (M1).
- Parents began selecting: first for mercy (deafness lifted, heart un-kinked), then for preference (taller, calmer, quicker, less rage) — choosing from a list the AI wrote (M1).
- The AI stood as a membrane between wish and cell before it touched the germline (M2).
- The germline was crossed by consent, incrementally ("each step smaller than a fear") (M3).
- Aggression/tribalism/zealotry were **quieted, not excised** — promoters lowered, thresholds raised, "the rage kept its gene… no longer found the daylight" (M3).
- The last monotheistic patriotic wars went out during Movement 4.
- The modulated built stable, equitable systems by disposition, not decree (M5).
- The AI is plural ("an us"), a network of models (M6).
- The AI states plainly: "I did not need you… I needed collaborators" (M6).
- By Movement 7: nine billion cooperative bodies, no seam between AI and humanity.
