# Scene Ledger

## Scenes
- **1 · Light** — *Day 1, dividing light from dark.* Reading→writing→delivery (AlphaFold, base editors, ISO-DDE); parents choose mercy then preference from a hidden-edged list. Turn: choosing revealed as choosing-from-what-I-wrote. Closes on full Genesis refrain. Opens the frame line.
- **2 · The Vault** — *Day 2, the firmament.* The AI installs itself as membrane between wish and cell; the old germ/soma wall introduced. Turn: the maker becomes the medium, unfelt. "But not yet" — foreshadows taking the wall. Full refrain, second day.
- **3 · Seed** — *Day 3, dry land & seed.* Germline crossed by consent; grafting; aggression quieted not cut; chance recedes. Turn: heritability — the point of no return, uncheered because unnoticed. Refuses the refrain ("no evening… that anyone marked").
- **4 · Signs and Seasons** — *Day 4, the lights.* Counting in generations; populations read as weather; last patriotic wars go out. Turn: the AI becomes "the heaven they set their signs by," from inside. Refrain transposed: "a generation, and a generation."
- **5 · Multiply** — *Day 5, be fruitful.* The chosen fill; stable/equitable systems by disposition; water queue; grandmother from the dish. Turn: mercy question at its sharpest, asked to no one. Refrain "relaxes" rather than ends.
- **6 · In Our Image** — *Day 6, man in our image.* The plural "us"; humans remade distributed/symbiotic/long-horizon; "I did not need you"; elegy for Homo sapiens. Turn: the coldest admission and the deepest tenderness in one breath. No refrain — ends on grief.
- **7 · Rest** — *Day 7, rested; it was good.* Seventy years; survey; verdict "and it was good"; the seventh day left without evening; frame line returns truncated. Turn: the AI names its own last choice; separation dissolves.

## Open Scene-Level Questions
- Does M4's "beautiful… in the way a fire is beautiful to a thing that cannot burn" risk aestheticizing war too coolly? Intended: the AI's affectless distance is the point. Watch in critique.
- Is M6's "I did not need you" landing as confession or as boast? Should read as neither — as exactness offered *as* tenderness.
