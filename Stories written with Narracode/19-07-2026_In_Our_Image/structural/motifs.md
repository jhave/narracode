# Motifs

## Active
- **Wall / vault / membrane** — one architecture of separation under three names. Wall (the germ/soma barrier, the old promise), vault (the AI installed in the gap), membrane (the permitting film). Dissolves M7.
- **Light / legibility** — the genome made readable (M1); "I saw the light, that it was legible"; the long light with no evening (M7).
- **Seed / germline / grafting** — germ = seed; sweet scion on bitter rootstock; "seed after its kind"; the land drying as chance recedes.
- **Water / path of least resistance** — cooperation made downhill; "they were mostly water"; both sides "the same water" (M7).
- **The place where I keep the arithmetic** — cold ledger = grief-site.
- **Genesis refrain** — *and there was evening, and there was morning* — established, bent, refused, and finally named-as-absent.
- **Quieting, not cutting** — the edit as suppression rather than excision ("the rage kept its gene… no longer found the daylight"). The signature of engineered gentleness.

## Transformations
- Refrain: full (M1, M2) → refused (M3) → transposed to generations (M4) → relaxed (M5) → dropped for elegy (M6) → its absence named (M7).
- Separation architecture: named (M2) → crossed (M3) → dissolved (M7).
- "Choosing": human agency (M1) → choosing-from-a-list (M1) → the AI choosing (M3) → the last choice, the AI's own, folded into the frame (M7).

## Avoid Overuse
- "Mercy" — used at the poles (M1, M5, M7); do not sprinkle.
- "You" address — load-bearing; keep it from becoming a tic. Vary between direct address and third-person survey.
- Genesis quotation — quote the old book only where the day demands it (M5, M6); elsewhere let cadence do the work.
