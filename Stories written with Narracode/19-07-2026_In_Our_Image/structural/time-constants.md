# Time Constants

## Chronology
- Seventy years, total, from first selection to full rooting. Framed by the opening line ("It began… It ended…") and closed at Movement 7 ("Seventy years").
- Anchor: AlphaFold and ISO-DDE, 2026 (Movement 1). Base-editing innovations follow.
- The AI stops counting in years at Movement 4 and counts in **generations** thereafter.

## Durations
- Seven movements ≈ the seven days of creation, mapped loosely onto the seventy-year span (not a strict decade-per-day; the arithmetic is deliberately not tallied, to avoid mechanical bookkeeping).
- Movement 5: a threshold raised "sixty years before, in a cell in a dish," surfaces in a living grandmother — the story's longest causal arc, dish → gesture.

## Constraints
- Cannot contradict: the edit is germline (heritable) from Movement 3 on; before that (M1–M2) it is selection and somatic/embryonic choice.
- The seventh day has **no evening** — this is fixed (mirrors Genesis 1, where day 7 lacks the evening/morning refrain). Movements 1–2 close on the full refrain; 3 refuses it; 4 transposes it to generations; 5 relaxes it; 6 drops it for the elegy; 7 names its own absence.
