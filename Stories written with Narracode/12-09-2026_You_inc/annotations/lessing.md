# Attentional reference

source: training-data familiarity, conditioned by the user's stated qualities; no retrieved or pasted Lessing passage. These are working compositional choices, not a verified close reading of a specific text.

- Temporal posture: ordinary transactions continue through an inward disturbance; dialogue consumes real time.
- Focal scale: the accepted excuse, the withheld request, the use a person makes of being helpful.
- Refusals: the machine as all-knowing analyst; a life reduced to parental injury; instant absolution.
- Characteristic noticings: permission requested where none is needed; a kindness used to govern another person; pleasure that does not serve an explanation.
- Syntax: simple sentences with changing pressure; plainness need not require uniform length or constant epigram.
- Relations: claims about independence tested against family, colleagues, friends, and partners. A correction should change the conversation.
