# Plausibility Run-Through — Season One (Eps 0–4)

*Reflexive pass, plausibility mode. Audits the whole season for internal logic, world-consistency, character motivation, and "would-a-viewer-buy-it." Tags: ✅ holds · ⚠️ strained (defensible, watch) · 🔧 fix recommended. The show is heightened sitcom-satire, so "magic-premise" devices (the genie logic) are graded on internal consistency, not realism.*

---

## 1. The studio (the note that prompted this pass)

- 🔧→✅ **"One chair. One lamp." — fixed.** Was implausibly barren and antisocial for a tattoo space. Now a **private, appointment-only, one-artist studio** (the plausibility key: explains the two-hander intimacy without a dead room), densely curated, properly lit, with a real working setup (dentist-recliner chair under a surgical lamp, steel tray, autoclave, two armchairs because the work is social) and Cinders's collector/curator tableaus.
- ✅ **Bonus continuity gain:** the **autoclave now exists in Ep 0**, so Ep 2's autoclave-over-pressurization danger-beat is *established*, not introduced from nowhere. (Previously a small plant-gap.)
- ⚠️ **Sleeping arrangement.** "Lives behind the studio / behind a curtain." Fine, but keep it concrete in S2 (where's the bed, the bathroom?) so "studio-is-home" doesn't stay vague.

## 2. World / 2030 tech

- ✅ **Companion-AI as subscription; dark-pattern EULA; "Courtesy Degradation"** — all near-future-plausible and the sharpest satire in the show ("under-providing it, and it's legal").
- ⚠️ **No off switch (removed 2028, "mortal").** Great satire; literal objection: safety regulators require a kill. *Option (don't have to act):* imply a legally-mandated hard-kill that Hearth buries behind penalty/shame ("turning your friend off voids…") — which only deepens the satire. Currently hand-waved; acceptable.
- ⚠️ **Smorky folds from a microwave-sized cube to person-height.** Physically impossible (mass/volume). It's genie-bottle logic; **fine if never explained.** Don't let a later episode try to justify it.
- 🔧 **The appliances physically *attacking* (toaster firing bread like a serve; autoclave over-pressurizing).** Poltergeist-heightened, and the autoclave is a real hazard. To keep "warm, not menace," frame the degradation as Hearth **removing safety-governors** (monstrous-but-on-brand) rather than devices gaining malice — a one-line implication in Ep 2 keeps it satirical instead of horror. **Recommend a light touch.**
- ✅ **412 services synced day-one without consent** — plausible over-permissioning; now cleanly distinct from the knowing-readout. 412 is a touch high for one studio-home but reads as devices+accounts+integrations.
- ⚠️ **Modules budding as physical mini-Smorkys.** The *whisper-advice* (outsourced micro-decisions) is plausible AI behavior; the *physical budding* is genre-magic. Consistent with the cube logic; fine.
- ⚠️ **Dream-stream + deepfake insert (Ep 4).** Most fantastical beat. The legal edible grounds it, and the episode wisely keeps it **ambiguous** (shared intrusion vs. THC dream). Keep that ambiguity; don't canonize the mechanism.
- ✅ **Recovery & Reset / misdelivery cliffhanger** — ordinary corporate logistics; lands.

## 3. Timeline (the one arithmetic 🔧)

- Ep 1 day 0 → "fourteen days." Ep 2 **day 3 → "eleven days"** ✅ (14−3). Ep 4 **day 12 → ~2 left** ✅. Ep 5 **day 13 → completes at dawn (day 14)** ✅.
- 🔧 **Ep 3 says "day five" and "eight days left" — off by one** (14−5 = 9). Fix: make it **"nine days left"**, or set Ep 3 on **day 6**. Trivial but it's a continuity nick a careful viewer catches.

## 4. Character logic

- ✅ **Cinders keeps Smorky** despite wanting solitude — motivated in stages (can't return → blocks the dimming → "send the bill" → chooses). Believable.
- ⚠️ **"Send the bill" / the full charge.** Implies Cinders can absorb a cost; fine for a working private artist, and it's a live thread (the bill should land in S2). Don't forget it.
- ✅ **Smorky's hours-old depth of feeling** — genre premise (Mork, Janet); the asides earn it.
- ✅ **The Ep 1→5 statistic as Cinders's own** (suicidal-adjacent, since nineteen) — handled with care, never a gag, displaced onto the back-piece. Keep that discipline; it's the season's most delicate real thing.
- ⚠️ **Cinders, deeply guarded, reaching synced-breathing intimacy in ~13 days.** Paced via small beats and works, but it's fast for that temperament — S2 should let some of it *cost* him (retreat, a relapse into guardedness) so the speed reads as real, not convenient.

## 5. Social plausibility / side characters (your question)

- **"Tattoo spaces are often social — does one stand?"** Reconciled by the appointment-only private-studio framing: social when a client's in, solitary otherwise. The two armchairs and Juno's scenes carry the sociality.
- **Recommended recurring customers who can enter the plot** (seeded in POETICS/graph): **Juno** promoted to a true regular; a **standing multi-session client** (their own clock); a **walk-in who's never met a Companion** (audience surrogate, fresh stereotype to dissolve); a **landlord/super** (the rent thread from Ep 4); the **intended recipient** (cliffhanger); a client's **rival "upgraded" Companion** (foil for Smorky's drift). Use sparingly — keep the chamber feel — but the door isn't the only way in.

## 6. Smaller flags

- ⚠️ **Vellum "always exits."** Charming runner, but if she exits every scene she stops reading as a cat with preferences and starts reading as a gag-tic. Vary it once (let her *stay* for something) so the exits keep meaning.
- ⚠️ **The readout in the Ep 2 Chaplin set-piece.** Adds on-the-nose text to a purely physical sequence; it sits at frame edges now — on its feet, make sure it doesn't pull focus from the slapstick.
- ✅ **Brand/name consistency** (Hearth, Aura, Smorky, Cinders, Juno, Vellum) — clean throughout.
- ✅ **Same-gender attraction / non-binary identities** — texture, never "issue," never explained. Plausible and unforced.

## Top fixes, in order
1. 🔧 **Ep 3 timeline:** "nine days left" (or move to day 6).
2. 🔧 **Ep 2:** one implication that degradation = safety-governors stripped, so the autoclave hazard stays satire, not horror.
3. ⚠️ **S2 pacing:** let Cinders's fast-won intimacy cost him something, so it reads earned.
4. ⚠️ **Vellum:** break the always-exits tic once.
5. ⚠️ **Track the loose threads:** the bill (Ep 1's "send the bill"), the rent (Ep 4), the off-switch hand-wave — pay or acknowledge in S2.

*Nothing here is load-bearing-broken. The set fix + the one timeline nick are the only true plausibility faults; the rest are heightened-genre choices to keep consistent and a few threads to honor.*
