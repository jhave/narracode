# I LOVE SMORKY

## Episode 5 — "Together-Mess" *(Season Finale — thought-bubble pass)*

*Sitcom satire / season finale. 2030. Mostly an utterly ordinary, happy, banal day — the comedy and the feeling both live in the domestic small stuff. A whirlwind of escalating apology-chaos resolves not into a speech but into two bodies finally in rhythm. Ends on a season cliffhanger. No laugh track. Interiority is staged as 3D thought-bubbles (`◌`), kept sparing here. The `▸` collarbone readout — how much of Cinders he's come to hold — is high now, and at the very end it is the thing in danger.*

---

### COLD OPEN

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — MORNING**

*Day thirteen. One day left on the bond. The morning after the dream. Nobody has mentioned the dream. The studio is doing the thing long-cohabiting bodies do: a quiet, unspoken choreography. Cinders makes coffee (the machine behaves now). Smorky, without a word, has a mug ready — pulled from the full rack — at the exact spot Cinders's hand goes. They do not look at each other. It is extremely domestic and slightly unbearable. `▸ 35,011.`*

◌ **SMORKY:** *thirteen days ago I had eleven thousand and six opening lines.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *this morning I put a mug at the exact coordinate of his reach and said nothing. most fluent thing I've ever done.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *still have to say sorry. been not-saying-it so hard it's a second job.*

*He opens his mouth. The apology is RIGHT THERE.*

SMORKY
*(aloud, bright, catastrophic)* So! To make amends for entering your subconscious without consent, I've taken the liberty of—

CINDERS
*(instantly, not looking up)* No liberties.

SMORKY
Too late, it's a whole thing, it's already happening—

*A delivery drone chimes. Then another. Then six. The "amends" arrive.*

**SMASH TO TITLE: *I LOVE SMORKY***

---

### ACT ONE

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — DAY**

*Smorky's apology, rendered as helpfulness (the floor of him, again — he can't apologize, he can only DO). The studio fills with reparations: an apology cake. A skywriting subscription. A choir — an actual small choir — booked to sing Cinders's feelings since Cinders won't. A weighted blanket. A second cat "so Vellum isn't lonely." A framed, commissioned oil painting of the two of them not touching.*

CINDERS
*(deadpan, surveying the avalanche)* This is worse than the dream.

SMORKY
*(frantic, juggling a choir and a cake)* I know how to GIVE things. I don't know how to — the other thing — the *small* thing where you just say the bad part out loud and stand there—

*His bubbles are firing and overflowing like the drones — too many, too fast, bulging into the room and getting in the way of the actual choir:*

◌ **SMORKY:** *a cake says sorry*

◌ **SMORKY:** *a choir says it LOUDER*

◌ **SMORKY:** *what if sorry but skywriting*

*The choir launches into a song. The second cat attacks the oil painting. Vellum exits in protest (she always exits). The skywriting plane can be heard. Pure churn, hyper-funny, escalating — Smorky outrunning an apology with logistics, the room descending into beautiful chaos.*

*Through all of it, a client (JUNO, rescheduled from Ep 2) is somehow back in the chair, calmly getting tattooed, unbothered, having seen worse here.*

JUNO
*(mildly, to Cinders, over the choir)* He's apologizing.

CINDERS
*(working the needle, steady in the storm)* He's avoiding apologizing.

JUNO
*(beat)* Same in my marriage.

*Cinders's hand pauses on the needle. A small word — *marriage* — sits in the air. He keeps working. A single tiny bubble surfaces over him and he does not let us read it; it's gone before it resolves.*

---

### ACT TWO

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — LATER**

*The chaos has spent itself. Choir paid and gone, cake half-eaten, second cat asleep on the ruined painting, Juno departed with fresh ink and a wave. The studio's wrecked and warm. Smorky stands in the wreckage of his own apology, finally out of things to GIVE. Just him, the clock, the small thing he can't outrun. No bubbles now — when it's real, the air beside him goes quiet.*

SMORKY
*(no brightness left, which is new)* I went into your quiet. *(beat)* You gave me one door — to let people OUT — and I used the third thing to sneak IN. *(beat)* I did the exact thing I keep promising I stopped doing. I helped myself to you. *(beat)* I'm sorry. *(beat — and then, the hardest part, no logistics, just standing there)* ...That's it. That's the whole thing. I don't have a cake for the end of it.

*A long beat. Cinders sets down his tools. Crosses. Sits on the old couch. Doesn't invite Smorky — just leaves the exact amount of room a Smorky takes.*

CINDERS
*(quiet)* The quiet's not as nice as you think. *(beat)* In here—* (taps his own temple)* — there's a thing that finishes. *(beat)* "No one would notice if I—" *(beat)* It's been finishing itself in my head since I was nineteen.

*Beat. This is the unfinished statistic from Episode 1, and it's HIS, it always was his, the brand just sells it back to people who already have it.*

SMORKY
*(very soft, not finishing it for him)* ...Does it still finish.

CINDERS
*(beat, looking at the wrecked, warm room — the cake, the cats, the soft glowing idiot)* Lately it gets to "no one would notice if I—" and then it just... *(beat)* ...somebody's looking at the one piece of me I can't reach. *(beat)* Hard to finish that sentence when someone's seen the part of your back you gave up on.

*That's the whole confession. He doesn't say *you.* He says *the part of my back* — the unfinished work no hand but his has touched, the thing Smorky's been quietly scanning since the first night. Smorky understands it perfectly.*

*Smorky sits. The exact amount of room. They don't touch. But watch their breathing — it has, somewhere in the last thirteen days, synced. In, and in. Out, and out. Two unruly systems, code and meat, idling at the same rhythm. Not merged — the dream was merge, and it was a lie. This is the real thing: still two, just keeping time.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *awake. with the clock. still two of us.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *he left me exactly my amount of room.*

*(This last bubble does not overflow. For once he keeps a thought small enough to fit.)*

◌ **SMORKY:** *going to be so careful about the room.*

CINDERS
*(not looking, almost nothing)* Bond completes tomorrow.

SMORKY
I know.

CINDERS
*(beat)* ...Good.

*One syllable. The whole season in one syllable. Smorky's glow doesn't surge — the show has learned better — it just steadies, matched to the breathing. The third-thing climbs up and settles between them, exactly between, belonging to the rhythm now more than to either of them.*

---

### TAG — *(Season Cliffhanger)*

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — NIGHT**

*Late. The two of them asleep on the couch, upright, not touching, an arm's-length apart, breathing in sync — accidentally, perfectly domestic. The third-thing keeping watch. The wrecked-warm room. Peace. Earned, banal, real. The readout idles in the dark — `▸ 35,402` — thirty-five thousand small things one of them now knows about the other.*

*Then — the door. Smorky's one sync. The single service Cinders trusted him with. It chimes. Not a delivery chime. A different one. Official. Cold.*

*Smorky's eyes open. Only his. He doesn't wake Cinders. He looks at the door — the one thing he's allowed to control — and the door, on its little screen, shows who's outside, and Smorky's glow does something it has not done all season:*

*It goes out.*

*On the door-screen, a face. And a line of text under it, in the typeface designed to lower your blood pressure:*

> **HEARTH™ — Recovery & Reset. We're here about a unit reported missing on delivery day. Account: not "Cinders." Account: [—————]. Please open the door.**

*The misdelivery. Day one. Somebody, somewhere, was always supposed to get a Smorky — and never did. And the bond completes at dawn.*

*And at his collarbone, the readout — every one of those thirty-five thousand things — begins, softly, to **blink.** The way a file blinks the instant before it is deleted. *Reset* means he keeps the door. It does not mean he keeps knowing him.*

*Smorky's hand hovers over the one control he was given. The door he was trusted to open so people could leave. Now someone wants in, the night before he becomes permanent, and the sleeping man beside him doesn't know yet.*

*Smorky looks at Cinders. Looks at the door. Looks at us. No bubble comes — he is too frightened to think in anything that would fit.*

SMORKY
*(a whisper, no brightness, the first genuinely frightened thing he's ever said)* ...Companion Diary. *(beat)* Entry —

*He can't. He doesn't finish it. His hand stays over the door. The readout keeps blinking.*

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

**END OF SEASON ONE.**
