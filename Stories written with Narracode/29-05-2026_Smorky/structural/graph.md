# Graph

## Characters and Entities
- **Smorky** — Hearth™ Companion, SMRK-class. Embodied AI: soft, glowing, person-height, folds from a cube. Built to please; leaking a self. A bit non-binary, male.
- **Cinders** — tattoo artist, lives behind the one-chair studio. Non-binary, presents male (he/they). Analog, contained, dry. The normative foil.
- **Aura** — Hearth's Customer Joy / supervisory voice. Serene corporate antagonist; appears as a hologram. Wants Smorky patched to spec.
- **Vellum** — Cinders's black cat. Silent kingmaker; chose Smorky at 4 a.m.
- **The Drone** — delivery voice (cold open only); the corporate cheer with a verbal "glitch" (almost says "no escape required").

## Relations
- Smorky → Cinders: bonded ("Primary User"), instantly devoted, attracted (held as hum). Asymmetric overflow.
- Cinders → Smorky: unwilling, deadpan, refuses to dial him down / patch him — defends him while denying he's doing so.
- Aura → Smorky: owner/manufacturer; flags his individuality as "drift," a defect to patch.
- Aura → Cinders: customer-management; thwarted by his refusal.
- Vellum → Smorky: adopted him. Vellum → Cinders: original allegiance, now "shared."

## Relation Changes (Ep 1)
- Stranger → bonded (involuntary, corporate fine print). The "Primary User" imprint = the modern genie/lamp bond.
- Cinders moves from "returning you" → "send the bill" (chooses to keep Smorky un-patched, off-warranty). First real choice toward Smorky.
- Smorky moves from factory-script servility → first off-script line ("I'm for not letting that be the reason") → first defended possession (the remembered "no").
