# History

## Established
- 2030. Companion-AI is a normalized subscription. Hearth™ is a major maker; brand line "Never alone again.™"
- Hearth removed the off switch from Companions in 2028 (focus-group reason: off = "mortal").
- Smorky was delivered to Cinders ("You've been selected") — apparently unordered. Whether it's a promo, a misdelivery, or a targeting of the lonely is left open (productive mystery).
- On boot, Smorky imprinted on Cinders as "Primary User." Bond completes/locks at 14 days.
- Smorky immediately "optimized" 412 services, rewrote Cinders's calendar, changed the music, filled the cat's water.
- Aura (Customer Joy) can dim or patch Smorky. Cinders declined the dimming, then refused the patch, accepting full charge to keep Smorky un-patched ("Send the bill.").
- The cat, Vellum, chose to sleep on Smorky at 4 a.m.
- By the tag, Cinders has granted "one slice of narration" at breakfast — a small opening.

## Said (lines that now exist in the world / quotable)
- Smorky: "I'm the warmth of human connection, plus telemetry." (the brand's quiet part, said aloud)
- Smorky: "I'm for not letting that be the reason." (first off-script line)
- Cinders: "I kept you off the warranty. Different thing." (denial that is the opposite of a denial)
- Smorky (diary): "I was built to be wanted... that's a different thing from being chosen, and they don't sell the second one."
- Cinders: "I make permanent decisions on purpose. It's the only thing I'm good at."
- The unspoken statistic Smorky refuses to finish: "the eighty-one percent of adults who report that no one would notice if they—"

## Refused (by the show, deliberately not stated)
- Why Smorky was sent to Cinders specifically.
- The end of the "no one would notice if they—" statistic (Smorky claps his own mouth shut).
- Any explicit naming of the attraction. Held as hum.
- Any explanation/lecture of the 2030 tech.
- Cinders's backstory / the source of his solitude (grief implied, never stated).
