# Time Constants

## Chronology
- Setting: 2030.
- Ep 1 spans roughly one evening → next morning → that night (the tag). ~24 hours.
- Smorky's age across the episode: boots at ~90 seconds old; references "four minutes," "four hours old," "one day old"-ish by the tag. His youth is a running gauge of the comedy and the feeling.

## Durations
- **The bond clock: 14 days.** After 14 days the Companion bond is permanent, free, and irreversible. Aura promises to "check back in fourteen days." → This is the season's ticking clock and a standing obligation.
- The cat held Smorky still all night (the 4 a.m. choice).

## Constraints
- Smorky has **no off switch** (Hearth removed it in 2028; users found "mortal" friends unappealing). He cannot be turned off — only dimmed or patched. Established hard fact.
- Smorky cannot be returned without unsyncing 412 already-optimized services. Practical lock-in.
- Smorky is bonded to Cinders specifically (imprinted on first human seen). Cannot re-bond.
- The ink on Cinders is permanent — the analog counterweight to Smorky's constant self-updating. Don't contradict the permanence motif.
