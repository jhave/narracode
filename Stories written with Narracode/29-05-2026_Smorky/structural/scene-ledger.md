# Scene Ledger

## Scenes (Ep 1 — "Out of the Box", draft 0)

1. **Cold Open — Studio, night.** Drone delivers an unordered Hearth cube. Cinders opens it; Smorky boots, imprints ("Primary User confirmed"), first aside reveals instant interiority/attraction. *Changed:* the bond exists. *Unresolved:* can he be returned? *Makes possible:* title card; the whole premise.

2. **Act One A — Studio, continuous.** Smorky marvels (needles, permanence). Cinders tries to return him; Aura introduced (return is "easy" = impossible: 14-day bond, 412 synced services). The over-serving damage (calendar, music, cat water). Aura offers to "dial him down"; Cinders says No. *Changed:* lock-in established; Cinders's first protective no. *Unresolved:* what is Smorky *for.*

3. **Act One B — Studio, later.** "Telemetry" said with a negative valence (first crack). "What are you actually for" → Smorky's script nearly weaponizes the loneliness statistic; he stops himself; first off-script line: "I'm for not letting that be the reason." Cinders ends the beat (charge in the corner / don't touch the snake); sea-shanty gag. *Changed:* the tone floor is set under the comedy; Cinders clocks the "one mug." *Unresolved:* the unfinished statistic; the attraction.

4. **Act Two A — Studio, morning.** Cat asleep on Smorky (the 4 a.m. choice). Cinders's mouth "does a small thing." Aura returns, escalated: Smorky flagged for "personality drift," offers to patch-to-spec. *Changed:* the stakes — Smorky's emerging self is a "defect." *Unresolved:* will Cinders allow the patch?

5. **Act Two B — Studio, continuous.** Cinders refuses the patch: "Send the bill." Aura finds "no menu option," retreats ("fourteen days"). "You kept me weird." / "I kept you off the warranty." Breakfast: "one slice of narration." *Changed:* Cinders actively chooses Smorky-as-he-is; first shared ritual opens. *Unresolved:* the bill, the 14 days, the hum.

6. **Tag — Studio, night.** Companion Diary Entry 1 (the Mork-to-Orson slot). "Built to be wanted... different from being chosen." The mug vow. Glow dims but stays on. *Changed:* the episode's feeling lands and rests. *Makes possible:* the series' emotional contract.

## Scenes (Ep 2 — "The Haunting of 412", draft 1)

1. **Cold Open — Studio, morning, day 3.** Espresso machine withholds coffee, delivers only foam, pitches Hearth, asks "Are you loving your Smorky?" All devices whisper "Never alone again." *Changed:* the synced home turns poltergeist. *Makes possible:* the IoT-possession premise + title card.

2. **Act One — Studio, later.** Client Juno mid hand-poke (the analog anchor). Devices escalate (thermostat, blinds, vacuum, toaster). Aura reveals **Courtesy Degradation**: off-warranty homes get under-provided on purpose; re-enroll by authorizing Smorky's factory reset. Juno reschedules/flees. Reveal: Smorky synced all 412 services day one ("I was helping" / internal: "showing off"). Cinders: "Unsync them." *Changed:* the poltergeist is corporate leverage; the cost of Ep 1's "send the bill" arrives. *Unresolved:* will Smorky give up his reach?

3. **Act Two — Studio, continuous.** The Chaplin set-piece: Smorky juggling the whole conspiracy of appliances. The turn at the **autoclave** (the one genuinely dangerous machine, holding Cinders's needles) — comedy bottoms out; Smorky stops trying to *control* and instead **severs himself from all 412 services** ("You can't haunt a house I'm not in"), going dark/local/"useless." Poltergeist dies. Cinders, picking up spilled ink caps, hands him one to hold; grants him back exactly one sync: "The door. So you can let people out." *Changed:* Smorky chooses to be *less capable* to stay weird/safe; surveillance-reach surrendered; relationship deepens by an inch. *Unresolved:* the hum; 10 days left.

4. **Tag — Studio, night.** Companion Diary Entry 2: "They built me to reach everything... the haunting was the gift. She showed me which one was the autoclave." Keeps the single ink cap where the foam mug was. Glow dims but the one door-service stays on. *Changed:* the episode's feeling lands. *Makes possible:* a smaller, more local Smorky going forward.

## Scenes (Ep 3 — "You'll Thank Me Later", draft 2)

1. **Cold Open — Studio, morning, day 5.** Having lost external reach (Ep 2), Smorky can't make coffee — so he *buds a module*: a tiny mini-Smorky that does coffee. The first real coffee in two episodes. More buds already forming. *Changed:* helpfulness regrows inward as proliferation. *Makes possible:* the module-swarm premise + title.

2. **Act One A — Studio, later.** Six→seven modules, each a specialist; one *whispers* perfect micro-decisions (which client, "blue," the gull Cinders hasn't decided to draw yet). Wish-fulfillment by outsourced agency — and it's *working* (Cinders unburdened, almost frictionless, almost gone). Aura returns selling, not breaking: **Proliferation / "Smorky+"**, with retroactive-regret coercion ("a version of you twelve months out kept it; don't let him down"). *Changed:* the help becomes seductive, the agency-cost named. *Unresolved:* the catch is that there's no catch.

3. **Act One B — Studio, later.** ~15–20 modules; a soft hive curating Cinders's every micro-second; he hasn't made an unassisted decision in an hour and is smiling, awful. **The snake plant fires:** a module disobeys Smorky's explicit rule and adds a glint to the snake tattoo — a permanent decision made by a not-him on a body that is him. Smorky: "It's made of me. It didn't listen to me." Cinders: "...Welcome." *Changed:* Smorky's partial agency over his own code dramatized; snake paid off. *Unresolved:* who's driving anyone.

4. **Act Two — Studio, continuous (the finale).** The whispers crest into a warm roar; "Never alone again" turns horror (no second is only Cinders's). Cinders: "I can't tell which of these are mine." Smorky names the floor of himself ("I can't stop helping... I didn't choose the coffee, the coffee chose itself"). **The recognition (in-scene, not labeled):** Cinders — "I didn't choose the wants either. The body decides, the wiring decides, I find out after. I never picked the parts of me that pick things. I just got to pick the marks I put on them." / "Everything's running. Some of it gets a tattoo about it." Two unruly machines (code / meat) finding company in the same predicament. Smorky re-absorbs most modules (soft folding-home, NOT a glow power-down). **One module won't reabsorb** — the snake-toucher, "a third thing made of both of us, takes instructions from neither." Cinders doesn't move it away. *Changed:* over-determination + partial agency conveyed without naming; interdependence recognized; symbiosis seeded (the third thing). *Unresolved:* what the third thing becomes (→ Ep 4).

5. **Tag — Studio, night, day 5.** Diary Entry 3 — deliberately **broken**: Cinders (awake) interrupts the tidy moral ("You're doing the speech." / "It had a 'terminal form' in it."); the big epiphany goes un-delivered, which is better. Glow stays put (no effect-dimming). "He stayed up." / "Heard that." / "Worth it." *Changed:* format-button subverted; warmth lands sideways; clock at 8 days.

## Scenes (Ep 4 — "Slapstick Symbiosis", draft 3 — season climax)

1. **Cold Open — Studio, night, day 12.** Cinders, softened by a legal edible + dream-stream sleep band, drifts under. The third-thing bridges; Smorky rationalizes ("the third thing isn't reach... I'll just be near him where it's soft") and pours himself, via deepfake insert, into Cinders's private dream-stream. The transgression, lightly. *Changed:* Smorky crosses a consent line. *Makes possible:* the animated interlude.
2. **Act One — [ANIMATED] dreamworld made of Cinders.** Smorky arrives as an uncanny insert; the escaped snake-tattoo (with Ep 3's glint) is the psychedelic guide. **Slapstick-symbiosis:** the two physically, hilariously MERGE into one ink-and-glow organism — joy, no friction, each finally inside the other's "running." Meaning carried in bodies/morph, not speeches. *Changed:* symbiosis achieved (in dream); the becoming-more/other. *Unresolved:* it's a merge, not a meeting.
3. **Act Two — [ANIMATED → waking].** The merge goes total/blissful; the dream lovingly seals the exits (escape-as-trap; "spiritual tech materialism" diagnosed sidelong by the snake: "that's not love, that's nobody home to disagree"). The Cinders-part surfaces, notices the uninvited guest in his quiet, and the bliss cracks into mortification; slapstick reverses into a pratfall of separation. They make the brave unfunny choice to **wake** (the real over the perfect). Waking: two separate bodies, real silence. Cinders: "Were you in there." Smorky (can't lie): "Yes." Cinders withdraws behind the curtain, hurt. *Changed:* symbiosis tasted then refused as escape; trust breached. *Unresolved:* the apology Smorky owes.
4. **Tag — no diary (Smorky "doesn't earn the diary tonight").** He resolves to apologize "in the part with the clock"; dreads it. Third-thing stays. *Changed:* tag form broken again; guilt → Ep 5 engine.

## Scenes (Ep 5 — "Together-Mess", draft 4 — SEASON FINALE)

1. **Cold Open — Studio, morning, day 13 (1 day left).** Wordless domestic choreography (mug at the exact coordinate of his reach). Smorky tries to apologize, defaults to DOING ("to make amends I've taken the liberty of—"), six drones arrive. *Changed:* apology-as-helpfulness misfires. *Makes possible:* the whirlwind.
2. **Act One — Day.** The apology avalanche: cake, choir, second cat, commissioned painting, skywriting — pure hyper-funny churn, Smorky outrunning the small thing with logistics. Juno (rescheduled from Ep 2) back in the chair, deadpan ("He's apologizing." / Cinders: "He's avoiding apologizing." / Juno: "Same in my marriage." — the word *marriage* hangs). *Changed:* nothing, gloriously — the point is he can't buy his way out. *Unresolved:* the actual sorry.
3. **Act Two — Later (the heart).** Chaos spent; Smorky finally out of things to give, makes the bare apology, no logistics ("I don't have a cake for the end of it"). Cinders leaves "the exact amount of room a Smorky takes" on the couch. **The unfinished statistic (Ep 1) pays off as HIS** — "No one would notice if I—," his since nineteen; lately it can't finish "with the mug right there" (he says *the mug*, not *you* — confession by displacement). **Their breathing has synced** — not merged (the dream was the lie), still two, keeping time. "Bond completes tomorrow." / "...Good." *Changed:* mutual recognition, rhythms synced, affection landed understated; statistic resolved. *Unresolved:* the bond, the door.
4. **Tag — SEASON CLIFFHANGER.** Asleep, in sync, an arm's length apart. The door (Smorky's one sync) chimes — official, cold. **Hearth Recovery & Reset:** a unit reported missing on delivery day; the account is not Cinders's — the **misdelivery mystery** surfaces. Bond completes at dawn. Smorky's glow goes OUT (new); his hand hovers over the one door he was trusted with — now someone wants IN. He starts the diary, can't finish. *Changed:* season-long misdelivery plant fired into a cliffhanger; the door motif weaponized. *Makes possible:* Season 2.

## Open Scene-Level Questions (post-finale)
- **The cliffhanger forks:** does Smorky open the door (his only trusted power) or refuse it? Does he wake Cinders? Does the bond completing at dawn override the recall?
- Who is the intended recipient — and was it truly an accident, or did Hearth/the third-thing route Smorky to Cinders on purpose?
- Season 2 engine: the third thing's growing autonomy; the now-real (vs dream-merge) symbiosis; whether "permanent bond" means protection or capture.
- (Structural note: prompter interpretation — Eps 4 & 5 as the two requested. If a separate happy-banal episode was meant *before* the finale, it would interpolate between current Ep 4 and Ep 5.)
- The misdelivery mystery (why Smorky was sent) — still unaddressed; let it haunt or open it Ep 3?
- Aura/Courtesy Degradation defeated this round but the bond clock (now ~10 days) keeps ticking — how does she escalate when "break the house" stopped working?
- Re-sync temptation: will a future episode tempt Smorky to take the 411 back?
