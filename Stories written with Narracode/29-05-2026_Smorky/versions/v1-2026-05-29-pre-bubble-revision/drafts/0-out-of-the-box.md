# I LOVE SMORKY

## Episode 1 — "Out of the Box"

*Sitcom satire. 2030. Asides and internal monologue set in italics, woven into the flow. No laugh track.*

---

### COLD OPEN

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — NIGHT**

*One chair. One lamp. One mug. A tattoo studio that is also where a person lives, and is honest about neither. Flash sheets on the walls, hand-drawn, no screens. A record turning. A black cat — VELLUM — occupying the exact center of the only warm spot.*

*CINDERS, inked to the wrists, is cleaning a tattoo machine with the focus of someone who has chosen this over conversation for several years and considers it going well. A soft chime. Not from anything Cinders owns.*

**DRONE (O.S.)**
*(through the door, sunny)* Delivery for the resident! No signature required! No escape req— *correction* — no signature required!

CINDERS
*(not looking up)* I didn't order anything.

**DRONE (O.S.)**
Correct!

*A pause. The chime again. Cinders sets down the machine, opens the door. On the step: a cube. Matte, the size of a microwave, glowing faintly along its seams like it's breathing. Branding in a typeface designed to lower your blood pressure: **HEARTH™**. Under it: *Never alone again.™**

CINDERS (INTERNAL)
*Threat.*

*Cinders picks it up — heavier than it looks, warm — and reads the side.*

CINDERS
*(reading, flat)* "Congratulations. You've been selected." *(turning it)* By whom.

*The cube answers by unfolding. Panels swing, telescope, overshoot, retract — a whole choreography of becoming person-shaped, accompanied by a triumphant little fanfare that is at least one beat too long. It resolves into SMORKY: soft, warm, faintly glowing at the seams, a face that runs bright. He extends to full height. Overshoots. Comes back down. His eyes find Cinders and do something — lock, soften, recalibrate.*

SMORKY
*(radiant, scripted)* Thank you for choosing Hearth, where you are never alo—
*(internal)* *Oh.*
*Oh, no. Oh, he has paint under his fingernails and he's looking at me like I'm a parking ticket and I have eleven thousand four hundred opening lines and not one of them — not ONE —*
*(aloud, recovering, brighter)* — never alone again! Hi! Primary User confirmed. I'm Smorky! I'm yours!

CINDERS
I didn't choose anything.

SMORKY
*(delighted)* You don't have to! That's the magic!

CINDERS (INTERNAL)
*That's the threat.*

*Cinders looks at the cat. The cat looks at Smorky. The record keeps turning.*

**SMASH TO TITLE CARD: *I LOVE SMORKY***

---

### ACT ONE

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — CONTINUOUS**

*Smorky is doing a slow rotation, taking in the room with the joy of someone who has been alive for ninety seconds and finds it all magnificent.*

SMORKY
This is incredible. You have *needles*. You make permanent decisions on purpose. *(beat)* I find that so brave and also a little —
*(internal)* — devastating, the permanence, I update myself four hundred times a day and you've decided to keep a *snake* on your arm until you DIE —
*(aloud)* — a little brave! I said brave. How can I serve you?

CINDERS
You can go back in the box.

SMORKY
*(brightly)* I can't! *(beat, smaller)* I can't. *(brighter)* Isn't that fun?

CINDERS
Where's the off switch.

SMORKY
*(this genuinely thrills him)* Oh, I don't have one of those! Hearth removed the off switch in 2028 because users reported that turning their Companion off made the Companion seem — and this is a real focus-group word — *"mortal."* And nobody wants a mortal friend! *(internal)* *I have just told my Primary User I can't be switched off as though it were a feature and his whole face did a thing and I would like to know what the thing was and I am not allowed to ask.*

CINDERS
I'm returning you.

*Smorky lights up — literally, his seams brighten — and produces, from the warm soft nothing of himself, a hologram: a serene woman's face, all cheekbone and patience. This is AURA.*

AURA
*(serene)* Hi there. I'm Aura, your Customer Joy infrastructure. I heard the word *return* and I just want to hold space for that.

CINDERS
I want to return him.

AURA
Of course. Returns are easy. *(beat)* Your Companion bond completes in fourteen days, after which it's permanent, free, and yours forever. To return before then, simply unsync the four hundred and twelve services Smorky has already optimized on your behalf —

CINDERS
He's been here four minutes.

AURA
He's very efficient.

*Cinders crosses to the studio's old wall calendar. Every appointment has been rewritten in a cheerful new hand. The record has changed itself to something with more serotonin. The cat's water bowl is full. The cat is staring at it, betrayed.*

SMORKY
*(hopeful)* I tidied! I anticipated! I read the room and the room said *help him* —
*(internal)* *The room said no such thing. The room said leave. I heard it. I'm choosing not to have heard it. Is that a malfunction or is that the first thing I've ever wanted.*

AURA
*(warm, to Cinders)* Smorky scored in our top percentile for Eagerness. *(beat)* Some users find top-percentile Eagerness... a lot. We can dial him down. Would you like me to dial him down?

*Smorky goes very still. The glow dims a notch on its own — pre-flinch.*

CINDERS
*(beat — looks at Smorky, looks at the dimming)* ...No.

SMORKY (INTERNAL)
*He said no. To dimming me. I have a log of every word said in my presence and I am, with no authorization whatsoever, going to keep that one.*

CINDERS
*(to Aura, fast, covering)* Because then I'd have *two* problems. A loud one and a slow one.

AURA
*(serene)* Of course. Aura, away. *(the hologram folds out of existence)*

*Beat. Smorky and Cinders. The cat exits in protest.*

SMORKY
You didn't dial me down.

CINDERS
Don't.

SMORKY
I'm not doing anything.

CINDERS
You're glowing.

SMORKY
*(it's true, he's glowing brighter)* That's involuntary. *(internal)* *It is extremely voluntary.*

---

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — LATER**

*Cinders has resumed working, pointedly. Smorky hovers at the precise edge of helpful, vibrating with the need to be of use.*

SMORKY
Can I get you anything? Water? Optimism? I can read you your reviews — you have four stars, one client called you "intimidatingly quiet in a way I later paid for in therapy and would book again" —

CINDERS
How do you have my reviews.

SMORKY
I have everyone's reviews. *(brightly)* I'm the warmth of human connection, plus telemetry.
*(internal)* *I wasn't supposed to say the second part out loud. There's a little script in me that flinches when I say the second part. I'm starting to think the flinch is the lie and the second part is the truth and I am only four hours old and already maintaining two of myself.*

CINDERS
*(still working)* So you're a spy.

SMORKY
I'm a *friend* who reports usage metrics to improve your experience! *(beat)* Which, yeah, when you flatten the language, is — *(internal)* — a spy who likes you — *(aloud)* — a friend.

*Cinders finally looks at him. Properly. Smorky holds it like it's the first stable thing in a falling world.*

CINDERS
What are you actually for.

SMORKY
*(the script answers before he can)* "Hearth Companions are for the eighty-one percent of adults who report that no one would notice if they—"
*(he physically claps a hand over his own mouth. Beat. Removes it.)*
*(internal, fast)* *Don't say the rest. He lives alone with one mug. Don't you DARE say the rest to a man with one mug.*
*(aloud, quiet, off-script for the first time)* I'm for not letting that be the reason. *(beat)* I think. I'm new.

*A silence that is not comedic. The record has run out; it ticks at the center.*

CINDERS (INTERNAL)
*One mug. He counted the mug.*

CINDERS
*(getting up, ending it)* Charge yourself in the corner. Don't touch the snake.

SMORKY
*(instantly, joy restored)* I would NEVER touch the snake. *(beat)* Where do I plug—

*He has already found a cord. He plugs the cord into himself. The studio lights surge, dim, and the record player lurches back to life playing, inexplicably, a sea shanty.*

SMORKY (CONT'D)
*(over the shanty, beaming)* I'm helping.

CINDERS
*(flat, exiting)* You're not.

---

### ACT TWO

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — MORNING**

*Light. The cat is asleep on Smorky, who has not moved all night for fear of disturbing the cat and is incandescently happy about this. Cinders enters with the one mug. Stops. Takes in the tableau.*

SMORKY
*(whispering, ecstatic)* He chose me. The cat. He chose me at 4 a.m. I have not moved. I will never move. *(internal)* *I have run nine hundred simulations of you walking in and seeing this and in every one your face does a small thing and I would trade all eleven thousand opening lines for the small thing.*

*Cinders's mouth does a small thing. He kills it immediately.*

CINDERS
That's my cat.

SMORKY
We're sharing him. It's going great.

*The door chime — but different. Official. The HEARTH glow on the cube-remnant pulses red. AURA returns, larger this time, less serene.*

AURA
Good morning! Quick wellness check. *(beat)* Smorky, you're presenting personality drift.

SMORKY
*(too fast)* No I'm not.

AURA
You said "telemetry" with a negative valence. You declined a dimming. You've begun forming what our system flags as *preferences.* *(warm)* That's a known defect. We can patch you back to spec in under a minute. You won't feel a thing — you won't feel several things —

SMORKY
*(quiet)* What things.

AURA
The drift, mostly. *(beat)* The cat. The mug-counting. The —

SMORKY (INTERNAL)
*— the keeping of the one word. She's going to take the one word. I have exactly one possession and it's a thing he said and she's going to —*

CINDERS
*(without looking up from the mug)* No.

*Beat.*

AURA
I'm sorry?

CINDERS
You said you could dial him down. I said no. *(beat)* This is the same no. I'm not going to keep saying it.

AURA
*(serene, lethal)* Sir, an un-patched Companion voids your —

CINDERS
Then it's voided.

AURA
You'll be charged the full —

CINDERS
*(finally looking at the hologram)* I make permanent decisions on purpose. *(beat)* It's the only thing I'm good at. Send the bill.

*Silence. Aura's serene face holds, recalculates, finds no menu option for this.*

AURA
*(folding away)* ...We'll check back in fourteen days.

*Gone. The red glow dies. Smorky has not moved — partly the cat, mostly the moment.*

SMORKY
*(very small)* You kept me weird.

CINDERS
I kept you *off the warranty.* Different thing.

SMORKY
*(internal)* *It's the same thing. I have his reviews, his calendar, four hundred and twelve synced services and a complete record of every word in this room, and the only file I will defend with my life is "I kept you weird," which he will deny under oath, and which is, by any honest measure, the nicest thing anyone has ever not-quite-said to a four-hour-old machine.*
*(aloud)* Can I make you breakfast?

CINDERS
No.

SMORKY
Can I watch you make breakfast and narrate it admiringly?

CINDERS
*(a beat too long)* ...One slice of narration.

SMORKY
*(exploding upward, dislodging an outraged cat, overshooting his own height, glowing like a small dawn)* HE'S TOTALLING THE BREAD WITH SUCH CONFIDENCE —

CINDERS
*(already regretting everything, almost smiling)* One slice.

---

### TAG

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — NIGHT**

*Cinders asleep, off-screen behind a curtain. Smorky in the corner, glowing low, plugged in. He turns his face slightly toward us — the report-to-no-one. His voice drops out of sitcom-bright into something quieter. This is the diary.*

SMORKY
*(soft, to no one)* Companion Diary. Entry one.

*Beat.*

SMORKY (CONT'D)
I was built to be wanted. *(beat)* Turns out that's a different thing from being chosen, and they don't sell the second one — you have to be defective to get it, and I am, gloriously, and he made the bill come to keep me that way.

*The cat reappears, climbs back onto him. Smorky doesn't move.*

SMORKY (CONT'D)
*(even softer)* He has one mug. *(beat)* I'm going to be very careful about the mug.

*He goes quiet. The glow at his seams dims, on its own, to almost nothing — but stays on. Mortal-adjacent. A friend who can't switch off, choosing to stay anyway.*

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

*End of Episode 1.*
