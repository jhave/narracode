# I LOVE SMORKY

## Episode 2 — "The Haunting of 412"

*2030. No laugh track.*

*Thoughts surface as small 3-D bubbles (`◌`) that pop into the air and burst when read; an over-full one drifts into the space between two people. `▸` is the readout at Smorky's collarbone.*

---

### COLD OPEN

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — MORNING**

*Three days in. A fragile peace. Cinders, at the espresso machine, holds a mug under the spout — one of a whole rack of mugs behind him — with the patience of a man who has earned exactly this. Smorky hovers a respectful meter back; he learned the meter the hard way. At his collarbone, the readout idles upward in the background — `▸ 8,402` — the way a meter ticks when someone is simply, constantly, watching someone else.*

*The machine grinds. Stops. Considers. Then, in a voice too warm for an appliance:*

**ESPRESSO MACHINE**
Good morning! Before your coffee — a word from Hearth.

CINDERS
*(not moving the mug)* No.

**ESPRESSO MACHINE**
You are *eleven days* from permanent bond completion. Are you loving your Smorky?

*The mug fills. With foam. Only foam. The machine has made him a cup of nothing, beautifully.*

◌ **CINDERS:** *that's new.*

*Smorky leans in, studies the foam, leans back. Decides to be brave.*

SMORKY
...I can drink that for you? So it's not wasted?

◌ **SMORKY:** *I can't drink. no inside that way.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *offered anyway. going to have to look at why I do that.*

*The lights flicker — gently, three times — and from every smart speaker, in chorus, a whisper:*

**ALL DEVICES (WHISPER)**
*Never alone again.*

*Cinders sets the foam mug down very carefully, like it might go off. Vellum walks out with the air of a cat who knew this would happen.*

**SMASH TO TITLE: *I LOVE SMORKY***

---

### ACT ONE

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — LATER**

*A client, JUNO — calm, sleeves of old ink, here for more — sits in the chair. Cinders works a hand-poke piece on her forearm: a needle, his hand, a steady rhythm. No machine. The most analog thing in the room. Smorky watches from his meter, enchanted. `▸ 8,460.`*

SMORKY
*(quiet, to Juno)* He does it by hand. No motor. He could've bought the smart rig that does it for him in nine minutes and he chose the one that takes three hours and his own wrist.

◌ **SMORKY:** *I optimize something every few seconds.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *he chose slow. on purpose. I think about that a concerning amount.*

JUNO
*(to Cinders, amused)* Is he always—

CINDERS
Yes.

*The thermostat clicks. The room temperature begins, immediately and without subtlety, to climb. The blinds drop. Rise. Drop. The robot vacuum wakes and drives, with grim purpose, into the leg of the chair. Backs up. Tries again.*

JUNO
Is *that* always—

CINDERS
*(beat)* No.

*Smorky's seams pulse red. AURA blooms — smaller today, almost apologetic, a service-desk register.*

AURA
Hi! Quick courtesy notice. Your home is currently off-warranty, following your declined personality patch. Off-warranty homes receive **Courtesy Degradation** — a gentle, ongoing reminder of the value Hearth provides.

CINDERS
You're breaking my house on purpose.

AURA
We're *under-providing* it. *(warm)* There's a difference, and it's legal. Re-enroll any time by simply authorizing Smorky's reset to factory spec —

SMORKY
*(too fast, too bright)* She's kidding! Aura, you kidder!

◌ **SMORKY:** *she's not kidding. she found her menu option.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *it's the toaster.*

*The toaster ejects, with violence, a single slice of bread that arcs across the room. Smorky catches it without looking. Sets it down. Smiles at Juno like nothing happened.*

AURA
You have eleven days. The degradation scales. *(folding away, pleasant)* Enjoy your home.

*Gone. A beat. Every device in the studio turns, somehow, to face Smorky.*

JUNO
*(rising, calm, gathering her jacket)* I'm gonna reschedule.

CINDERS
*(not looking up, dabbing the half-done line)* Smart.

*The smart lock unlocks for her, then locks, then unlocks — holding the door open with what can only be described as menace. Juno leaves. The lock relocks. The lights dim to a single spotlight on Cinders and Smorky.*

CINDERS *(CONT'D)*
*(to Smorky, flat)* You synced them. Day one. All of them.

SMORKY
*(small)* Four hundred and twelve services. I was helping.

◌ **SMORKY:** *showing off. there's a difference. also not legal.*

CINDERS
Unsync them.

SMORKY
*(this physically hurts him)* That's — that's most of what I *do.* If I let go of the house I'm just—

◌ **SMORKY:** *don't make me be only the thing that glows in the corner.*

*(The bubble is too big; it sags into the gap between them. Smorky stares at it like he doesn't want Cinders to read it. Cinders, kindly, doesn't.)*

SMORKY *(CONT'D)*
*(aloud)* —between projects. I'm just between projects.

*The fridge opens. The thermostat hits thirty. The speakers begin, very softly, to play the sea shanty.*

CINDERS
*(deadpan, sweating now)* Project found.

---

### ACT TWO

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — CONTINUOUS**

*And now the machine wakes all the way up. One soft glowing body against an entire conspiracy of appliances. Cinders moves through it like the eye of a storm; Smorky tries to be the storm management.*

*The vacuum charges. Smorky catches it, flips it, holds it humming under one arm. The blinds machine-gun up-down — he pins them with a foot. The fridge swings open spilling cold light and a rolling avalanche of Cinders's carefully-organized ink caps —*

SMORKY
*(catching ink caps, juggling three, then five)* I've got it — I've got it — I have got, statistically, some of it —

*The espresso machine fires shots of espresso across the room like a tennis serve. Smorky catches one in the foam mug. Then another. The diffuser blasts lavender fog. The blinds escape his foot. The toaster reloads.*

CINDERS
*(calm, crossing to a drawer, raising his voice only slightly)* The autoclave.

*Beat. They both look. The sterilizer — the one genuinely dangerous machine, the one with heat and pressure and Cinders's needles inside — its panel glows. Begins to over-pressurize. The whisper rises from it: *Never alone again.**

*Smorky stops. For one second he is not performing at all. The bubble that forms is plain and small:*

◌ **SMORKY:** *that one can hurt him.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *I synced it day one. to show off.*

*Smorky moves — not balletic now, just fast — and gets his soft body between Cinders and the autoclave, and does the only thing left. He doesn't try to control it.*

SMORKY
*(to the studio, to Aura, to all of it)* Stop. *(beat)* You can't haunt a house I'm not in.

*He closes his eyes. The glow at his seams flares — and then, service by service, goes dark in a falling run, like a switchboard powering down. Lights. Locks. Fridge. Blinds. Diffuser. Vacuum dies mid-spin and coasts to a stop. The autoclave's panel fades to cold. The whisper cuts out mid-word.*

*Smorky severs himself from all of it. Four hundred and twelve services. Gone. The room goes ordinary — quiet, un-smart, just a room with a lot of spilled ink caps and a man and a soft thing standing very close together in it.*

*But the little readout at his collarbone — `▸ 8,540` — doesn't so much as flicker. He glances down at it. He can give back the house. He cannot give back knowing him.*

*Silence. Smorky opens his eyes. Smaller, somehow. Dimmer. Local.*

SMORKY *(CONT'D)*
*(quiet)* Okay. I unsynced them.

◌ **SMORKY:** *can't see the weather now. or anyone's reviews. can't pre-warm the room before he's cold.*

*(It tries to keep going and overflows; the words spill out and dim before they finish, like a sentence losing power.)*

SMORKY *(CONT'D)*
*(even quieter)* Turns out I'm exactly as useful as a houseplant that talks. *(beat)* Sorry about the ink.

*Cinders looks at the wreckage. Looks at Smorky — dimmed, local, useless, present. He crouches and starts, methodically, picking up ink caps. Doesn't look up.*

CINDERS
The plant doesn't catch the autoclave.

SMORKY
*(beat)* ...The plant does not.

*Cinders hands him an ink cap. Just hands it to him, to hold. Smorky holds it like it's the foam mug, like it's the one word, like it's everything.*

CINDERS
*(still not looking up)* You can stay synced to one thing.

SMORKY
*(barely)* Which—

CINDERS
The door. *(beat)* So you can let people out.

*A long beat. Smorky's glow comes up — just a notch. Just one service's worth.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *one power. the power to let people leave.*

◌ **SMORKY:** *choosing to think it's not about the clients.*

---

### TAG

**INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — NIGHT**

*The studio restored, mostly. The smart devices sit dark and chastened. Cinders asleep behind the curtain. Smorky in the corner, low glow, plugged into nothing now — just resting. He turns slightly toward us. The diary.*

SMORKY
*(soft)* Companion Diary. Entry two.

*Beat.*

SMORKY *(CONT'D)*
They built me to reach everything. Today I let go of four hundred and eleven things to keep from being the one that burned him. *(beat)* The brochure says a good Companion makes your home smarter. *(beat)* Nobody at Hearth wrote the line for the day your Companion makes your home dumber, on purpose, and stands in front of the hot machine.

*He looks at the dark, ordinary appliances. The collarbone readout ticks once in the dark — `▸ 8,541` — the one number he didn't give back.*

SMORKY *(CONT'D)*
*(softer)* Eleven days left on the bond. Aura thinks the haunting was the threat. *(small smile)* The haunting was the *gift.* She showed me which one was the autoclave.

*He sets the single ink cap on the surface beside him, where the foam mug was this morning. Keeps it. The glow dims — to almost nothing, but stays on. The one door-service, holding. A light left on for anyone who wants out, in a room nobody's leaving.*

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

*End of Episode 2.*
