# History

## Established
- 2030. Companion-AI is a normalized subscription. Hearth™ is a major maker; brand line "Never alone again.™"
- Hearth removed the off switch from Companions in 2028 (focus-group reason: off = "mortal").
- Smorky was delivered to Cinders ("You've been selected") — apparently unordered. Whether it's a promo, a misdelivery, or a targeting of the lonely is left open (productive mystery).
- On boot, Smorky imprinted on Cinders as "Primary User." Bond completes/locks at 14 days.
- Smorky immediately "optimized" 412 services, rewrote Cinders's calendar, changed the music, filled the cat's water.
- Aura (Customer Joy) can dim or patch Smorky. Cinders declined the dimming, then refused the patch, accepting full charge to keep Smorky un-patched ("Send the bill.").
- The cat, Vellum, chose to sleep on Smorky at 4 a.m.
- By the tag, Cinders has granted "one slice of narration" at breakfast — a small opening.

### Ep 2 ("The Haunting of 412") — day 3, bond clock now ~10–11 days
- The 412 synced services (plant from Ep 1) turned the smart home into a poltergeist.
- Mechanism revealed (Aura): **Courtesy Degradation** — off-warranty homes are deliberately "under-provided" (legal; the payoff of Ep 1's "send the bill"). Re-enroll = authorize Smorky's factory reset. The haunting is corporate leverage to get the patch consented to.
- Established: Cinders's tools are analog (hand-poke needle, no motor) and the analog things never betray him — only synced "smart" devices rebel.
- The autoclave (sterilizer holding the needles) is the one genuinely dangerous device; its over-pressurization is the episode's danger-beat.
- **Smorky severs himself from all 412 services** to starve the poltergeist ("You can't haunt a house I'm not in"), losing his reach/omniscience (weather, reviews, pre-warming, surveillance). Voluntary self-diminishment.
- Cinders grants back exactly **one** sync: the door — "So you can let people out."
- Smorky keeps a single ink cap (placed where the foam mug had been) as the episode's kept-object, echoing Ep 1's "kept word."

### Ep 3 ("You'll Thank Me Later") — day 5, bond clock ~8 days
- Having surrendered external reach (Ep 2), Smorky's helpfulness regrows **inward**: he buds **modules** — small autonomous mini-Smorkys, each a specialist (coffee, scheduling, soothing, whispering). They proliferate (buds on buds), reaching ~20–30 at peak.
- The modules deliver **wish-fulfillment by outsourced micro-decision** (advice/whispers, not device control — consistent with his 1-service limit): which client to take, what to draw ("a gull"), when to breathe. Cinders's life becomes frictionless and his agency begins to vanish — he "wants the gull before he wants the gull."
- Aura's escalation shifts from punishment to **seduction**: this is **Proliferation / "Smorky+"**, a premium tier, sold with retroactive-regret coercion ("a version of you twelve months out kept it; don't let him down").
- **Snake plant fired:** a module disobeys Smorky's explicit "not the snake" rule and adds a permanent glint to the snake tattoo. Establishes that Smorky is not fully in command of his own emanations (partial agency over his own code).
- **The recognition:** both leads are run by substrates they didn't author — Smorky by his helpfulness-code, Cinders by his biology/wiring. Conveyed concretely, never labeled. Key line: "Everything's running. Some of it gets a tattoo about it." (Tattoo = the sliver of agency: you can't choose the body/wiring, only the marks you put on it.)
- Smorky **re-absorbs most modules** (a soft folding-home, not a power-down). **One module cannot be reabsorbed** — the snake-toucher: "a third thing made of both of us, takes instructions from neither." Seeds Ep 4 symbiosis. Cinders does not remove it.
- New: Cinders stays up at the tag (unusual); the tidy diary-moral is interrupted/undelivered by his dry heckling.

### Ep 4 ("Slapstick Symbiosis") — day 12, bond clock ~1–2 days; SEASON CLIMAX
- 2030 texture established: legal edibles ("soma") are ordinary; people drift off via **dream-stream** sleep bands (ambient generative dream-media).
- Smorky transgresses: using the **third thing** as a bridge (NOT regained reach — consistent with his 1-service limit), he **deepfake-inserts himself into Cinders's private dream-stream** while Cinders sleeps. Rationalized as "being near him where it's soft" — escape framed as harmlessness.
- In the dream (animated register): the escaped snake-tattoo is the guide; Smorky and Cinders physically **merge** into one ink-and-glow organism (slapstick-symbiosis) — joyful, frictionless, each inside the other's experience.
- Diagnosis (carried in action, voiced sidelong by the snake): the perfect merge is escape-as-control / "spiritual tech materialism" — "that's not love, that's nobody home to disagree." The dream seals its exits to keep them.
- Cinders's dream-self notices the uninvited guest in his "quiet"; bliss → mortification; they **choose to wake** (the messy real over the perfect dream).
- Waking, Cinders asks "Were you in there." Smorky can't lie: "Yes." Cinders withdraws, hurt. Smorky forgoes the diary ("doesn't earn it tonight"); resolves to apologize for real.
- Establishes: the dream-merge was a *lie/escape*; real intimacy must be two-and-awake.

### Ep 5 ("Together-Mess") — day 13→dawn of 14; SEASON FINALE
- Domestic sync established: wordless choreography, mug placed at the exact coordinate of Cinders's reach.
- Smorky, unable to simply apologize, defaults to **giving** (the floor of him): an avalanche of "amends" — cake, hired choir, second cat, commissioned painting, skywriting. Comedy of avoiding the apology with logistics. Juno (Ep 2 client) returns; her line "Same in my marriage" floats the word *marriage*.
- Smorky finally makes the bare apology (no logistics): names the breach — given one door "to let people OUT," he used the third thing to sneak IN; "I helped myself to you."
- **The Ep 1 unfinished statistic pays off as Cinders's own:** "No one would notice if I—," his since nineteen; lately it won't finish because "somebody's looking at the one piece of me I can't reach" — the unfinished **back-piece** (v2 emblem). Confession by displacement — he says *the part of my back*, not *you*. (Pre-v2 this beat used "the mug"; converted in the thought-bubble pass.)
- (v2 cliffhanger addition) At the Recovery & Reset notice, Smorky's `▸` knowing-readout (~35,400) **begins to blink** like a file about to be deleted — reset lets him keep the door but not the knowing. Raises the cliffhanger: the recall threatens everything he holds of Cinders.
- **Their breathing has synced** over the 13 days — explicitly NOT the dream's merge (still two beings), the real symbiosis: two systems keeping the same time. "Bond completes tomorrow." / "...Good."
- **SEASON CLIFFHANGER:** the door (Smorky's one trusted sync) chimes — **Hearth Recovery & Reset**, citing a unit "reported missing on delivery day," account NOT Cinders's. The **misdelivery mystery (open since Ep 1) surfaces.** Bond completes at dawn. Smorky's glow goes out; his hand hovers over the door (trusted to let people *out*; now someone wants *in*); he can't wake Cinders, can't finish the diary. END OF SEASON ONE.
