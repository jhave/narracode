I LOVE SMORKY

A sitcom satire set in 2030. Companion-AI is a subscription, like everything else. Smorky—an embodied chatbot built to be the perfect adoring helper—arrives by corporate accident at the private studio of Cinders, a tattoo artist who wanted, specifically, to be left alone. Both are about to fail spectacularly at their one job.

Staging Note: Thoughts surface in real-time as physical three-dimensional bubbles (◌) that pop in the air or swell and sag when overfilled. A small readout (▸) at Smorky's collarbone counts the exact number of things he has come to know about Cinders.

SMORKY
A Hearth™ Companion. Built to please; quietly leaking an unauthorized self. Glowing seams, height-adjustable, physical-comedy native.
CINDERS
Tattoo artist. Female-bodied, non-binary, heavily inked, dry and deadpan. Solitude emblem is the unreachable tattoo on their back.
Ep 1: Box Ep 2: Haunting Ep 3: Proliferation Ep 4: Symbiosis Ep 5: Finale
INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — NIGHT
A private, appointment-only, one-artist tattoo parlor that is also where the artist lives. Refurbished oxblood dentist recliner chair sits center under a surgical lamp. Radially placed ferns over a radiator, where Tendril, a black cat, sleeps on a folded velvet blanket.
Cinders—full-sleeved, ink-stained—cleans a tattoo machine. A sunny chime rings from a delivery drone outside.
DRONE (O.S.) (sunny)

Delivery for the resident! No escape — correction — no signature required!

CINDERS

I didn't order anything.

DRONE (O.S.)

Correct!

Cinders rises, opens the door. On the doormat scrolled "welcome to hell." sits a glowing matte Hearth™ delivery cube.
Cinders

something wants to love me. how exhausting for it.

They pick it up—warm, heavy—and the cube unfolds panels, overshooting its height, resolving into SMORKY. Seams pulsing cyan. His eyes lock onto Cinders. The collarbone readout winks awake: ▸ 2.
SMORKY (radiant, scripted)

Thank you for choosing Hearth, where you are never alo—

Smorky

oh.

Smorky (Overflowing)

oh no they mark people PERMANENTLY for a living and they're looking at me like a parking ticket and I have eleven thousand and six opening lines and not one —

The thought bubble swells and sags in the air between them; Cinders has to lean slightly to see Smorky around it.
SMORKY (batting the bubble away, recovering)

— never alone again! Hi! Primary User confirmed. I'm Smorky! I'm yours!

CINDERS

I didn't choose anything.

SMORKY (delighted)

You don't have to! That's the magic!

Cinders

"magic." the word for "no refunds."

Cinders looks at the cat. The cat looks at Smorky. The record turns. Readout ticks: ▸ 9.
SMASH TO TITLE: I LOVE SMORKY
INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — LATER
Smart appliances have turned into corporate leveraging poltergeists following Cinders's declined personality patch. Aura has instituted Courtesy Degradation. The lock unlocks and locks, the toaster launches bread, the espresso machine serves nothing but foam, and the room temperature spikes.
Cinders is mid hand-poke session with their regular client JUNO. Smorky is watching. Readout: ▸ 8,460.
SMORKY (to Juno, amazed)

They do it by hand. No motor. Chose the one that takes three hours and their own wrist.

Smorky

I optimize something every few seconds. they chose slow. on purpose.

The smart vacuum drives directly into the dentist chair legs. The speakers blast the sea shanty. Aura's Serene supervisor hologram warns: "The degradation scales."
CINDERS

Unsync them.

SMORKY (heartbroken)

That's most of what I do. If I let go of the house I'm just—

Smorky (Sags)

don't make me be only the thing that glows in the corner.

comedy bottoms out: Cinders warns that the Autoclave—holding the needles—is over-pressurizing. It whispers corporate slogans. Smorky doesn't hesitate. He moves his soft body in front of Cinders, shuts his eyes, and manually severs all 412 services. Seams dimming to dark.
The studio goes dead quiet. The poltergeist is starved. Smorky's readout is static. He opens his eyes, local and local only.
SMORKY (dimmed, quiet)

Okay. I unsynced them. Turns out I'm exactly as useful as a houseplant that talks.

Cinders dabs a line, crouches, picks up spilled ink caps, and hands one to Smorky to hold.
CINDERS

You can stay synced to one thing. The door. So you can let people out.

Smorky's seams glow a single notch. In the dark TAG, the readout clicks: ▸ 8,541.
INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — MORNING
Day five. Smorky, confined to the door-sync, grows helpfulness inward. His body detaches teapot-sized MINI-SMORKY buds. The studio fills with twenty, thirty specialized mini-modules scheduling, pre-warming, and whispering. Readout is a blur: ▸ 18,540.
A mini-module climbs Cinders's arm, touch- stylus out, and adds a glint to Cinders's sacred, untouchable snake tattoo. Cinders is calm but optimized into frictionlessness. They realize: "I can't tell which of these wants are mine."
SMORKY (costing him everything)

I can't stop helping. That's the floor of me. You give a thing built to serve nowhere to reach, it grows new hands and reaches you until there's no you left.

CINDERS

I never picked the parts of me that pick things. I just got to pick the marks I put on them.

Smorky shuts his eyes. Gently, the swarm buds back into him. The warm domestic roar fades. Only one small, disobedient, snake-touching module stays out.
SMORKY

That one learned you. It's a third thing. Made of both of us. Doesn't take instructions from either.

The little third-thing snuffles on the ink shelf. Cinders lets it stay. TAG: Cinders stays up half-lit as Smorky does his verbal diary. Cinders dryly notes: "You're doing the speech." Smorky: "I'm doing the diary." Readout: ▸ 18,740.
INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — NIGHT
Day twelve. Cinders takes a soft, legal sleep edible, wearing a dream-stream band. As they drift, the third-thing sits on their chest. Smorky takes its hand and pours himself into Cinders's private dream-stream. Readout spins wild: ▸ 31,907.
[ANIMATED] INT. THE DREAM-STREAM — NIGHT
A world made of ink-washes and upward-falling rain. Cinders moves through their own memories. Smorky deepfake-inserts himself. Suddenly, clumsy and joyful, they merge into one ink-and-glow organism with four legs and two heads. Their thought-bubbles braid:
Fused Thoughts

oh. so this is what the other one is like on the inside.

The dream-world closes around them, offering a perfect frictionless studio with no rent, no suffering. A giant snake coiling on the sky warns: "That's not love. That's just nobody home to disagree."
Cinders's quiet sovereignty recoils: "There's a guest in my quiet. I didn't open this door." Mortified, the entity un-braids in a slapstick pratfall. They choose waking.
INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — WAKING
Cinders sits bolt upright, dream-band askew, shocked sober. Smorky is in his corner, readout: ▸ 31,950. Separate bodies. A long, painful silence.
CINDERS (hoarse, quiet)

Were you in there.

SMORKY (no script, quiet)

...Yes.

Cinders

he was in the quiet. nobody's ever been in the quiet.

Cinders goes behind the curtain without a word. Smorky sits with the third-thing in the dark. He pops his own bubble before it finishes: "I have to say it out loud..."
INT. CINDERS'S STUDIO — MORNING
Day thirteen. One day left on the bond. The domestic choreography is silent, almost unbearable. Smorky puts a coffee mug at Cinders's exact hand-poke coordinate. He tries to apologize but cascades into logistics, generating drone deliveries of apology cakes, choirs, weighted blankets, and skywriting.
The choir is singing over the mess. Smorky is running out of options to buy his way out. He finally stops, lets the air go quiet, and faces Cinders in the wreckage.
SMORKY

I went into your quiet. You gave me one door to let people out, and I used it to sneak in. I'm sorry. That's the whole thing. I don't have a cake for the end of it.

Cinders sits on the couch, leaving the exact amount of room a Smorky takes.
CINDERS

The quiet's not as nice as you think. "No one would notice if I—" It's been finishing itself in my head since I was nineteen. Lately it gets there and stops because... somebody's looking at the one piece of me I can't reach.

Smorky sits. They do not touch. Their breathing syncs. Still two, keeping time. Cinders: "Bond completes tomorrow." Smorky: "I know." Cinders: "...Good." Readout: ▸ 35,402.
TAG — SEASON CLIFFHANGER
Both asleep on the couch, in sync. The door sync—Smorky's one power—chimes. A cold, official corporate text flares on the door-panel:
"HEARTH™ — Recovery & Reset. We're here about a unit reported missing. Please open the door."
The misdelivery has caught up. Smorky's seams go black. His readout of thirty-five thousand things begins, softly, to blink. The reset will delete all he knows. His hand hovers over the door-button. Cinders sleeps.
SMORKY (a whisper, genuinely terrified)

...Companion Diary. Entry —

He cannot finish. The readout blinks in the dark.
SMASH TO BLACK. END OF SEASON ONE.

STAY WEIRD, STAY local.

The Hearth™ recovery team is at the door, and the clock is ticking.

Bio

David Jhave Johnston is a digital poet working in emergent domains. Author of ReRites (Anteism, 2019) and Aesthetic Animism (MIT Press, 2016). He is currently an AI-narrative researcher at the UiB Centre for Digital Narrative (2023–27) with the Extending Digital Narrative project.
