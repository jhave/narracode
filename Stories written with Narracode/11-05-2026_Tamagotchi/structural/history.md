## Established in prose

### About Trygve
- 76 years old, lives alone in Bergen, on Skuteviken (with a window, an armchair, a routine, his rain).
- Bought a man-in-Oslo's company in 1998.
- Carries a small carry-on with a handle and a strap; uses the handle.
- Is rude as a posture, not as an outburst — his cruelties arrive already-formed.
- Norwegian native; switches to English when annoyed.
- Has been thinking about *Watch them all cry* for three days before the transformation, after a recent dinner with the Oslo man.
- The transformation: small object, faux-aluminium, screen-front, etched *Trygve Aas* on the bezel, button on the right side. **Voice agency belongs to whoever holds him.**
- Hears everything.
- Has spoken aloud for the first time at the Coffee Company counter when Imani pressed his button — first in Norwegian (*"Hva i helvete er dette. Hvor er telefonen min."*), then in English ("What the hell is this. Where is my phone. Who has — who is touching me. I want to speak to a manager. I want to —" muted mid-clause).
- During the Schiphol morning rush, hung on the magnet beside the Tap and Pay, he noticed Imani's wrist-tic, her unattended notifications from NAOMI SCHOOL, the Loro Piana customer's ignoring-look, **and noticed that he was not enjoying it.**
- He has registered *if only I can get back* in interior monologue, in Norwegian, in a register he had not used inside his own head since his mother had been alive.
- He has been carried in: a chest pocket (Marielle), a lanyard (Anouk), a jacket interior pocket (Anouk), a magnet (Imani), a high-vis breast pocket (Karim), a wooden cargo crate (current).

### About the world
- The *Watch them all cry* site is a crypto-loss leaderboard with a video feed of people losing money in real time, brokered by a clause-as-product, in a black-and-cyan typeface designed (apparently) by a Gulf-monarchy intern.
- The transformation mechanism (per POETICS) is a "super-intelligent AI browser plugin magic (inexplicably)." The story does not explain it.
- Bergen Flesland operates with night cleaners (left-AirPod-only contractors), an SAS desk, a lounge with sparkling water and complimentary sandwiches, and a soft Norwegian-then-English PA voice.
- KLM Bergen→Amsterdam morning service uses the D-pier (D14 at Bergen).
- Schiphol Pier D/E junction has a Coffee Company kiosk, an H&M, parked Cathay Pacific 777s out the window.
- The Tap and Pay terminal animation: contactless icon → green checkmark, designed by a São Paulo agency in 2019, the universal visual grammar of consent in the post-cash economy.
- KLM Cargo runs a Bangkok 747-400ERF freighter from stand H32 at Schiphol.

## Refused (per POETICS, do not drift into)

- Class tourism in worker portraits — every worker named and particular.
- A clean redemption arc.
- Sentimentality about being held.
- Lagerlöf-pastiche or Yago-pastiche surface.
- Naming the uploads inside the prose.
- Magic-realist signposting.

- Letting the Lagerlöf register become uniformly grave — must also have play, magic, joy.
