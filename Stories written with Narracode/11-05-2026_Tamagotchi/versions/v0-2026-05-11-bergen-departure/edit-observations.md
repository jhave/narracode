# Edit observations — Loop 0

The prompter did not textually edit `drafts/0-bergen-departure.md`. All edits this loop fell on `POETICS.md`, including the answers to the seven Loop-0 open questions. The patterns below are derived from diffing the prompter's POETICS revisions against my original Initiator draft.

## 1. Mechanistic specificity over abstraction

Where I had elision, the prompter wanted texture made specific.

- "illicit website" → "illicit **crypto** website"
- "converted without warning" → "converted without warning **by a super-intelligent AI browser plugin magic (inexplicably)**"
- Added "(**living like a little elf that can used as a keychain**)" — clarifies form-factor and surfaces the Nils-elf parallel
- Added "(**the name etched over the device is 'Trygve Aas' not 'Tamagotchi**')" — explicit naming
- Added "**watch them all cry**" as the actual slogan / banner-text
- Added the **button-press / muting mechanic** in full

**Reading.** The prompter does not want the transformation to be only-a-mood. It is a mechanism with parts: a plugin, a button, a mute. The supernatural and the technical are not in opposition; they are the same operation seen from two angles. I should resist the literary temptation to leave mechanisms vague — the texture wants nameable parts.

## 2. Tonal balance correction (Lagerlöf register is not only grave)

In *Tonal compound*, the prompter inserted: "Yet also **insightful magical playful joyous**."

**Reading.** I had framed Lagerlöf's register as patient/attentive/low-key only. The prompter is reminding me that Nils' great quality is also play and lift — there is delight in the goose-flight, in the recognition of small creatures, in the magic. Future drafts should not let the patient prose become uniformly grave. The magic needs joy in it.

## 3. Class-foregrounding

In the second sentence of the premise, "a sensibility built for refusal" → "a sensibility built for refusal, **endowed with privilege**".

**Reading.** Trygve's cruelty is not only personal temperament. It is class-positional. Future drafts should keep the class element legible — not as theme-statement but as the texture of how he speaks, what he assumes, what he does not see.

## 4. The ending closed (and a longing-line added)

In *Inversions of Nils*: my "Trygve's home is the question. His Bergen apartment is no longer scaled for him." → prompter's "**Landing in Bergen, the story loops, somehow he is transformed back to real size.**"

In *Change of heart*: appended "**He thinks if only he can get back...**"

**Reading.** The story now has a homing arc. Trygve, post-transformation, has a *desire to return* that drives him. The ending is a closed loop, not held-open — he is restored at home in Bergen. Future drafts should let this longing surface in his interiority, especially in the quieter moments. He wants to go home; the question the story poses is whether the man who returns is the same as the one who left.

## 5. Global scope

"the smell of a Norwegian airport jetway in November" → "the smell of a airport **and cities around the world** in November"

**Reading.** The story is global. The airport-economy that carries him is multi-continental. I had been thinking too Bergen-locally; the prompter wants the world.

## 6. External agency over Trygve's voice

The prompter explicitly added: "IF you press a button on his side he can speak in any language. People who carry him just think he is an irate fun chatbot being to be let out. Occasionally he is muted or unmuted."

**Reading — and a self-correction.** My draft 0 wrote "He could feel ... that pressing the button would let him speak. He did not press it" — implying Trygve has voluntary control. POETICS is unambiguous: others press the button. He is muted/unmuted by his carriers. He has zero agency over when he speaks. The "did not press" line in draft 0 should be re-read as "could not reach" rather than "chose not to" — the phenomenology of feeling a button you cannot operate.

This is the highest-priority correction for draft 1: the button-press reveal happens when someone (Imani) figures it out at the counter. The character note the prompter raised — "someone who might scream for help, demand assistance" — is the right intuition: Trygve *would* shout if he could; the dramatic constraint is that the button is on his side and he has no side-pressing capacity. His voice is at the mercy of his carriers' curiosity.

## 7. Norwegian-texture additions

"wet wool, kerosene, hand sanitizer" → "wet wool, kerosene, hand sanitizer, **snus**"

**Reading.** Bergen-specifics matter when the prose is in Bergen. Snus is the right detail — small, exactly Norwegian, on every counter of every Norwegian airport in some form.

## 8. Hearing-everything

Added: "**hears everything**"

**Reading.** Trygve is now an audient first, a speaker barely. He hears through pockets, through fabric, through walls of the cargo hold. The prose should treat him as an attentive ear before it treats him as a face. (Already partially honored in draft 0 — the Tagalog Catholic podcast bleed, Anouk's heart in the pocket — but worth foregrounding more.)

## 9. The "3D" Tamagotchi

"He is now a screen-creature requiring care" → "He is now a **3D** screen-creature requiring care"

**Reading.** Volumetric, not flat. He has heft. He weighs something in a palm.
