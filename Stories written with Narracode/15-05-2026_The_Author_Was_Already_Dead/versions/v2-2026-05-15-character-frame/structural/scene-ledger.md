# Scene Ledger

## Scenes

### 1 — Arrival (`drafts/1-arrival.md`)

- **Act**: I.
- **Location/time**: Marek Halsey's seventh-floor apartment, morning, two days after the death.
- **Focal pressure**: The room is the room. The narrator must reckon with absence-as-ordinariness.
- **What changed**: Reader meets the narrator (agent, 31 years); the dead author (Marek Halsey, novelist with one book and a decades-long second project); the room as primary venue. Three objects planted: empty FedEx envelope, password-locked laptop, unentered second bedroom.
- **What remains unresolved**: Where is the phone. Whether the corpus exists. What killed Marek. What the envelope was for. What is in the second bedroom.
- **Makes possible**: Act II — the encounter with the phone. The second bedroom is the most likely site of that encounter, but not the only one (the phone could be on a kitchen counter, in a pocket of the coat by the door, charging on the bedside table).

## Open Scene-Level Questions

- Should Act II open with the second bedroom or with another beat first (the narrator walking the perimeter of the living room, the coat by the door, the bathroom)?
- When the phone is found, does the narrator know immediately what it is, or does she discover the love affair gradually as she begins to read?
- How is the corpus first heard/read on the page? Direct quotation? Paraphrase? Hybrid?
