# Time Constants

## Chronology

- Marek's first novel: published ~thirty-eight years before story-present.
- Narrator has been his agent: thirty-one years.
- They have lunched every six weeks for thirty years: ≈260 lunches.
- Marek shifted to voice-dictation: "his last decade" (≈10 years).
- Waiter retired: March 2026.
- Marek died: two nights before scene 1.
- Scene 1: morning, two days after the death.
- Setting: 2026, contemporary.

## Durations

- Walk-and-dictate composition sessions (implied): the phone holds recordings or transcriptions with timestamps. Length of an individual dictation: TBD.
- The narrator's drive home for the receipt with the password: across the city, non-trivial.

## Constraints

- Marek does not appear alive in the story. No flashbacks to him animate.
- The narrator only knows what she can see, hear, smell, remember.
- The phone has a battery state; it can be dead, locked, recoverable.
- The transcript was generated in 2026 by a consumer voice-to-text system; errors are of the kind that system makes.
- Police have removed the body and partially the tape. They will not appear as procedural figures on-page.
