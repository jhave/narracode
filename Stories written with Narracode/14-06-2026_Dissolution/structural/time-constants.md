# TIME-CONSTANTS — Dissolution Disequilibrium

## Chronology (ordered)
1. **Father's death** — Act I, Ward 7A, QEII Halifax. The establishing wound. (Recent past.)
2. **Return to Frogner** — Monique flies back to Oslo; loss "packed into luggage."
3. **Abscess on hip** — internalized stress somaticizes; repeated lancings and salt-water washes under local anesthetic.
4. **12-day Dicloxacillin course** — prescribed by GP. Infection persists.
5. **Culture result → MRSA confirmed** — GP's face changes. 
6. **MSIS registration** — Monique entered into the Norwegian National MRSA Registry as a documented vector.
7. **10-day Tetracycline course** — prescribed. Follow-up swab: infection gone but Monique remains *colonized* (nasal passages, skin flora).
8. **5-day decolonization protocol** — twice-daily Hibiscrub scrub, 3×-daily Mupirocin nasal ointment, daily 60°C laundry.
9. **University cleaner strike weekend** — Saturday solo sanitization with industrial Isopropanol.
10. **Washing-machine gasket rupture** — gray flood across bathroom floor; text misunderstanding with landlady.
11. **Aduro 9 flashback** — winter black-glass incident (chronologically earlier, surfaced here as memory).
12. **3:38 AM nightmare** — dog/cat dream; full panic.
13. **WhatsApp call to Sophia** — midnight breakdown; "psychotic equilibrium failure."
14. **AI sleep intervention** — Process S / Process C; resistance; mechanical execution. (Over the following days.)
15. **Third post-protocol MRSA swab → clean** — GP logs: "Decolonization protocol completed successfully. Patient cleared."
16. **Sara's plumbing fix** — outflow secured with a plastic twist-tie.
17. **Aduro 9 fix** — wire pipe cleaner from Jernia clears the clean-burn air intakes; system resets.
18. **Suitcase scene** — afternoon Frogner light; summer departure to Sophia imminent.

- **Daily** laundry at strict **60°C**.
- **12-day** Dicloxacillin course (failed).

- **10:30 AM** sharp espresso cutoff (post-intervention rule).
- **8:30 PM** "simulated sunset" in the Frogner flat (post-intervention rule).

## Constraints
- **Strict 15-minute** weekly clinic consultations with the GP.
- **60°C** mandatory wash temperature for MRSA decontamination.
- **Mandatory MSIS registration** — non-discretionary public-health reporting.
- **EES bottleneck risk** — headlines cite a 1-in-7 statistical chance the Frankfurt transit faces European Entry/Exit System cancellation; Monique's counter-reading: six out of seven planes fly.

## Time-feel note
Time in this story is measured in courses (12-day, 10-day), cycles (60°C wash), and repetitions (twice/three-times daily), not in conventional scene-time. Duration is the texture; the grind of protocol is the experience.
