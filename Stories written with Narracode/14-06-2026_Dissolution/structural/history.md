# HISTORY — Dissolution Disequilibrium

Canonical record of what has happened, been said, and been refused. Composition must remain consistent with this ledger.

## Established (facts true in the story world)
- Monique's father is dead — Acadian, 85, terminal cancer, died in Ward 7A, Centennial Building, QEII Health Sciences Centre, Halifax.
- Monique flew back to Frogner, Oslo, after the death.
- A painful abscess developed on her hip; repeatedly lanced and washed with salt water under local anesthetic.
- GP prescribed a 12-day Dicloxacillin course; it failed to clear the infection.
- Culture result confirmed **MRSA**.
- Monique was entered into the **MSIS** (Norwegian National MRSA Registry) as a documented public-health vector.
- GP prescribed a 10-day Tetracycline course; the infection cleared but Monique remained **colonized** (nasal passages and skin flora).
- A 5-day decolonization protocol was executed: twice-daily Hibiscrub (4% Chlorhexidine gluconate), 3×-daily Mupirocin nasal ointment, daily 60°C laundry.
- A university cleaners' strike occurred at UiO; Monique sanitized her workplace alone on a Saturday with industrial Isopropanol.
- The 14-year-old Electrolux washing machine's rubber door gasket ruptured mid-cycle, flooding the bathroom.
- The Frogner building's chimneys were broken for years and were repaired this year, after all five co-owners finally agreed to fund the structural overhaul; the Aduro 9 wood stove was installed as part of that restoration.
- Monique has not used the Aduro 9 since the winter black-glass incident (compressed newspaper choked the flame, glass went pitch-black, she feared burning down the historic building).
- A 3:38 AM nightmare (separating a dog and a cat) triggered full panic.
- Monique made a WhatsApp video call to Sophia in Nova Scotia and broke down.
- The AI sleep intervention occurred over the following days; Monique resisted, then mechanically executed the rules.
- The third post-protocol MRSA swab returned **clean**.
- **Sara** assessed the washing-machine outflow and secured the pipe with a plastic twist-tie.
- The Aduro 9 was fixed: a flexible wire pipe cleaner from Jernia, plus AI-assisted blueprints and a colleague's advice, cleared the soot-blocked clean-burn air intakes.
- Monique is packing to fly home to Sophia for the summer.

## Said (canonical lines / utterances)

- Monique to Sophia on WhatsApp, mid-breakdown: she names it a **"psychotic equilibrium failure"**; says lack of sleep is making her senile; says she cannot survive the week.
- GP's clinical note: **"Decolonization protocol completed successfully. Patient cleared."**
