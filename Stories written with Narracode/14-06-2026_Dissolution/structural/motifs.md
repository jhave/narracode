# MOTIFS — Dissolution Disequilibrium

Recurring material and conceptual figures. Tracked as Active / Transformations / At Risk of Overuse. Motifs here are concrete and load-bearing — they carry feeling the prose refuses to state.

## Active

### Temperature
The story's central sensory register. It moves through the body and the apartment as both threat and (eventually) neutrality.
- 60°C mandatory wash temperature for MRSA decontamination.
- Hibiscrub (4% Chlorhexidine gluconate) burning on perimenopausal skin.
- Perimenopausal hot flashes — heat rising without external cause.
- Pitch-black stove glass — the Aduro 9 incident, heat that turned to soot and fear.
- Freezing rain on muddy Oslo hill trails — the cold she is told to walk into for outdoor lux.
- The cold chair at 4 AM she is told to leave her warm bed for.
- Kefir cold from the fridge — the closing, neutral temperature. Heat that has stopped meaning anything.

### Bureaucratic registration
The body and the self rendered as record.
- MSIS — the Norwegian National MRSA Registry. Her body acquires a state shadow.
- The strict 15-minute weekly clinic slot — care measured and bounded.
- The GP's clinical notes — "Decolonization protocol completed successfully. Patient cleared."
- EES (European Entry/Exit System) bottleneck headlines — the 1-in-7 cancellation statistic.

### The body as foreign terrain
The colonized self; territory she no longer fully owns.
- The MRSA colony in nasal passages and skin flora — habitat, occupation.
- Mupirocin altering taste and smell — the senses estranged.
- Perimenopausal skin drying, rash.
- Arms tingling after the nightmare — the body running its own alarm.
- The abscess on the hip — grief surfacing as an object in the flesh.

### Clean / colonized binary
The organizing opposition; the word "colonization" doing triple duty (bacterial, historical, professional).
- The decolonization protocol itself.
- Frogner's serene affluent surfaces — chestnut avenues, 19th-century facades — against the contamination she carries.
- Isopropanol sanitization of every surface at the university.
- The clean swab as the only verdict that counts.

### Distance collapsed
Proximity achieved only through mediation.
- The Halifax–Oslo WhatsApp video call.
- Sophia's face framed by her Nova Scotia wooden office.
- Intimacy delivered through digital lag; presence as signal.

## Transformations (planned trajectories)
- **Temperature: threat → neutral.** Begins as hot flash, burning scrub, black-glass terror, freezing rain. Ends as kefir from the fridge and ordinary afternoon light. The motif cools as the crisis dissolves. This shift carries the resolution without stating it.
- **Colonization: dread → joke → cleared.** The word arrives as diagnosis, is turned by Monique into the dark sociological joke, and ends as a logged clinical clearance — bureaucratic, anticlimactic, complete.
- **Registration: weight → release.** MSIS as a burden conferred on the body; the final clinical note discharges it. The EES statistic is the final registration she reads, and she reads it cleanly: six out of seven planes fly.

## At Risk of Overuse
- **The irony of the sociologist.** The expert-becomes-patient figure is the spine of the story and therefore must be used sparingly and precisely. State it once (the colonization joke); let everything else imply it. Repetition would turn the premise into a punchline and collapse the dry register.
- **Metaphor pressure on temperature.** Because temperature is so dominant, composition will be tempted to editorialize it ("the heat of her grief"). Refused. Temperature stays literal — degrees, flashes, rain, kefir. The reader does the linking.
