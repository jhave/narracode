# OBLIGATIONS — Dissolution Disequilibrium

Promises the story has made to the reader and must honor. Tracked as Active / Resolved / At Risk.

## Active
- **The abscess wound as physical correlate of grief.** It must land as bodily fact — lanced, washed, burning — and must NOT be abstracted into metaphor. The reader infers the displacement; the prose never states it.
- **Sophia's face as the anchor.** The WhatsApp frame must register as genuine relief — a real face doing real work in a real wooden Nova Scotia office — not merely as a narrative device that resolves a beat. She is the still point; she must read as such without being sentimentalized.
- **The MSIS registry as public-health weight.** It must feel like a real status conferred on Monique's body — a bureaucratic shadow — not as decorative local color. The dread is institutional.
- **The Aduro 9 must be fixed on the page.** The winter's failed beginning (black glass, the fear of burning down the building) must be completed by the soot-clearing in Act III. The restoration of the stove mirrors the restoration of the self — but this must never be stated.
- **The suitcases must close the story without announcing closure.** The final action is mundane (a folded sweater) and must carry the weight unannounced. No summary sentence may tell the reader the crisis is over.

## At Risk of Neglect
- **The dark sociological colonization joke.** "In my line of work, colonization is rarely, if ever, a good thing." Must land with dry precision in the clinic — not telegraphed, not set up, not explained afterward. Easy to over-frame; must stay throwaway.
- **The father, never spoken, never sentimentalized.** Risk: composition reaches for a tender flashback to fill the emotional gap. The gap is the point. Resist.

## Resolved After Completion (planned payoffs)
- **Father's death processed into packing motion.** The grief that is never named is displaced first into the abscess, then into protocol, finally discharged as the ordinary motion of packing — the folded sweater. This is the only "repair" the story offers.
- **The cascade resolved through small competencies, not catharsis.** Sara's twist-tie, the wire pipe cleaner, the espresso cutoff. The crisis dissolves; it is not overcome dramatically.

## Cathartic inflection point (governing constraint)
NOT the WhatsApp call (peak crisis). It is the **folded sweater** — Monique picks up a mundane object and the crisis recedes without announcement. All composition should protect this moment from premature relief and from explicit framing.
