# SCENE-LEDGER — Dissolution Disequilibrium

Eight planned scenes across three acts. Each entry records: setting, what happens, what it establishes, and what it opens (the door into the next scene). Status: all PLANNED at init.

---

## ACT I — Destabilization

### Scene 0 — the-palliative-room
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Setting:** Ward 7A, Centennial Building, QEII Health Sciences Centre (Halifax Infirmary), South Park Street, Halifax.
- **What happens:** Monique sits bedside with her 85-year-old Acadian father on the terminal-cancer trajectory under comfort-care protocols. Her professional certainty as a grief sociologist shatters in the room. She packs the loss into luggage and flies back to Europe.
- **What it establishes:** The originating wound. The expert's armor cracked before the story even reaches Oslo. The body as a container for unprocessed loss.
- **What it opens:** Grief displaced into luggage; the body as a site that will somaticize what the mind refuses. Leads to the abscess.

### Scene 1 — the-colony
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Setting:** The Frogner clinic; the GP's 15-minute slot.
- **What happens:** The abscess on the hip; repeated lancings and salt-water washes. 12-day Dicloxacillin fails. Culture result flashes on the screen; the GP's face changes — MRSA. MSIS registration. 10-day Tetracycline; follow-up swab shows infection gone but colonization remains. Monique delivers the dark sociological joke.
- **What it establishes:** Monique's new identity as a documented public-health vector. The first dark irony. Medical bureaucracy as weight.
- **What it opens:** The decolonization protocol.

## ACT II — Proliferating Paranoia

### Scene 2 — the-decolonization
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Setting:** The Frogner flat. Five days, twice daily.
- **What happens:** The mechanical regime — full-body Hibiscrub (4% chlorhexidine gluconate) left burning on perimenopausal skin; Mupirocin nasal ointment three times daily on cotton swabs, altering taste and smell; daily laundry marathon at strict 60°C, sanitizing every personal item touched.
- **What it establishes:** Bodily invasion by antiseptic. Control attempted through ritual. Duration as texture.
- **What it opens:** Domestic friction spilling out of the laundry overdrive.

### Scene 3 — the-ghost-in-the-chimney
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Setting:** UiO sociology department (Saturday, cleaner strike); the Frogner flat (gasket rupture, flashback).
- **What happens:** Cleaners' strike at UiO; Monique sneaks in alone on a Saturday with industrial Isopropanol, spraying every door handle, tap, toilet lever, lounge counter, seminar table. The 14-year-old Electrolux gasket ruptures mid-cycle — gray flood across the bathroom. Frantic text misunderstanding with the anxious landlady across the language barrier. Flashback: the winter Aduro 9 black-glass incident; the building's newly repaired chimneys; the stove as a triumph of restoration she could not use.
- **What it establishes:** Paranoia accumulating across body, workplace, and home. The apartment as adversary. The stove as unfinished business.
- **What it opens:** The sleep breakdown.

### Scene 4 — the-nightmare
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Setting:** The Frogner flat, 3:38 AM; the WhatsApp frame to Nova Scotia.
- **What happens:** Sleep architecture disintegrates under night sweats and adrenaline. The dream — separating a snarling dog from an attacking cat, lifting the hissing cat overhead, the dog snapping, claws on bare arms, flinging the cat into the next room. Wakes at 3:38 AM in full panic, arms tingling. Opens a WhatsApp video call to Sophia; sees her calm face against the wooden office backdrop; breaks down completely. Names it a "psychotic equilibrium failure"; says lack of sleep is making her senile; says she cannot survive the week.
- **What it establishes:** Crisis peak. The expert at the furthest point from her own theory. Sophia as the still point.
- **What it opens:** The AI sleep intervention.

## ACT III — Banal Dissolution of Crisis

### Scene 5 — the-ai-sleep
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Setting:** The Frogner flat, over the following days.
- **What happens:** Monique engages an AI assistant with deep academic skepticism. It walks her through Process S (adenosine accumulation) and Process C (circadian light tracking via the suprachiasmatic nucleus); explains that her half-sleeping drift is training her brain to read the mattress as a war zone. She fights the advice — leaving a warm bed for a cold chair at 4 AM, walking muddy Oslo hill trails in freezing rain to gather outdoor lux. Out of sheer exhaustion she begins mechanically executing the rules: espresso cutoff at 10:30 AM sharp, 8:30 PM simulated sunset.
- **What it establishes:** Recovery as mechanical, not insightful. The body retrained by procedure, against the will of the expert.
- **What it opens:** The clearing — body and apartment beginning to reset.

### Scene 6 — the-banal-reset
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Setting:** The Frogner clinic; the Frogner flat (plumbing, stove).
- **What happens:** Third and final post-protocol swab: clean. The GP logs the definitive note: "Decolonization protocol completed successfully. Patient cleared." The landlady has left the outflow line disconnected, claiming a vital piece is missing. Sara — 63, Swedish, eight months into a late-career gender transition, former professional welder — assesses the plumbing, sees the landlady simply misread the mounting bracket, secures the outflow pipe with a plastic twist-tie. The apocalyptic flood threat dissolves through casual competence. Then, with a colleague's advice and a flexible wire pipe cleaner from the local Jernia, plus AI-assisted blueprints, Monique clears the soot-blocked clean-burn air intakes of the Aduro 9. Air pathways open; the system resets.
- **What it establishes:** The cascade resolving through small competencies. Grace arriving as plumbing and soot, not revelation.
- **What it opens:** Departure.

### Scene 7 — the-suitcases
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Setting:** The Frogner flat, afternoon light.
- **What happens:** Hot flashes settling, skin rash clearing, gut recovering on daily Norwegian kefir. Headlines warn of a 1-in-7 chance her Frankfurt transit faces EES cancellation bottlenecks; her mind reads it cleanly — six out of seven planes fly. She looks at her open suitcases. Turns off her screen. Picks up a folded sweater. Begins packing to fly home to Sophia for the summer. The nightmare has lost its architecture, dissolving back into the quiet routine of a normal life.
- **What it establishes:** The story ends in motion, not arrival. No catharsis — the next mundane action. The folded sweater as the cathartic inflection point, unannounced.
- **What it opens:** Nothing. The story closes here, mid-gesture.
