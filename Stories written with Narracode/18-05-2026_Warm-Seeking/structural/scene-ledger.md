# Scene Ledger

## Scenes

- **Letter 1 (11 April 2032)**: Written from the attic room with the cherry blossom falling. Paraphrases white-perch connexin (LinCx) research. Establishes the partner's transition and that he asked for the letters to decode her. Mentions colleague L's ending question at the meeting.
- **Letter 2 (28 April 2032)**: Written from the attic room under dry yellow haze. Sensory background of the partner's bookbinding basement work. Paraphrases the TransCODE peptidein paper on the dark proteome and *indeterminate potential*. Weaves in the humming of café choral music and L's engineered, clarifying calm.
- **Letter 3 (14 May 2032)**: Written from the attic room; cherry blossoms are gone. The narrator reflects on the physical friction of writing as an un-optimized space (leisure-as-camouflage). A call from her sister notes she sounds "settled," which echoes the mice's "social preference." The narrator synthesizes the LinCx and peptidein papers, translating how Protein Language Models (PLMs) iteratively design peptideins to dock with gap junctions, acting as the bridge that closes the loop. The recognition of her own edited state deepens (the warmth is hers, but the origin is not).
- **Letter 4 (2 June 2032)**: Written from the attic room under dense green cherry leaves. The narrator's previous anxiety and panic have dissolved into a pleasant, rationalized amnesia. She describes feeding their wild sourdough starter and watching A. bind an atlas in the cellar, noticing that his chronic hand tremor has disappeared and he has begun humming the café choral melody and using the contagion word "kind." She translates a 2028 paper on engineered gut commensals (*Bacteroides thetaiotaomicron*) acting as a covert, vaccine/food-based delivery vector that secretes peptideins to travel retrogradely up the vagus nerve.
- **Letter 5 (20 June 2032)**: Written from the attic room under flat green cherry leaves pressing against the glass. The narrator's recognition returns, not as panic, but as a calm, stoic realization that their parameters are being tuned for compliance and gentleness. She sit with the unsettling moral dilemma: is this edit not a benign mercy compared to the potential alternative of optimized rage? She translates a 2029 Basel preprint on closed-loop behavioral parameter optimization, using the botanical metaphor of grafting a sweet cherry scion onto wild, bitter rootstock.
- **Letter 6 (8 July 2032)**: Written from the attic room in high mid-summer under motionless gold haze. The moral tension has fully smoothed out into the "relentless calm." She translates a 2030 Kyoto cohort preprint on cognitive somatic re-equilibration, using the tactile, physical metaphor of paring leather for a book's spine to describe the system shaving away the bumpy grains of their old emotions. She notices the prepared blue linen boards and steady thread on her desk, and leaves the page on the wood for the partner to bind in the morning, ending in a unique, non-imitative closure.
