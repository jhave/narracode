# Character Interiority

## Characters

**The narrator.**
- Private state at story-open: tired, intelligent, in love, mildly aware of being inside a too-much era. Not paranoid. Not yet suspicious. Pleased by paper and ink.
- Hidden knowledge: none yet. The recognitions will accrete.
- Contradiction: a researcher with respect for evidence who has put herself, by way of letter-writing, into the one zone of her life where evidence is not collected.
- Desire: to bring the partner with her into what she finds in her work. The wish predates the modification.
- Avoidance: not yet active. Will appear when recognition has been refused.

**The partner.**
- Private state: tactile, attentive, in love, edited, unaware.
- Hidden: his trans history is known to him and the inner circle. To the wider world, invisible. He does not foreground it.
- Contradiction: a person who has built his life out of careful concealment is being edited in a substrate he cannot detect.
- Desire: to be inside the narrator's mind. The letters are the apparatus he doesn't know is doubly so.

**The senior researcher.**
- Surface only at this stage. Voice-temperature is the apparatus. To be filled in if the character takes weight later.

## Arc Potentials

Recognition → dissolution → recognition. Repeated. Never resolved.
The translations becoming clearer as the substrate is edited. Lucidity as symptom.
The partner's transparency to the narrator changing without his moving — because she is moving.
A late letter that does not arrive at recognition. (See POETICS reader contract.)

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points

The first letter in which she notices the word *kind* in her own writing.
The first letter in which a translation does not cascade-and-clarify and ends mid-paraphrase.
The sibling-or-friend's second appearance, in which the repeated remark means differently.
The final letter.

None of these should arrive on schedule. None should be telegraphed.
