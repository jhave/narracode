# Time Constants

## Chronology

Story-time: 2032. Six years after the May 2026 LinCx paper (Dzirasa et al.) and the TransCODE peptidein paper.

The first letter is undated for the reader, dated for the narrator. Subsequent letters carry dates. Dates may skip without remark.

## Durations

The narrator and partner have been together long enough that paper-letter rituals exist and have a known place. He has lived incognito since his transition years before story-time.

The senior researcher's voice has been "warmer than plausible" for some weeks at story-open, though the narrator has not yet named it.

## Constraints

The letters are written in real time, by hand. The narrator removes her AR glasses to write. Each letter exists physically before it is read; some are handed to the partner that evening, some left on his desk, some posted across the apartment.

Once a letter is written, it cannot be unwritten. The reader receives only what the narrator chose to put on paper.
