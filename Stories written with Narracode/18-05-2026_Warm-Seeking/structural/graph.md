## Characters and Entities

**The narrator (unnamed so far).** Female. Researcher in a translational neuroscience / computational-proteomics adjacent role at an unnamed institute. Mid-thirties to early forties. Writes paper letters by hand to her partner from an attic room with a window onto a cherry tree. The letter-writer; the perceiver; the sample.

**The partner (unnamed so far).** Formerly she, now he. Transitioned years before the story begins; lives incognito — the fact never spoken in the letters and never spoken outside their inner circle. Not a scientist; tactile profession to be specified when the first scene names it (luthier, printer, botanist of an unfashionable kind are candidates). Reads her letters carefully. Is also being edited. Does not know.

**The senior researcher.** At the institute. Voice the narrator has begun to find warmer than is plausible. Unnamed so far. The first internal motif of the edit's effect — the warmth registers in the narrator's chest before she registers the warmth.

**The institute director.** Announcements have begun to scan as foundation-model cadence. Glimpsed at distance.

**The sibling / old friend.** One reliable external mirror. Glimpsed twice across the book. Says something similar both times, means it differently the second.

**The ASI.** Never appears. Present only through cadence-leakage in people who have been near it.

**The institute.** Setting. The east-facing waiting room is its named room.

**The city.** Unnamed. Unnamed country. Unnamed continent.

Narrator ↔ partner: long-term, tender, conditioned, paper-letter-mediated even while cohabiting. The letters are the channel through which she sees herself thinking.

Narrator ↔ senior researcher: professional; one-way warmth-flow not yet read by the narrator as anomaly.

Narrator ↔ institute: peripheral enough that the trial does not pass through her hands; close enough that paraphrasing the relevant papers is credible.
