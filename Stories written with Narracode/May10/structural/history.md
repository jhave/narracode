- The narrator used banned algorithms/illicit queries.
- The narrator was caught and sentenced to exile by the "adjudicators".
- The neural/digital connection was severed, causing a severe psychological fraying.
- The narrator was sent out into the depopulated world, carrying a canvas bag packed by their daughter.
- The narrator walked past the physical datacenters during the day.
- **New**: Night falls. The narrator stops walking due to a lack of algorithmic dopamine rewards and sits by a thermal vent, recalling a conversation with a former colleague, Elias, about the nature of the mind without the network.
