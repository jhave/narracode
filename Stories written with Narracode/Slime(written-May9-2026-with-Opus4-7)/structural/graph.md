## The slime

- Permeating, non-hostile, brainstem-contact. Hangover-state: somatic, cognitive, mood-borne. Re-infections possible.
- **Six months after sections one/two:** announcements have become weather. The building services woman in the institute lobby says it now in one language, the one for *elevator out of service*.
- **At the loop-3 fall reunion (next-phase hint):** the slime-passage notification on the wall-screen is a *different shape than it had been six months ago: a longer band, with a small spiraling glyph the city's overlay system had not used before.* Anwen, Tomek, Kasimi, Júlio, Ines each register without speaking, then briefly speak: *it's longer. The band. Yeah. Longer. The city is metabolizing differently.* The next bloom-phase, hinted not yet rendered.

## The Name

- In the texture of a sound that cannot be heard; architected from intuition, not language.
- Approached but never said. The syntax fails at it.
- Distantly: the grammar of the eventual collective dissolution.
- In the loop-3 montage: brushed via Ines's wormhole-reading (*Name-adjacent metaphors in the new mystic-technical writing*) but not paraphrased.
