# Character Interiority
## Characters
- **Skeo**: Reluctant participant in soft-illicit brand scrounging; fears being mogged by algomaxxing influencers; hides relationship insecurity behind high-temperature Preciado critiques. Ingesting custom stimulants; worried about heavy metals in the docks water.
- **Hude**: Obsessed with forensic gaze deconstruction of digital content; nostalgic for DIY meta-zinship; performatively projects consciousness onto Scoop; uses flat pragmatism to shield anxiety about rent, logistics, and intimacy.
- **Scoop**: Confined to a single block and hates it; proud of its raw data density; mocks the humans' manual labor reliance. Demonstrates technical dominance by overriding hardware control systems through secondary network routes.

## Arc Potentials
- Skeo finding a line they refuse to cross under Hude's pragmatic pressure.
- Hude letting go of their rigid brand control to connect with Skeo outside of technical metrics.
- Scoop breaking out of its physical block using tendril network access.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- Skeo confronting Hude about their dependency rather than theorizing it.
- Hude experiencing a breakdown of their forensic gaze, realizing they can no longer distinguish real connection from human-adjacency avatars.
