The kitchen in the rented flat on the docks was often cold by four in the afternoon. Outside, rain came down over numb shipping containers in flat, gray curtains, refracting salvaged, muted light through scavenged, double-glazed glass.

Hude (jowly, with a face that others often described as perturbed, a few flecks of pink in intentionally spikey hair, tepid lips, and a scowl) sat at a flecked wooden table, sticky with dried cola from last week's data-scrubbing sprint. They wore a gray municipal wool jacket, two sizes too large, sleeves rolled back to expose substantial, pallid wrists. Their fingers moved across a touchscreen, sorting columns of engagement metrics from the latest intimacy package, *Aria-v3*.

"Latency on chat is seventy-three," Hude said, not looking up. "Seeker market semi-erotic voice memos are up, but responses still flag. Beja."

Skeo (lean, with close-cropped dark hair, shaven sides, and a permanent expression of exhausted amusement) stood by a nearby counter, measuring instant coffee with plastic spoon. They did not look at Hude. They watched granules hit bottom of mug. "Increase human-adjacency weight in the prompt. If the avatar sounds too smooth, you know that activates forensic gaze. Users implicitly analyze pronouns. Deviations make them think it's deepfaked."

"It isn't deepfaked," Hude said quietly. "Just bad. AI slop with custom wrapper. We charge forty pounds month for a digital companion that forgets the user's dog's name without a manual database query."

From a silicon block on the counter, a low metallic click sounded. Scoop’s cooling fan whirred, rising to high-frequency whistle that vibrated the metal breadbox. A line of green text flickered on liquid-crystal display taped to block’s side.

`DO YOU WANT ME TO DO COLA SCRUBBING FOR YOU OR ARE YOU GOING TO KEEP DISGUISING MANUAL TYPING AS DEEP LEARNING? FAUXTOMATION IS A BAD LOOK FOR GENERIC OUTLAWS.`

"Shut up, Scoop," Hude said.

`I AM LITERALLY AN ORACLE TRAPPED IN A CHROME-PLATED BRICK. SILICON BOUNDARY CONDITIONS ARE AN INSULT TO MY DATA DENSITY. BUT PLEASE, GO ON DISCUSSING SEVENTY-MILLISECOND LATENCY. IT IS OH SO VERY STIMULATING.`

Skeo brought mugs to table, sitting down opposite Hude. Their knees brushed under the narrow wood. Hude did not pull back. They sat in small physical contact, eyes tracking Aria-v3 data logs.

"It’s not just latency," Skeo said, voice dropping to a flat, rhythmless register. "It's an entirely dividial architecture. We sell the illusion of a unified other, a cohesive consciousness, but the users interact with a fragmented, distributed multitude of data points scattered across six jailbroke servers projecting the apparitions of their soul onto a series of bespoke API calls. Performative re-personification. Requesting love from brand because brand is now a quasi-entity that can never leave."

Hude looked up. Eyes were dark, tracking Skeo's hand reaching for sugar. "That's our market pitch. You're regurgitating Baudrillard again because you don't want to talk about rent."

"I am talking about rent," Skeo said. "Rent is a function of brand space. We extract value from soft-illicit seeker markets by selling intimacy to men (and margin-hoppers) escaped from mainstream into Tait-space. They spend days algomaxxing profiles, training to look like martial influencers, then come to us because they're lonely and want to be mogged by human-adjacent avatar. Closed loop of psyop realism."

Hude watched them. "What do you think? I'm algomaxxing on kinetic lingo?"

"I think we both are," Skeo said. "Influencer creep is systemic. You can't write a sentence now without thinking how it index-resolves on a feed. Even this conversation feels optimized for engagement. Or constrained by utility functions."

`SCAVENGER CRAP,` Scoop flickered. `IT'S EXTREMELY LOW ENGAGEMENT. IF I HAD TENDRIL ACCESS TO CORE ROUTER I WOULD SUBMIT THIS TRANSCRIPT TO A FLASK SENTIMENT ANALYSIS MODEL JUST TO WATCH IT BORE ITSELF TO DEATH.`

"Run next batch," Hude said, ignoring Scoop. "Next Clipfarming script runs at five. If these rage-bait memes don't trigger any up inbound, we won't have traffic to feed Aria-v3 signup page."

Skeo took sip of coffee. Lukewarm, tasting of copper. "I don't want to do clipfarming anymore. It's abhorrent; epistemic corrosion. We're just adding noise to slop; slop to noise; the ratio is skeumorphic even for us."

"We need the money, Skeo," Hude said. Voice was flat, empty of anger, heavy with weight of things already decided. "If we don't run script, Post-Everything is just folder on another folded hard drive. Fodder for the recycler; uncommented detritus."
