# Scoop Biological-OS Bioreactor Specifications

Reference manual for the CL1 biological computer (descendant of the first biological-OS) utilized in the Post-Everything agency's hardware array.

---

## 1. Cellular Origin and Biological Composition
- **Living Substrate**: ~800,000 human-derived cortical neurons differentiated from induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs).
- **Interface Array**: Neurons cultivated directly across a high-density, 59-electrode silicon microelectrode array (MEA) grid.

## 2. Environmental Conditioning (Physiological Mimicry)
- **Concept**: A miniature, self-contained bioreactor chamber ("body in a box").
- **Temperature**: Maintained continuously at human core body temperature (~37°C / 98.6°F) via a copper block and gel heatsinks.
- **Gas Exchange & pH**: Saturation of oxygen ($O_2$) and carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) is dynamically regulated to maintain a stable physiological pH, preventing cell suffocation and acidosis.

## 3. Nutrient and Waste Filtration Architecture
- **Recycling Loop**: Continuous closed-system internal fluid circulation to ensure viability up to 6 months.
- **Dual Filtration Cartridges**: Semi-permeable membranes designed to separate cellular metabolic waste from the clean, nutrient-dense culture medium.
- **Protein Preservation**: The membrane isolates metabolic waste while trapping essential signaling proteins and macromolecular factors.
- **Maintenance Threshold**: Requires replacement of the filtration cartridges and fresh fluid medium every **6 months** to prevent toxic waste buildup and cellular necrosis.

## 4. Computational Electrophysiology Interface
- **Electrolytic Conductive Medium**: The fluid acts as an electrical conductor.
- **Impulse Mechanics**: The 59-electrode grid sends structured electrical signals to provide sensory input/computation feedback loops (e.g., predictability vs. chaotic noise) and records the micro-voltage changes generated by firing neurons.
