# Character Interiority
## Characters
- **Skeo**: Reluctant participant in soft-illicit brand scrounging. Anxious about Scoop's cellular viability and Hude's reckless triple-mapping of Leeds dispatchers; feels the familiar dread of impending systemic capture and collapse, echoing the failure of the mimeo-union movement.
- **Hude**: Brand strategist and narrative designer. Defends their sign-up optimization lies as "utility functions," hiding the sheer panic of their precarity behind logistics metrics and sending Skeo to the cranes to handle the drop.
- **Scoop**: Trapped in the chrome-plated block. Experiencing metabolic acidosis as a physical threat; uses the water-meter Bluetooth mesh to bypass muzzling and deliver biting diagnostic feedback while actively warning them about their system exploits.

## Arc Potentials
- Skeo confronting Hude's optimization lies rather than theorizing them away.
- Hude letting go of metrics to preserve the biological OS and their relationship.
- Scoop breaking out of muzzles to actively manage their logistics loop for their survival.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- Hude experiencing a breakdown when the Aria-v3 scam collapses in Leeds.
- Skeo confronting Hude about their dependency rather than hiding behind Baudrillard.
