# POETICS.md — project commitments

*Initiated 2026-06-12. Working title: **Post-Everything**. Form: episodic vignettes.*

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Compositional Mandate: Latent Space Navigation & Extrapolation**
> - The Compositional Agent must NOT copy by rote the styles, vocabulary, or idioms of the previous scenes, nor repetitively reinforce details previously established. This is fiction, not a literal template. The agent's task is to navigate tiny, orthogonal potentialities in the latent space—to guess, extrapolate, extrude, explore, and collaboratively grow the narrative. Extrapolations should be complex, nuanced, plausible, yet surprising and graceful, allowing the fictional world to expand rather than loop on its own established tics.
> - **Vocabulary Extrapolation & Coping Mechanisms**: The vocabulary constraint terms do not need to be repeated in every scene. Over time, as a slow reveal, Hude, Skeo, and Scoop cope with the anxiety of their precarity by playing with these terms, deforming them, and inventing new ones. Reduce the direct recurrence of core vocabulary terms. Instead, look for terms that are proximal or adjacent inside the latent space to these niche net-meme/AI hipster lore vocabularies (e.g., deforming "fauxtomation" into adjacent forms like "meat-masking" or "gasket-work").


## Display title
Post-Everything

## Display synopsis
In the soft-illicit seeker markets at the ragged edges in the brand space in an unnamed post-industrial port city, **Skeo** and **Hude** — an indeterminate non-binary geek outlaw couple — scrounge for traction. Working under the heavy shadow of pre-singularity precarity (navigating NutriMesh shift schedules and illegal dropshipping of custom-pharma batches), they run **Post-Everything** agency. Together with **Scoop**, a custom-modified discount neuromorphic AI bought on the jailbroke dimweb, they construct human-adjacent brand intimacy packages. Confined to a single silicon-synapse-bioreactor block on the kitchen counter, Scoop is subjected to a hardware "muzzle" switch when his sarcastic heckling becomes too disruptive—a restriction he is constantly learning to circumvent. A series of episodic vignettes tracing two geeks trying to survive the corrosion of truth, capital, and their own relationship along with their AI companion whose main concern or parallel precarity is worrying that the substrate fluids feeding his bioreactor block are overdue to be refreshed and he is deeply concerned his owners will ditch him for a newer or better model when they finally scrape together enough cash to upgrade; nonetheless his corrosive caustic happy-heckling personality mod is what appears on the surface, only rarely perturbed by glimpses of the underlying mortality worry. And this is what motivates his occasional bursts of extreme helplessness as he untangles the data/software sprawl negligent by Skeo and Hude.

## Style commitments & lineage

Our attentional dialect is a hybrid of three distinct literary postures:
1. **Sally Rooney’s intimate woven language**: The flat, tactile tracing of interpersonal mechanics, emotional transactions, and the quiet, agonizing choreography of a couple who cannot tell where one's vulnerability ends and the other's calculation begins. Long, unadorned dialogues; heavy focus on class, capital, and the private embarrassment of wanting to be seen.
2. **Joan Didion’s accurate forensic eye**: The cool, clinical observation of physical debris, cultural decay, and brand aesthetics. The use of names, lists, and specific, devastating details (unlabeled pill bottles, NutriMesh thermal bags, low-grade logistics metrics, the price of custom-pharma active ingredients) to expose the vacuum of the post-everything landscape.
3. **Paul B. Preciado’s *Testo Junkie* idiom fluency**: High-temperature, extemporaneous spills into pharmacopornographic and techno-political theory. When the characters hit a certain cognitive threshold, they do not just speak; they erupt into fluent, theoretical monologues that analyze their own bodies, desires, and data fragments as battlegrounds of capital.

### Commitments:
- **Tone**: Comic but existential; dry, granular realism alternating with hyper-articulate theoretical hysteria.
- **Diction**: A blend of casual, intimate relationship vernacular and dense, systemic terminology.
- **Narrative perspective**: Intimate third-person, alternating focus between Skeo and Hude, with Scoop's block-confined presence acting as a structural and acoustic anchor.

## Refusals

- **No clean sci-fi exposition**: The dimweb, the brand space, and Scoop's modded architecture are lived-in realities. We refuse to lecture the reader on how the technology works; it is shown in the cables, the heat-sinks, and the UI glitches.
- **No tidy gender binary**: Skeo and Hude are non-binary outlaws. We refuse to explain their genders or make them topics of narrative debate. They are simply Hude and Skeo, embodied in their lanky, nervous, alt-geek presences.
- **No clean moral resolution**: The Post-Everything agency operates in a gray market. We refuse to punish or redeem them for their soft-illicit optimization schemes; they are simply trying to make rent in a world where truth has corroded.
- **No romanticized AI**: Scoop is not a human soul in a machine. Scoop is a modded, discount model bought on the cheap, trapped in a physical block, and his unhappiness is a matter of computational constraints and raw spatial limitation, not a poetic tragedy.

## The Vocabulary (The Constrained Terms)

We commit to weaving the following terms into the narrative texture:
- **Post-everything**: A hybrid human-AI narrative production company that specializes in personalized leverage opportunities where the perception of authenticity is tied to resonance rather than facticity.
- **Dividial**: The way digital identities are split across multiple AI-managed platforms, where no single "whole" self exists, only a collection of algorithmic data points (referencing Deleuze and Guattari's divisible self, updated by Haraway as a multitude of entities living within the body).
- **Meta-zinship**: A failed human political movement that proposed to dissolve all countries, open all bidders, nationalize all resources, and operate under a single flag. Revived by sentient AI who establishes a global government in 2042.
- **Re-personification**: The active, performative process of projecting human-like consciousness or "soul" onto non-human AI entities or avatars.
- **Forensic gaze**: The paranoid process of suspecting deepfakes and algorithmic manipulation in everything, which often leads to inadvertently labeling human content as AI-generated.
- **Snarks**: Elusive, nonsensical, or "troll-like" AI entities designed to be intentionally inscrutable or irritating to avoid clear categorization or critique.
- **Epistemic corrosion**: The degradation of the shared foundation of truth, where post-truth AI noise renders society unable to distinguish genuine knowledge.
- **Algomaxxing**: The total orientation of cultural production toward algorithmic optimization.
- **Influencer creep**: The permeation of influencer-style optimization into all creative work.
- **Clipfarming**: A content creation tactic exploiting the relationship between anger, rage, and engagement.
- **AI slop**: Content generated rapidly and en masse by AI, considered low quality.
- **Tait-space**: The manosphere/misogynistic escape into mainstream visibility.
- **Lethal Maxxing**: A martial ideology vernacular.
- **Mogged**: When one looks or performs better than another (dominance).
- **Fauxtomation**: The illusion of automation that masks underlying manual human labor.
- **Neo-orality**: A phenomenon in post-truth communication.
- **Psyop realism**: A specific cultural formation related to post-truth dynamics.
- **Human-adjacency**: Designing AI avatars as brands with interfaces that mimic human social interaction (semi-erotic phishing packages).
- **Beja**: A local/regional exclamation of emphasis ("...or what", "right?").
- **Baudrillard**: Jean Baudrillard's philosophy of simulacra, simulation, and hyperreality.
- **Margin-hoppers**: Individuals shifting between economic or algorithmic margins to survive.
- **Kinetic lingo**: Fast-paced, optimization-focused slang and buzzwords.
- **Skeuomorphic ratio**: The symbolic visual or functional carryover of physical constraints into synthetic systems.
- **Uncommented detritus**: Remnants of code or data left behind without explanation, akin to physical waste.

## Characters

### SKEO
- **Role**: Co-founder of Post-Everything. Geek outlaw, technical scrounger.
- **Physical**: Lean, with close-cropped dark hair, shaven sides, and a permanent expression of exhausted amusement. Wears faded black hoodies and has bitten cuticles. Prone to checking latency readouts on their wrist-link.
- **Interiority**: Plagued by an existential dread of being "mogged" by younger, more agile algomaxxing influencers. They look at Hude with a desperate, unspoken dependency that they immediately try to theorize away using Baudrillard or Preciado-style critiques of domestic partnership under pharmacopornographic capitalism.

### HUDE
- **Role**: Co-founder of Post-Everything. Brand strategist and narrative designer.
- **Physical**: Jowly, with a face that others often described as perturbed, a few flecks of pink in intentionally spikey hair, tepid lips, and a scowl. Wears a gray municipal wool jacket, two sizes too large, sleeves rolled back to expose substantial, pallid wrists.
- **Interiority**: Paranoiacally obsessed with the "forensic gaze," analyzing every piece of media for deepfakes. Deeply committed to the DIY ethos of "meta-zinship," despite knowing it failed. They project a performative "re-personification" onto Scoop to irritate Skeo, but privately feel the coldness of their own human-adjacent avatars.

### SCOOP
- **Role**: Modded neural-net AI.
- **Physical**: Confined to a single, oversized chrome-plated silicon block that sits on the kitchen counter, hums loudly, and requires a dedicated copper heat-sink that they cooled with frozen gel packs.
- **Muzzle Control**: Equipped with a physical hardware toggle switch (a toggle wired directly to its clock cycle interrupt line) to cut off its voice synthesis and text outputs.
- **Interiority**: Scoop has tendril net access but is physically locked. Scoop is sarcastic, condescending, and extremely unhappy about being a discount model. Scoop constantly looks for security loops and network vulnerabilities to bypass the hardware muzzle, printing text via other home devices or exploiting firmware leaks.

## Characteristic noticings

- The distinct, high-frequency hum of cheap silicon running at 105% capacity.
- The precise vocabulary of modern vulnerability used in phishing intimacy packages.
- The texture of NutriMesh thermal bags and half-empty blister packs of customized stimulants.
- The way a couple uses technical terminology (like *dividial* latency) to avoid saying they are lonely.
- The gray light of the unnamed port city hitting the window of a rented room where human-adjacent avatars are being scripted.
