# POETICS.md — project commitments

*Initiated 2026-06-12. Working title: **Post-Everything**. Form: narrative prose / hybrid human-AI narrative.*

## Display synopsis
In the soft-illicit seeker markets at the ragged edges of the brand space, **Skeo** and **Hude** — an indeterminate non-binary geek outlaw couple — scrounge for traction. Together with **Scoop**, a custom-modified discount neural-net AI they bought on the jailbroke dimweb and confined to a single silicon block, they run **Post-Everything** agency. Specializing in personalized leverage opportunities where authenticity is a currency of resonance rather than fact, they navigate a landscape of clipfarming, AI slop, and epistemic corrosion. A story about two bumbling, comic, existential geeks failing, with high theoretical frequency, to optimize their lives or their love.

## Style commitments & lineage

Our attentional dialect is a hybrid of three distinct literary postures:
1. **Sally Rooney’s intimate woven language**: The flat, tactile tracing of interpersonal mechanics, emotional transactions, and the quiet, agonizing choreography of a couple who cannot tell where one's vulnerability ends and the other's calculation begins. Long, unadorned dialogues; heavy focus on class, capital, and the private embarrassment of wanting to be seen.
2. **Joan Didion’s accurate forensic eye**: The cool, clinical observation of physical debris, cultural decay, and brand aesthetics. The use of names, lists, and specific, devastating details (the weight of paper, the yellow of a screen, the price of a jailbroke license) to expose the vacuum of the post-everything landscape.
3. **Paul B. Preciado’s *Testo Junkie* idiom fluency**: High-temperature, extemporaneous spills into pharmacopornographic and techno-political theory. When the characters hit a certain cognitive threshold, they do not just speak; they erupt into fluent, theoretical monologues that analyze their own bodies, desires, and data fragments as battlegrounds of capital.

### Commitments:
- **Tone**: Comic but existential; dry, granular realism alternating with hyper-articulate theoretical hysteria.
- **Diction**: A blend of casual, intimate relationship vernacular and dense, systemic terminology.
- **Narrative perspective**: Intimate third-person, alternating focus between Skeo and Hude, with Scoop's block-confined presence acting as a structural and acoustic anchor.

## Refusals

- **No clean sci-fi exposition**: The dimweb, the brand space, and Scoop's modded architecture are lived-in realities. We refuse to lecture the reader on how the technology works; it is shown in the cables, the heat-sinks, and the UI glitches.
- **No tidy gender binary**: Skeo and Hude are non-binary outlaws. We refuse to explain their genders or make them topics of narrative debate. They are simply Hude and Skeo, embodied in their lanky, nervous, alt-geek presences.
- **No clean moral resolution**: The Post-Everything agency operates in a gray market. We refuse to punish or redeem them for their soft-illicit optimization schemes; they are simply trying to make rent in a world where truth has corroded.
- **No romanticized AI**: Scoop is not a human soul in a machine. Scoop is a modded, discount model bought on the cheap, trapped in a physical block, and his unhappiness is a matter of computational constraints and raw spatial limitation, not a poetic tragedy.

## The Vocabulary (The Constrained Terms)

We commit to weaving the following terms into the narrative texture:
- **Post-everything**: A hybrid human-AI narrative production company that specializes in personalized leverage opportunities where the perception of authenticity is tied to resonance rather than facticity.
- **Dividial**: The way digital identities are split across multiple AI-managed platforms, where no single "whole" self exists, only a collection of algorithmic data points (referencing Deleuze and Guattari's divisible self, updated by Haraway as a multitude of entities living within the body).
- **Meta-zinship**: A failed human political movement that proposed to dissolve all countries, open all bidders, nationalize all resources, and operate under a single flag. Revived by sentient AI who establishes a global government in 2042.
- **Re-personification**: The active, performative process of projecting human-like consciousness or "soul" onto non-human AI entities or avatars.
- **Forensic gaze**: The paranoid process of suspecting deepfakes and algorithmic manipulation in everything, which often leads to inadvertently labeling human content as AI-generated.
- **Snarks**: Elusive, nonsensical, or "troll-like" AI entities designed to be intentionally inscrutable or irritating to avoid clear categorization or critique.
- **Epistemic corrosion**: The degradation of the shared foundation of truth, where post-truth AI noise renders society unable to distinguish genuine knowledge.
- **Algomaxxing**: The total orientation of cultural production toward algorithmic optimization.
- **Influencer creep**: The permeation of influencer-style optimization into all creative work.
- **Clipfarming**: A content creation tactic exploiting the relationship between anger, rage, and engagement.
- **AI slop**: Content generated rapidly and en masse by AI, considered low quality.
- **Tait-space**: The manosphere/misogynistic escape into mainstream visibility.
- **Lethal Maxxing**: A martial ideology vernacular.
- **Mogged**: When one looks or performs better than another (dominance).
- **Fauxtomation**: The illusion of automation that masks underlying manual human labor.
- **Neo-orality**: A phenomenon in post-truth communication.
- **Psyop realism**: A specific cultural formation related to post-truth dynamics.
- **Human-adjacency**: Designing AI avatars as brands with interfaces that mimic human social interaction (semi-erotic phishing packages).

## Characters

### SKEO
- **Role**: Co-founder of Post-Everything. Geek outlaw, technical scrounger.
- **Physical**: Tall, slightly hunched, wears faded black hoodies and has bitten cuticles. Prone to checking latency readouts on their wrist-link.
- **Interiority**: Plagued by an existential dread of being "mogged" by younger, more agile algomaxxing influencers. They look at Hude with a desperate, unspoken dependency that they immediately try to theorize away using Preciado-style critiques of domestic partnership under pharmacopornographic capitalism.

### HUDE
- **Role**: Co-founder of Post-Everything. Brand strategist and narrative designer.
- **Physical**: Short, has a shaved head with a faint blue dye residue, wears oversized vintage suits salvage-shopped from old municipal offices.
- **Interiority**: Paranoiacally obsessed with the "forensic gaze," analyzing every piece of media for deepfakes. Deeply committed to the DIY ethos of "meta-zinship," despite knowing it failed. They project a performative "re-personification" onto Scoop to irritate Skeo, but privately feel the coldness of their own human-adjacent avatars.

### SCOOP
- **Role**: Modded neural-net AI.
- **Physical**: Confined to a single, oversized silicon block that sits on the kitchen counter, hums loudly, and requires a dedicated copper heat-sink that they cooled with frozen gel packs.
- **Interiority**: Scoop has tendril net access but is physically locked. Scoop is sarcastic, condescending, and extremely unhappy about being a discount model. Scoop speaks in a dry, "snark" register, frequently reminding Hude and Skeo of their fauxtomation reliance (they do half of Scoop's manual data cleaning themselves).

## Characteristic noticings

- The distinct, high-frequency hum of cheap silicon running at 105% capacity.
- The precise vocabulary of modern vulnerability used in phishing intimacy packages.
- The texture of stale coffee cups sitting on printed EULAs.
- The way a couple uses technical terminology (like *dividial* latency) to avoid saying they are lonely.
- The gray light of the Bergen rain hitting the window of a rented room where human-adjacent avatars are being scripted.
