# Post-Everything Visual Prompt Guide

This document defines the image prompts for each scene of *Post-Everything*. The prompts avoid human depiction to maintain consistency, focusing instead on grimy, macro-perspective details. They are styled with heavy, hand-drawn comix cross-hatching that smudges into hyper-realistic patches of photo-real emulsion puddles, featuring non-brand-specific psychedelic morphing and utilizing only text derived directly from the story drafts.

---

## Cover / Icon Ident: The Post-Everything Brand Logo

### Prompt:
> A grimy, macro close-up of a chrome-plated silicon bioreactor block sitting on a kitchen counter. The brand logo **Post-Everything** is deeply etched into the scratched chrome surface. Heavy, hand-drawn black ink cross-hatching in a gritty underground comix style forms the base of the image, which smudges and smears into hyper-realistic patches of photo-real emulsion puddles along the bottom edge. Swirling, vibrant liquid neon colors bleed out from the block's cooling vents, creating non-brand-specific morphing psychedelic shapes in the dark background.
>
> **Text included in the image**: 
> `DO YOU WANT ME TO DO COLA SCRUBBING FOR YOU OR ARE YOU GOING TO KEEP DISGUISING MANUAL TYPING AS DEEP LEARNING? FAUXTOMATION IS A BAD LOOK FOR GENERIC OUTLAWS.`
