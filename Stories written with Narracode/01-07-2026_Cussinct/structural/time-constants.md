# Time Constants

## Chronology
- Story opens: last week of June 2026, Henrietta's move-in.
- Spans: roughly late June through mid-August 2026 (heat dome onset → Canada Day weekend → thunderstorm that breaks the heat).

## Durations
- Heat dome: multi-week event, intensifying then breaking via thunderstorm.
- Comedy nights: weekly, Tuesdays, 7pm, established as ongoing ritual from scene 1 onward.
- COMPASS pilot: framed as a 90-day trial beginning roughly concurrent with Henrietta's arrival.

## Constraints
- Carlin has been dead since 2008 (in-world fact, referenced not re-litigated).
- Quebec's Bill 96 language politics are live background pressure on all signage/admin interactions — cannot be waved away as resolved.
- Cozy Grove's HVAC is 1990s-era and cannot be instantly fixed; heat is a real physical constraint on scenes, not just mood.
