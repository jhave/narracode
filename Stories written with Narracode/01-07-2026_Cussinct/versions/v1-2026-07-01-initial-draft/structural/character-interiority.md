# Character Interiority

## Characters
- **Henrietta**: uses Carlin like scripture; fears being managed into blandness by new ownership; withheld grief for her husband.
- **Bram**: dismantles/defends every bit as syllogism; secretly moved by late Carlin in a way that feels too personal to admit; Kantian anti-AI stance.
- **Winnie**: burned out of squeamishness by thirty years of oncology; pro-diagnostic-AI from hard experience of missed scans; impatient with her own aging body.
- **Zdenka**: grieves trees/forests as she would people; heat dome and haze are bodily wounds, not abstractions.
- **Marjorie**: gap between performed scandal and real, rare profanity.
- **Selam**: code-switches Tigrinya/joual without noticing which fires first under stress; wary of AI from prior job-replacement experience; surprised by her own fondness for Henrietta.
- **Kit**: internet-native English profanity vs. inherited Bisaya profanity feel different in the body; ambivalent-to-hostile on AI generally, yet becomes the de facto COMPASS operator.
- **Ginette**: privately delighted, professionally obligated to complain; most theologically loaded sacres in the building.
- **Dr. Dhillon**: rarely swears, only in Punjabi under real duress — must land harder for its rarity.

## Arc Potentials
- Henrietta: insurgency as grief-management → possible moment where the ritual's real function (mourning, not just comedy) surfaces.
- Selam: the comedy nights as the first Cozy Grove space that feels built for her, not just staffed by her.
- Bram/Henrietta: old flirtation surfacing or pointedly not surfacing.
- Winnie/Bram: AI-diagnostics argument as proxy for larger mortality-management disagreement.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- A real medical emergency during a comedy night, forcing Dhillon's rare Punjabi curse and testing COMPASS's actual usefulness under pressure.
- The heat dome breaking (thunderstorm) coinciding with an emotional break in one resident's composure.
- Ginette finally attending openly instead of complaining, or the reverse — actually shutting it down and having to face the room.
