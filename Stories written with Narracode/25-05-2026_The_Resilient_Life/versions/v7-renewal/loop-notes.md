# Loop Notes — Section VII: Renewal

## Section
VII. Renewal — night; the projector's blue under Calen's door; Sherri's hand at six inches, then on the door, then off; she does not knock; the morning recapitulates the opening; the ok level is 74.3; the day is going.

## What the critique found
- Critical "not" violation: "Not the warm yellow of the bedroom lamp. Not the white of the ceiling." Two sentence fragments defined the blue strip of light by comparative negation. Required revision.
- Other functional negations ("She did not knock," "She did not sleep") all permitted.
- Borderline "without a field / without a category / without a logged metric" — judged permitted as functional-structural language about the system's actual absences.
- Section overlong by ~130 words; bathroom-and-bed sequence had small slack.
- The Carlin landing (the standalone "74.3.") confirmed as the right finish.

## What the revision changed
- Replaced the "Not the warm yellow / Not the white" fragments with a clean positive definition: the gap is eight centimeters of carpet and a strip of light; the strip is a deep flickering blue with characters moving in it.
- Reworded "She did not know the name for what was happening in her chest. It was a thing without a field on the Display..." to "She had no name for what was happening in her chest. The Display had no field for it. The Academy intake portal had no category for it. The Compact participation index had no logged metric for it." This shifts the negations from defining the feeling to defining the system's absences — the feeling is positive (Calen, behind the door, making something by hand), the system's absences are positive facts about the system.
- Tightened the bathroom-bed sequence; cut "she walked back past Calen's door without stopping" to "she walked past it without stopping" and removed the second "without stopping" phrasing.
- Trimmed the community-garden hour from the morning inventory list; the absorption from VI is now implicit rather than re-stated.
- Kept the final "74.3." standalone as the landing.

## What the section achieves
Renders the Bellow ending the project required: a moment of feeling the Display cannot hold, that opens at the door and closes when the hand comes off and the routine resumes. The hand at six inches is the partial arc — Sherri touches the edge of the system and does not break from it; the touching is enough. The closing morning is the Babbitt loop closed: the same time, the same robe, the same cup, the same number, with the orientation countdown moved by one. The story ends on the number alone — 74.3 — and the reader carries forward both the grief and the acceptance, fused.
