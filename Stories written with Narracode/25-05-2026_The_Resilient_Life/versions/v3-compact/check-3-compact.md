# Critique — Section III: Compact

## Binality check
The booster speech is the heart of the section and it lands. Sherri's "this is a place where my family can become itself" is exactly the Lewis register — full sincerity, no irony from inside Sherri, irony entirely structural. The proud declaration about being above the threshold reads as the warmth it is for her and as the indicting frame for the reader, simultaneously. Good. The eight years, the southern quarter, the proof / the proof construction at the end of the speech — these earn the Babbitt comparison.

Where the binality weakens slightly: the description of Branda Halling threatens to tip into editorial portraiture (the lemon bars / minutes / believing-good lines are well-handled but verge on excess). Could be tighter, leaning into the warmth without quite as much narrator scaffolding.

## Kitsch-integrity check
Kitsch lands beautifully: thirty-one of thirty-two quarterlies, the soft-print lanyard, the artificial planters, the Wellness Rating to 7.41 specifically (the trailing decimal). The integrity comes through Joane's "Marcus had the violin. The violin was easy." — a single line that does the whole gendered displacement of Calen's problem.

## Carlin check
The Carlin moment: "*Calen codes alone in the dark and the form has no field for it.*" This is the little wind, lands well. There is also a quieter Carlin gesture at the booster speech itself — the speech is Lewis-register sincere, but reading it inside the section it does the Carlin acceptance work: Sherri sees clearly only in flashes, then continues. Good.

The phrase "The number was the number." — already used in Section I. It is a deliberate echo / motif, which can work, but appearing twice already in a seven-section story it risks becoming a tic. Worth considering whether the section needs its own slightly different Carlin landing line rather than this echo.

No comparative-negation characterization found. Clean.

## Vocabulary check
Present: the Compact, the application, the ok level, the Display, the Wellness Rating, the southern quarter, Drave's commission line, "the line is stable" register. The Bright is absent — correct for the interior meeting setting. The orientation is gestured at via "fifteen weeks from her Academy submission" — wait. Earlier sections established "the orientation was in twenty days." This is a continuity error. The story is supposed to take place across a single representative day; the orientation is twenty days away in Section I. Section III happens that same day. So "fifteen weeks" is wrong — should be twenty days or three weeks.

This needs correction in the revision.

## Length and rhythm
Target ~750 words. Current draft runs ~920 words — long by ~170. The Branda Halling description and the elevator coda are the most compressible.

## Revision targets

1. **The continuity error.** "Fifteen weeks" must become "twenty days" (or "three weeks") to align with Section I. The application deadline pressure is the story's central forward gauge; this number matters.

2. **The repeated "The number was the number."** Replace with a section-specific Carlin landing. The Compact section's natural Carlin move would strip the participation index or the proof-construction into something flat. Possible: "The participation logged." Or: "She had attended thirty-one of thirty-two. Marcus had the violin." Pull a stripped-flat sentence from inside the kitsch already on the page.

3. **The Branda description and lemon-bars beat.** Tighten. Branda's portrait can lose one clause. The lemon bars can do their work without quite the warmth-of-narrator pressure on them.

4. **The elevator coda.** Currently four sentences. Could compress to two; the section's weight is in the speech and the Joane exchange.
