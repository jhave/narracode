# Critique — Section V: Application

## Binality check
The form-mechanics sequence is the kitsch-engine of the section — the dropdown options, the soft-red banner, the regional registry returning "EU collective, non-accredited for North American intake purposes." These are precisely the bureaucratic-comedy register. The integrity comes through the keys down the hall (*tk tk tk*) which break into the cascade and don't quite get registered, then become the spine of Sherri's perception by the end. Both registers are present and fused, especially at the moment where the form rejections and Calen's keys are in the same paragraph.

The strongest binality move: "*My daughter is doing the only thing she does that she chose, and the form has no field for it, and I am the one filling out the form.*" This is the full collision — Sherri sees it briefly, and the next sentence is the cursor in the saved-draft field. Acceptance as the punchline.

## Carlin check
Two Carlin moves in this section:
1. The "EU collective, non-accredited for North American intake purposes" landing of the algorave search — this is a Carlin list-that-tips: the searches running through TidalCycles, live coding, algorave, and the form returning the most precise nullification.
2. The italicized declarative "*My daughter is doing the only thing she does that she chose, and the form has no field for it, and I am the one filling out the form.*" — this is the wind. Stripped, present-tense, articulated, then passing.

Both work. The second one is slightly long — could strip further. Possibly: "*Calen does one thing she chose. The form has no field. I am filling out the form.*" — but the longer rhythm has its own grace and may be the better choice. Marginal call.

## Revision targets

1. **The form-rejection sequence.** Three paragraphs (STEM Enrichment / Performing Arts / Studio Arts) is one too many — the list could end at two with a final "Other" beat being the implied closure. Or keep three but tighten each by a sentence.

2. **The cascade tipping into the half-apple / Quintards / Bright / Calen list.** This is good but slightly long; the list-that-tips Carlin move would work harder with one fewer item. Consider cutting one of the middle items.

3. **The closing two paragraphs.** "Sherri stood. Her knees were stiff..." through the end is good but the stand-and-hand-on-counter beat could compress to a single sentence; the return to the essay is the right structural close.

4. Consider whether the final paragraph should end on the typing of the essay (current) or on the keys continuing (which would be the binality move — Sherri returns to the form, Calen continues the score). Current ending is fine; alternative might be sharper. Worth testing.
