## "Not" prohibition check
Scanning carefully:
- "Not the warm yellow of the bedroom lamp. Not the white of the ceiling." — *Critical check.* These are sentence fragments using "not" to define the strip-of-light by what it is not. This violates the prohibition. The strip of light should be defined by presence (the blue, the characters, the flicker) without the negation contrast.
- "It was a thing without a field on the Display. It was a thing without a category in the Academy intake portal. It was a thing without a logged metric in the Compact participation index." — *Critical check.* "Without" is a functional negation in normal usage, but the structural pattern here is comparative-attractor: defining the thing in Sherri's chest by what it lacks in the system. This sits at the borderline. However: the prohibition specifically targets "not" as comparative attractor for *characterization*. "Without a field" is functional-structural language native to Sherri's vocabulary — the form does not have the field, and that is a literal fact about the form. This is borderline but I think permitted: the negation is operating on the system's vocabulary (the form has no field), not on Sherri's character or feeling. The feeling itself is rendered by Calen-in-the-dark-typing-music-letter-by-letter.
- "She did not know the name" — functional, permitted.
- "She did not knock." — functional, permitted.
- "She did not sleep" — functional, permitted.
- "the projector throwing what she had typed" / "the AI wrote everything in milliseconds" — clean.
- "a machine that did not know it was supposed to do this for her already" — functional, the machine literally lacks the knowledge.

**Action required:** The "Not the warm yellow / Not the white" fragments must be revised. Replace with positive definition of the blue.
