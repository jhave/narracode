The seven revised sections cohere as a single representative day with the loop closing on the morning at 5:47 the next day, the orientation countdown moved from twenty to nineteen. The vocabulary is consistent across all sections — the Display, the ok level, her picture, his line, the Compact, the Bright, the application, the orientation. No vocabulary drift. The half-apple is planted in Section I, lived through Section II, invoked in Section V, and present in Sherri's hand at the door in Section VII. The single-day structure holds. The orientation deadline (twenty days at story-open, nineteen at story-close) is the forward gauge throughout. The Compact's quarterly attendance and the Saturday garden sign-up bracket the revolt — the system absorbs the revolt back into itself between V and VI.

## Triptych position

The triptych position is felt without being named. Sherri has no vocabulary for the Contrition Epoch or the Lush Epoch. Her horizon ends at the Bright. The squeezed-middle position is rendered through the geography: SLD-7 is above the Deep Grid zones and below the Green Ring towers, and the Holums chose the southern quarter for school-zone eligibility. The pressure from above (the Bright as aspiration without architectural vocabulary) and the pressure from below (the Quintards descending out, Drave's commission tracking the descent) both act on the household. The reader sees the position. Sherri inhabits it.

The Babbitt-figure displacement is achieved: Drave is the warm, broad-stroke, optimistic provider whose continuation principle holds. Sherri is the wife who keeps the system running around him with the consciousness Babbitt himself could never have had. The Bellow density lives inside Sherri; the Lewis architecture sits around Drave.

The story achieves the peculiar binality the prompter asked for in most sections. The kitsch is the excess of appropriateness: the eight pieces "without anyone having decided eight," the participation index ticking after the garden sign-up, the Display showing 74.3 in the corner while the Extracurricular field rejects the algorave, the seventy-eight percent progress bar, the soft-print Compact lanyard, the air filter on its soft cycle at eleven seventeen. The binality is strongest in Sections II, V, and VII — the kitchen, the application, and the hallway. Section IV (Kessler) holds it almost as well, with the Quintard-kitchen empathetic projection.

The reader will follow but the seam shows.

This compresses the moment but it still sits at the upper edge of what the Carlin register can absorb without tipping into editorial. A future pass might consider whether the answer arrives in two sentences rather than four — but the current four-sentence form has structural rightness for the section's function as the assembled-tonight clarity beat.
- The repetition of "the day was going" appears in II, IV, VI, VII. This is a deliberate motif (Sherri's adapted "seize the day"), but the rhythm of repetition is tight in a single day's story. A future pass might vary one of the instances.
- Sections I and VII bracket the story with deliberate recapitulation (5:47, the robe, the cup, the kitchen wall, the Display, the ok level). This is the loop closing. It works structurally; whether it works at the pace the reader wants is a question for the reader.

## What the story is

A single representative day in Sherri Holum's interior, rendered in cascade form with the operative vocabulary native, with the comedy held inside the warmth and the warmth held inside the management and the management held inside the love. The Display closes for forty-seven minutes and reopens. The hand stops at six inches from the door and does not knock. The morning returns. The ok level is 74.3. The day is going. The reader carries forward both the laugh and the grief, fused in the same substance, which is the substance Sherri lives in.
