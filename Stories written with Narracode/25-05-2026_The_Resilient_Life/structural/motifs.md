# Motifs

## Active

- **The Score (74.3)**: the number Drave checks every morning. Each time it appears it should register slightly differently — stable, then anxious, then normalized, then, at the end, just a number.
- **The Range**: the name Drave has given the distance to the Green Ring towers. An aspiration organized as geography. Should appear early and late.
- **The guitar through the wall**: the only thing in Drave's world that has no score, no metric, no optimization value. The sound of something outside the system. Must not be over-explained.
- **The Compliance Dashboard**: Sherri's domain. A screen showing the household's composite metrics. It should appear as something warm and domestic — like a kitchen calendar — until the reader begins to see it otherwise.
- **Zone-adjacent**: Drave's professional vocabulary bleeding into his personal geography. He is "zone-adjacent" to both the Academy school zone and the Range. The phrase should accumulate irony without being pointed at.

## Transformations

- The Score: 74.3 → same number, different weight, by the end.
- The Range: aspiration → the reader should be able to see it as distance, though Drave never does.

## Avoid Overuse

- The guitar: once or twice, not more. Its power is in its restraint.
- "Zone-adjacent": a professional term, not a running joke.
