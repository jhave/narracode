# Character Interiority

## Characters

**Sherri Holum** — the center of consciousness

- Desire: to launch Calen successfully through the Academy. To hold the Score above 72. To keep Drave's commission band stable. To maintain the household at the optimization frontier she has chosen as her life's domain. Her desire is for the system to work for her family, executed through the Dashboard with all the skill she has cultivated.
- Love: real, warm, organizing every minute. The love takes the form of metrics because the metrics are the available form. She would refuse the suggestion that the form has colonized the love. From her interior the form *is* the love.
- Shame: the half-apple in the second drawer. She finds it. She enters the nutrition data within the acceptable range. She closes the drawer. The shame has no name and no field. It returns each evening.
- Avoidance: the direct conversation with Calen about eating. Sherri is certain — correctly — that naming the pattern would accelerate it. She manages around it. The managing-around is maternal love. The managing-around is also the system's logic, which prefers data within range over signal raised.
- Hidden knowledge: that the algorave coding matters to Calen in a way the Academy application does not. Sherri has stood in the hallway and listened. The knowledge is held below the level of articulable thought. It surfaces in small specific moments and resubmerges before it can be named.
- Contradiction: Sherri's competence is the engine of Calen's disappearance. Sherri's love is the form of the optimization that is squeezing Calen into the visible track and out of the body and out of the room. The reader must feel this without Sherri saying it. Sherri's interior must hold the love as primary, with the structural critique present in the rhythm of the prose and the selection of detail.
- Bellow note: the consciousness circles. A thought about the Score becomes a thought about Calen's lunch becomes a thought about the application essay becomes a thought about Drave's band becomes a thought about Calen. The circling is the form love has taken under pressure. The day is being seized; the day is passing.

**Drave Holum** — Sherri's husband, secondary

- He believes the language of his work. "Transition facilitation," "lifestyle rebalancing." The belief is sincere.
- He loves Sherri and Calen with the same warm, slightly broad-stroke attention he brings to his clients. He laughs at the wrong moments and means the laughs.
- He is one of the things on Sherri's Dashboard. He would refuse this framing. From his interior he is the provider, the partner, the steady commission band.
- He misses what Sherri sees about Calen. The missing is part of why Sherri carries it alone.
- His arc is the Babbitt arc displaced one position over: he continues. The system holds him. He is the household's stability and the household's blind spot at the same time.

**Calen Holum** — daughter, 14

- She types in the dark with the projector throwing TidalCycles syntax across the wall. The code is the score. The performance is for an audience she has yet to find. The practice is for itself.
- She eats methodically. Eight bites of the apple. The second half wrapped, placed in the drawer. The data within range. The body in slow withdrawal from the kitchen.
- She knows her mother stands in the hallway sometimes. She lets this happen. She has no language for it yet.
- She will get into the Academy or she will not. She has located a thing she cannot say: that the Academy is the route into a life she can see in full and refuses with a refusal she has not yet named.
- The algorave code is her resistance. She has yet to articulate it as resistance. From her interior it is the thing she does that is hers.
- Arc potential: the Babbitt's-daughter moment, gendered and weighted. A glimpse of an orientation Sherri can almost see. A moment that opens between mother and daughter and that the Dashboard closes around. Sherri blesses something she cannot follow. Calen receives the blessing and continues into the code.

## Arc Potentials

- Sherri: a partial arc. She does not break from the system; she touches its edge. The touching is enough. The Bellow ending — a moment of feeling the Dashboard cannot hold — opens at Section VII. The system continues; Sherri continues; the reader carries the rupture forward.
- Drave: no arc, by design. He is the continuation principle.
- Calen: an offstage arc, glimpsed through Sherri's consciousness. The reader senses Calen's interior trajectory through Sherri's oblique attention. Calen's disappearance continues; Calen's craft continues; the two trajectories run in parallel through the story's seven sections.
- The arc is finally the reader's, structured by the gap between what Sherri can see and what the prose makes visible.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points

- **The kitchen (Section II)**: Sherri watches Calen eat the half-apple. The data is entered. The drawer is closed. The morning continues. Cathartic in its restraint.
- **The Stewardship Circle (Section III)**: Sherri's booster moment. Civic faith rendered with full sincerity. The reader feels the warmth and the trap as a single substance. The comic Lewis register.
- **The application (Section V)**: the algorave question. Sherri's framing — "computational arts enrichment" — slides off the form. She tries another phrasing. The phrasing slides off again. The Dashboard has no category. A small Bellow circling: the mind returning and returning to Calen.
- **The revolt (Section VI)**: Sherri closes the Dashboard tab for an afternoon. An hour passes. The system continues. The return is silent.
- **The hallway (Section VII)**: Calen typing in the dark; the projector throwing syntax. Sherri at the door. The moment opens. Sherri reaches for the available language; the language falls short; the moment closes. Sherri steps back to her own room.
- **The morning after (end of Section VII)**: the Score at 5:47. The number holds at 74.3, or it moves to 74.4, or it moves to 74.2. The household continues. The reader carries the grief Sherri has lived through and has yet to name.
