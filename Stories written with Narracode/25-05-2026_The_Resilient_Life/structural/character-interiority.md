# Character Interiority

## Characters

**Drave Holum**
- Desire: to maintain; to provide; to arrive, eventually, at the Range. Not to transform — to secure.
- Shame: the one he won't name — that his work (Transition Facilitation) is the process he fears for himself. He helps people become what he is afraid of becoming.
- Avoidance: the score below 70. The Watch status. The conversation about what happens if. He manages around these the way a tongue manages around a sore tooth.
- Hidden knowledge: he almost knows that the distance between 74.3 and the Range is not a distance with a direction. He has had this thought. It has not formed into words.
- Contradiction: his genuine love for Calen organized as the Academy application. The investment thesis has colonized the love's expression. He is not wrong that he loves Calen; he is wrong about what loving Calen requires.

**Sherri Holum**
- The dashboard is not her cage. It is her competence, her domain, her contribution. She is genuinely skilled at it. Render this without condescension.
- She has noticed something about Calen that she has not said to Drave. (What? TBD by Compositional agent — something about the guitar, or about the application, or about what Calen actually wants.)

**Calen Holum**
- He knows the guitar playing through the wall is something his parents don't have a category for. He does not know what to do with this.
- He will get into the Academy or he won't. He doesn't know which outcome he wants. This is the thing he cannot say.
- His arc potential: the Babbitt's-son moment — not necessarily rebellion, but the glimpse of a different orientation to the world, which Drave blesses and cannot follow.

## Arc Potentials

- Drave: no arc, by design. The return to conformity is the point. He does not change; the system continues.
- The arc is the reader's, not Drave's.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points

- The work scene (Section III): Drave sees, briefly, what he facilitates — not as injustice but as something he doesn't have a word for. He finds the word "transition" and uses it and the moment closes.
- The Calen scene (Section V or VII): the guitar through the wall, heard and not-heard. The love that cannot speak itself in the language available.
- The ending: Drave looks at 74.3 the morning after. It is the same number. This should be the cathartic moment — not a cry but a stillness.
