## Cumulative Arc

- Sherri's interior trajectory is a single day's cascade that touches the edge of the system at three distinct moments (the Compact's Carlin wind, the Application form, the hallway), each more weighted than the last. The hallway is the partial break that does not break. The morning resumes.
- Drave's trajectory is continuation. The Quintards are the doppelganger he transitions out. He sleeps. His hand finds her hip. The line holds.
- Calen's trajectory runs offstage: the methodical eating, the second drawer, the keys through the wall, the blue projector at eleven seventeen. The reader sees her through Sherri's oblique attention. The application is the visible track; the code is the invisible track; the two run parallel through all seven sections.
- The Bright appears in Sections I, V (cascade), VI (towers lit, moving down), and VII (morning). Sherri's private word stays private throughout.
- The half-apple appears in I (planted in the drawer), II (the morning's cut, the wrapping, the placement), V (in the cascade), VII (invoked by the hand that sliced it). It is the object the Dashboard cannot hold and that the story's consciousness returns to.
- The Quintards appear in IV (in Drave's mouth) and in Sherri's cascade in IV and VI. They are the household that the ok level has stopped optimizing for.
