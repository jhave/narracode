# Reader State

## Current Understanding

*(Pre-draft — what a reader of POETICS would understand)*

- Drave Holum is a sincere, good-faith participant in a system that is eroding the ground he stands on.
- The story is the middle panel of a triptych about civilizational self-destruction: neither the purifying cancellation nor the gorging feast, but the compliant center.
- The Lewis inheritance means: free indirect discourse, the booster warmth that is also the trap, the return to conformity as ending.

## Expectations

- A representative day or short period in Drave's life.
- A moment of revolt or almost-revolt, handled without mockery.
- A genuine moment between Drave and Calen that carries the Babbitt-son weight.
- A return to the morning, the Score, the loop.

## Plausible Defiance Paths

- The Compositional agent might take the revolt further than planned — let it if it earns it, but watch that Drave's return is still credible.
- The Sherri character might become more present than anticipated — allow this; her competence with the Dashboard is a story in itself.
- The work scene might generate more pathos than the structure anticipates — the client Drave facilitates could become a mirror.

## Productive Mystery

- What does Calen actually want? (The reader should feel this question without it being answered.)
- What is Drave's specific recurring fantasy? (Must be concrete and small — the reader should recognize it as the one real thing he has, and watch him put it away.)
- Is 74.3 rising or falling? (The reader may wonder; Drave should not specify; the ambiguity is the point.)

## Accidental Confusion Risk

- Do not let Drave become satirically aware of his situation — free indirect discourse requires that he not see what we see.
- Do not let SLD-7 become a prison camp — its pleasantness is structural to the satire.
- Do not confuse Drave's "the Range" (his hopeful name for the towers) with the reader's understanding of it as distance — maintain the dual register.
