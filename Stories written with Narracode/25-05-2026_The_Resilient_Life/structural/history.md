# History

## Established

- SLD-7 is a mid-tier residential zone in the Great Lakes Corridor (formerly Milwaukee-Chicago), administered by the Meridian Regional Authority.
- The Holums are in the southern quarter of SLD-7, within the Academy school zone eligibility boundary.
- Drave facilitates residential transitions for families whose Scores have dropped. He does this well and compassionately.
- The Resilience Score (composite: financial, health, civic participation, environmental footprint, community rating) governs access to: mortgage rates, school zone, health tier, community amenities.
- Calen plays acoustic guitar — an old instrument, no sheet music, sometimes heard through his bedroom wall.
- The Green Ring towers are visible from SLD-7 on clear days. Drave calls this distance "the Range."
- The Community Stewardship Circle meets. Participation is logged. Logged participation affects the Score.
