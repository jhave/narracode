# History

## Established

- SLD-7 is a mid-tier residential zone in the Great Lakes Corridor (formerly Milwaukee-Chicago), administered by the Meridian Regional Authority.
- The Holums are in the southern quarter of SLD-7, within the Academy school zone eligibility boundary; chosen deliberately by Sherri in year three with the Academy already in mind.
- Drave facilitates residential transitions for families whose Scores have dropped. He does this well and compassionately. He works at Kessler & Norde, Tier Two, tracking mid-band.
- The ok level (the household composite metric — financial, health, civic participation, environmental footprint, child development indices) governs access to: mortgage rates, school zone, health tier, community amenities. Threshold at 72. Watch status begins below 70. The Holums sit at 74.3, "stable," for several months.
- The Compact (the SLD-7 Community Stewardship Compact, also called the Stewardship Circle) meets quarterly. Thirty-eight households at full enrollment. Sherri has attended thirty-one of the last thirty-two quarterlies. Branda Halling chairs. Marien Polk and Joane Reyes-Albright are members. Logged participation feeds the participation index, which feeds the ok level.
- The Bright is Sherri's private name for the visible Green Ring towers seen from the kitchen window. She coined it years ago and has never said it aloud.
- The Meridian Academy for Exceptional Futures requires household ok level ≥ 72 at submission. Calen's orientation is twenty days away (story-day) / nineteen days away (next morning).
- The application has an intake portal with eight sections: Biographical, Household Profile, Academic Record, Wellness Aggregate, Portfolio Uploads, Essay (in revision), References, Extracurricular. The Extracurricular field requires Activity Category, Institutional Affiliation, description, and verifying documentation. Calen's algorave/TidalCycles practice has no compatible category. Sherri has saved the field as draft with "independent creative practice" entered and the Institutional Affiliation field blank.
- Calen codes algoraves in TidalCycles. By hand. In her bedroom in the dark with the projector throwing the syntax onto the wall. The performance is for an audience she has yet to find.
- The half-apple: sliced into eight pieces at breakfast; Calen eats four; wraps the remaining four cut-side-to-cut-side in wax paper from the second drawer; places it back in the second drawer. Sherri will find it later and log it into the variable allotment.
- The Quintard family: a household Drave is transitioning out of SLD-7 into SLD-9. The husband says *we built this life*. The wife already knows. The daughter is sixteen, almost out. They will be in SLD-9 by the end of summer.
- The community garden has a tomato bed maintained by Compact volunteers. Sherri banks volunteer hours there; signs up Saturday at nine on the evening of the story.
- The Wellness Rating of SLD-7 is 7.41. Mobility fleet uptime is 99.2.

## Refused

- Sherri does not name Calen's eating pattern aloud and does not raise it directly with Calen. She manages around it because direct naming might accelerate it.
- Sherri does not knock on Calen's door at eleven seventeen.
- Sherri does not say *the Bright* aloud at any point in the story. The word remains her private vocabulary.
- The other triptych panels (the Contrition Epoch, the Lush Epoch) are never named or referenced in Sherri's interior. Her horizon ends at the Range / the Bright.
- The narrator does not editorialize. The author's intelligence lives in selection and rhythm.
- The ending refuses softening. The ok level holds. The household will be fine. The grief is the reader's.
