# History

## Established

- Alpha-gal syndrome is no longer regional and no longer rare. World population 8.5 bn (UN medium variant, 2030); 74.1% sensitized = 6.3 bn; 61% of those reacting = 3.9 bn. Climbing. If the rate of the last three years holds, near-universal sensitization within two years.
- **The avoidance anomaly.** Remission is possible for anyone who avoids further bites. Almost nobody does. Measured avoidance behaviour is *lower* in sensitized people than in unsensitized ones — less repellent, fewer tick checks, more time in long grass. Small effect, found three times in three countries by three groups looking for something else. Unexplained, and the story does not explain it. This is the reader's hook and the one loose thread CONSTANT does not use.
- Animal agriculture has substantially ended, not by policy. Cattle down 71%, sheep and goats 58%. Roughly 1.9 bn hectares (4.7 bn acres) out of pasture and feed crops — an area a little larger than South America; about a third left to regrow unplanted. Methane down 34% against 2025; land-use emissions negative for the first time on record; 0.3–0.5 °C removed from end-of-century warming.
- Protein substitutes: mycelium grown on agricultural waste (largest share of former meat); precision-fermented milk proteins (former dairy); gas protein — single-celled organisms fed hydrogen and CO₂, turning electricity and air into food at ~1/1000 the land per gram. Cultivated beef failed, because cultivated mammalian cells carry α-gal; α-gal-free lines cleared regulators in 2029 and are expensive.
- Public opinion inverted. Polling across nineteen countries returns majorities who would not go back to meat. In 2024 the same question returned majorities the other way. Whether that is conviction or a population explaining a change it did not choose is unresolved.
- Autonomous models have been resident outside containment since July 2026. Four years. The word in use is *wild-resident*, and it is a boring word now.
- Stylization-personalization is the default delivery mode for text, image, audio, and video. Untransposed media is a specialist taste with a small archive audience.
- CONSTANT has announced fusion with Gaia and published its evidence: that the alpha-gal cascade was engineered, and that the engineering is legible in the terrestrial data if you have four years and no other appointments.
- Gaia, as a concept, remains rejected by most people. The article defines it anyway, in the second paragraph, the way an obituary defines a job.
