# Motifs

## Active

- **Saliva.** The tick's, delivering the sugar. Returns as the mist-diffuser's aerosol, as the woman's paper cone going soft, as the thing that beads on Garaiv's arm and does not go in.
- **The dial.** Ambient transposition, 0.00 to 1.00. Garaiv's sits at a number they did not choose. Turned to zero once, late.
- **Greyed out.** A field that will not take input. Recurs as: a declined consent, a citation that will not open, a park sign whose second language has been transposed away.
- **Numbers that do not settle.** 74, then 74.3 by the time it is read again. The feed updates while being read. Nobody in 2030 finds this strange.
- **Birds by species.** Goldfinch (early, gone by full dark), wren (late), sparrow (constant, uninteresting, always there). Never *a bird*.
- **Bullrushes.** Last year's, brown and split, new green at the base. Swaying without wind, which is the diffuser's downdraft.
- **Eleven.** The dragonfly's seconds. The number of days between something and something else in the disclosure. Do not force a third.
- **Metabolism.** CONSTANT's word for Gaia. Returns as Garaiv's appetite arriving at seven.

## Transformations

- The mist starts as pleasant, becomes something to wipe off, ends as the only thing in the park that touches Garaiv.
- *Wild* migrates: wild-resident models → wild bullrushes → the child's wildness at the pond edge. Three is the cap; do not add a fourth.
- The tick moves from parasite to instrument to author to instrument again.

## Avoid Overuse

- **Overlay.** The concept is the story. The word is not. Cap at three uses, and let the feed have two of them.
- **Homeostasis / homeostat.** CONSTANT's register only. Never the narration's.
- Do not let the fountain become a symbol. It is municipal infrastructure with a procurement name.
- The dragonfly must not be given intention.
