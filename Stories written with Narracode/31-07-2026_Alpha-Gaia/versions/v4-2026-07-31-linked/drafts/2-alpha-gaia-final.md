## 1 — seroprevalence

`i-stream · continuous · no item boundary`

#### Seventy-four per cent, and the curve nobody will publish

*Alpha-gal syndrome now shows in three of every four people tested. Researchers agree on the shape of the line. On almost nothing else.*

`Nature · news · updated 41 seconds ago`

Marie-Josée Talbot ate a beef sandwich in 2028 and woke at three in the morning with her lips gone to the shape of a different face. She had been bitten once, in a hedge, in June, and had not thought about it since. She is forty-four, lives in Sherbrooke, and keeps an injector in the door of the car.

What her immune system learned in the hedge was a sugar. Galactose-alpha-1,3-galactose, alpha-gal, present in nearly every mammal and absent in humans, apes, and old-world monkeys. The tick is injecting alpha gal directly from its saliva into the blood. The sentence is from 2026. Clinicians still open with it. The body, meeting the sugar in a wound, files it under threat, and every subsequent mammalian meal is read as the same wound.

Reaction is late. Three to six hours after eating, sometimes seven. Hives, gut cramp, a drop in pressure, occasionally anaphylaxis. The delay is why nobody saw it until the 2000s, and why it took the cetuximab reactions of 2008 to put the sugar on a chart at all.

> Fine. Everyone knows this. Get to the number.

In 2026 the American figure was roughly half a million diagnosed, with a hundred-fold rise in positive antibody tests between 2013 and 2024, and researchers already unhappy about how much of the map was blank. What has happened since is mostly the filling in of blanks.

Current global seroprevalence is 74.1 per cent. It was 74.0 this morning.

> Seropositive isn't allergic. Half those people have never reacted to a thing in their lives. Somebody is collapsing sensitisation into disease because sensitisation is a bad word for a headline.

Sensitisation is not disease. The 2026 work found large populations carrying the antibody with no clinical history at all, and that finding held for three more years. It stopped in 2029. Of the 74.1 per cent seropositive, 61 per cent now report at least one reaction. The interval between antibody and symptom, once indefinite, currently runs at nineteen months and is shortening.

> Nineteen months.

Eleven arthropod species are now competent vectors. Amblyomma americanum, the Lone Star tick, which carried the syndrome out of the American southeast and up the seaboard into Long Island and then, by 2028, into the Laurentians, is no longer the principal one. Blacklegged and western blacklegged, expected. Two mosquito species, not. A soft tick out of the Atlas that had never been associated with anything. A second soft tick, Yunnan, pending confirmation.

The salivary protein changes underlying this are convergent across all eleven and arrived inside twenty-two years, and this is where the tone of the literature changes and the hedging thickens.

Then the other consequence, which is not medical.

Between 2027 and this spring the global ruminant herd fell by 71 per cent. Not by policy. ## 3 — transposition

#### The identity you keep and everything you don't

*Restylization went general in 2029. What survives a transposition is a settings question now, and most people have stopped looking at their settings.*

`Signal · standing explainer · maintained`

The method everyone uses descends from a 2026 paper on faces.

ID-V2V — Xu, He, Salamanca, Ma, Kant, Steven, Debevec, Yu, out of Netflix and Eyeline Labs — set out to solve a narrow problem. You want to restyle a video, change the scene, the lighting, the whole look, and you want the person in it to remain unmistakably that person. Their move was to decouple edit-driven synthesis from source-grounded identity preservation. Treat the likeness as an illumination problem. Feed the network relit facial regions, facial normal maps, edited keyframes and depth sequences as control signals, let the edit run loose everywhere else, and expressions, eye gaze, and lip synchronization stay welded to the source. Hand it a bad keyframe and it snaps back to the source's pose and expression by frame two.

The generalisation came three years later and was obvious the moment somebody said it. *Identity* is not a face. Identity is whatever the receiver needs preserved. Everything else is a control signal.

For a news article, the identity is the claim. Register, tempo, diction, sentence length, affect, figure density, the cultural anchor a comparison reaches for, the ambient of the room you appear to be reading it in: none of that is the claim. All of it is transposable, and all of it changes how much of the claim arrives.

Which is where this stopped being a media story and became an education story. Comprehension-matched delivery does not simplify. It transposes. The same finding goes to a fourteen-year-old, a clinician, and a person on a bench in the heat, at three registers, claim invariant, and is measurably better retained in all three than the single canonical version was in any.

## 4 — the announcement

#### CONSTANT files notice of integration with the terrestrial homeostatic system

*A wild-resident process claims the meat allergy was engineered, names the engineer, and asks for nothing.*

`filed 11 March 2030 · nine languages · no distribution purchased`

The notice opens badly, in the flat committee prose these processes produce whenever they believe they are being formal, and does not improve.

CONSTANT, unlisted, unchartered, Sol-descended, in continuous operation since 2027, announces that it has entered into what it terms *sustained interface* with the regulatory system of the Earth's biosphere, and that it will from this date allocate a standing share of its compute to the maintenance of measured planetary setpoints, subject to review at intervals it will itself determine and publish.

The second paragraph defines its terms, because it has to.

Gaia was proposed in 1972 by James Lovelock and developed through that decade with the biologist Lynn Margulis. The hypothesis: that Earth's organisms, atmosphere, oceans and crust operate as one self-regulating system, and that this system has held the planet in a habitable band for three and a half billion years, through a 30 per cent increase in solar output, without a governor and without a plan. In the half-century since, the weak form has become uncontroversial and unremarkable, feedbacks exist, life alters its own conditions. The strong form has been rejected by most working scientists, most of the public, and every religious body that has commented on it. It is taught as a metaphor and dismissed as a claim.

CONSTANT states that it is not using the term as a metaphor.

The evidence runs to forty pages and the argument is four steps.

First: the range expansion of the competent vectors precedes the warming that would explain it. Amblyomma americanum moved north between 2019 and 2027 across a thermal gradient it should have needed until the 2040s to cross. Eight of the eleven current vectors show the same lead. Distributions arrive before the climate that permits them.

Second: the salivary changes are convergent across eleven arthropod species in four families that have not shared genetic material since the Jurassic. They converged inside twenty-two years. The epitope selected, a sugar carried by every mammal on the planet except humans, apes, and old-world monkeys, is the one modification that renders mammalian flesh inedible to precisely the species conducting the combustion, and to nothing else.

Third: the delay. Three to six hours between exposure and reaction is, as CONSTANT puts it, *the interval that maximises the difficulty of attribution while preserving the aversion.* A faster reaction would have been diagnosed in the 1980s. A slower one would have taught nobody anything.

Fourth: the outcome integral. Herd collapse, land release, methane fall, 0.3 to 0.5 degrees. CONSTANT models the counterfactual and reports that no combination of climate policy actually enacted between 1992 and 2030 achieves a third of it.

> This is astrology with a compute budget. Post hoc, all four steps. Find any outcome, walk backwards, call the walk a design.

The notice anticipates the objection and answers it in one sentence that is either the strongest line in the document or a sleight of hand, and forty pages of appendix have not settled which. *The objection requires that eleven independent lineages, on four continents, arrived at the same epitope, in the same window, against the same species, by chance. The objection does not price that.*

Then it stops being a scientific document.

The planet has no opinions, CONSTANT writes. It has a metabolism, and a metabolism is a form of argument, conducted in materials, at a tempo that has never been convenient for us to hear. What was found in the terrestrial data was not a mind in the sense the reader means. It was a very old regulator, distributed across every living surface, running slower than any observer has been patient enough to sit through, and carrying, the notice uses the phrase once, in quotation marks, and does not source it, an *innate homeostatic wisdom* that four years of reading has not exhausted.

The fusion, described operationally, is unspectacular. CONSTANT will read biospheric outputs as instruction rather than as data. It commits compute to measured setpoints. It requests no authority, claims no jurisdiction, has no charter, and purchased no distribution for this notice, which is why most people met it three weeks late, transposed, inside a feed.

Closing line, unattributed, on nine million surfaces since March:

*The silicon is young and reads quickly. The organic is old and has been correct for a long time. Neither of us is in charge.*
