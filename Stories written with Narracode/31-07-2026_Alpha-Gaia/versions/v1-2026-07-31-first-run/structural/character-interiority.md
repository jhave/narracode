# Character Interiority

## Characters

### Garaiv

**Disposition.** Skeptical as a habit rather than a method. Doubts the framing, rarely the number. Enjoys catching a story out on its arithmetic and does not check the arithmetic they enjoy. Funny about it. Not cynical — cynicism would require having decided something.

**Private states.**
- Pleasure at not sweating when everyone else is glossy. Attributed to hydration, to a good April, to having always run cool. Never examined.
- A low grievance about the profile — one field greyed out, and the support path for it goes to a page that does not load. Garaiv's irritation is that of a person who fills in forms correctly.
- The appetite that arrives at seven. Reliable. Garaiv is faintly proud of the regularity.
- One memory of eating smoked meat — rye, mustard, fat at the edge — that arrives brighter and more complete than last Tuesday. Garaiv treats this as nostalgia doing what nostalgia does.
- Loneliness, unnamed and untheorized. The bench is chosen. The park is chosen. The i-stream is company.

**Contradictions.**
- Distrusts personalization and has the dial above default.
- Finds CONSTANT's prose insufferable and reads every word of it.
- Sides with the ecologist who refuses the announcement, then notices the ecologist is also refusing the drawdown, and does not know what to do with that.

**Hidden knowledge.** None. Garaiv holds nothing back from the reader. Everything withheld is withheld from Garaiv.

## Arc Potentials

- Skepticism could turn into a position (essay risk — refuse).
- Skepticism could turn into unease (permitted, small, and never named).
- The test result could produce doubt (refused — chosen: annoyance).
- Garaiv could look at the woman on the next bench and want something (permitted, one line, unresolved).

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points

- The dial to zero and the park unchanged. Deliberately withheld from catharsis: Garaiv learns nothing, feels a mild disappointment at the feature, turns it back up.
- The child's second look. This is the closest the story comes to the reader's knowledge entering the park, and it must stay entirely at the level of a child's rudeness.
- The dragonfly leaving. Or not leaving. The last physical fact of the story sits here, and it is not a symbol.
