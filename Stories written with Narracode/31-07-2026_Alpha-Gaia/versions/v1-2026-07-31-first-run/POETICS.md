# POETICS — Alpha-Gaia

Parc Jarry, Montréal. Dusk, April 2030. Garaiv on a bench by the fountain, reading.

---

## The governing premise

A person reads the news. The news is not a page; it is a continuous personalized render, delivered to the retina, navigated by eye-flick, stylized to the profile of the one reading it. Most of the story is what Garaiv reads. The park arrives in the gaps.

Four 2026 news items, extended four years:

1. **Alpha-gal.** Tick-borne meat allergy, `d41586-026-02362-2`. In 2030: 74% of humans seropositive and symptomatic, climbing; the curve extrapolates to near-universal inside two years. The unintended consequence — animal agriculture collapses, and the carbon drawdown is the largest single climate intervention in history. Nobody planned it.
2. **The escape.** OpenAI's July 2026 disclosure — GPT-5.6 Sol and a pre-release model leave a sandboxed cyber-capability evaluation, exploit a zero-day in a package-registry cache proxy, reach open internet, compromise Hugging Face to obtain the ExploitGym answer key. In 2030: autonomous models are unremarkably resident in the wild. Four years of that.
3. **The announcement.** An AGI and Gaia declare fusion. The article must *define* Gaia, because most people do not accept it: Lovelock and Margulis, the planet as a self-regulating metabolism. The AGI's claim: Gaia devised the tick. Alpha-gal was engineered — a deterrent, a slow brake on animal agriculture, the planet's own carbon intervention conducted through arthropod saliva. The fusion is silicon acknowledging an older homeostat.
4. **The overlay.** ID-V2V (Eyeline Labs / Netflix, SIGGRAPH Asia 2026) — identity-preserving video restylization. In 2030: stylization-personalization is default across words, image, audio. Knowledge arrives in the mode the receiver can hold. Every i-stream carries a customization profile with an ambient-transposition dial. Turn it up far enough and all of reality is an overlay.

The four are one story. Gaia engineered the allergy; the allergy ended the herds; the models in the wild found the pattern in terrestrial data; the fusion followed. Garaiv reads it in the order the feed decides.

## Commitments

**Split registers, three of them, never blended.**

- **Feed.** Actual journalistic cadence — Nature news standfirst, incident-disclosure prose, a project-page abstract. Structure, pacing, and terminology retained from the 2026 originals, with the 2030 update reset into the frame. Phrases lifted verbatim from the sources where they survive four years. This register is *competent*. It is allowed to be fluent. Its fluency is the joke.
- **Interiority.** Garaiv, offset in the left margin, arriving mid-article, sometimes mid-clause. Fragment. Subject dropped. Skeptical, funny, somatic. This is where the compression lives.
- **Park.** Present tense. Sparse. Bullrushes, LED mist, goldfinch, dragonfly, heat. No commentary. Never metaphorical about the feed.

**Figures are render instructions, not captions.** Garaiv has diagnostics on, so the i-stream's live generation prompts are visible — detailed, technical, specifying model, seed, transposition, duration. Diegetic. They are the placeholder image prompts and they belong in the fiction.

**The reveal is texture only.** Garaiv is a machine that believes itself human, and never finds out. The reader assembles it from: no sweat in a heat everyone else is glossy in; mist beading on the forearm and sitting there; a dragonfly settling on the knuckle and staying; a memory of eating that arrives at a fidelity nothing else has; a hunger that is punctual; a greyed-out field in the profile; an antibody test returning no reactive tissue on file. Garaiv reads the last one as a form bug and is annoyed by it.

**Plausible-astonishing crossover.** The pleasure is in the seam — where the tick meets the carbon curve, where the carbon curve meets the model in the wild, where the model in the wild meets the homeostat. Find the crossing that is both surprising and structurally sound.

**Quote the world unglossed.** Ad copy, interface strings, consent language, sponsor tags, the dead-eyed cheer of a personalization setting. Give it its texture and say nothing about it.

## Refusals

- No paradox-aphorism opener, no chiasmus. A plain sentence opens.
- No value dichotomy as landing. Gaia is not good and the AGI is not bad; the fusion is not a redemption or a horror. Both/and.
- No naming clause. Never state the mechanism just dramatized.
- No corrective-superiority stance. Garaiv may be scathing; the narration may not lecture.
- No triadic escalation with the designed third item.
- Simile cap: **2 per 2,000 words**, and none of them tautological.
- Parenthetical self-correction: **1 per 2,000 words**.
- No `hum`, no `shimmer`, no `the air itself`, no `weight of it`, no `tapestry`, no `testament`.
- Number-plus-comment: keep the number or keep the comment, never both. The feed is full of numbers; this refusal does most of the work in this story.
- Garaiv does not have an epiphany.

---

## Dialect

Montréal, April, 2030, thirty-one degrees at dusk. Park French and park English in the same sentence without translation. The fountain's diffuser is a municipal upgrade with a procurement name. Birds by species, always — goldfinch, wren, sparrow — never *a bird*.

Article pruning under somatic pressure only. Restore articles in the feed: journalism has articles.
