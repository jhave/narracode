## New classes for the registry

Two constructions found here that `master_ai_tells.md` does not hold. Appending per `post_draft_tell_scan` action 5.

**Class 18 — Statistic-with-a-shrug.** A figure followed by a clause performing worldly non-surprise at it. *"…and no parliament voted for any of it."* Distinct from class 2: the comment supplies knowingness rather than feeling. Kept once here (it is the §1 close and it is the story's argument); flagged because three more were written and cut during composition.

**Class 19 — The false-plainness list.** Four to nine concrete nouns delivered flat, the last one arriving from a different order of magnitude or century, to produce authority through range. *"…methane inventories, the seed-catalogue archive, phenology notebooks kept by a Norfolk schoolteacher between 1961 and 1994."* Very effective; becomes a signature the third time a draft does it. Rate cap: **1 per section, 2 per piece.** This draft has two. At cap.
