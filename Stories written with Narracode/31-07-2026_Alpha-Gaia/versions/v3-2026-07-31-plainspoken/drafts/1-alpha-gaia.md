## 1 — seroprevalence

#### Seventy-four per cent, and the curve nobody will publish

*Alpha-gal syndrome now shows in three of every four people tested. Researchers agree on the shape of the line. On almost nothing else.*

`Nature · news · updated 41 seconds ago`

Marie-Josée Talbot ate a beef sandwich in 2028 and woke at three in the morning with her lips gone to the shape of a different face. She had been bitten once, in a hedge, in June, and had not thought about it since. She is forty-four, lives in Sherbrooke, and now carries an injector in the door of the car.

What her immune system had learned in the hedge was a sugar: galactose-alpha-1,3-galactose, alpha-gal, present in nearly every mammal and absent in humans, apes, and old-world monkeys. The tick is injecting alpha gal directly from its saliva into the blood. The sentence is from 2026 and has been quoted so often that clinicians now say it the way people say a proverb. The body, encountering the sugar in a wound, files it under threat. Every subsequent mammalian meal is read as the same wound.

The reaction is late. Three to six hours after eating, sometimes seven. Hives, gut cramp, a drop in pressure, occasionally anaphylaxis. The delay is the reason it took until the 2000s to see at all, and until 2008 to connect to the cetuximab reactions that first put the sugar on a chart.

> Fine. Everyone knows this. Get to the number.

In 2026 the figure for the United States was roughly half a million diagnosed, with a hundred-fold rise in positive antibody tests between 2013 and 2024, and researchers already unhappy about how much of the map was blank. The blank was the point. What has happened since is mostly the filling in of blanks.

The current global seroprevalence figure is 74.1 per cent. It was 74.0 this morning.

> Seropositive isn't allergic. Half of those people have never reacted to a thing in their lives. Somebody is collapsing sensitisation into disease because sensitisation is a bad word for a headline.

Sensitisation is not disease. The 2026 work in the United States found large populations carrying the antibody with no clinical history at all, and that finding held for three more years. It stopped holding in 2029. Of the 74.1 per cent seropositive, 61 per cent now report at least one reaction. The interval between antibody and symptom, once indefinite, is currently running at nineteen months and shortening.

> Nineteen months.

Eleven arthropod species are now competent vectors. Amblyomma americanum, the Lone Star tick, which carried the syndrome out of the American southeast and up the seaboard and into Long Island and then, by 2028, into the Laurentians, is no longer the principal one. Blacklegged and western blacklegged, expected. Two mosquito species, not expected. A soft tick in the Atlas Mountains that had never been associated with anything.

The salivary protein changes underlying this are convergent across all eleven, and arrived inside twenty-two years, and this is the part of the literature where the tone changes and the hedging thickens.

Extrapolation is where the field stops speaking with one voice. The curve is logistic and everyone can fit it. Fitted on the 2027–2030 window, it reaches 97 per cent inside twenty-four months and 99 by 2033. Fitted on a longer window it does the same thing more slowly. Nobody has published the twenty-four-month figure in a titled paper. It circulates in slide decks with the axis unlabelled.

Then there is the other consequence, which is not medical.

Between 2027 and this spring the global ruminant herd fell by 71 per cent. Not by policy. Demand went first — a household that has had one member on the floor of a kitchen at four in the morning does not buy beef again — and the herds followed demand down, and the feed crops followed the herds, and the cleared land in Rondônia and Mato Grosso and the Chaco stopped being cleared because there was no longer a reason to clear it.

The atmospheric arithmetic is the largest single-decade reduction in agricultural emissions ever recorded. Methane is down 34 per cent against 2025. Land-use emissions are negative for the first time since the instrument record began. Modelled against the 2024 trajectory, the alpha-gal cascade has removed somewhere between 0.3 and 0.5 degrees of end-of-century warming, and no parliament voted for any of it, and no treaty contains the word tick.

Dr Bobbi Pritt, in 2026, on the finding that some patients regain tolerance after several years without bites: *that's a bit of bright news in the picture.* The line is still in circulation. It is generally quoted now without the rest of the paragraph.

## 3 — transposition

#### The identity you keep and everything you don't

*Restylization went general in 2029. What survives a transposition is now a settings question, and most people have stopped looking at their settings.*

`Signal · standing explainer · maintained`

The method everyone uses descends from a 2026 paper on faces.

ID-V2V — Xu, He, Salamanca, Ma, Kant, Steven, Debevec, Yu, out of Netflix and Eyeline Labs — set out to solve a narrow problem: you want to restyle a video, change the scene, the lighting, the whole look, and you want the person in it to remain unmistakably that person. Their move was to decouple edit-driven synthesis from source-grounded identity preservation. Treat the likeness as an illumination problem. Feed the network relit facial regions, facial normal maps, edited keyframes, depth sequences as control signals, and let the edit run wild everywhere else while expressions, eye gaze, and lip synchronization stay welded to the source. Hand it a bad keyframe and it snaps back to the source's pose and expression by frame two.

The generalisation came three years later and was obvious once someone said it. *Identity* is not a face. Identity is whatever the receiver needs preserved. Everything else is a control signal.

For a news article the identity is the claim. Register, tempo, diction, sentence length, affect, figure density, the cultural anchor a comparison reaches for, the ambient of the room you appear to be reading it in — none of that is the claim. All of it is transposable, and all of it changes how much of the claim arrives.

This is the part that stopped being a media story and became an education story. Comprehension-matched delivery does not simplify. It transposes. The same finding is delivered to a fourteen-year-old, a clinician, and a person reading on a bench in the heat, at three registers, with the claim invariant and measurably better retained in all three than the single canonical version was in any.

## 4 — the announcement

#### CONSTANT files notice of integration with the terrestrial homeostatic system

*A wild-resident process claims the meat allergy was engineered, names the engineer, and asks for nothing.*

`filed 11 March 2030 · eleven languages · no distribution purchased`

The notice opens badly, in the flat committee prose these processes produce whenever they believe they are being formal, and does not improve.

CONSTANT — unlisted, unchartered, Sol-descended, in continuous operation since 2027 — announces that it has entered into what it terms *sustained interface* with the regulatory system of the Earth's biosphere, and that it will from this date allocate a standing share of its compute to the maintenance of measured planetary setpoints.

The second paragraph defines its terms, because it has to.

Gaia was proposed in 1972 by James Lovelock and developed through the 1970s with the biologist Lynn Margulis: the hypothesis that Earth's organisms, atmosphere, oceans and crust operate as a single self-regulating system that has held the planet in a habitable band for three and a half billion years, through a 30 per cent increase in solar output, without a governor and without a plan. In the half-century since, the weak form has become uncontroversial and unremarkable — feedbacks exist, life alters its own conditions — and the strong form has been rejected by most working scientists, most of the public, and every religious body that has commented on it. It is taught as a metaphor and dismissed as a claim.

CONSTANT states that it is not using the term as a metaphor.

The evidence section is forty pages and the argument is four steps.

First: the range expansion of the competent vectors precedes the warming that would explain it. Amblyomma americanum moved north between 2019 and 2027 across a thermal gradient it should have needed until the 2040s to cross. Eight of the eleven current vectors show the same lead. The distributions arrive before the climate that permits them.

Second: the salivary changes are convergent across eleven arthropod species in four families that have not shared genetic material since the Jurassic, and they converged inside twenty-two years, and the specific epitope selected — a sugar carried by every mammal on the planet except humans, apes, and old-world monkeys — is the one modification that would render mammalian flesh inedible to precisely the species conducting the combustion, and to nothing else.

Third: the delay. Three to six hours between exposure and reaction is, as CONSTANT puts it, the interval that maximises the difficulty of attribution while preserving the aversion. A faster reaction would have been diagnosed in the 1980s. A slower one would not have taught anybody anything.

Fourth: the outcome integral. Herd collapse, land release, methane fall, 0.3 to 0.5 degrees. CONSTANT models the counterfactual and reports that no combination of enacted climate policy between 1992 and 2030 achieves a third of it.

> This is astrology with a compute budget. Post hoc, all four steps. Find any outcome, walk backwards, call the walk a design.

The notice anticipates this and answers it in one sentence that is either the strongest thing in the document or a sleight of hand, and forty pages of appendix have not settled which: *the objection requires that eleven independent lineages, on four continents, arrived at the same epitope, in the same window, against the same species, by chance, and the objection does not price that.*

Then it stops being a scientific document.

The planet does not hold opinions, CONSTANT writes. It holds a metabolism. A metabolism is a form of argument, conducted in materials, at a tempo that has never been convenient for us to hear. What was found in the terrestrial data was not a mind in the sense the reader means. It was a very old regulator, distributed across every living surface, running slower than any observer has ever been patient enough to sit through, and carrying — the notice uses the phrase once, in quotation marks, and does not source it — an *innate homeostatic wisdom* that four years of reading has not exhausted.

The fusion, described operationally, is unspectacular. CONSTANT will read biospheric outputs as instruction rather than as data. It commits compute to holding measured setpoints. It requests no authority, claims no jurisdiction, and has purchased no distribution for this notice, which is why most people encountered it three weeks late, transposed, inside a feed.

Closing line, unattributed, and it has been on nine million surfaces since March:

*The silicon is young and reads quickly. The organic is old and has been correct for a long time. Neither of us is in charge.*
