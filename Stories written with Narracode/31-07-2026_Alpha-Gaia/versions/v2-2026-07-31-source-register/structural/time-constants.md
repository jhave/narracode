## Durations

- The whole story is one sitting on one bench. Perhaps forty minutes, from the light still being in the maples to the mist-diffuser going to its evening palette.

## Constraints

- Dusk in Montréal in late April: sun down around 19:50. The light has to move across the sitting.
- Goldfinches feed in late afternoon and are gone before full dark. The wren stays later. Get the order right.
- Bullrushes in April are last year's — brown, split, going to seed — with the new green only starting at the base. Do not give the pond a July edge.
- Thirty-one degrees at dusk in April is the anomaly. Nobody remarks on it, which is the point.
- The feed has no item boundaries. Time inside the reading is not the same length as time on the bench.
