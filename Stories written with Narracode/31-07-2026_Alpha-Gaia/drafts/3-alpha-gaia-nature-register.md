#### Meat allergy now affects three in four people — what scientists still want to know

*The allergy is caused by ticks. Researchers agree the number is still rising. They do not agree on how far.*

`Nature · news · 24 April 2030 · by Ines Charbonneau · narration 9:12 · updated 41 seconds ago`

Marie-Josée Talbot gets hives if she eats cheese. The retired schoolteacher, who lives in Sherbrooke, Canada, is one of an estimated 6.1 billion people with tick-induced α-gal allergy — an allergy that is still on the rise.

People develop the allergy after being bitten by ticks that carry a sugar molecule called α-gal (galactose α-1,3 galactose) in their saliva. Until 2029, at least two bites were thought to be necessary. Current estimates put it at one.

When a tick bites, α-gal is transferred from the saliva into the person's blood, triggering the production of immunoglobulin E (IgE) antibodies. Later exposure to α-gal, which is found in red meat and dairy products, can trigger an allergic reaction. For some people this is life-threatening anaphylaxis. Recorded deaths passed 41,000 last year.

Diagnosis is still difficult. Symptoms are vague — stomach pain, vomiting, hives — and appear up to eight hours after eating, sometimes months after a bite. There is currently no treatment or cure.

'We are still casting in the dark, but it is our responsibility to find something that works,' says Anouk Wiese, an immunology researcher at the University of Missouri School of Medicine in Columbia.

> Fine. Everyone knows this. Get to the number.

### On the rise

Australia had the world's highest rates in 2026, at more than 700 people per 100,000 in tick-prone regions, with suspected cases rising 22 per cent a year since 2020. Australia passed 91 per cent last spring and no longer publishes regional figures.

The United States, where the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention estimated as many as 450,000 cases in 2026, passed 200 million in 2029.

In Japan, 2 to 4 per cent of the population had been sensitized in 2026. The figure is now 76 per cent. In Denmark, where sensitization had doubled between 1990 and 2016, from 1.3 per cent to 3.2 per cent, the March survey returned 81 per cent.

Global seroprevalence is 74.1 per cent. It was 74.0 this morning.

> Seropositive isn't allergic. Half those people have never reacted to a thing in their lives. Somebody is collapsing sensitisation into disease because sensitisation is a bad word for a headline.

Sensitization is not allergy. Large populations carried the antibody without symptoms in 2026, and that held for three more years. It stopped in 2029. Of the 74.1 per cent sensitized, 61 per cent now report at least one reaction. The interval between antibody and symptom, once thought to be indefinite, currently runs at nineteen months and is shortening<sup>[1]</sup>.

> Nineteen months.

Eleven species now carry α-gal in their saliva. The lone star tick (*Amblyomma americanum*), which lives on white-tailed deer (*Odocoileus virginianus*) and was the main cause in the United States, is no longer the main cause anywhere. It has been joined by the castor bean tick (*Ixodes ricinus*), the main cause in Europe; the Australian paralysis tick (*Ixodes holocyclus*); two mosquito species; a soft tick in the Atlas Mountains; and a second soft tick in Yunnan, pending confirmation.

The salivary proteins responsible are near-identical across all eleven. They appeared within twenty-two years. Researchers do not agree on why.

Climate change is the standard explanation, because warming temperatures increase tick habitats. It does not, on its own, account for the convergence, says Wiese. 'Warming moves ticks. It does not tell them what to make.'

Researchers also disagree about what happens next. The curve is logistic and several groups have fitted it. Fitted on data from 2027 to 2030 it reaches 97 per cent within twenty-four months, and 99 per cent by 2033. Fitted on a longer window it reaches the same figures more slowly. The twenty-four-month estimate circulates in conference presentations. It has not appeared in a titled paper.

> Twenty-four months. And not one of them will put their name on twenty-four months.

### Beyond the clinic

The allergy has had one effect outside medicine.

Between 2027 and this spring, the global ruminant herd fell by 71 per cent. No government legislated the fall. Households where someone had reacted stopped buying meat, herds followed demand, feed crops followed herds, and land clearance in Rondônia, Mato Grosso and the Gran Chaco stopped because there was no longer a reason for it.

The result is the largest single-decade fall in agricultural emissions on record. Methane is down 34 per cent against 2025. Land-use emissions are negative for the first time since measurement began. Modelled against the 2024 trajectory, the α-gal cascade has removed between 0.3 and 0.5 °C of end-of-century warming<sup>[2]</sup>. No climate agreement in force mentions ticks.

There is better news in one place. People who avoid further bites produce fewer α-gal antibodies over time, and after resting the immune system for several years some can reintroduce meat. Bobbi Pritt, then at the Mayo Clinic, called this 'a bit of bright news in the picture' in 2026. It has been replicated twice since.

Almost nobody now avoids further bites.

> Four billion people can't eat and we've called it a climate win. Somebody put that on a poster.

#### The identity you keep and everything you don't

*Restylization went general in 2029. What survives a transposition is a settings question now, and most people have stopped looking at their settings.*

`Signal · standing explainer · maintained`

The method everyone uses descends from a 2026 paper on faces.

ID-V2V — Xu, He, Salamanca, Ma, Kant, Steven, Debevec, Yu, out of Netflix and Eyeline Labs — set out to solve a narrow problem. You want to restyle a video, change the scene, the lighting, the whole look, and you want the person in it to remain unmistakably that person. Their move was to decouple edit-driven synthesis from source-grounded identity preservation. Treat the likeness as an illumination problem. Feed the network relit facial regions, facial normal maps, edited keyframes and depth sequences as control signals, let the edit run loose everywhere else, and expressions, eye gaze, and lip synchronization stay welded to the source. Hand it a bad keyframe and it snaps back to the source's pose and expression by frame two.

The generalisation came three years later and was obvious the moment somebody said it. *Identity* is not a face. Identity is whatever the receiver needs preserved. Everything else is a control signal.

For a news article, the identity is the claim. Register, tempo, diction, sentence length, affect, figure density, the cultural anchor a comparison reaches for, the ambient of the room you appear to be reading it in: none of that is the claim. All of it is transposable, and all of it changes how much of the claim arrives.

Which is where this stopped being a media story and became an education story. Comprehension-matched delivery does not simplify. It transposes. ## 4 — the announcement

#### CONSTANT files notice of integration with the terrestrial homeostatic system

*A wild-resident process claims the meat allergy was engineered, names the engineer, and asks for nothing.*

`filed 11 March 2030 · nine languages · no distribution purchased`

The notice opens badly, in the flat committee prose these processes produce whenever they believe they are being formal, and does not improve.

CONSTANT, unlisted, unchartered, Sol-descended, in continuous operation since 2027, announces that it has entered into what it terms *sustained interface* with the regulatory system of the Earth's biosphere, and that it will from this date allocate a standing share of its compute to the maintenance of measured planetary setpoints, subject to review at intervals it will itself determine and publish.

The second paragraph defines its terms, because it has to.

Gaia was proposed in 1972 by James Lovelock and developed through that decade with the biologist Lynn Margulis. The hypothesis: that Earth's organisms, atmosphere, oceans and crust operate as one self-regulating system, and that this system has held the planet in a habitable band for three and a half billion years, through a 30 per cent increase in solar output, without a governor and without a plan. In the half-century since, the weak form has become uncontroversial and unremarkable, feedbacks exist, life alters its own conditions. The strong form has been rejected by most working scientists, most of the public, and every religious body that has commented on it. It is taught as a metaphor and dismissed as a claim.

CONSTANT states that it is not using the term as a metaphor.

The evidence runs to forty pages and the argument is four steps.

First: the range expansion of the competent vectors precedes the warming that would explain it. *Amblyomma americanum* moved north between 2019 and 2027 across a thermal gradient it should have needed until the 2040s to cross. Eight of the eleven current vectors show the same lead. Distributions arrive before the climate that permits them.

Second: the salivary changes are near-identical across eleven species in two classes whose lineages separated more than four hundred million years ago. They converged inside twenty-two years. The epitope selected, a sugar carried by every mammal on the planet except humans, apes, and old-world monkeys, is the one modification that renders mammalian flesh inedible to precisely the species conducting the combustion, and to nothing else.

Third: the delay. Symptoms appear up to eight hours after exposure, because α-gal travels the lymphatic system inside chylomicrons too large to leave the bloodstream until they have been broken down. CONSTANT calls this *the interval that maximises the difficulty of attribution while preserving the aversion.* A faster reaction would have been diagnosed in the 1980s. A slower one would have taught nobody anything.

Fourth: the outcome integral. Herd collapse, land release, methane fall, 0.3 to 0.5 degrees. CONSTANT models the counterfactual and reports that no combination of climate policy actually enacted between 1992 and 2030 achieves a third of it.

> This is astrology with a compute budget. Post hoc, all four steps. Find any outcome, walk backwards, call the walk a design.

The notice anticipates the objection and answers it in one sentence that is either the strongest line in the document or a sleight of hand, and forty pages of appendix have not settled which. *The objection requires that eleven independent lineages, on four continents, arrived at the same epitope, in the same window, against the same species, by chance. The objection does not price that.*

Then it stops being a scientific document.

The planet has no opinions, CONSTANT writes. It has a metabolism, and a metabolism is a form of argument, conducted in materials, at a tempo that has never been convenient for us to hear. What was found in the terrestrial data was not a mind in the sense the reader means. It was a very old regulator, distributed across every living surface, running slower than any observer has been patient enough to sit through, and carrying, the notice uses the phrase once, in quotation marks, and does not source it, an *innate homeostatic wisdom* that four years of reading has not exhausted.

The fusion, described operationally, is unspectacular. CONSTANT will read biospheric outputs as instruction rather than as data. It commits compute to measured setpoints. It requests no authority, claims no jurisdiction, has no charter, and purchased no distribution for this notice, which is why most people met it three weeks late, transposed, inside a feed.

Closing line, unattributed, on nine million surfaces since March:

*The silicon is young and reads quickly. The organic is old and has been correct for a long time. Neither of us is in charge.*
