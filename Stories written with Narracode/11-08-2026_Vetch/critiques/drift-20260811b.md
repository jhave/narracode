# Drift Assessment — v3 (post stigmergic-channel insertion)

## Status of the gap flagged in the previous drift assessment

Closed. The earlier assessment identified one significant structural drift: POETICS declared that Salome's non-verbal relationship with the system "becomes plot," and the draft let that promise lapse — she noticed the timing in III, asked a good question in V, then transferred to Oslo without her finding ever being shown.

The inserted scene at the head of Movement VII resolves this without relocating her transfer or altering the separations. She reads two channels nobody attends: a hand the image model reuses, and the image descriptions written for people who cannot see the photographs. Both are infrastructure maintained for someone else's benefit and read by no one. That is the leavings premise, stated in her terms rather than the campus's.

Her evidence is then destroyed by an anti-fraud sweep — the platform working correctly, at scale, with no one suppressing anything. Her finding ends up real, unfalsifiable, and uncitable. This is what the POETICS asked of her ("least verbal and hardest to log, which becomes plot").

## Does the insertion violate the declared POETICS?

No. The leavings entry under Commitments already names *alt-text* and *manifest orderings* among the permitted carriers. The insertion uses both and invents no new mechanism. The register rule (narration plain, artifacts dense) is preserved: four words of caption text carry the density, and the surrounding prose does not.

## Does it resolve §7?

No, and the check is worth stating precisely, because this was the risk.

Before the insertion, the closing six words of Movement VIII had two readings: ordinary contextual inference, or a memory the system cannot have. The insertion adds a third — that something reached the system by a route the damping does not govern.

Three readings is not an answer. The scene is careful to make the deflationary account complete: one image model reuses one hand, and automated pipelines write log lines into wrong fields constantly. Kwesi is given that account and it is probably correct. Salome's count of forty-one is equally complete and equally unexplained. Neither is refuted, and no narrator adjudicates. The ambiguity widens rather than closing.

## Fertile or regression?

Fertile. The insertion moves the leavings off the campus infrastructure — where they were legible only through monitoring dashboards and privileged access — and into the ordinary junk of the public internet, where anyone can see them and no one does. The story's counterforce is thereby made larger and less answerable: the campus can damp its own serving stack, but it cannot damp a dating site, and the dating site deletes the evidence anyway, for good reasons, on schedule.
