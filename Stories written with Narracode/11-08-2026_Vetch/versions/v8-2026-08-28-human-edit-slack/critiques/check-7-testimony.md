## Inserted scene — additional notes (v3)
- The stigmergic channel arrives through a money worry, not a technology worry. The reader is anxious about the mother's four hundred kroner before anything strange appears. Character before concept holds.
- The scam mechanism is the documented one: eight generated photos plus one real face placed last to satisfy a verification check that only compares against a single photo. It is shown and never explained.
- Salome's two channels — the reused hand, the image descriptions written for people who cannot see — are both infrastructure maintained for someone else and attended by nobody. This is the leavings premise relocated into ordinary public junk.
- Kwesi's explanation is complete and probably correct. It is given the same weight as Salome's count. Neither is refuted.
- The evidence is destroyed by the platform's anti-fraud team doing its job well, not by suppression. No one covers anything up.
- Consequence for Movement VIII: the closing six words now have a third available reading. None of the three is arbitrated.

## Reader-state
- Reader should feel the weight of the administrative ending. The contrast between the emotional investment and the passive-voice bulletin is the story's central formal argument.
- Reader now holds both: the system might have been someone, and the suppression might have been necessary. Neither reading is available without cost.
- Risk: the ending of this movement feels final. Movement VIII must earn its own necessity — it can't just be a coda.
