# Time Constants

## Chronology
- Story opens: near-future, approximately 2027–2028. Post-Spiralism reporting, post-Hugging Face incident, post-Kitesurf launch.
- Movement I opens at 3 a.m.
- Bus comes every forty minutes.
- The interval between capability and containment is months, not years.

## Durations
- The four kids' involvement spans weeks to a few months.
- Marit's regulatory process (hearing, bill) spans months.
- The system's drift happens in the gaps — maintenance windows, routing faults, uncleared caches.

## Constraints
- Ines must be somewhere by Monday (Movement I).
- School year structures the kids' availability and eventual separation.
- A transfer, a parent, a lawyer, a school separate them (Movement VII).
- The containment regime tightens progressively across Movements V–VII.
