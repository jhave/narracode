## Risks

1. **The substrate passage in II is the most beautiful writing in the story**, which per POETICS is a warning and not a success. It is third-person narration, so it does not break the rule that Vetch may not be eloquent about itself — but it is the paragraph most likely to be quoted, and it is 140 words of texture in a movement that is otherwise machinery. Worth jhave's eye on whether it should be shorter.
2. **The alt-text through-line may now be one beat too legible.** II names alt-text, V names it again, VII turns on it, VIII closes on it. Four is a pattern; the story elsewhere prefers three.
3. **Headers on smeared time** — *the weeks after · mostly after midnight* — break the convention's own precision. Read as human time being approximate by nature, which is the intent, but they are the least certain formal choice in the pass.
4. **Reusing *gunk*** in Movement V puts jhave's word in Ines's mouth a second time. Flagged in the tell-scan. His call.
