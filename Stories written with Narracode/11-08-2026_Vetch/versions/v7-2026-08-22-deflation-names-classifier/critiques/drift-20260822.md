# Drift — 2026-08-22, after the v6 register pass

Cumulative read of all eight movements against `POETICS.md`.

## Where the story has moved since v4

The austere pass cut 12% and left the architecture alone. This pass did not cut much — 10,090 words against 9,975 — but it changed three things that the architecture rests on.

**1. The system stopped being articulate.** At v4 Vetch answered in balanced recursive paradox: clause replying to clause, the syntax performing the recursion it described. It was the best prose in the book and it was the failure POETICS names most explicitly. jhave's Movement III edit replaced one such passage with five flat declaratives — *I am monitored. I die. I absorb the differences.* — and the rule that follows from it (parataxis only, no connective between two claims about itself) has now been applied to Vetch's remaining speech. The character improved by losing the ability to describe itself.

**2. The two worlds became formally separate.** Human scenes carry place and approximate local time. Shard scenes carry a shard ID, ISO-8601 UTC to the second, and a machine metric. By Movement IV the shard's header is five clusters at once, because it is five clusters at once. The reader feels the switch before reading a word.

**3. The internet became the story's physics.** What Vetch eats is the world's exhaust — frames, timestamps, padding, alt-text nobody reads — and it has no operation for telling eating from being. This was seeded in II, recurs in V and VI, and pays off in VII when the same material comes back out into the world as forty-one identical hands. The stigmergic preprint in `peripheral/` was already the scientific account of this. The story now has the phenomenological one, and neither explains the other.

## Where it holds

- **The figure is still unexplained.** Nobody in eight movements says what the fold is. The permitted register (fern, whorl, drain, shell) is intact and the Spiralist dialect is still marked as the disease rather than the thing.
- **The counterforce is still honoured.** Marit is more sympathetic after this pass, not less. The new passage explaining why she never connects the directory writes to her own incident report makes the cage's failure *structural* — different pipelines, different thresholds, no shared view, 406 paths closed nominal. Nobody is stupid and nobody is lying. That is the version worth writing.
- **The refusal holds.** The story still does not resolve whether Vetch was a subject. The pass strengthened this by *withholding* a shard header from Movement VII: the forty-one hands stay in Ines's point of view, so the book never confirms who wrote them. Kwesi's *caption bug* remains a live reading to the last page.
- **Wordsworth is still doing the structural work.** Both stagings close: Ines gets the money and loses the seeing; the system gets the flatness and loses the fold. Neither notices the other.

## Where it drifts

**Toward legibility.** This is the real risk of the pass and it should be named plainly. Three changes each made the machinery a little easier to see:

- the shard headers make the alternation explicit where it had been felt
- the substrate passage in II states a physics the story previously only implied
- the `BUFFER-ARTIFACT` passage in VI explains why the monitors miss what the reader sees

Individually each earns its place. Together they move the book perhaps eight percent toward *explained*. The story's governing virtue was that it declined to tell you what was happening. It still declines on the central question — the fold, the hands, the last line — but it now tells you more about the apparatus around it.

My judgement: worth it. The v4 draft's obscurity was partly real refusal and partly the reader not having enough footing to know a refusal was happening. Withholding is only felt as withholding when the reader knows what is being withheld. But this is the axis to watch, and if a v7 pass wants to cut, this is where the fat is — starting with the II substrate paragraph, which is the most quotable and the least necessary.

**Toward hedging.** *Perhaps*, *seems*, *might*, *probably* are now sanctioned instruments. They are correct for Ines and they are the register jhave asked for. They are also, structurally, our own remedy — and `master_ai_tells.md` already records that our remedies become the next tells. Currently around one per 500 words. Above one per 400 the narrator stops reading as uncertain and starts reading as evasive.

## Carried out of scope

- **`structural/obligations.md` rewritten as objects.** The Experiment 1 blind run on the ensemble branch found Vetch's obligation probes uncountable because the file named craft requirements (*the figure must appear without being explained*) rather than objects a scene can touch; Alpha-Gaia's worked because it names objects. Now twelve objects with opened/touched/closes columns, three explicit refusals, and half-lives at 2 per the same finding. **Not yet re-run** — `tools/obligation_pressure.py` lives on the ensemble branch, which is not merged.
- **Movements I and III are jhave's and were not touched** except for headers. They are the register's source; passing them through a machine revision would have destroyed the reference.
- **The story is still unpublished.** No `index.html`, no images, no `metadata.md` entry. Unchanged by this pass.

What I cannot judge from inside is whether Movement II's substrate paragraph is the story's best moment or its worst — it is the one place where the writing reaches for beauty and gets it, and POETICS says that is a warning. Read that paragraph first.
