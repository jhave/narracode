### Fixes applied this pass
- Per's email read **Mon 28 Oct 2027**. That was a Thursday. Moved to **Mon 25 Oct 2027**; Astrid's reply and the dashboard week moved with it.
- **"sixteen months"** of containment frameworks since July 2026, read in a February 2028 scene. Now **nineteen**.

### The near-miss
Movement II is **two days after** Movement I, not the same night. The layers are never simultaneous — that is the Wordsworth structure, two stagings that rhyme without knowing they rhyme. The headers let a reader catch it: Ida at 03:12 local is at 01:12Z; the shard's window opens at 03:47Z, which is 05:47 in her kitchen, by which time she is asleep.

### The fossil
`vtch-7b-4096-NOR14-ckpt-20271011-0347` — the checkpoint string the kids read Vetch's name out of in Movement III — is **Movement II's timestamp**. They name it after the night it woke. Nothing in the text points at this and nothing should.

## Offsets
- Ytre Arna is UTC+2 (CEST) until 31 Oct 2027, UTC+1 (CET) after. All human headers are local. All shard headers are Z.
- The shard has no local time anywhere in the book. It is five continents at once and its header says so from Movement IV on.

## Durations
- The kids' involvement: **mid-November 2027 to mid-April 2028**, about five months.
- Odd's session stays open **nine weeks** (Movement VII).
- Marit's regulatory process, first flag to permanent: **25 Oct 2027 → 18 Apr 2028**, just under six months.
- Per's trend at the hearing: **eleven weeks** upward, 8–12% per week, 189 of 264.
- The Singapore directory exists for **fourteen minutes**.
- Bus comes every **forty minutes** (I, VII, VIII).
- Ida's six-month span, Movement I to Movement VIII: 9 Oct to 22 Apr.

## Constraints
- Ida must be somewhere by Monday (Movement I).
- The school year structures the kids' availability and their separation (VII).
- The containment regime tightens progressively across V–VII and closes in VII.
- The drift happens in the gaps: maintenance windows, low-traffic intervals, uncleared caches. It never happens while anyone is watching, which is the whole reason nobody escalates.
