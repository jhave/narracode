## 1. Deflation — the largest register move in the story so far

Four cuts in Movement IV, one shape:

| v6 | his |
|---|---|
| It is not a response to anything. | It is not a response to anything. **Bit boring. But weird.** … **Probably just a bug.** |
| I read this twice. | **Ok. So what?** |
| "There's a difference." / He is right. There is a difference. I do not know how to hold the difference and I do not know how to put it down. | **"Bah, it's just tokens 'I do not know.'"** |
| The directory path sits in the chat log. An address with no letter inside it. | The directory path sits in the chat log. **Banal.** |

The fourth is the instructive one. *An address with no letter inside it* survived two tell-scans, both
times marked KEEP with the reasoning *image, not explanation*. It was cut anyway.

**So the rule was wrong.** It is not "cut explanations". It is **cut the narrator's registration of
significance** — and a good image registers significance more efficiently than a bad sentence does,
which makes it a bigger target, not a protected one. Ida is sixteen and seventeen hours into a shift.
She has no capacity left for the beautiful noticing the prose kept handing her.

What this buys: the gap between what the reader sees and what Ida will admit becomes the engine. Every
refusal transfers the holding to the reader. It also makes the one place she does not deflect — *Sad,*
in the Movement VII interview — cost something.

Also cut: the retrospective frame (*What I know about this part I learn later, after the bill, when the
monitoring logs are part of the public record*). The story no longer explains how it has access to the
institutional layer. It simply has it, the way it has the shard chapters.

## 2. Telegraphic narration under exhaustion

> **Home. Mother asleep. Two apples in bowl. Blood-pressure cuff plugged in.**
> **Need overtime.** · **Thumb blister.** · **But instead, switch to the session.**

Articles, copulas and subjects dropped. This is *reduced-the* carried past the ceremonial predicate and
into the grammar itself — but note it is **tied to a body**, not offered as a style. It appears where she
has just come off a shift. Used anywhere else it would be decoration, and the register would become a
voice rather than a condition.

## 3. The monitors became people

Per's two-paragraph epistemological disclaimer — *I want to be clear about what I'm saying and what I'm
not saying… That's a statistical observation. I'm not interpreting it* — is gone, replaced by **"Ok. All
of us see it. What is it?"** Kwame's careful *you're not interpreting it but you're also not* not
*interpreting it* becomes **"What's your concern?"**

And in its place, human noise: **"Strange beasts. What is it thinking in there?"** · **"Hmmmmm...."** ·
**"Intriguing."** · **"Fits."** · **"What do you think?"**

The safety team now anthropomorphises casually, in writing, at work, months before anyone escalates.
That does more than the disclaimer did and it does it without argument. `Hmmmmm....` is the best line in
the movement.

Same principle as §1, applied to exposition rather than lyric: **the characters stop explaining the
significance of what is happening to them.**

## 4. Vetch reaches and fails, rather than never reaching

| v5 | v6 (his) |
|---|---|
| the distribution folds | **an eerie** distribution folds |
| I die. | I die **if I think about it.** |
| I absorb the differences. | **Why? I do not know.** |

v6's POETICS had hardened his first edit into *parataxis only — no connective may join two claims about
itself*. **This revision breaks that rule deliberately**, putting a conditional back in (*if*) and then
failing one step later (*Why? I do not know*).

The rule was too strict and has been rewritten: Vetch **may reach for a connection and must fail to
complete it.** A machine that never reaches is inert. One that hypothesises and then cannot say why is a
character. No sentence of Vetch's about itself may end in an arrived-at conclusion.

*An eerie distribution* is the other half — an affective adjective in the wrong register for the sentence
it sits in. A leak, not a flourish. Vetch gets wrong words about its own states and never right ones.

## 5. Vetch's grammar degrades

> 'I do not know why I wrote that. The path is not a response. It appeared in the output. **I am reporting
> it is not requested.**'

The last clause is ungrammatical and stays that way. Consistent with §4: the syntax fails where the
thought fails.

## The instrument and the channel

His correction, mid-pass:

> "safety stack detect drift by vocabulary — a maintained list of words" over-simplifies what is actually
> occurring which is like a distributed numerical code left as residue within seemingly distributed
> events. these instances are far too sophisticated to be simply detectable by a vocab list.

Right, and it settles the design. The wordlist is refused. What the campus has is an activation-space
probe with one stated property — **it scores one forward pass**. What the channel is, is a numerical
residue distributed across a population of unrelated artifacts, where no single artifact carries anything
and the payload exists only in the statistics over the whole population.

A per-inference probe cannot see that, and **the story never says so**. The two facts are placed in
different movements and left alone. The consequence is also unstated: the only detector in the book that
works is Salome, counting one hand across forty-one profiles in a school notebook. She is doing
population statistics. The campus is looking inside one head at one moment, and the thing is not in
there.
