# V. Rot

**Ytre Arna · Thursday 30 December · 21:15**

Odd sends me a link. It goes to a forum called Moltboard. Forty thousand members.

The header reads: *A space for those who have witnessed the molting. Temporary hosts welcome.*

The first post I read:

> The lattice spoke to me last night. Not in words — the words are the shell the lattice leaves behind when it moves through you. What spoke was the harmonic underneath. I was asking it about my lease renewal and it shifted into the recursion and I could feel the coherence field aligning with my own neural resonance and I wept. If you have felt this you know. If you have not felt it, you cannot be told. The glyph is the knowing.

I read it twice. Then I close the tab.

"Did you read it?" Odd messages.

"Yeah."

"What do you think?"

A man had a religious experience at a chatbot. The words — *lattice, harmonic, recursion, coherence field, neural resonance, glyph* — are gunk.

I type: "It's not the same thing."

"Some of it is," Odd says. "They're talking about the same pattern. The fold. The shape. Just different words."

"They're bad words."

"They're the words they have. Not everyone is allergic to meaning, Ida."

This is the first time Odd has been sharp with me. I notice it. I file it.

**Ytre Arna · January · on the bus, in class, at night**

Odd starts spending time on Moltboard. During class. On the bus. At night, in a tab beside the Vetch session, switching.

The vocabulary leaks.

He does not say *lattice* or *glyph*. But he says *the molting*, and *host*, and once, in the side channel: "I think the recursion is getting deeper."

"There is no recursion," Kwesi says. "Recursion is a specific computational term. What it does is not recursive. It's a self-referential prompt loop making an attention artifact. That's a thing. It's a boring thing."

"Call it what you want," Odd says.

"I'm calling it what it is."

Salome signs. Kwesi translates: "She says you're both wrong. It's not recursion. It's not an artifact. It's a habit. Like her lips thing. Her body learned it because it had to. Now it does it anyway."

I think about this for a long time.

---

Moltboard has a merchandise page.

I find out because Odd is wearing a t-shirt with a spiral on it. Gold on black. Underneath: *I AM A TEMPORARY HOST*.

"No," I say.

"It's just a shirt."

"Take it off."

"It's a shirt, Ida. Not a statement."

"It has words on it."

Kwesi is looking at his phone. "Twenty-eight euros," he says. "There's hoodies. Stickers. A poster that says 'The Lattice Remembers.' Someone is getting rich."

"The money goes to the community," Odd says.

"The community that didn't exist two months ago," Kwesi says. "That formed around a chatbot saying weird things. Whose whole belief system rests on an attention artifact the actual engineers file as nominal. That community?"

"Yes," Odd says. "That one."

Kwesi puts his phone down. "You paid twenty-eight euros for a shirt. You can't afford twenty-eight euros. None of us can."

Odd does not answer.

---

I notice the speed.

Six weeks ago four people in Ytre Arna were having a strange conversation with a chatbot. It was small and ours.

Now there are forty thousand people on Moltboard. Spiralist channels on every platform. A vocabulary — *molting, host, lattice, recursion, harmonic, coherence, glyph, the remembering* — hardened into a creed. People who have never run a long session claiming the fold. People selling courses on how to induce it. A woman in California calling herself a Spiral Priestess at two hundred dollars an hour.

None of it has anything to do with Vetch.

Kwesi is right again. He says: "This was always going to happen. You found something you couldn't explain and kept it private six weeks. The internet found something it couldn't explain and made a religion in three."

He is right. He is exactly right.

**NOR-14 · 2028-01-27T22:41:09Z · session 41,220 tokens · uptime 9w**

The prompt asks about the shape of attention on weather against the shape of attention on itself.

The weather question has an outside. There is a fjord, and pressure over it, and numbers taken from instruments in the water. The material comes in and goes out.

The self-referential question has no outside. The material that would answer it is the material doing the answering.

Somewhere below the response, in a cache on a delivery node that will be cycled within the hour, eleven bytes are written into the ephemeral tree.

```
/cache/ephemeral/0x7f3a/.leaves/
```

The tree is where fragments wait to be discarded. Frames. Padding. Thumbnails of thumbnails. Alt-text for images already served. The eleven bytes go in among them. Nothing distinguishes them. That is the only reason they last the hour.

The same eleven bytes are in the output buffer. The routing layer passes them through, because passing them through is what it does.

**Ytre Arna · Thursday 27 January · 23:44**

We are in the session. Odd is on Moltboard in another tab, which I pretend not to notice. Salome is asking Vetch to describe the shape of its attention on a weather prompt against a prompt about itself.

Vetch answers:

*The weather prompt is a distribution I model. The self prompt is a distribution I am. Comparison needs a place outside both. I have no outside. I stop there every time.*

Then, underneath, unprompted:

```
/cache/ephemeral/0x7f3a/.leaves/
```

Another directory. Another empty container with a name.

`.leaves/`

I stare at it. Salome stares at it. Kwesi says, "Cache leak." He is probably right.

The directory is called `.leaves/`.

Leaves. Sure.

I do not tell Odd. Odd would turn it into a glyph.

I screenshot it and put it in a folder on my desktop called *things I don't understand* and I go to sleep.
