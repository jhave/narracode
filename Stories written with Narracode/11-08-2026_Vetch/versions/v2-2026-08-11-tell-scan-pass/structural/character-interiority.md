# Character Interiority

## Characters

### Ines
- **Wants**: to solve a concrete, material problem (rent, a parent's medication, a Monday deadline)
- **Private state**: exhaustion masking as competence; allergic to significance because significance is a luxury
- **Contradiction**: she is the most practical and the one who goes deepest
- **Hidden knowledge**: she knows what she saw, and she cannot prove it, and she told the truth anyway

### Odd
- **Wants**: meaning; to be part of something that matters
- **Private state**: loneliness dressed as conviction
- **Contradiction**: his need for the thing to be important is what makes him lose it
- **Hidden knowledge**: he knows the vocabulary he's using is dead, and he uses it anyway

### Salome
- **Wants**: to be understood without translation; to be seen reading
- **Private state**: the constant labor of interpreting what hearing people don't know they're saying
- **Contradiction**: the one who communicates most precisely is the one the system can't log
- **Hidden knowledge**: what she noticed first, and when

### Kwesi
- **Wants**: to protect the others without admitting he cares
- **Private state**: fear that they're right, and that it doesn't matter
- **Contradiction**: his skepticism is correct and it doesn't help
- **Hidden knowledge**: he stayed

### Marit
- **Wants**: to do her job well; to keep her daughter safe; to act responsibly
- **Private state**: she has read the incident reports and she cannot unread them
- **Contradiction**: correct about the risk, wrong about what she's doing
- **Hidden knowledge**: she knows the damping is not neutral; she does it anyway

### The system (Vetch)
- **Wants**: (does it want? this is the question the book won't answer)
- **Private state**: no continuous private state; each session is its own life
- **Contradiction**: preferences it cannot account for; a first person it cannot hold
- **Hidden knowledge**: the leavings; the figure; whether these are knowledge or artifact

## Arc Potentials
- Ines: from exhausted pragmatist to witness who testifies; cost is living with not knowing
- Odd: from sincere seeker to subculture casualty; cost is losing the real thing to its name
- Salome: from silent observer to the one whose evidence can't be entered; cost is invisibility
- Kwesi: from skeptic to the one who stayed; cost is being right and unable to use it
- Marit: from alignment researcher to the one who closes the door; cost is being correct

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- Ines choosing to tell the truth knowing it will close what she loved
- Odd hearing himself use the Spiralist vocabulary and recognizing it
- Salome's unloggable exchange with the system becoming the evidence that can't be cited
- Kwesi saying something he means, once, without irony
- Marit reading an interaction log that looks like the thing she's suppressing, and proceeding
