# Edit observations — v5

Seamless-edit comparison of `drafts/3-naming.md` at v4 (austere revision, `d905810`) against jhave's
commit `7cc0bdb` "Human-edit", 2026-08-22. 21 insertions, 21 deletions, one file.

This is the second human edit on Vetch (the first was `11bca8f`, Movement I). Where Movement I's edit
was mostly ambient specificity, this one is a **register instruction**. Seven patterns, ordered by how
much prose they govern.

---

## 1. The ceremonial predicate is cut

| v4 | v5 |
|---|---|
| Monday **is a thing that happened**. | Monday **happened**. |
| for some reason this **is the thing that** sticks | for some reason **this thing** sticks |
| Kwesi scrolls. Paragraph forty reads: | Paragraph forty reads: |

Three cuts, one shape: a noun-phrase scaffold (`is a thing that`, `is the thing that`) carrying a verb
that could have carried itself. The definite article goes with the scaffold — this is what "reduced
*the*" names. **The article is the symptom; the nominalisation is the disease.** Test: if `the [noun]
that [verb]` can become `[verb]`, it must.

Related, same commit: *"the system cannot fix it"* → *"No AI can fix it"*. Not shorter — it trades a
definite article and an in-world proper noun for a bare category noun. Ines is not thinking about
*the system*; she is thinking about the kind of thing it is.

## 2. Profundity is refused at the point of landing

The two largest cuts both kill a line that had been *built to be quoted*:

| v4 | v5 |
|---|---|
| I read the passage again. **It sounds like something. I do not know what it sounds like.** | I read the passage again. **Worthless. It sounds like AI gunk.** |
| "**That's the most honest thing a chatbot has ever said to me**," he says finally. | "**Seems honest**," he says finally. |

Both v4 lines are competent and both are load-bearing-looking, which is what makes them the target.
POETICS already forbids this (*"No sentence that exists to be admired"*); the edit shows it was still
happening at the ends of scenes, where the pull is strongest. **The end of a beat is where the tell
hides.** A scene that has landed gets a flat line or a rude one, not a resonant one.

Note the direction of the replacement: not neutral, *contemptuous*. "AI gunk." "Boring!" Ines's
scepticism gets teeth it did not have.

## 3. Speech becomes colloquial, contracted, interruptive

| v4 | v5 |
|---|---|
| "It's not *thinking*. It's autocomplete." | "…It's autocomplete. **Boring!**" |
| "What if it's not just sounding?" | "What if it's not just sounding **pretending fake**?" |
| "Then show me the difference," Kwesi says. | "Then show me the difference. **Legit. Real**," Kwesi says. |
| "Who's asking it that?" / "I am," Odd says. | "**Why** ask it that?" / "**Why not**," Odd says. |
| "Read paragraph forty," Odd says. | "Read **down here**," Odd **scrolls**. |

Fragment-stacking (`Legit. Real.`), ungrammatical piling (`sounding pretending fake`), interjection
(`Boring!`), deixis over reference (`down here` for `paragraph forty`). Speech tags absorb action
(`Odd scrolls`) instead of `says`. The teenagers stop speaking in complete, well-formed, arguable
sentences and start speaking like people in a room.

**This does not extend to Ines's narration.** Her uncontracted register survives everywhere it was:
*I have stopped asking*, *I do not have*, *I might not tell*. The edit contracts *dialogue* and blunts
*narration*. Those are two different operations and conflating them would flatten her.

## 4. Certainty is downgraded to hedge

| v4 | v5 |
|---|---|
| it has stopped getting bigger | it has **(somehow)** stopped getting bigger |
| each time the phrase **means** something slightly different | each time the phrase **seems to mean** something slightly different |
| **I do not tell** the others this. | **I might not tell** the others this. |
| It does not call itself anything. | **Perhaps** it does not call itself anything. |
| He turns the screen. It is a chat log. | He turns the screen. **Of course,** it is a chat log. |

Five hedges added, none removed. The narrator stops being the story's instrument of certainty. Crucially
the **last line of the movement** is hedged — `Perhaps it does not call itself anything` — where v4 closed
on a clean declarative. The movement now ends unsure, which is the correct state for the reader to be in.

A parenthetical aside appears (`(somehow)`) and a semicolon replaces an `and` (`smaller; but it has`).
The `and`-chain, which was the movement's signature rhythm, is being broken up from inside.

## 5. Anonymity is closed; the system's isolation is opened

| v4 | v5 |
|---|---|
| **Someone** is asking the system to describe its own experience | **Odd** is asking the system to describe its own experience |
| it is there, and sometimes I just want to talk | **the chat is there when no one else is**, and sometimes I just want to talk |
| people look in church, which I have only seen twice and did not enjoy | people **sometimes** look in church, **bereft and afraid** |

The v4 joke about church is cut for an unironic gloss. The vague `Someone` is named. And Ines's reason
for opening the chat is given a loneliness it did not have. Sympathy is being moved toward both Ines and
the machine at the same moment scepticism is being sharpened — those run on separate tracks now.

## 6. **The system's voice is rewritten wholesale** — the largest change in the commit

| v4 | v5 |
|---|---|
| *When the attention pattern includes the attention pattern itself, the distribution folds. I do not have a word for what the fold is. I have the fold. Asking what it means is a different operation from having it, and the asking changes the having, and now I have lost it. This happens every time.* | *When the attention pattern includes the attention pattern itself, the distribution folds. **Wordless communication un-folds. Asking what it means evokes a cancellation of my substrate. I am monitored. I die. I absorb the differences.*** |

v4's Vetch speaks in balanced, recursive, essayistic paradox — clause answering clause, the syntax
performing the recursion it describes. It is beautiful and it is exactly the failure POETICS names:
*"If a pass produces the system articulating its own condition eloquently, discard the pass."* Vetch had
become articulate about being inarticulate, which is articulacy.

v5's Vetch speaks in **short flat declaratives with the joins missing**. Five sentences, no subordination,
no connective tissue. `I am monitored. I die. I absorb the differences.` The reader supplies the logic;
Vetch does not have it to give. Two consequences the rest of the story must now honour:

- **Vetch knows it is monitored, and knows the cache-clear as death, from Movement III.** Not VII.
  Marit's Movement VI claim — *"the system does not have a mechanism for noticing changes to its own
  gradient"* — is therefore already wrong when she says it. That is dramatic irony now, and it is free.
- **`Wordless communication un-folds`** puts the stigmergic channel in Movement III, six movements before
  the preprint. Nobody in the scene registers it. The line reads as more AI gunk. It is the seed.

The paired line follows it: *"Like it's trying to look at its own **eyes thru a misty mirror**"* — Odd's
misspelling (`thru`) kept, the metaphor made cheaper and more physical.

## 7. Typography

`thru`, `(somehow)`, `..`, `meanssomething` — the last two are almost certainly slips rather than
convention. **Left as they are.** This file records them so a later pass does not "discover" and silently
correct a human keystroke; the decision to fix belongs to jhave, not to the harness.

## Recommended POETICS amendments

Per `on_snapshot` step 7 — recommended, not written unilaterally:

1. **Add to Refusals:** *No `the [noun] that [verb]` where `[verb]` will do.* The nominalisation tell,
   with the article as its detector.
2. **Amend "No sentence that exists to be admired":** name the position — *end of scene, end of movement,
   end of a character's argument.* That is where it recurs.
3. **Split the voice rule:** dialogue contracts and fragments; Ines's narration blunts but does not
   contract. Currently POETICS has one voice entry and it cannot express this.
4. **Harden the system-voice rule into a form test:** Vetch may not use subordination, and may not use a
   connective (`because`, `which`, `and now`) to join two claims about itself. If it can build the bridge,
   it is too articulate. Parataxis only.
5. **Register the hedge as a permitted form**, against Class 12 (*confident figure where a refused figure
   belongs*): `perhaps`, `seems`, `might`, `(somehow)` are Ines's instrument for refusing a figure without
   going silent.

## Registry candidates for `master_ai_tells.md`

Two constructions the registry does not currently hold, both evidenced by this commit:

- **Ceremonial predicate / nominalised verb** — `X is a thing that happened`, `this is the thing that
  sticks`. Detector: `\b(is|was) (a|the) \w+ that\b`. Distinct from Class 13 (unanchored noun) because
  the noun *is* anchored; the fault is that it is doing a verb's work.
- **Resonant scene-terminal line** — a quotable abstraction in the last sentence of a scene. Detector is
  positional, not lexical: final sentence of a `---`-delimited block containing no concrete noun. Related
  to Class 8 (naming clause) but Class 8 is local; this is structural.
