# Reference pressures

source: training-data; broad working interpretations, not annotations of supplied passages. No source wording is imported.

Temporal posture: ordinary tasks can expand; a large civilizational change can pass as inconvenient local news. Focal scale: mouth, work surface, the person preparing the next bite. Refusals: no instructive tour or narrator who understands every consequence. Characteristic noticings: how shared work survives failed intimacy; which bodily experiences embarrass a service. Syntactic strain: allow a thought to lengthen because the speaker is resisting its conclusion, not to meet a cadence target. Relational texture: affection and resentment can coexist without a verdict.

Seuss permits delight in articulation; Saint-Exupéry permits emotional accessibility; Vonnegut permits comic scale changes and compromised people. Broad thematic questions of language, dependency and adaptation are the Delany/Butler connection. These permissions are transformed into a new situation rather than an imitation of an individual author.
