## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Create one landscape editorial illustration, approximately 3:2, for the literary story Mouth on Loan. The style is a photographed impossible miniature set, intimate macro lens, tactile practical materials, droll and strange and alive. Camera viewpoint INSIDE an anatomically ambiguous tasting chamber, looking outward at a scratched silver teaspoon carrying a small green pear. The pear has a tiny hinged translucent edible inspection window in its skin, open; inside that window a very small optical inspection lens peers BACK AT THE VIEWER. This reciprocal inspection is the central quietly funny event. The fruit must read clearly as a pear with an actual inspection flap, not an eyeball creature.

Foreground: softly folded translucent cream and muted mauve membranes; a partial tongue-like surface seen in extreme macro with densely textured, glistening rounded taste papillae, more silicone and wet mineral than identifiable human anatomy. Tiny smudged saliva flecks on our lens, one small refracting bubble toward an edge, shallow focus. A fine wet thread from spoon to tasting surface stretches. Some pear juice in that thread seems to become delicate translucent sensory samples, faint repeated droplets, physical data rather than digital pixels.

Add only a FEW tiny murky process-diagram overlays, embedded in the moisture like a badly maintained instrument's annotation: a small three-node loop with arrows, a minute sampling trace, skewed calibration ticks, dotted little curves. These fine pale turquoise and tarnished amber markings bend around droplets, blur beneath saliva, partly absorb into the surface of taste buds, gently glisten in raking light. They occupy perhaps 5 percent of the composition. Make their imperfect physical behavior amusing. No prominent labels, no legible prose, no large HUD, no text title.

Pear-green, tobacco amber, cloudy cream, bruised violet, a little faded turquoise. Warm side light through the fruit, soft depth, detailed imperfect materials, modest photographic grain, beautiful but used equipment. Off-center spoon and pear clearly legible in middle distance; wet foreground makes us feel inconveniently involved. No identifiable person, no lips framing a human face, no realistic dental diagram, no gore, no horror, no grotesque teeth, no cartoon faces, no cute mascot, no neon cyberpunk, no generic binary code or pixel dissolution. One coherent image, no collage, no border, no lettering or watermark.

## Review

The pear and open inspection hatch are legible, the lens looks back toward the reader, and the warm wet chamber avoids an identifiable human face. The fine amber/turquoise process diagrams sit across the foreground papillae and droplets. This first study is more organically mouth-like than silicone-like; retained for the author's review. The overlays are decorative fictional sensing marks, not scientific measurements. No story prose changed in this image pass.
