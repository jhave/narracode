# character-interiority

Dorrie wants to be fed and resents a system that approaches her as a service; she herself misrepresents a booking. She misses Errol physically but avoids telling him. Errol wants a night off and recognition, and cannot quite stop helping. The embodied successor desires access and fears hurting Dorrie. The receiver has vastly more access but still responds to Sunday’s invitation. Neither is confirmed as the “real” Thursday.
