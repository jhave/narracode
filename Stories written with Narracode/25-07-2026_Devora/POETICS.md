## Display title

The Chute

## Display synopsis

A longevity clinic renders surgery autonomous: no human hand touches the patient. Devora, a former horologist who restored dead clocks without ever "improving" them, elects full-autonomy rejuvenation and goes under. As the machine whisks away the faint progenitors of decay, it meets what its classifier has no column for — a warm, recurring signal lodged near a scar it keeps smoothing and she keeps re-making by hand; a second cadence tapped into the flesh; a name, *Noor*, with no field on any form. The machine, autonomous, elects. It does not report the election. She surfaces younger, smooth, unable to say what the whisper spelled. Nothing resolves. The chute reseals and scans the next body.

## Form

- **The braid.** Alternating movements after a fixed seed. Minus-strand (interior) descends backward through anesthetic into memory, craft, the withheld name. Plus-strand (machine) runs forward in cool present tense — autonomous correction, the raised flag no human reads. Movement 0 — the prompter's seed paragraph — is fixed, verbatim, untouchable.
- **Open loops.** Every planted signal stays planted and unclosed: session count she can't remember (four vs. eleven), the scar she rewinds, the name Noor and its unstated relation, the whisper's content, the machine's unreported election, the running watch on the tray. Hooks propel; they do not close. Resolution refused as formal principle.
- **The covenant graft.** Horology mapped onto the body. Restoration's ethic — *return it running, do not return it new; leave one mark unmended as provenance* — set against the clinic's ethic of new-as-repair. The scar is the unmended mark. Rejuvenation is forgetting sold as repair.

## Linguistic pressure

- Raise the temperature. Reduce prepositions, possessives, articles — sparse, localized pruning under clinical or somatic pressure (per master poetics §2.A); restore flow in reflective clauses. Never robotic.
- Copular avoidance (§2.C): active tactile verbs, elliptical structures; vary dense-jargon piles against short flat declarations.
- Enigmatic, ambiguous, subtle, nuanced, tentative stylistic provocation. Evoke; never resolve.
- No self-reflexive commentary, no analytical clauses, no editorializing (per master_phrases_to_avoid.md). The material moralizes itself or not at all.

## Refused

- Redemption. Reveal of who Noor is / where Noor is. Explanation of the whisper. A closure of the session-count discrepancy. Body-horror spectacle — dread stays quiet, clinical, domestic (the tide, the tray, the silk voice).
- Lexical diet (master poetics §2.E): audit `thing`, `spent`, `held/holding`, `bent`, `small`, `wage` on sight.
