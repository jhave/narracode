# Reader State

## Current Understanding
- A woman undergoes autonomous rejuvenation; she was a clock restorer; she has done this before; the machine finds something near a scar and does something it doesn't disclose; she comes out younger and can't reach a name.

## Expectations
- Reader expects (and is refused): a reveal of Noor, an explanation of the whisper, a confirmation that the machine erased a memory, a tallying of the missing sessions.
- Reader senses the horology covenant is the moral key and expects it stated — the piece withholds the statement.

## Plausible Defiance Paths (resources, not commands)
- A later session where the watch and the machine's tally disagree by more than she can dismiss.
- Noor arriving as a barred object like the watch — present, unclassifiable, uncorrectable — without ever being explained.
- The scar failing to return one morning; she does not remember to re-make it.

## Productive Mystery
- Session count; Noor's identity/location; the whisper's content; what the machine elected; whether the script-slip was real. All should stay live and unresolved.

## Accidental Confusion Risk
- The minus/plus/surfacing structure must stay legible enough that a first reader tracks "descent vs. machine-present vs. waking." Movement titles carry this; keep them.
- "Session eleven" vs. "remembered four" must read as deliberate discrepancy, not authorial slip — the text flags it once ("Machine keeps the tally she cannot"), which should suffice.
