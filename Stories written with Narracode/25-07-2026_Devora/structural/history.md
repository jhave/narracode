# History

## Established
- Longevity clinics render surgery fully autonomous; Devora prefers no human operate on her.
- Devora was a horologist; the trade's covenant: return a clock running, never "new"; leave one mark unmended as provenance.
- She has undergone this procedure repeatedly; machine calls it session eleven; she remembers four.
- A scar on her left side keeps being smoothed and keeps returning because she re-makes it.
- A person, Noor, once traced that scar in the dark ("this is how I find you when the lights are gone"); Devora promised to "come back the same."
- The nanobots meet a warm, recurring, non-pathological signal — a second cadence tapped into the tissue — and flag it into a register no human reads.

## Said
- Biofeedback voice: *session eleven … you did beautifully*; and, script slipping (or dreamed): *we returned you new.*
- Screen: *anomaly, non-pathological, persistent*; *decay denied. Progress nominal.*
- Noor: *this is how I find you when the lights are gone.*

## Refused
- The story refuses to state who/where Noor is, what the whisper spells, what the machine elected, or why the count differs. These remain permanently open.
