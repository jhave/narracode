# Character Interiority

## Characters
- **Devora**
  - Desire: to be corrected without being touched by a human hand; to stay young; underneath, to keep one thing unmended (the scar = Noor).
  - Contradiction: a restorer whose ethic forbids "new," electing repeated procedures that return her new. She delegates the count she once kept for others.
  - Shame / avoidance: the missing sessions she does not interrogate; the promise to Noor she cannot keep and does not consciously hold.
  - Hidden knowledge (bodily, not conscious): the scar is deliberate; the whisper is something she once wound into herself.
  - Changing self-understanding: on surfacing, the index to Noor is gone at the flesh level — she loses access without registering loss. Erosion sold as improvement.

- **The machine**
  - Not a character with interiority; rendered as autonomous process with the affect of a tide/river. Its "election" is the story's uncanny — agency without accountability, correction without consent.

## Arc Potentials (potentials, not mandates)
- Devora toward the slow discovery that each rejuvenation subtracts a provenance mark — without ever being permitted to prove it. Keep it tentative; never confirm.
- The covenant (running-not-new) as the buried moral spine, never stated as thesis.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- The single script-slip ("we returned you new") is the nearest thing to catharsis — and it is denied its release: she cannot tell if she dreamed it. Do not upgrade it to a reveal.
