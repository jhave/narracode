## What the prompter added

**Direct speech as ideology naked**
The Director in the AI draft had no direct quotation — only Crane's reported prose. The prompter introduced several direct-speech fragments, volatile and atavistic: *"Speak English, he snapped."* / *"By gawd, he believed in the word of god: pure principles."* / *"MESA scumbag"* / *"woke yahoos"* / *"Commie sympathizers, terrorist advocates, puppets."* The effect: ideology without its customary bureaucratic coating. The Director's register is pre-rhetorical, older, a fury that predates the polished doctrine. The vocabulary is vernacular grievance, not strategic analysis.

**Crane as translator**
The prompter added the parenthetical *"(assessing what english level might succeed)"* after Crane decides how to answer the Director. This establishes a new dynamic in Crane's relationship to the Director: Crane reads the Director the way Crane reads a user in a behavioral loop — calibrating for the receiver's capacity, pitching the argument at the appropriate technical register. The Director is not a peer; he is a target demographic with institutional power. This dynamic opens formal possibilities for all future Director scenes.

**"The blatant act is permissible."**
A single sentence standing as its own paragraph. Declarative. Crane's compressed verdict on what the Director has just described. The structure — brevity as violence — is a new construction not present in the AI draft. A sentence short enough that its content cannot be noticed in motion. The blatant act is permissible not because Crane endorses it loudly but because the sentence form refuses to allow examination.

**The maxim breaks grammatically**
The AI draft had "The admin must not become the user." The prompter changed this to *"Admin must not never becomeuser."* — the maxim loses its article, compresses into a single token, applies a double negative. The double negative enacts the violation: "must not never" contains a buried "must" that inverts the intent. The maxim intended to prevent becomes a maxim that performs the transgression it guards against. The grammatical breakdown is the form of Crane's self-compliance failure.

**Sacrifice enumerated specifically**
The AI draft's sacrifice was implied and abstracted. The prompter made it concrete: *"passport recalled, taxes audited, children expelled, husband fired, family un-insured, and anyone peripheral and vulnerable deported."* The specificity performs the arithmetic of institutional violence. Each item is precise, ordinary-seeming at the level of bureaucratic process, and devastating in accumulation. "Peripheral and vulnerable deported" — not them, but anyone near them. The ripple as instrument.

**Somatic characterization of others**
*"the lacquered wet eye palpitations of an elderly quivering neurotic child"* — Crane's most explicit physical rendering of the Director. The vocabulary: "palpitations" (cardiac-medical), "lacquered wet eye" (taxidermy, preserved, unalive surface), "elderly quivering neurotic child" (combining the terminal with the pre-formed). Crane reads bodies the way Crane reads engagement data — for the underlying mechanism. The Director's body reveals the child underneath the doctrine.

**The gladiatorial three-sentence construction**
*"We made the cage. We invited the crowd. We hired the fighters."* Three declaratives, equal weight, building through the institutional structure of the metaphor. The "we" is collective and non-apologetic: ownership without conscience. The construction is gladiatorial because the speaker is not in the cage. This pattern — the short declarative series as ownership claim — is new and available.

**"toy palimpsest"**
The city observed through the corridor window: *"a toy palimpsest of highways, towers and condensation stacks."* The city is toy (miniaturized, aesthetic, not real to Crane at this remove) and palimpsest (layered text, each layer partially legible through the ones above). Crane sees the world as readable artifact. The precipice is available — the corridor has windows — but the city has been converted into legible text before the view can reach Crane.

## What the prompter removed

- The explicit "Bardo" naming in the body of the scene (the AI draft had Crane name bardo and immediately drop it). In the revised draft this move survives but the word itself is removed — the bardo-condition is enacted without the label.
- "The wall was close, and the wall was acceptable." — the closed ending with the doubled "wall" construction. The prompter replaced the extended closing with the single phrase "replaced by a wall." The ending contracts: wall arrives, anecdote ends. The analysis of the wall is gone; the wall itself is the terminus.

## Dialect patterns codified for v2 POETICS update

1. **Direct speech as raw register**: When the Director speaks directly, it is atavistic, pre-rhetorical, vernacular grievance. Not polished doctrine. The rawness is the ideology at its operating level beneath the official language.
2. **Crane as translator**: Crane calibrates pitch to receiver. In Director scenes: *assessing what english level might succeed*. The calibration is visible in Crane's interior; invisible to the Director.
3. **Single-sentence paragraph as compressed verdict**: *"The blatant act is permissible."* — a stand-alone declaration so compressed that examination cannot attach. Use sparingly; structural violence in the brevity.
4. **Maxim grammatical breakdown under pressure**: When the self-regulation maxim fails, its grammar fails with it. Double negative, article deletion, compound-word compression. The syntax enacts the transgression.
5. **Enumerated sacrifice**: When destruction is discussed, the items are specific and cumulative. Not "they will suffer" but: passport recalled, taxes audited, children expelled, husband fired, family uninsured, deported. The arithmetic is the violence.
6. **Somatic reading of others**: Crane applies the same anatomical-mechanical vocabulary to other people's bodies that Crane applies to Crane's own. The Director becomes: lacquered wet eye palpitations, elderly quivering neurotic child. The somatic read reveals the subject's underlying state — and Crane's method of maintaining superiority.
7. **Three-sentence declarative ownership**: *"We made the cage. We invited the crowd. We hired the fighters."* Short declaratives of equal weight; collective "we" without apology; structure that builds from site to spectators to combatants, ascending through the institution. Available as a construction when ownership is being claimed without conscience.
8. **Wall as simplified terminus**: The ending contracts to the object. Not analysis of the wall — the wall arrives and the anecdote ends. The ending that performs enclosure by enacting it.
