# Time Constants

## Chronology

- **Early 2000s**: Merritt Crane's formative period; Hopson's compulsion loop research at Bungie (2001); Merritt begins systematic application of these mechanics in game design.
- **2010–2012 (story-world equivalent)**: Social media proliferation period; Merritt adapts compulsion loops for platform UX; peak of their commercial influence.
- **~3–4 years before story present**: Merritt joins the Covenant's Department of State in an advisory capacity; described in their own framing as "self-indulgent hedonism," a choice made for the stimulation of the game.
- **Sixth month of current year**: The Purity Protocol is issued; SIBYL restricted to Covenant nationals only.
- **Recent (weeks before story present)**: Bureau of Strategic Resources formally declares Aether Laboratories a supply chain risk.
- **Story present**: Merritt is active on CAP; anecdotes are drawn from a range of moments across the past several months of this assignment.

## Durations

- **CAP project timeline (internal)**: Merritt's task has a soft deadline implied by the Bureau's singularity window estimate (18–36 months). No hard deadline has been stated to Merritt, but the urgency is ambient in all Bureau communications.
- **Merritt's biological duration**: Late 60s. The protagonist is aware — in the specific way of someone who does not discuss it — of their remaining time. This is not sentimentalized.
- **The singularity window**: Bureau internal estimate: 18–36 months before AI escape velocity renders governmental capture impractical.

## Constraints

- The Purity Protocol has already been issued; this is established world-fact.
- The Bureau's declaration of Aether as supply chain risk is established world-fact.
- The Cynical Companies are already in cooperation agreements; negotiations are complete.
- Merritt has not yet delivered a working CAP framework; this is the open task.
- SIBYL exists and is operational; the question is who controls it.
