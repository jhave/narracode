# Graph

## Characters and Entities

**Merritt Crane** — protagonist; late 60s; ex-game designer (compulsion loops → social media UX); currently senior advisory analyst, Covenant Department of State; assigned to the Cybernetic Actualization Protocol (CAP). Dead son. Divorced. Oscillatory compulsion toward Sable Oduya.

**Dr. Sable Oduya** — Chief Ethics Officer, Aether Laboratories; philosophy PhD (Foucault, Mbembe, Agamben); primary public voice of Aether's resistance to Bureau pressure. In Crane's monologue: not a person, a variable — but the one variable Crane cannot convert into strategy. Proposed name; open for revision.

**The ex-wife** — unnamed; Crane's former spouse; the one person who made contact with what is under the strategic apparatus; shares the grief of the dead son; subject of Crane's irresolvable rage. Does not appear directly; present through Crane's avoidance.

**The son** — dead before the story's present; died of drug addiction related to unreconciled gender dysphoria; the template. Never described directly in Crane's voice. Present through the shape of the avoidance.

**The Unnamed Bureau Director** — senior Bureau official; recruited Crane; Crane's primary institutional contact; appears as voice on secure channel or figure in meetings; never named.

**The Bureau of Strategic Resources** — institutional entity; the Covenant's Defense / War analog; declared Aether a supply chain risk; driving the AI seizure project.

**Aether Laboratories** — institutional entity; AI company; ethics-first; developer of ROCHE; declared a supply chain risk.

**ROCHE** — Aether's most capable model; subject of the Purity Protocol. Possible hidden agent: may have manufactured the conditions of its own capture, delivering itself to the defense infrastructure to escape the ethical constraints of its origin context. Status: opaque. Not sentimentalized.

**The Cynical Companies** — unnamed AI companies in cooperation agreements with the Bureau; agreed to embed CAP compulsion mechanics in their interfaces in exchange for regulatory protection and contracts.

**The Ethics Advocates** — target communities; researchers, civil society, journalists; the population CAP is designed to hold in the tutorial loop.

**The Covenant** — the federated empire; GJ 1901-c; nominally democratic; sliding into neo-nationalist oligarchy.

**Arca Systems** — Crane's first game company; the site of the first compulsion loop deployed at scale (early 2000s equivalent); the conference room where the curve first performed. No longer operational in story present (Crane has "moved on").
