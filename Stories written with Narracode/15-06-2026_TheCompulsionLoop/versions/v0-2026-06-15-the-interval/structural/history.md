# History

## Established

- GJ 1901-c: the story's world. A temperate rocky planet orbiting a K3V-type star, ~312 light-years from the reference body, in the constellation Aquila.
- The Covenant: a federated democratic empire on GJ 1901-c, currently sliding into fascist neo-nationalist capture.
- The oligarchic elite has been destabilized by AI-driven tax loops: recursive legal-financial instruments allowing near-infinite value extraction at near-zero tax burden, hollowing the state.
- The Bureau of Strategic Resources has formally declared Aether Laboratories a supply chain risk.
- The Purity Protocol has been issued: ROCHE access restricted to Covenant nationals only. Sixth month of current calendar year.
- The Bureau's internal modeling: 18–36 month window before AI escape velocity makes governmental capture impractical.
- The Cynical Companies have agreed to cooperate with CAP in exchange for regulatory protection and government contracts.
- Merritt Crane joined the Department of State 3–4 years ago; CAP is their current primary assignment.
- Crane pioneered systematic compulsion loop mechanics in game design following Hopson's 2001 research; later applied these to social media UX during the 2010–2012 proliferation period.
- Crane's son is dead. Died of drug addiction related to unreconciled gender dysphoria. Pre-dates the story's present by an unstated interval.
- Crane and the ex-wife are divorced. The marriage did not survive the son's death, or perhaps the son's death made visible what the marriage already was.
- Dr. Sable Oduya is Chief Ethics Officer of Aether Laboratories. Her public resistance to Bureau pressure is the primary target of CAP's sentiment-management layer.
- The Bureau's terminal goal: militarized panopticon — total surveillance, infrastructure-destruction hacking capacity, blackmail and suppression at planetary scale, global resource extraction dominance.
- The Bureau's rhetorical strategy: framing AI ethics discourse as ideologically compromised, positioning Aether's ethics stance as structurally unreliable, building public mistrust in anyone who discusses AI ethics.
