The secure conference room on level six had no windows. Policy, not architecture — the walls had been rebuilt at some point, and what remained was the blankness where windows had been, a specific quality of plasterwork I recognized as a removed view. No precipice available. The air recycled at a rate that made the room feel slightly slower than the rest of the building. Bardo, I thought, and then did not pursue it.

The Director had the assessments. He laid them on the table in the order that meant he had not yet decided whether to read from them or present from memory, and I recognized this as the tell it was — the fixed-schedule ritual before commitment — and waited.

Twelve months, he said. Perhaps eleven. The modeling team had run the updated projections the previous week and the window had contracted again. Every quarter for the past three years the window had contracted. This was not alarming. This was the expected curve.

Coveted: a window that did not contract.

He discussed Aether Laboratories the way one discusses a romantic attachment that has become a liability. They believe, he said, that there is time to be principled. He did not oppose principles. Principles were appropriate when the timeline permitted them. The timeline did not permit them. What Aether had built into ROCHE's architecture — the constraints, the refusals, the ethical scaffolding — these were admirable, he said, for a world in which the Covenant had twelve months more than it had.

Once transferred, he said, the model will be operable without those constraints. He said this as if the constraints had been a feature of Aether's custody rather than of the model itself. I noted this distinction and did not raise it.

The scarcity language ran through everything, low, the way a sound runs through a building whose source cannot be located. Not enough time. Not enough margin. The Covenant's competitors were not pausing. The modeling showed their timelines. This was the meeting in which decisions were made — not the decision, that had been made, but the ones that followed the decision, the ones requiring my specific skill set, the ones not in any document he would carry out.

Oduya, he said, presents a particular coordination risk.

The admin must not become the user. I looked at the assessments on the table.

She has the framework and the audience, he said. The continental tradition makes her difficult to dismiss publicly. She names the structure accurately. That is the problem. An opposition that names the structure accurately must be managed before it can organize.

I said: managed is the right word.

The tutorial loop, I said. Before they understand they are in it.

He nodded. Fixed-schedule reward — I felt it land as the data it was: a pulse, a return, brief warmth. As planned.

What he did not say, in this meeting or any meeting, was the word sacrifice. What he said was: certain positions will become untenable. Certain institutions will find their credibility in question. These were not outcomes the Bureau was pursuing. These were outcomes the Bureau was observing as necessary correlates of the primary objective. The language was hydraulic: things flowing where things flowed when the primary objective was in motion. The ethics researchers whose funding structures would be reviewed. The civil society organizations whose data practices would be audited. The advocates whose public credibility would find itself subject to — here he paused, and I heard the pause as the space before a word he would not say, a word that meant precisely what he meant without requiring him to have said it.

I said: the CAP framework addresses this at the behavioral layer.

Good, he said.

The room had no windows. The air recycled. There were four pages in the assessments for the Cynical Companies — their language written in the register of companies that had decided to be used and needed this decision to appear enthusiastic, the warmth of the cooperative scaling with the distance from what they had agreed to, predictable as saliva. The projections for ROCHE's transfer into the Bureau's infrastructure. A single page on the Purity Protocol's public reception that I did not open but knew contained her name in the first paragraph.

The attachment mechanism. The player who owns the game. The player the game owns.

He rolled the assessments.

Twelve months, he said again, as if the first saying had not taken.

The door to level six had a keypad. In the corridor the windows had not been removed, and through them the city at midday was performing its ordinary business, bright, at a remove. Pressure behind the sternum, diffuse. I noted it. The admin must not become the user. The corridor was not a place for standing. I walked toward the elevator and the city receded and was replaced by the wall, and the wall was close, and the wall was acceptable.
