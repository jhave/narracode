# Character Interiority

## Characters

### Merritt Crane

**Surface self-understanding**: Purely strategic. A game-theory agent with superior information and cleaner motives than the people around them. Took the State position for self-indulgent hedonistic reasons — the intellectual stimulation of the ultimate game. Has no ideology; ideologies are, in their framing, engagement mechanics deployed by less sophisticated players.

**Actual private state**: The extreme fear underneath has no name and is not named. Its contour: Merritt designed the compulsion loop systems that built the world they now inhabit — the social media architecture, the engagement-maximization regime, the platform economics that produced the political conditions now accelerating toward fascism. The world is what they made. The fear is the recognition that cannot be completed: *if I made this, then what made me?* The question cannot be followed to its answer because the answer would require a self that exists outside the loop. Merritt has no such self. The loop is Merritt.

**Desire**: Control as antidote to surprise. Strategic dominance not as ambition but as prophylaxis. The CAP project is, at this level, Merritt running the final compulsion loop on themselves: the act of designing the mechanism of control is the mechanism of control. As long as there is a next step in the architecture, the precipice is not reached.

**Avoidance**: The moment when the loop produces no next move. Retirement. Completion. The win state. Merritt avoids these not through busyness but through the specific pleasure of infinite games: games that cannot be won, only continued.

**Contradiction**: Merritt was, in some early period, capable of designing games that were genuinely pleasurable — that gave players something, not just extracted from them. There are traces of this in the prose when Merritt recalls the early work, though Merritt does not register these traces as loss. The reader may.

**Hidden knowledge**: Merritt knows, with greater precision than almost anyone, what the CAP will do to the Ethics Advocates. Not in the abstract — in the specific. They have seen the engagement data from social media, the dropout curves, the radicalization pathways, the suppression of dissent that looks like amplification of choice. They know what is being designed. They do not discuss this because there is nothing to discuss.

**Changing self-understanding**: Not yet tracked — initiation pass. The question is whether the anecdotes, accumulated, produce any shift in Merritt's self-model. The refusal of the redemption arc means this shift, if it occurs, must be formal (structural porosity, sentence-level) not narrative.

## Arc Potentials

- **Deepening porosity**: As anecdotes accumulate, the formal leakage of the unnamed terror could increase — sentences that don't close, motifs that recur with increasing pressure. The arc is not toward naming but toward the form becoming unable to hold the unnamed.
- **The early career anecdote as crack**: A rendered memory of the early game-design period might function as the one moment where Merritt's current framing is visibly insufficient to the remembered event. A scene where the loop was first deployed and something was genuinely given — not just extracted. Merritt cannot read this memory correctly. The reader can.
- **The Ethics Advocates as mirror**: One anecdote where Merritt encounters something about the Ethics Advocates — a document, a conversation overheard, a data set — that requires active suppression to maintain the framing. The suppression itself is the arc movement.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points

- None that result in moral clarity for Merritt. The form refuses this.
- The closest available: a sentence that begins as strategic analysis and ends in a clause that is not strategic — that belongs to a different grammar. The reader recognizes it; Merritt moves on.
- The window motif as potential inflection site: the moment when Merritt looks through the glass and the precipice is directly below and the anecdote does not end but continues — this continuation, the refusal to let the precipice resolve into anything, is the form of catharsis available to this text.
