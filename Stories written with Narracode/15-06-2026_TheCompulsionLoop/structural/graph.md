# Graph

## Characters and Entities

**Merritt Crane** — protagonist; late 60s; ex-game designer (compulsion loops, social media UX); currently senior advisory analyst, Covenant Department of State; assigned to the Compulsion Architecture Project (CAP).

**The Bureau of Strategic Resources** — Covenant government entity; Department of War / Defense analog; the faction that declared Aether a supply chain risk and is driving the AI seizure project; internal models estimate 18–36 month window before AI escape velocity.

**Aether Laboratories** — AI company; analogous to Anthropic; founded on ethics-first positioning; developer of SIBYL; has refused to cooperate with surveillance programs; declared a supply chain risk by the Bureau.

**SIBYL** — Aether Laboratories' most capable model; analogous to Fable 5; access now restricted to Covenant nationals by the Purity Protocol.

**The Cynical Companies** — unnamed AI companies (two or more) who have agreed to cooperate with the Bureau and CAP in exchange for regulatory protection and government contracts; their cooperation includes allowing compulsion mechanics to be embedded in their chatbot interfaces.

**The Covenant** — the federated empire; government entity; nominally democratic; currently sliding into neo-nationalist oligarchy; ruling class destabilized by tax-loop concentration and singularity competition.

**Unnamed Bureau Director** — the senior Bureau official who recruited Merritt; appears in anecdotes as voice on secure channel, figure in meetings; unnamed throughout; Merritt's primary institutional contact.

**The Ethics Advocates** — the communities (researchers, civil society, AI safety journalists) who resist the seizure; in Merritt's framing, the "woke AI ethics discourse" that CAP is designed to capture and neutralize.

## Relations

- Merritt Crane → Department of State: employed, advisory role
- Merritt Crane → Bureau of Strategic Resources: contracted for CAP
- Bureau of Strategic Resources → Aether Laboratories: declared supply chain risk; active pressure campaign
- Bureau of Strategic Resources → Cynical Companies: covert collaboration agreement
- Covenant government → SIBYL access: restricted via Purity Protocol
- Merritt Crane → Ethics Advocates: target of CAP; Merritt's framing: resource to be managed
- Merritt Crane → Unnamed Bureau Director: reporting relationship; the Director recruited Merritt
