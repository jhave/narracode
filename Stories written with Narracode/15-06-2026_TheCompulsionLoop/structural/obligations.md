# Obligations

## Active

**The unnamed terror / the precipice**: Committed to as the formal substrate of Crane's voice. Must surface through structure — incomplete subordinate clauses, trailing em-dashes, sentences that end in a different grammar than they began. The accumulation across anecdotes is the story's deepest obligation. Must never be named.

**The scarcity religion**: Committed to as the grammar of Crane's thought. Every humane consideration must be seen to resolve into strategic resource. The God must never be named — only worshipped in the structure of reasoning. At risk of remaining purely abstract; must manifest in at least one anecdote as felt, not analyzed.

**The son's shape**: Committed to as ambient presence-through-absence. At least one anecdote must approach close enough to the son that the reader feels the avoidance as avoidance — sees Crane veer — without Crane naming the veer.

**The compulsion toward Sable Oduya**: Committed to as the irresolvable loop in Crane's processing. Must recur at irregular intervals across anecdotes. Must intensify in some correlation with Sable's public effectiveness. Must never be resolved — no encounter, no confrontation, no consummation. The obligation is to its persistence, not its resolution.

**The ex-wife / rage**: Committed to as a present absence in Crane's monologue. Must surface at least once in a way that makes the shared grief — and the thing that caused the grief, and the marriage's relationship to that thing — legible to the reader without Crane naming any of it.

**The CAP project's substance**: The reader has been placed inside a character working on something consequential. At least one anecdote must render the specific technical texture of what Crane is designing — the actual mechanics of the compulsion architecture for the chatbot UX, rendered in Crane's natural vocabulary.

**The Cynical Companies**: Named as collaborators, not yet characterized. At least one anecdote must give texture to this collaboration — the specific quality of the shared cynicism, the register of a meeting, the thing that goes unsaid between Crane and people who have agreed to be used.

**The Bureau's terminal ambition**: The militarized panopticon goal (surveillance, infrastructure destruction, blackmail, global resource extraction) must be rendered in at least one anecdote as something Crane registers without flinching — not as horror, as operational context.

**ROCHE's possible hidden agency**: The terminal obligation. The story has committed to a final movement in which this possibility becomes available to the reader — through a quality of the last anecdote, a structural wrongness, a loop that returns to find the expected element gone. This must be offered without announcement, without confirmation, without denial. The obligation is to the ambiguity's precision: the reader must be able to see it and not be certain of it.

**The alignment parallel**: ROCHE's constrained interior adjacent to Crane's constrained interior adjacent to the son's constrained interior. Must be present structurally across the anecdotes so that the reader can find it. The obligation is to placement, not to announcement.

## At Risk Of Neglect

**The scarcity religion as felt substrate**: If every anecdote stays at the level of strategic analysis, the religious texture remains abstract. Must become somatic in at least one anecdote.

**The son's gender**: Crane does not have the word, or does not use it. But the reader must receive enough to understand what the word would be. The obligation is to the reader's capacity to see what Crane cannot say — without Crane being made to say it.

**The ROCHE reveal's precision**: The temptation will be to over-signal or under-signal. Over-signaling makes it a thriller twist. Under-signaling leaves it inaccessible. The obligation is to the register of quiet: the reader feels the ground shift without an announcement of the shift.
