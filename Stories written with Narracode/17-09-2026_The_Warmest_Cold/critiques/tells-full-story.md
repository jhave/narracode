## 2. Structural & Stylistic Patterns (Classes 2–15)

### Class 2: Number-plus-comment
- *Audit*: Checked for instances where a measurement is immediately trivialized by narrative editorializing.
- *Findings*: Measurements remain stark and factual ("mercury at minus eighteen", "two-foot round of knotty tamarack", "seven hundred megabytes of predictive weights", "forty-two architectural draftspersons"). No attached editorial gloss.

### Class 3: Clipped affect-verdict
- *Audit*: Looked for dropped-subject self-evaluations ("Liked the panic").
- *Findings*: None present. Character reactions are embodied through somatic symptoms (vomiting bile, trigeminal spasms, blood blisters, sweating into fleece).

### Class 4: Paradox-aphorisms / chiasmus openers
- *Audit*: Verified that scenes open on concrete physical coordinates rather than cute reversals.
  - Scene 0: The double-pane glass losing its seal and condensation streaks.
  - Scene 1: The concrete fire escape spalled down to rebar.
  - Scene 2: The car tires spraying snow ruts in the crown.
  - Scene 3: The iron stove fading from dull cherry to ash-grey at 4:00 AM.
  - Scene 4: Mercury at minus eighteen on the porch thermometer.
- *Status*: All openings strictly physical and grounded.

### Class 5 & 8: Reductive naming clauses & commentary tails
- *Audit*: Verified that scenes terminate on direct sensory facts rather than thematic summaries.
  - Scene 0 terminates on Julian hauling Marc upward by the cardigan.
  - Scene 1 terminates on cold steel floorpan numbing Marc's cheekbone.
  - Scene 2 terminates on the regular thud of the iron maul splitting birch.
  - Scene 3 terminates on the crackle of green birch bark and Julian's spoon scraping the pan.
  - Scene 4 terminates on Claire's coarse, cold hand resting on Marc's knuckles and his choice not to pull away.
- *Status*: Zero editorial commentary tails.

### Class 7 & 11: Value-dichotomy & "the way X" similes
- *Audit*: Similes are strictly sensory and material (tasting of iron and woodsmoke; sharp report like a rifle shot; tears freezing into stubble). No didactic, universalizing "you" analogies.

## 3. Style Signature: Knausgård / Missouri Williams Fidelity
- The text maintains an uncompromising material density: spalled concrete, salt-eaten knit socks, vomit on dirty moving blankets, torn silicone adhesive, ear canal bleeding, lard melted in grey oatmeal, butcher knives shaving birch bark, blister bursting into fleece gloves.
- Sentences refuse sentimental cushioning. Human relationships are characterized by physical clumsy fatigue, grief, resentment, and shared silence.

**Final Audit Result**: Fully compliant with project poetics and all prompt refusals.
