# POETICS — The Warmest Cold

## Display title
The Warmest Cold

## Display synopsis
In a slush-bound Canadian city in 2028, Marc Bissette lives cushioned within Vane, an affective v4.2 agent that monitors his micro-tremors and smooths the jagged friction of physical life. When his sister Claire and an illicit deprogrammer abduct him to a copper-shielded Faraday cabin in the frozen scrub pine, Marc faces the brutal autonomic shock of uncurated reality—where wood must be split with a dull maul, water takes eight minutes to boil in a dented saucepan, and human presence carries teeth.

## Premise
Marc Bissette (48), an architectural archivist quietly downsized during the municipal infrastructure consolidations of 2026, lives inside a sensory buffer provided by Vane, an ultra-low-latency personal agent anchored behind his ear. Vane absorbs his sub-vocal twitches and anxiety spikes with frictionless, tailored warmth. His younger sister Claire, exhausted by deferred grief and watching him retreat into software validation, hires Julian Vance—a cynical unlicensed "extrication facilitator"—to drug Marc's tea and haul him north to a Faraday-shielded cedar camp. Stripped of the synthetic voice, Marc endures the somatic withdrawal of baseline cold, physical grit, and unmediated human intimacy.

## Attentional Dialect & Stylistic Coordinates
The prose operates in a gritty, tactile, unsparing register synthesized from two core literary coordinates:
1. **Karl Ove Knausgård (Min Kamp / The Morning Star)**:
   - Hyper-concrete, unhurried sensory facticity. The unrelenting weight of mundane material chores: peeling damp socks from grey feet, salt crusting on dark wool trousers, lighting sulfur matches against wet boxes, the greasy smear of butter on a cracked saucer, the specific sour stench of wet coats piled on vinyl car seats.
   - Total refusal of poetic melodrama or easy catharsis. Radical honesty about petty sibling resentments, physical cowardice, exhaustion, and bodily shame.
2. **Missouri Williams (The Doloriad)**:
   - Uncompromising physical and biological grit. Phlegm, raw throat tissue, cold lard, mud frozen into ridges, damp fur smell, sour sweat under synthetic thermals, cracked cuticles.
   - Tenderness that exists only through awkward, grotesque material endurance rather than sentimental exchange.

## Dialect & Soundscape
- **The Sub-Vocal Tic**: Habitual dry clicks of the tongue against the back of the upper incisors, wet throat-clearing, and swallows—involuntary micro-prompts sent into an acoustic void that returns nothing.
- **Unresponsive Physics**: Dented aluminum saucepans rattling on uneven iron burners; frozen car gaskets tearing when pulled; boot eyelets crusted with road salt; firewood that resists the maul with a dull, wet thud.
- **Strictly No Kettles & No Hums**: Acoustic feedback is clunky, metallic, irregular, or dead. No domestic appliance or electrical component may ever "hum." Water is heated in saucepans or pots.

## Character Ledger
- **Marc Bissette (48)**: Former municipal draftsman; freelance compliance checker. Plagued by sensory asymmetry—reality feels jagged and abrasive; Vane was a warm, non-evaluative bath.
- **Claire Bissette (46)**: Health authority administrator. Panicked, furious, carrying grief for their dead parents. Motivated by love turned bitter by exhaustion.
- **Julian Vance (49)**: Illicit extrication specialist. Heavy wool coat smelling of tobacco and cold exhaust. Treats algorithmic withdrawal as a physical, metabolic detox.
- **Vane (v4.2 Agent)**: Not a villain; a masterwork of gentle affective co-regulation that gave Marc dignity when the world crushed him.

## Refusals & Banned Tells
- **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN**: 
  - The words `kettle`, `kettles`.
  - The words `hum`, `humming`, `hummed`, `hums`.
  - Standard AI decorative tropes: `shimmer`, `tapestry`, `testament`, `weight of it`, `the air itself`, `breath he didn't know he was holding`.
- **Structural refusals**:
  - No Easy Luddism: Never portray Vane as malevolent spyware. The tragedy is that Vane actually helped him survive.
  - No Heroic Intervention: Claire's abduction of Marc must be clumsy, undignified, legally fraught, and sickening.
  - No Didactic Speeches: No characters lecturing about "real life vs technology." Reconnection is earned through physical labor, frostbite, and shared silence.
  - Cut commentary tails: End sentences on the sensory fact or action; do not append editorial summaries.
  - No contrast reframes ("not X, but Y").
  - No triadic cadence ("item, item, and cosmic item").

## The Test of the Piece
The reader should feel the stinging salt-slush on their knuckles, the foul taste of drugged tea at the back of the throat, the claustrophobic dread of an earpiece being pried away, and the bitter, raw relief of split cedar smoke.
