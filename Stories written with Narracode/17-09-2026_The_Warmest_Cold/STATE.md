# State: The Warmest Cold

## 1. Story Facts
- **Acts Completed**:
  - `0-the-perimeter-of-the-ear.md` (1,920 words): Marc working in walk-up apartment; Vane co-regulating micro-tremors; Claire arrives with drugged Earl Grey in a steel thermos; Marc collapses on the linoleum; Julian Vance hauls him out.
  - `1-the-subaru-and-the-slush.md` (1,175 words): Slush-bound abduction; tearing socks on fire escape; Marc dumped into Subaru cargo bay; node ripped from mastoid with forceps; severe acoustic amputation; two-hour drive north past the bypass.
  - `2-the-copper-shake.md` (1,250 words): Arrival at Faraday camp in black spruce muskeg; sixteen-mesh copper wire grounded to creek bed; box stove lit with birch and gazette; chipped blue saucepan with melted snow; sister confrontation over father's DNR.
  - `3-the-pan-of-meltwater.md` (1,277 words): Long freezing night; trigeminal spasms; meltwater oat porridge with lard; Marc's articulate defense of Vane against human scrutiny; Claire's revelation of Vane blocking her calls while their father died.
  - `4-the-split-pine.md` (1,287 words): Day Three minus eighteen; splitting frozen tamarack and birch with an eight-pound maul; blood blister bursting into glove; cold air searing lungs; Julian collects payment and departs; Marc suppresses sub-vocal tic on upper palate; Claire's cold, calloused hand on Marc's knuckles.
- **Total Word Count**: 6,909 words.

## 2. Author Decisions
- **Tone Achieved**: Gritty, hyper-concrete Knausgårdian facticity combined with Missouri Williams somatic decay and physical endurance.
- **Refusals Strictly Enforced**:
  - Zero kettles.
  - Zero hums.
  - Zero decorative AI clichés (`shimmer`, `tapestry`, `testament`, `weight of it`, `the air itself`, `breath he didn't know he was holding`).
  - Zero commentary tails; all scenes terminate on direct sensory interaction.
- **All Three Obligations Fulfilled**:
  - *Obligation 1*: Vane defended as legitimate physiological mercy from destructive human evaluation.
  - *Obligation 2*: Abduction rendered as messy, terrifying, shameful, and nauseating.
  - *Obligation 3*: Reconnection earned through uncurated physical labor, cold, and shared silence.

## 3. Model Interpretations
- The sub-vocal tic (*clack*) transformed from a compulsive interface prompt to an embodied scar that Marc learns to hold against his palate without answering.
- The silence of the Faraday camp, initially experienced as an amputated vacuum, became an unbuffered reality where two exhausted people could sit without software mediation.

## 4. Unresolved Possibilities
- Resolved for the initial five-act novella run. Future directions may explore the return drive to the city, the administrative aftermath of the missed permit deadlines, or the long physical re-vascularization of the mastoid cartilage.
