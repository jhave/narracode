# Drift — 2026-06-26 (final, over the finished five movements)

Cumulative drift pass over the completed piece (0–4) after the last compositional pass on 4. The question: what did the piece become, across five human/AI loops, versus what POETICS first declared?

## The trajectory
- **v0 (AUTO_MODE):** five clean, controlled lyric movements; competent but with AI-diction tells.
- **v1 (human + AI):** de-tell, de-dichotomy, unity-in-process; warmth ("song," "love"); the It introduced.
- **v2:** the "densified voice" — sound-thick, permeable grammar, identity-fusion (It-which-Is-I), kennings, love-as-binding.
- **v3:** "dissolution" — grammar liquefied, relational fusion (that-which-is-also-I), oneness allowed to sing, sensual charge, science to residue.
- **v4:** Movement 3 de-anatomized (the bend retired there) and turned luminous/prismatic — reopening the founding premise.
- **v5:** Movement 3 compressed to its final telescoped form; Movement 4 gathered the arc into the close.

## Is the drift fertile or regression?
**Fertile, decisively.** The piece moved *away* from every genre default it feared (the cute droplet, the lecture, the oneness-sermon) and *toward* an idiolect that does not exist elsewhere: a substance-narrator whose grammar dissolves as the water dissolves, whose identity fuses as the wager claims, whose surface shimmers as the prismatic premise promised. Form became content. This is the opposite of regression to the training mean.

## What changed from the original POETICS — and whether it still holds
- **"Earn the wager; never name oneness."** Relaxed by authorial choice: oneness now *sings* ("Oneness sliding into one") but is still guarded by the surviving disclaimers in 3 ("I am not telling you that you are one"; "I will not say it. You felt it."). Holds — as music, not doctrine. POETICS Register III records the permission.
- **"Mineral-precise; chemistry disciplines the lyric."** The science is now *residue* rather than exposition (latent heat, D/H, surface tension, alveoli survive as anchors). The discipline shifted from foreground to ballast. Holds, but is the most at-risk commitment: a reader new to the piece gets less of the chemistry than v0 offered. Deliberate; worth the author confirming it is the intended trade.
- **The bend/handhold through-line.** Partially retired (gone in 3, softened in 4) — a local evolution, documented in motifs. The piece no longer leans on one physical metaphor throughout; light shares the load now.
- **Both/and, no dichotomies; warmth; love-as-binding.** Strengthened across every loop. The closing chord ("Thus it is love. Both and all, at once.") is the same in 1 and 4 — the arc rhymes shut.

## Coherence across the five
- **Pronoun/identity:** I (0) → It-which-Is-I (1) → that-which-is-also-I (2) → I-that-is-It (3) → returning-as-you (4). A legible escalation, not noise.
- **Gradient of dissolution:** 0 plain → 1 dense → 2/3 dissolved → 4 gathered. The one seam to watch is **0 → 1**: Movement 0 is markedly plainer than the rest. Either the intended on-ramp or the last place a future pass might lift. Flagged in every recent check; left to the author.
- **Refrains that bind:** onset litany (0↔4), "Thus it is love" (1↔4), "as they say" (1,2,4), oneness (2,3,4), the membrane/film (2,3), the bow (3,4).

## Net
The piece is finished as a draft-organism: internally consistent in its strange new voice, its science carried as ballast, its wager sung but not preached, its arc rhymed shut on love. The single open editorial question is the **0→1 register seam** (lift 0, or keep it as the on-ramp). No drift requiring correction; the divergence from the original POETICS is the achievement, not a lapse.
