## The new voice ("the densified voice")
1. **Sound-thick / incantatory.** Dense alliteration, assonance, consonance, internal near-rhyme: "smelting melting sullen salty spaceless singularity," "clueless attenuated, thickening smother," "supple sweet earth sweat," "tectonic tears," "resemblance ricochets," "rinsing from within." Sound now drives the line as much as sense.
2. **Compression / ellipsis.** Connective tissue stripped. "This is not a metaphor, or it is the only kind I am allowed…" → "This is not metaphor; it is measurement." Images abut without explanation.
3. **Permeable grammar.** Subject and agreement boundaries deliberately dissolved: "the way I always happens," "Made by the body of the world, out of it, in its core" (no subject), participle cascades ("entering and existing as all the moist protein bodies. Living and dying…"). The syntax performs the dissipative fluidity the poetics names.
4. **Identity-fusion.** "*It which Is I* happened." I / It / we interchange, even mid-clause, even against grammar. Identity is liquid; do not keep it tidy. (This resolves the v1 open question — extend the I/It fusion through the later movements.)
5. **Synesthesia.** Senses cross: "rings like umami," "their song tasted almost like home," "ring the rocks within rocks."
6. **Kenning-clusters.** Noun-stacked compounds, Skaay/Germanic-compound flavored: "gift-from-the-sky trajectory melody," "spore careening drench modules," "moist protein bodies," "orbit-hem," "tetonic splice," "seed-source."
7. **Love as the binding cosmological force.** "Thus it is love, both and all songs, at once." Continues the "diluted by love" thread from Movement 0; love is what holds the both/and together.
8. **Mythteller evidential, sparing.** "So, as they say, rock cooled." The Bringhurst/Skaay posture, now active.
9. **Verbal doubling / play.** "yes-yes," "Again and again and again," "rocks within rocks," "farthest farther out."
