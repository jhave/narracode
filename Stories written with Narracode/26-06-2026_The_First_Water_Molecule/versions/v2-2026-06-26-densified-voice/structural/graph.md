# Graph

## Characters and Entities
- **The narrating water** — the only continuous "entity"; a substance, not a person. Speaks as I / we / this / it / you depending on phase and scale. Has no edges; coheres and dissolves.
- **Provenances (ancestral water-kin, distinguished by D/H signature), ranked by weight in the story:**
  - *Homegrown water* **(primary — the mother)** — born of atmospheric hydrogen + oxygen-rich magma ocean; **made here, inside, not delivered.** Most of Earth's water. The narrating water's deepest, truest provenance.
  - *Cosmic / quasar water* — the >12-billion-light-year reservoir, ~140× trillion ocean-masses; the species-onset of the molecule, the far myth (not the source of *Earth's* specific water).
  - *Asteroidal water* **(minority strain — the visiting cousin)** — Ryugu, Winchcombe; "almost perfectly matched," but a topping-up, not the source.
  - *Cometary water* **(discredited cousin)** — high D/H (Halley, via Giotto); looks like family but mostly isn't; partial kin (Hartley 2, 12P/Pons-Brooks).
- **Hosts / membranes (transient, not characters):** silicate dust grain, magma ocean, cloud, shoreline, root hair, stoma, gut, bloodstream, alveolus, eye-film, a stranger's exhaled breath.

## Relations
- The narrating water *is* each provenance and each host in turn — relation is identity-across-time, not interaction between separate beings.
- Membrane = the recurring relational site: where inside/outside negotiate and water is the negotiation.
- Speculative relation (the wager): all wet things in subtle synchrony — a single tidal field, not a set of separate drops.
