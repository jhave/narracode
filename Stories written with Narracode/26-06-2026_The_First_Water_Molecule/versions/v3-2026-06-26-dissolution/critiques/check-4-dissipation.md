# Check — 4 · Dissipation

- **Continuity.** Surface tension as "inward handhold," evaporation as per-molecule departure each carrying its latent-heat wage, the source cooling by exactly that amount — all consistent with chemistry established in 0/2. The tear's itinerary (ocean → cloud → rain → stranger → tear → vapor → snow) is a faithful water-cycle without ever using the schoolroom phrase.
- **Obligations.** Discharges the "no redemption, only continuation" commitment cleanly ("do not lend me your ending"). The originating cadence returns transformed — "Like the first sound… Like the first light… molecule of water arising. Not in space. Not anymore… Arising here" — which is the structural payoff the harness asked for. The final "Drink." converts the whole piece into address/instruction without sentimentality.
- **Motifs.** Latent-heat-as-only-comfort pays off the seed from 2. Handhold/bend resolves. Onset returns. All three primary motifs land in-movement.
- **Scene function.** Release and continuation. The "the journey is yours… I lent you both [beginning and end]" passage is the thematic keystone — it retroactively reframes movements 0–3 as the reader's need, not water's. Strong.
- **Voice/default.** **Highest sentiment risk in the piece** is the grief paragraph ("your grief borrowed me for an afternoon"). It mostly earns itself by staying material (the tear's literal route, the heat taken up), but "the only comfort I am built to give" leans toward the consolation the poetics warned against. Flag for the human edit: test whether it tips into reassurance.
- **Reader-state.** Aims to make the reader feel their own mouth/eye as ocean (the explicit poetics test). "the spit behind your teeth as you read this" is the direct bid; it works if the preceding movements banked enough embodiment (they did, esp. 2). Ending lands as continuation, not closure.
