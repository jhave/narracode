# Obligations

## Active
- **The originating image** must surface obliquely and pay off as felt pressure, never as recited fact: "Like the first sound, like the first light. Like the first? Molecule of water arising in space."
- **The synchrony wager** must be *earned through texture* by the end — a reader should suspect mutual wet-awareness without finding the sentence that asserted it.
- **Provenance memory (D/H)** is planted as a recurring "taste"; it should recur transformed, not merely repeat.
- **The homegrown truth must register as felt, not stated:** "Earth made most of its water by itself." The reader should come to feel the water as *native, never foreign* — born from inside the planet — without the prose announcing the hypothesis. This is the figure that must rhyme with the synchrony wager (origin-from-within ↔ knowing-from-within).
- **The membrane motif** must accumulate across scales toward the synchrony movement.

## At Risk Of Neglect
- Keeping the science *metabolized* (perception, not exposition) under the pull toward explaining.
- Keeping the lyric *mineral-precise* — chemistry disciplining the lyricism so it doesn't float free.
- Preserving indifference-and-intimacy together (water does not love us); guard against anthropocentric sentiment.
