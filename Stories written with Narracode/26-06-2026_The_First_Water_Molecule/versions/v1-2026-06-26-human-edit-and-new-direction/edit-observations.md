## Intuitions (first principles to apply)
1. **Cut paradox-aphorism / chiasmus openers.** "the temptation is the only thing that is first" is a composite that lands squarely in AI latent space. Refuse the family: "the end of me is the part I do best, and I have never managed it" (4), "built a wall to keep me out and that is how I got in" (2). Go succinct; let a plain sentence open.
2. **Strip the reductive "which is only X."** It deflates the image and reads as a tic. Let the image stand, or make the completion *generative* ("as song," "by love") rather than diminishing ("agreeing to carry").
3. **Warm the cosmology.** The prompter swapped grudging mechanism for tenderness and generation — love and song as cosmological forces. Aligns with the new *Metaphysics* (nourishment, harmony). Apply, but stay this side of sentiment (Refused still holds).
4. **De-correct the narrator.** Remove "everyone gets it wrong," "I want to be exact," the superiority over "storytellers." The water witnesses and gathers; it adds, it does not refute. This is the single biggest tonal shift and must propagate through the rest of 0 (grafs 4–6: "undoes the storytellers," "the storytellers also miss," "what they pretend to own," "most of the story is not that") and through 1 ("the better story and that is exactly what is wrong with it").
5. **Loosen identity.** I/me → It/this where the "I" is egoic or asserting. Keep the experiential "I" (I was bent). Identity is a phase, not a fixed self. Do not mechanically de-"I" the whole piece — the prompter left graf 3's "I" intact.
6. **Drop articles for compression** where it sharpens ("the first sound" → "first sound").
7. **No value dichotomies.** better/worse, right/wrong, true/false — out. Provenances become kin arriving by different roads, not rivals; the homegrown truth stays as fact, loses its scorn. Replace "wrong" with "Perhaps" / "also this."

## Risk to watch in the edit pass
Over-applying #1 and #4 could sand off the voice's confidence and leave it limp. The goal is *witness*, not *timidity* — plain and warm, still strange and exact. And the two tutelary styles (Nils architecture, Skaay plainness) are seasonings: a light evidential "they say" and more parataxis in the origin-deep passages, not a wholesale costume.
