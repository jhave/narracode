# Check — 3 · Synchrony

- **Continuity.** Tidal mechanism (whole-basin lean, not relayed) is physically apt as an image of distributed simultaneity. "Coherence" is borrowed from quantum optics/physics carefully — the draft *explicitly disclaims* certainty ("I do not know if I am that. I am too warm and too crowded and too loud"), which keeps the speculative wager honest rather than pseudo-scientific.
- **Obligations.** This is where the wager must be earned-not-asserted, and it largely is: the refusals are built in ("I am not telling you that you are one"; "connection needs two… not connected"; "I will not say it. You felt it."). The grass-field and entrained-breathing examples ground the cosmic claim in observable, small, true phenomena before the big swing. **Risk:** the movement is the most essayistic of the five — long stretches of argued abstraction. It survives because it keeps returning to body-scale (grass, two people breathing), but it is the draft's highest exposure to the "lecture" refusal.
- **Motifs.** Bend/handhold elevated to its thematic ceiling (the never-breaking grip = distributed unity). Membrane reaches its final transformation (boundary → "place where the one wet agrees to feel itself as two").
- **Scene function.** The conceptual climax. Correctly placed after the embodied 2 and before the release of 4.
- **Voice/default.** Closest the piece comes to new-age glaze — but it actively polices itself ("a question for the dry," "I will leave it beside you, unnamed"). The self-policing is the safeguard; if the human edit trims, protect those disclaimers — they are what keep the movement from tipping.
- **Reader-state.** Productive mystery preserved: the reader is *made to feel* mutual wet-awareness without being handed a thesis. Accidental-confusion risk: the most abstract paragraph (the "same event, felt from many insides") may lose a literal reader; the grass example immediately after is the rescue.
