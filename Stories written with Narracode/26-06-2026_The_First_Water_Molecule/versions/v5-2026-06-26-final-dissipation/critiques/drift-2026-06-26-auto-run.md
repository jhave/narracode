## Where it drifted — and whether the drift is fertile

- **Toward explicitness (fertile-but-correctable).** The strongest drift is the draft naming its own central rhyme and, twice, referencing "the poetics" from inside the fiction. This is *not* genre regression — it is the opposite, a draft so committed to its architecture that it points at it. Fertile in that it proves the structure is real and felt; correctable in the edit by subtraction. This is the single thing to fix.

- **Toward the essay (watch).** Movement 3 drifted closest to argued abstraction. Held inside the lyric by disclaimers and body-scale returns. Fertile so far — the wager *needs* some conceptual air — but it is the boundary the next edit should patrol.

- **The handhold/bend motif became dominant.** Unplanned but productive: the dipole's never-quite-breaking grip ended up underwriting cohesion-tension (2), surface tension (4), *and* the synchrony image (3). This emergent unification is the happiest drift — the piece found its own central physical metaphor and let one true fact (the 104.5° bend) carry the whole thematic load. Keep it; just don't add more instances.

- **Scale handling stayed disciplined.** No drift toward gratuitous cosmic grandiosity; the quasar reservoir appears once (0), is invoked once more structurally (4), and otherwise the piece stays at body/biosphere scale. Good restraint against the obvious temptation.

## New ground vs. regression
The piece is finding fertile new ground, not regressing to tropes. The water-narrator genre's gravity well (whimsy, education, oneness-uplift) was avoided. The genuinely new move — water's *homegrown* origin as the mirror of *knowing-from-within* — is the piece's own, and it is doing real thematic work rather than sitting as decoration.
