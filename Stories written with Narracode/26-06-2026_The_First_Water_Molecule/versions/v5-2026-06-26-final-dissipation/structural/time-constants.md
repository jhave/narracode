# Time Constants

## Chronology
- **Onset (no beginning):** first water molecule arising in space from energy potentials — "didn't have a beginning, it just arose."
- **Deep cosmic time:** quasar water reservoir at >12 billion light-years (≈ early universe).
- **Solar-system formation:** water on dust grains, comets, asteroids; delivery and/or homegrown synthesis on early Earth.
- **Biological time:** entry into living membranes; the water cycle as continuous present.
- **Single-breath time:** ~4 seconds inside a stranger's lung — the dilated micro-crossing.

## Durations
- Deep-time durations to be *compressed*; single membrane-crossings to be *dilated*. The asymmetry is the form.

## Constraints
- Water does not know the future (trans-local, not omniscient).
- Real chemistry must hold: bond angle 104.5°, ice less dense than liquid, latent heat carried off on evaporation, hydrogen bonding, surface tension/meniscus.
- D/H signature is a memory water carries; it cannot be contradicted once a provenance is established in a passage.
- No phase-change without its physical cost/condition (heat in, heat out).
