# Motifs

## Active
- **The membrane / meniscus / threshold** — cell wall, alveolus, stoma, root hair, gill, cloud edge, shoreline, skin, the surface film of an eye. The site where inside/outside negotiate.
- **Pronoun drift** — I / we / this / it / you exchanging places to track phase- and scale-change. (Structural device as much as motif.)
- **Provenance as taste** — D/H signature surfacing as a faint recognition of where this water came from; heavy water and light water as kin with different memories.
- **Made-here / origin-from-within** — the magma ocean as mother; water sweated out of molten rock; *native, never foreign.* The planet self-watering. Should rhyme structurally with the synchrony motif (knowing-from-within).
- **"No beginning, only onset"** — the first-light / first-sound / first-molecule cadence.
- **Latent heat / the loan** — what is carried away on a breath or an evaporation; the gift that is also a leaving.
- **Scale collision** — quasar reservoir ↔ capillary ↔ tear-film ↔ a single bond on interstellar dust.

## Transformations (status across drafts)
- Membrane: physical site (2) → medium of mutual sensing (3). Done; escalation holds.
- "Onset" cadence: planted (0), returned transformed at close (4) — origin → continuation. Done.
- **Handhold/dipole bend** became the load-bearing through-line: cold (0) → xylem rope (2) → surface tension (4) → the never-quite-breaking grip that underwrites synchrony (3). Strong; watch for over-reliance in any future edit.
- Latent heat introduced as motif in 2, paid off as "the only comfort" in 4.

## Avoid Overuse
- The word "flow" and its cognates; the literal "water cycle" framing; "drop/droplet" as a cute unit.
- Repeating the 104.5° / ice-density facts more than once each — chemistry as seasoning, not refrain.
- Over-deploying pronoun drift until it becomes a tic rather than a perception.
- **AI-diction tells (v1):** paradox-aphorism openers / chiasmus; the reductive "which is only X"; the "not X, it is Y / The difference is everything" balance as flourish; the corrective-superiority stance toward "the storytellers." (See POETICS → Refused.)
- **Value dichotomies (v1):** better/worse, right/wrong, true/false waters/stories/origins. Replace ranking with gathering, correction with inclusion.

## Added v1 — new motif threads
- **Unity-in-process / implicit recognition** — dissonance and harmony, permeability and contraction held together, not opposed; the narrator moving through phases of recognition with what it passes through. Both/and over either/or.
- **Identity-fluidity (I / It / this)** — the voice loosening from egoic "I" to "the It that is speaking" and back; identity as another phase-state, not a fixed self.
- **Warmth as cosmological force** — per the human edit: "song," "love," nourishment where the draft had grudging mechanism. Generation, not deflation.
- **Architecture-revealed (Nils) + mythteller plainness (Skaay)** — the world's structure shown to a borne-along traveler; cosmology spoken as plain marvel with sparing evidential "they say." Concentrate in 0 and 4; light touch elsewhere.
