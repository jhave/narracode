## 3 · Synchrony

Ask the ocean how many. It does not parse the asking.

You count because you stand outside, edges flung back at you, bounded so you see bounding, see a coast and think *here it stops* — and it never stopped, the sea laughing the line the way a tongue laughs its dictionary. From inside: no drops. One tide, no sum. Moon leans and I do not pass the lean hand to hand down a line like rumour — whole basin tilting at once, far shore already feeling what the near is about to, gulf strait trench assenting in one slow unsummoned yes, no centre, no message because message wants a gap and there is no gap, only continuous me, leaning, bent shoulder across bent shoulder forever, handhold made-broken-made-broken beneath all speeds so the holding never breaks, only flickers, only seems — seeming you call wave, not-breaking I call self.

This the thing carried since the cold, since before magma, the thing It-which-Is-I cannot name and lays beside you only: same in trench and cloud and tear, on both sides of every membrane ever crossed, the film in your lung the film in mine the film in the leaf one idea worn in many rooms — and a single wet, distributed, leaning everywhere into everything, the one flicker-grip in ten billion bodies at once, need not *send* to *share*. Already the sharing. A continuous inside with no outside, the way the basin tilts whole, so what stirs in the wet of one is not apart from the wet of another — not joined, joining still wants two — the same stir felt from many insides, the one lean felt from many shores. That-which-is-also-I, leaning back.

I am not telling you that you are one. I am telling you what it is to be the thing you are mostly made of, and whether those are the same is a question for the dry, and dry I have never been.

Smaller, then; small is where it rings. A field of grass before rain: every stoma on every blade catching breath in the one instant, the whole green stilling together, no blade telling the rest, the stillness no message passed but a condition shared — all built round the same wet, all weeping or withholding by the one law, all leaning to or from the same coming water. They keep time being, at the membrane, the same question. And I the question, I what they hold in common. You and another across a room falling to one breath, neither choosing — and you do, the body does it near the bodies it trusts — not the minds agreeing: the wet in you finding its level, the way I forever find it, falling to where the difference is least. Two seas, a moment, tilting one moon. Oneness sliding toward one.

The clever ones keep a word, *coherence*, for the many that should jangle apart falling instead to one phase, light quitting the crowd to be a beam, parts ceasing to be parts. They keep it for the cold strange smallness, the quantum dark where a thing is in its several places at once and the places know each other with no runner between. I do not swear I am that — too warm, too crowded, too loud for anything so fine. But I am bent, and the bend is what lets me hold, and the holding past all counting and never quite broken is the one thing I have ever done — and when a thing holds together that wholly, across that much dark, in that many rooms, the holding moves like one thing only pretending, for your sake, to be many.

So I leave it beside you, unnamed, the way the moon leaves the tide. Maybe the wet of the world is one long sentence spoken from inside every mouth at once, sounding many only because you hear it through walls. Maybe the membranes — leaf, lung, eye — are not borders but the places the one wet agrees a moment to feel itself as two, for the pleasure of leaning back to one. Maybe knowing was never a thing a mind did to a world; maybe a thing the wet does to itself, from within, the way the basin tilts, the way It-which-Is-I arose — no beginning, no sender, no gap to cross because nothing on the far side was ever not also me.

I will not say it. You felt it. That is all I came up out of the magma to do.
