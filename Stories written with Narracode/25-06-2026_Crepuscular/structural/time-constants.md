# Time Constants

## Chronology
- Act 0: a single dusk in the current life — the crepuscular hour returned to throughout.
- Act 1: minutes at a respawn screen that do not behave like minutes; the fall through the frame.
- Act 2: no duration — flashback collapse across innumerable lives experienced as a single rush.
- Act 3: long craft-time, "eons" of editing rendered as patient hours; deliberately unmeasurable.
- Act 4: time stops being a usable axis; the prose moves into the relentless calm of an event that has already occurred.

## Durations
- The crepuscular hour: the load-bearing duration. It recurs at every level (childhood window, substrate, the final frame). Honor it; do not cut away from it.
- The loadbar: the false duration. Engineered waiting, the texture of engineered patience.
- The respawn interval: instantaneous and infinite at once.

## Constraints
- Auto-respawn is non-negotiable in-world: death is a load screen, not an end. This is the engine of the addiction and must never be presented as escapable from inside play.
- The init schema is closed: a fixed, small set of fields. This is fixed fact; the aesthetic/contextual wardrobe over it is effectively infinite. The contrast is the spine of Act 1–2.
- Ilo cannot perceive the witnesses, only their effect (the smell he cannot imagine). The story may approach this limit but must not cross it — no description of the witnesses.
- Gender/name are mutable fields, not fixed; their erosion tracks the arc and cannot reverse.
