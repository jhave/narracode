# Graph

## Characters and Entities
- **Ilo** — the player. A young man in his current life (one of innumerable). Name is a field value (Finnish: *joy*); gender is a construct, eroded across the arc. Protagonist until the protagonist position itself dissolves.
- **The current avatar** — the body Ilo wears in Act 0: a specific young man with a specific childhood window, a sister, a city.
- **Sava** — the sister in the current life. Plays *Life* too; the believer who never falls through. Functions as the held mirror: what Ilo would be if he had not slipped.
- **Life** — the game. The most popular game ever made; auto-respawn, infinite-seeming character creator, planetary install base. Not a metaphor inside the story — the operative reality.
- **The makers / the Studio** — never seen. Inferred through the schema, the patch notes, the tuning. The optimizers of engagement.
- **The substrate** — the system level beneath character creation, where the archetypes are stored: the closed core under the open surface.
- **The archetypes (init schema)** — the few dozen fields from which all characters spawn. Thousands of named archetypes, but the architecture is closed.
- **Test entities** — avatars Ilo spawns and observes after he becomes the engineer; he never inhabits them, only measures their effectiveness.
- **crepuscular** — the drama. The frame revealed in Act 4: the millions of games are a single episode of it.
- **The witnesses** — beings so strange Ilo cannot imagine their smell. Never described, only indicated. The outermost known harness.

## Relations
- Ilo ⟶ Life: addict ⟶ platform, then user ⟶ exploit, then engineer ⟶ codebase, then author ⟶ harness.
- Ilo ⟷ Sava: woken ⟷ sleeping; the relation is grief, not conflict.
- Makers ⟶ Ilo: designers ⟶ optimized subject. Asymmetric, invisible, never confronted.
- Witnesses ⟶ everything: audience ⟶ drama. The relation Ilo ends inside and cannot see out of.

## Relation Changes
- Act 1: Ilo's relation to Life changes from inhabitant to reader.
- Act 3: from reader to editor; the relation to other avatars changes from peer to maker (he becomes, locally, the thing the makers were).
- Act 4: the engineer position is revealed as another inhabited role; "Ilo" stops being a stable node and becomes a process in the system. The graph loses its center.
