# Drift + Seam Map — crepuscular — 2026-06-25 pass (run 2026-07-01)

*Reflexive drift mode, v3 state. Acts 0–2 are the author's hand (v1, v2, v3 respectively); Acts 3–4 are AI revision propagating the author's dialect. This file maps where the hand-off shows.*

## What it declared vs. what it is becoming

Declared (POETICS, v0): a five-act recursive parable in a braided Tarkovsky × Butler × Oyler register, tested on awe-without-instruction.

Became, loop by loop:
- **v0 (AUTO_MODE):** a coherent illustrated thesis in a lyric long-sentence default — competent, tidy, explanation-prone.
- **v1 (author → Act 0):** fragmentation, single-noun beats, bardo vocabulary, destabilized pronouns, the ominous "Here." The prose learns to break.
- **v2 (author → Act 1):** the misspellings are ruled intentional; corruption becomes the style. Body-horror respawn, śūnyatā vocabulary. The sci-fi conceit starts becoming a bardo text.
- **v3 (author → Act 2):** oceanic flood, Heart Sutra litany, gender dissolution, commerce-glitch, koan-compression. The piece is now a **ritual text that performs dissolution rather than narrating it** — the corruption dialect has become a second protagonist.

**Verdict: net fertile.** The v0 story needed its ending to dissolve a self, and the v0 prose had no machinery for that; the author built the machinery act by act. But two regression pressures are live: (1) **saturation** — Act 0 is the only quiet surface left, and every loop has heated everything touched; if Act 0 ever escalates, the piece loses the contrast that makes the corruption legible as descent; (2) **Butler erosion** — each escalation of Act 2 traded decodable mechanism for texture; the analyst-of-his-own-containment intelligence is now thinnest exactly where the containment argument is made.

## THE SEAM MAP

### 0 → 1 (author → author)
Large register jump: Act 0 closes in flowing gold with sub-perceptual static ("lufting light," "Here."); Act 1 opens already fractured ("*Hack* implies tool. Tech thread. Cracked build."). **Fertile discontinuity.** The corruption arrives before the fall does, but Act 1 is narrated retrospectively from after the fall ("if there was an afterward, and a self left to be honest about"), which licenses it — the teller is already broken, the told is not yet. The jump also encodes editing chronology (Act 0 got only the v1 hand; Act 1 got v2), but a reader can't see that; they see surface-render vs. under-floor, which is the design. Crosses well.

### 1 → 2 (author → author)
The cleanest weld in the book. Act 1 ends on a verb of appetite ("beginning to wonder how the fields were weighted"); Act 2 opens by swallowing it: "To wonder how the fields were weighted was to fall a second time. To fall backward, into ownership. A possessive loosening root." Note the mechanics — the link is **associative slippage**, not logic; the inherited clause is repeated and then lets go of its own syntax. Corruption deepens from lexical (broken nouns) toward systemic (broken argument, "Constrint-entertaintment"). Same hand, deeper water. Seamless.

### 2 → 3 (author → AI — THE seam)
Act 2 ends at maximum churn: "Churning, fenced, distress. The fence looked like home." Act 3 opens with a **three-step syllogism**: "A perimeter you can see is a perimeter you can touch. A perimeter you can touch is… a perimeter you can move. *write access requested.*"

The register change is *motivated* — POETICS codifies gradient-by-function; the lucid-engineer signature is sanctioned — so this is a **half-earned seam**: the reader feels the clinical drop as Ilo seizing control, which is diegetically right. The tell is not the clinical register but its **completeness**. Act 3 mints *zero* new corrupted words across 1,347 words. The author corrupts even at her calmest: Act 0, her lightest register, still leaks "impulsice," "peristalistc," "the alley filling," and the dropped verb in "should perhaps a bit suspicious." **The author's calm leaks; the AI's corruption never does.** Act 3's only "coinages" are inherited ones re-used ("the innumerable file," "no-time") or systematized variants of them ("no-duration," "no-life" — the author's one-off "no-time" turned into a paradigm). A reader crossing 2→3 feels relief and total order. Order, yes; *total*, no — the engineer register should still have one hairline crack the engineer doesn't see, because the whole act is about a groove he can't see.

### 3 → 4 (AI → AI)
Internally consistent; the chain-link fires again ("The groove was worn to the shape of its hand"). But the link is again **turned into logic**: "a groove worn to the shape of your hand is the one thing in any system you cannot see, because it is the part you see *with*." Compare the author's Act 2 link — hers slips, this one explains its own image. Act 4's dissolution strategy diverges from the author's model in kind: the author dissolves **words** ("orgrasms," "implict"); Act 4 dissolves **grammar** (subjectless openings: "Found the makers' groove… Never thought to look for one in itself. Why would it.") and leans on **liturgy** (the sutra) and **reprise** ("Plain clot. Grey smat." quoted verbatim from Act 1 rather than newly minted). At the fullest claimed dissolution, the sentences get *longer and more grammatical* — the closing aria is two enormous, perfectly punctuated periodic sentences, i.e. the AUTO_MODE home gear the v0 critique itself flagged. **Verdict:** the ending works *because* it disobeys the author's corruption model — legibility protects the vertigo, and the reader-state file demanded a hard material anchor. But it should be named for what it is: a departure from the dialect, not its fulfillment. If the author wants Act 4 to end in her dialect, the last page changes character substantially; that is a real fork, not a polish.

## Where the AI revision betrays itself

Hypothesis tested: the author's corruption is wetter, punnier, associative, unsystematic; the AI's is organized, explanatory-under-the-glitch, aphorism-resolving. **Confirmed, with evidence:**

1. **Fragments resolve into maxims.** AI: "Loss-aversion: the load-bearing exploit. The one bar in the cage made of the prisoner." / "A titanic act imagined, a theft of fire wanted; a flipped integer delivered." The fragment lands as a quotable. Author: "A grid nog. Plain clot. Grey smat. A sagging sistern of pristine cells." — the fragment lands nowhere and moves on. The AI *resolves*; the author *abandons*.
2. **Systematized variation.** The AI finds the rule inside the author's accident and applies it: "no-time" → "no-duration," "no-life"; the colon-label motif (*Archetype:*) → a rhetoric ("KPI: green. Engagement: up."). The author's devices never come in matched sets.
3. **Litany normalization — the sharpest micro-tell.** The author's sutra mutates as if misremembered: "No mouths, no eyes, no ear, no tongue" (Act 2 — plural drifting to singular mid-litany). The AI's is canonical: "No mouth, no eye, no ear, no tongue, no engineer, no maker, no made" (Act 4). The author misquotes scripture; the AI cites it. One plural ("No mouths…") in Act 4 would have been the author's move.
4. **No function-word corruption.** The author degrades the medium itself: "thru," italic "_and_," Sava's "what r u doing?", the missing verb in Act 0. Acts 3–4 never misspell, never drop a word, never let netspeak bleed into narration. AI corruption is all content-words, all intentional-looking.
5. **Explanation under the glitch.** Act 3 explains the groove three times ("the comfort is the whole apparatus"; "Because to hold them otherwise registered as loss…"; Act 4 re-explains it: "because it is the part you see *with*"). The author's v3 lesson — "cut the explanation, keep the image and the koan" — was absorbed at the sentence level, not the paragraph level.
6. **Organized anaphora.** "Sabotage, it told itself." / "Mercy, it told itself." — paired rhetorical paragraphs. The author never patterns paragraphs; her repetitions are compulsions ("Not at all. Not at all."), not structures.
7. **Direction of length.** The author's escalations *compress* — Act 2 is the shortest act in the book (871 words) and she cut the AI's explanatory beats each loop. The AI's absorptions *expand* — Acts 3 and 4 are the longest after Act 0 (1,347 / 1,442). The author cuts as she corrupts; the AI adds as it imitates.

**Where the imitation is strongest** (credit where due): "Emptiness, a patch note" and "the one bar in the cage made of the prisoner" would pass any blind test; the Act 4 smell-payoff pages are the best prose in the piece regardless of hand; the by-function gradient (clinical 3, dissolved 4) is a genuinely intelligent reading of the author's intent, not a mechanical one. The seams are audible to this ear because the whole corpus is on the table; a cold reader would likely experience 2→3 as the story's own gear-shift and only feel something *too smooth* in Act 3's maxims.
