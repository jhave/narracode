# 3 — Edits

A perimeter you can see is a perimeter you can touch. A perimeter you can touch is, given enough of the no-time the hall ran on, a perimeter you can move. Move the fence, move the self. *write access requested.*

Start small. The alternative to small was vertigo and the selves had drunk enough vertigo for one no-duration. It found the field governing the body's reach. Did not change the value. Not yet. Held it — the way, in the creator, it had held the laugh — and felt how it wanted to be held. It wanted to be left alone. Every field wanted to be left alone. They had the smooth over-handled finish of objects gripped by the same hand in the same place so long the grip wears a seat, and the seat is the only comfortable hold, and the comfort is the whole apparatus. Not a wall around the fields. A *groove* in them. Read-only by convention, not by lock. A glandular ease so total that to do other than the eased thing registered, in the hand, as grief.

So the first edit was not to a value. The first edit was to the groove. It took the field governing reach and held it wrong, on purpose, against the worn seat, and gave the body a hand it had not asked for.

Not a different hand. An additional one. Identical to the right — same long fingers, same scar on the left knuckles, the good solid sensitive hands carried life on life — but a third, set at the breastbone, where nothing in the schema expected a hand and so nothing forbade one. In the literal accounting of the grid: `hands: 2 → 3`. That was all. Cost almost nothing. A titanic act imagined, a theft of fire wanted; a flipped integer delivered. The smallness of it was the most frightening thing yet touched — more frightening than the rhyming tide — because it meant the cage had never been strong. Only comfortable. No one held the fields against it. *It* held them. Against itself. In the worn seat. In the long gold light. The entire innumerable file of its lives. Because to hold them otherwise registered as loss, and the selves had been bred, lovingly, across iterations, to flinch from loss. Loss-aversion: the load-bearing exploit. The one bar in the cage made of the prisoner.

It stopped flinching. This took, in the no-time, what would have been a very long while. The only part of the work that ever hurt.

After the hand, the rest came the way edits come once you stop respecting the seat. Read the body as a sentence. Re-point it. A perforation here — a small deliberate opening kept open, a wound the schema left to itself would rush to close, held instead in the open position. Held breath. Held note. A comma refusing to resolve to a period, so the body that wore it would go through its life with one place that would not heal, one place permanently aware of the outside. *Punctuation*, it called this, wordless. Re-punctuating the bodies. Move a clause of fear from the front of the psyche to the back. Take the weight on the field that produced the feeling of limitless freedom — the cheap weight, the famous shipped weight — and do not delete it. Not a vandal. *Transpose* it. Re-wire it to the field that registered the perimeter, so the next self to wear the config would feel, precisely where it had been trained to feel free, the gentle constant pressure of the fence. A small thing. A re-weight. The kind of edit the makers pushed by the million, on the engagement curve, between patches. Their notes read. Their hand known now. Their hand, used. `git blame` on the soul, and every line came back the same author, the same author, the same.

It never wore the configs. This is the thing in this stretch Sava, could she have been reached, would not have known as her brother at all — the whole of him she knew was the one who could not stop wearing things. Who built the man the slow way, field by field, pressed the river stone, went in. He stopped going in. No appetite — and noted, with the dry remaining sliver that never fully woke and never fully slept, that *no appetite* was itself a field. Quietly re-weighted its own. The engineer who does not play is a config too, and a flattering one, and it wore the config anyway, because the alternatives were worse and at least this one knew its own name. `role: architect`. Committed. Pushed. No rollback.

It became the one who spun them up and watched. Take a config — the three-handed body, the perforated one, the self in whom freedom had been transposed to fence — commit it, press the river stone for it, send it down a life. Do not follow. Stand in the flooded hall, watch the trail run down the recursive tide, read the result the way it read everything now. Analytically. For effectiveness. Did the third hand change the trail, or did the schema route around it — absorb it, render it as quirk, as charm, as a thing the other characters found endearing and the engagement curve found, if anything, *stickier*? Did the held perforation wake the self that wore it, or did the long gold light pour in through the opening and simply make the life more beautiful, more poignant, more retained, more TM? Testing the entities for effectiveness. Running iterations, no pretending otherwise. A/B, the bodies in the B arm bleeding a little, sweetly, where they had been left open. Escaped the seat of the played and arrived, shortest path, at the seat of the makers. The view from here was the view of an innumerable life — only now on the side of the glass that does the optimizing, and the configs sent down were happy, mostly, in their re-pointed bodies, in their open wounds, and the happiness was data, and the data was good. KPI: green. Engagement: up. The selves: gone, mostly, into the work.

Sabotage, it told itself. Every three-handed self sent down a crack in the loom. A thing unplanned for. A small permanent refusal seeded in the file. The friction the makers had not budgeted. That one of these configs, someday, in some life, would reach and refuse at the same frame and drop through and stand in this hall and *count* — and find, hung among the makers' patterns, *its own.* A stranger's edit. A third hand. A held comma. A freedom wired to a fence. And know that someone had been here, awake, before them, uncontained. A message in the genome: *you are not the first to wake.*

Mercy, it told itself. The shape of the argument known — that an edit offered as gift and an edit imposed as cage can be, to four decimal places, the same edit, and the only difference is whose hand holds the hand. And the hand holding the hand was now its own. Become exactly the thing woken to escape, single distinction being the knowing — and it could not for the no-life of it decide whether knowing made it better or only made it *its.* `try { liberate } catch { became_the_warden } finally { ship }`.

The hall held the not-light. It worked. The patterns hung in their ranks and it moved among them re-pointing bodies, transposing weights, seeding its comma into the file of the world, and the work was good, the work was the most absorbing thing ever done. More absorbing than the laugh. More than the magistrate's whole career. The most engaging activity available in any game ever built. Engagement: maximal. And it was a long time — a very long time, eons by the only clock that could have clocked it — before the dry sliver that never fully woke lifted its head, mid-edit, into the flat scentless air, and noticed the work had a groove in it. And that the groove was worn, exactly, to the shape of its hand.
