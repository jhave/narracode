## What holds

**The recursion is still form, not twist — and the acts are now welded.** Every act opens by picking up the previous act's final clause ("To wonder how the fields were weighted was to fall a second time"; "The groove was worn to the shape of its hand"). By Act 4 the maker-seat being "played from above" completes a figure the reader has crossed four times. The chain-link device arrived with the author's Act 2 and was correctly propagated.

**Comfort, not control.** The draft's best idea survived three escalation loops intact and got sharper: "the cage had never been strong. Only comfortable" (Act 3); "Loss-aversion: the load-bearing exploit. The one bar in the cage made of the prisoner." The containment thesis stays scarier than its Matrix ancestor because no one is holding the bars.

**The smell is engineered correctly across all five acts.** Seeded as absence ("Noticed the smell not having a smell," Act 1), tied to love ("broth and woodsmoke," Act 2), kept warm ("the flat scentless air," Act 3), paid as the one unconfigurable thing (Act 4). The ending refuses to name awe and lands cold: "Buffering. The witnesses leaned closer. Here."

**Wit and emptiness fuse at the peaks.** "Emptiness, a patch note." "Bardo itemized loot box." "a proud bestiary of lore… so rich it composted." "the deaths, which were also, in their way, very sticky." This is the register POETICS asked for — the joke surviving the dissolution, dry and load-bearing.

**Sava carries the counter-argument, and wins her scene.** "*how is that fake? It's fake real.*" — the thesis's strongest rebuttal given to the character the reader is invited to condescend to, and she is right in every way that can be lived. The song's cruelty "authored by no one" is the piece's most Butler moment: the system working as designed, on both of them at once.

**Act 0's quiet is now load-bearing.** As the only act still in the light register, its polish reads as the surface render the whole story indicts. Its long-held golden hour (the eleven minutes on the laugh) is the draft's best Tarkovsky duration. Protect it from further escalation.

## Against the poetics, point by point

**Tarkovsky duration.** Holds in Act 0 (the laugh; the light held "the whole while") and at the Act 4 climax, which does not cut on the awe — the final two paragraphs are one held shot. **Slips in Act 2**: the v3 escalation burned off nearly all duration. The wig, the broth, the bridge-builder are sub-second flickers inside an idea-flux; nothing in the act is *held*. The standing obligation ("protect at least one held, slow image per act") is currently unmet there. Act 3's held field ("felt how it wanted to be held") is duration of a kind, but abstract.

**Butler concreteness.** Strong in 0, 1, 3 — the schema is decodable, the third hand is an integer, the perforation is a specific wound held open. **Thins in Act 2**, exactly where the demolition argument lives: "cog capacity vectorals," "Gritty permeable apparatus," "a weighted field sag in the soft flesh of certainty" give rhythm where the captive-reads-the-lock intelligence should give mechanism. The obligations file flagged this risk ("concreteness could thin into abstraction"); it is happening, by the author's own hand.

**Oyler wit.** Alive in every act, correctly thinnest in 4 (the wisecracking self is dissolving; "Turtles. Forty-some turtles. All the way." is now decorative, not load-bearing — right call). Act 2's mid-section runs the longest stretch without puncture; the solemnity ratio there is the highest in the book.

**The refusals.**
- *Red-pill uplift:* refused and inverted — waking is retention, authorship is the deepest trap. Clean.
- *Sentimental humanism:* refused — "what remained was not a freed self standing at last outside the glass. No outside the glass. No self to stand." Clean.
- *Theme-naming:* mostly held, but Act 1 walks the ridge: "the illusion of being free had been a cage. Any face. Any century… Anything except free." This is the narrator stating the thesis in near-slogan form. The "8x5" paragraph two beats later carries the same freight by image; the slogan beat is the one place the reader is told what to conclude.
- *Copula trap:* largely avoided; the one big copular map is the Act 4 reveal itself ("the whole of it… was one episode"). Defensible — the reveal wants flatness — but the neighboring sentence already maps by image ("It was the light the thing was *lit by*"), so the copula may be cuttable. Ear call.
- *Editorializing tail-clause:* clean. The nearest instance, "and noted… that *no appetite* was itself a field" (Act 3), earns its keep as wit.

## Where it defaults / where corruption becomes a crutch

**The tic inventory (all hands):**
- *"Not X. Y." corrective opener* — 3 in Act 1, 3 in Act 3 ("Not a different hand. An additional one." / "Not a vandal. *Transpose* it."). Effective singly; at this density it becomes the prose's reflex for precision.
- *Colon-aphorism / label rhetoric in Act 3* — `role: architect`, "KPI: green. Engagement: up," "Engagement: maximal," plus four backticked code spans. The prior check's flag (thin to the 2–3 strongest) was not applied; the count is still ~6. `git blame` on the soul and `hands: 2 → 3` are the keepers.
- *"the way" simile scaffold* — 7 instances in Act 0, 10 in Act 4. Several Act 4 instances are earned callbacks (the cup drop), but ten in the dissolution act keeps the syntax in explaining mode at the moment the poetics wants it failing.
- *"more than atoms"* — now 7 occurrences across Acts 0/1/2/4. The prior recommendation was ration to a bookend; instead it multiplied. Cut to Act 0 + one Act 4 instance.
- *The "Here." bookend is diluted.* Act 0 ends on "Here."; Act 4 now says it twice — mid-act ("The work wanted to be witnessed. *Here.*") and at the close. The mid-act instance spends the ending's coin early. Demote or cut it.

**Corruption as crutch — the specific spots.** The dialect earns its keep when the broken word is a compressed pun or image: *orgrasms*, *Constrint-entertaintment*, *sagging sistern*, "Same. Loop. New. Kin. Skin." It becomes a crutch where the noise carries no decodable charge: "cog capacity vectorals," "Sinister careening implict," "Gritty permeable apparatus," "Patterns rething data" (Act 2, middle stretch). In those beats unreadability is doing the work an image should — the reader registers *texture: corrupted* and moves on unchanged. The test: if a coinage un-puns to nothing, it is set dressing.

**Smell-motif rationing.** Mentions per act: 1 / 3 / 4 / 1 / 8. Act 4's count is the extended single payoff and fine; the risk is upstream — Act 2's four keep-warm touches are one or two more than "seed once or twice" wants.

## Per-act notes

**Act 0.** The strongest sustained act: seduction rendered with full duration, the lightest glitch ("impulsice," "lufting," "the alley filling") reading as sub-perceptual static under the gold. **Act 2.** The boldest act and the most damaged. **Act 3.** The most controlled act; the argument (comfort as apparatus, the sabotage/mercy koan) is the draft's intellectual peak. But it over-resolves: fragments keep landing as quotable maxims ("A titanic act imagined, a theft of fire wanted; a flipped integer delivered"), the groove is explained three times, and the code-snippet count exceeds the standing flag. The Sava absence-beat ("could she have been reached") satisfies the obligation, but thinly — one clause for the whole cost of waking.

**Act 4.** The ending works: the sutra carries the climax, the smell pays, the last line is cold and earned. The middle shows strain — corruption by reprise ("Plain clot. Grey smat." quoted from Act 1 rather than newly minted) and by regularized litany. Note the final aria is long, grammatical, perfectly punctuated — legibility protects the vertigo, but it is the author's dialect suspended, not fulfilled (see the seams file).
