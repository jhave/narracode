# History

## Established
- *Life* is the most popular game ever made and is played by effectively everyone; respawn is automatic.
- Character creation presents a vast array of parameters that produce a sense of limitless freedom.
- Ilo, in his current life, has a sister, Sava, and a childhood window that holds dusk light.
- Through a casual hack — almost a clerical mistake at the respawn frame — Ilo reaches the substrate.
- The substrate stores the archetypes. The surface offers infinite aesthetic/contextual/visual variation; the core init is a closed schema of a few dozen fields.
- The makers discovered long ago that people are satisfied with a constrained schema if it is tuned for ease plus just enough friction.
- *Life* serves a simulacrum of diversity while optimizing for engagement and stickiness.
- Avatars are manufactured, sculpted, engineered for the efficient absorption of attention into phenomena.
- Ilo begins editing: an extra hand (identical, essential), a perforation, re-weighted psyche, sabotaged init archetypes.
- Ilo becomes the engineer — he stops entering play except to test entities for effectiveness.
- Ilo rewrites until the realm he edits from is itself in question.
- The engineer/author position is itself a role held by an engagement harness.
- The millions of games are one episode of a drama called *crepuscular*, witnessed by unimaginable beings.
