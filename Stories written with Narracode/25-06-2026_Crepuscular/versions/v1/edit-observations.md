## The edits, by category

### 1. Long sentences broken into short declaratives and fragments
The single most consistent move. The opening compound sentence "…late afternoon, and it had been late afternoon for some time. This was a thing the game did well. It held the hour…" becomes four short sentences: "…late afternoon. It had been late afternoon for some time. The game did this well. It held thresholds…". This is the human enacting, by example, the critique's flagged fix for the long-sentence mannerism. **The dense accumulating sentence is kept for the idea-passages but is now broken by flat, short declaratives.**

### 2. Single-word abstract-noun beats
New, load-bearing rhythm device. Paragraphs and clauses now land on isolated nouns: **"A chasm of potential. Freedom."** / **"Pride."** / the final **"Here."** These function as punctuation-stops — the abstraction stated bare, then dropped. Used sparingly and always at a beat's end.

### 3. Article-pruning for telegraphic compression
"It held thresholds — sun low but not gone, air gold not grey —". Articles dropped under focus, restored in the flowing clauses. Matches the master-poetics "cadence under edit." Slightly heavier hand than my original.

### 4. Glitch-texture: coinage, distortion, absurd precision
Deliberate roughening that mirrors the simulation's bugginess:
- *onboarding* → ***upboarding** hour* (intentional inversion — ascension, not intake)
- "four hundred times" → "**5034** hundred times"; "forty years" → "**forty one** years" (uncanny over-precision)
- "imagined rooms" → "imagined **ricochets**"; "reach for commit" → "**carved** toward commit"
- Possible-intentional / possible-typo distortions: "impuls**ice**", "perista**listc**", "**lufting** light", "the alley filling" (was "valley"). **Ambiguity flagged** — see open question below. These read as either glitch-texture (consistent with the theme) or fast-typing artifacts.

### 5. The constructed self foregrounded early (pronoun instability pulled forward from Act 4)
"a laugh ***it**, this being that would be a **me**, would carry*"; "where his hand, this finetuned affection of ***his** harbored proclivities*, knew it would"; "the river stone was warming lustrous in ***his hands***". The Act-4 dissolution-of-the-"he" is now seeded in Act 0 via italicized, destabilized pronouns. **The self is marked as a field from the first scene.**

### 6. Bardo / reincarnation / theological vocabulary added
"the **bardo** in-between space of decisive impulsice aesthetic **reincarnation**"; "the creator" → "**Creation**" (capital C). The respawn engine is now explicitly framed in Tibetan-Buddhist intermediate-state terms and given a faint demiurgic capital. This deepens the gnostic register POETICS already named.

### 7. Inline UI / found-text labels dropped mid-prose
"***Customizable:***" floating inline; *upboarding hour* italicized as a system term. Interface language intrudes into the narration as found objects.

### 8. Somatic immediacy via gerund fragments
"**Scrutinizing** the backs of his hands, **flipping** them over."; "cupped and pressed **between wet palms**"; "good **solid sensitive** hands". More present-tense bodily action, less explanatory framing ("good hands for a person who would turn out to need them" → "good solid sensitive hands").

### 9. The watching presence made explicit and ominous at the close
Original ending: "newly aware that there was another room." Edited: "the way **body** turns on hearing a **cup drop** — …newly aware that there was **another active presence. Here.**" The witnesses-theme (Act 4) is now seeded as dread in Act 0's final beat. "Here." stands alone.
