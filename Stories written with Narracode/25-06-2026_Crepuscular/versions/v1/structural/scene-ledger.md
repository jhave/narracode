# Scene Ledger

## Scenes
- **0 — Character Creation.** Inside *Life* at the crepuscular hour. Establishes platform, propagation, the seductive creator, Sava as believer, the loadbar. Focal pressure: the sweetness and totality of the freedom. Changes: a single frame stutters — the surface shows a seam. Unresolved: what is under the seam. Makes possible: the hack.
- **1 — The Casual Hack.** The almost-mistake at the respawn frame drops Ilo into the substrate. Focal pressure: dawning analytic recognition. Changes: he sees the closed core under the infinite surface; relation to Life shifts inhabitant→reader. Unresolved: how closed, and across how many lives. Makes possible: the branch-trail flashbacks.
- **2 — The Branch Trails.** Flashback collapse down the literal branch-pathway of innumerable lives. Focal pressure: the demolition of "freedom." Changes: he understands variation was cosmetic, friction was tuned, bodies were engineered for absorption; wakes from zombie-form into meta-awareness of containment. Unresolved: what to *do* with knowing. Makes possible: the edits.
- **3 — Edits.** Reconfiguration. Adds the hand, the perforation, re-weights the psyche, ships sabotaged archetypes, becomes the engineer who tests entities but never plays. Focal pressure: the pleasure and ethics of authorship. Changes: Ilo becomes, locally, a maker. Unresolved: whether the engineer is free or just better-placed. Makes possible: the second awakening.
- **4 — crepuscular.** He rewrites until his own realm is in question; learns the author-seat is harnessed; the games are one episode of *crepuscular*, watched by the unimaginable. Focal pressure: dissolution of the "he" into system. Changes: the protagonist position ends. Unresolved (by design): the witnesses; the smell. Makes possible: nothing — the relentless calm.

- Where exactly to seed the smell motif before Act 4 (candidate: Act 1 substrate, as a sensory absence; Act 2 as the one thing the lives never contained).
- How literal to make the patch-note found-text (lean: one or two short drops, Act 0 and Act 3).
