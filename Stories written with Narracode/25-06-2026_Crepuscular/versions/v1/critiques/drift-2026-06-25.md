# Drift — crepuscular — 2026-06-25

*Reflexive drift mode. What is the piece becoming, and is the difference from the declared POETICS fertile or regression?*

## What it declared it would be
A five-act recursive parable: a young man wakes from the game *Life*, learns his freedom is a render over a closed schema, becomes the engineer of the schema, and discovers the engineer-seat is itself harnessed — the whole thing one episode of a drama, *crepuscular*, watched by the unimaginable. Tarkovsky × Butler × Oyler. The ending tests on awe-without-instruction.

## What it became
Almost exactly that, which is itself worth interrogating — AUTO_MODE drafts that hit the blueprint cleanly can be coherent *because* they stayed inside the idea and never got surprised by the prose. The fertile drift to watch for in the human pass is whether the piece has room to become stranger than its own thesis.

**Fertile drifts (keep):**
- The piece quietly became a story about *comfort* rather than *control*. The blueprint framed containment as an "apparatus"; the draft discovered (Act 3, the groove; the third hand as flipped integer) that no one is holding the bars — the prisoner holds them because the alternative grip feels like grief. This is a better, sadder, more original idea than the one commissioned. Protect it.
- The Butler "mercy vs. sabotage" ambiguity grew past its brief and became the moral engine of Act 3. Good. It keeps the protagonist from being a hero.
- The recursion became *gnostic* in flavor (the spark waking in the fabrication, the demiurge who is himself an archon) more than *sci-fi*. The reference to Borges/Gnosticism in POETICS earned its place; the draft leans there and is better for it.

**Regressive pressures (resist):**
- *Toward the essay.* The strongest gravitational pull in the draft is from story toward treatise — most visible in Act 2. The piece keeps wanting to *explain* its containment thesis. Every time it does, it gets less frightening. The human edit should starve the explanations and feed the images.
- *Toward the totalizing sentence.* The voice's signature long sentence is becoming the voice's only sentence. This is genre regression of a specific kind — "lyrical-profound LLM cadence" — that flattens distinctiveness. The fix is not fewer long sentences; it is more short ones, for contrast.
- *Toward tidy nihilism.* The recursion risks resolving into a too-clean "there is no escape, all the way up" — which is a kind of closure, and POETICS refuses announced closure. The draft mostly avoids this by ending on a sensory limit (the smell) rather than a philosophical conclusion, but the final "enough" pulls back toward tidiness. The colder the ending, the less the piece will have drifted into the consolation of a completed idea.

## Is the difference fertile or regression?
Net fertile. The draft's discoveries (comfort over control; the gnostic register; mercy/sabotage as engine) are richer than the commission. Its regressions are all the predictable ones of unedited idea-prose and are all addressable by compression and rhythm in the seamless-edit loop — none require re-architecting. The piece is becoming a genuine parable rather than an illustrated thesis, and the single thing standing between the current draft and that better version is the human author's red pen on the explanatory passages and the long-sentence default.

## One question for the prompter
The draft keeps Sava off-page after Act 2. Is the cost-of-waking (the loved one left sleeping, happily, inside the schema) sufficiently paid by her single appearance, or does the piece want one more small, late Sava beat — an absence rather than a scene — to make the grief of waking land before the dissolution erases the self that could feel it? This is the one place the architecture could productively grow.
