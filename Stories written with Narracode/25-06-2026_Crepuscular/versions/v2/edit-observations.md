## The escalation, by category

### 1. Glitch-corruption as deliberate texture (the resolved open question)
Words are now intentionally broken/blended: "**grid nog**", "**Plain clot. Grey smat.**", "**sagging sistern**" (cistern × sister), "**flicky-flack**", "**binary-gland**", "_and_" / "thru". The one genuine typo the author flagged for correction — "trheads" → "threads" — is the exception that proves the rule: *that* one was an error; the rest are chosen. **Rule inferred: let the text corrupt the deeper into the substrate it goes.**

### 2. Emptiness / śūnyatā / anatta vocabulary
Buddhist negative-theology register, far past v1's "bardo": "**To know nothingness.**", "**timeless, incomplete, changeless, thoughtless and perturbed**", "**nothingness-light**", "**what-is-without-body**", "osmosis incapable of any dexterity". The respawn machinery is now explicitly a wheel of emptiness.

### 3. Body-horror somatics
The fall is now visceral and obstetric: "**a wall with a suction tunnel in it**", "**swept into a womb**", "**binary-gland, bardo glistening**", "**spasming river stone in cold hands**", "**He gasped into the substrate**", "**red-rimmed eyes**", "pores on a body". Reincarnation rendered as a birth canal.

### 4. Subject-dissolution pulled hard forward
The "he" is already failing in Act 1: "if **what-is-without-body** stood", "**Awareness churned** in a slow circle", "**the one** was falling through", "**Absorbing** air that did not exist" (subjectless gerund openings). The Act-4 endpoint is now seeded at the first descent.

### 5. Aggressive fragmentation / subjectless declaratives
Beyond v1: "**Counted, calibrated.**" / "**Mute.**" / "**Scentless.**" / "**Not at all. Not at all.**" / "Tech thread. Cracked build." Noun-and-participle fragments stand as whole sentences.

### 6. Code/UI intrusions sharpened, with a buried joke
"**Deception would not compile.**", "the screen behind the screen, **a debug matrix**", "**8x5**" (= 40, quietly literalizing "forty-some" as grid dimensions), "*Archetype:* #". The system's own language eats the narration.

### 7. Oyler wit preserved as tonal puncture
The corruption does not kill the wit: "did not glow like **a friendly radioactive donut**", "a proud bestiary of lore so rich **it composted**", "their brief, useless, **and endless** conversations". The joke survives the dissolution — important; without it the register would curdle into solemn mush.

### 8. Number-drift as instability
"**Forty-fold. Forty-one. Perhaps forty-two.**" The count itself now wavers — the grid resists being pinned, mirroring v1's uncanny "5034".
