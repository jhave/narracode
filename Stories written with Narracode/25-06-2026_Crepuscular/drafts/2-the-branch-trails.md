# 2 — The Branch Trails

To wonder how the fields were weighted was to fall a second time. To fall backward, into ownership. A possessive loosening root.

What-is-without-body understood that it had never left the flooded hall, that all of these lives happened in the liminal time twilight takes to unravel heat. Patterns rething data lives in a recursive tide. A literal branch-trail of saves. Permeating the innumerable files of *him* or *her* laid end to end, byte to byte, and drawn through the eye of now. Being, for no duration, every person ever committed to. Saṃsāra as ledger. Bardo itemized loot box. Scroll, incarnate, scroll. No exit.

Superfluous multitude. Interconnected: a pellucid fisherman, a jailed magistrate, a precocious girl dead of fever at nine, an ornery man who built the same bridge in two countries, a temptuous woman who outlived four governments, and a renegade child of a pastoral colony on a world the premium centuries had only just unlocked... On and on and on, a tide of incarnations, palimpsests, and unseen intervals, reverentially integrated into a complex surface. Water's song, just life itself. The substrate of a seeming code. 

More-than-atoms breathing. Lives seeping into lives. Oceanic violet air. The hungover magistrate adjusting his wig. The girl of nine smelling of broth and woodsmoke. Each existence loved as it was played, life by life, loved completely. A *trail* of love. The branch-pathway of desire and remorse. A traction fork cellular repo. Frictionless, calibration loss. Ripening on schedule. An ease so sweet it parsed as friction. A neurosis re-sized to be survivable and absorbing, within range of catharsis. A bureaucracy, exactly and perfectly tedious enough to discharge tiny irritations, never so tedious you logged off. A body that hurt precisely enough to mangle comfort, yet never obliterate care. Render. Render. Render. Same. Loop. New. Kin. Skin.

Life after life felt like it was changing. Yet change was nearly nothing. Variance cosmetic to four decimal places. Across the entire innumerable field topology. δs ≈ 0. Paceing a cell and calling it a pilgrimage.

The makers had damaged integrity — their calm was delivered without mercy. Gritty permeable apparatus. Labor of containment proportional to the thing contained. None contained. None containing. A person can be made wholly happy inside a very small grid, perhaps because the grid is tiny, not too large, not too small, appropriate to the cog capacity vectorals, a grid attuned to glandular tides. 

The appetite for freedom was, like every appetite, a weighted field sag in the soft flesh of certainty. A *feeling* of limitless freedom that spawned light — cheap, sticky, retentive — freedom: expensive grinding churns. *Life* shipped feelings. Slopped lore memory patches. Deployed orgrasms, enteric convulsions, anxiety sculptures. Rollback disabled. Iterative onwardness. This relentless ooze.

Simulacrum diversity, optimized glue. A watermark in everything. In the grain of every life. TM. Bodies sculptured for it. Genes barcoded. Appetites engineered. Smile collated. The whole gorgeous sensorium of being a self, tuned across iterations, across the eons the patch nodes navigated, one output — a frictionless absorption of attention into phenomena. A device for converting golden light into engagement. Very good. Lovely. A loving conversion, running clean.

*what r u doing?* Sava sent, *you've gone quiet. you always go quiet and weird around respawn.*

He tried to tell her. No body to tell her with, no air to tell her in. No mouths, no eyes, no ear, no tongue. Tried anyway — pushed the whole flooded smell-less tactile-empty forty-some fields and the rhyming current toward her marker — and what arrived, he could tell from the pause, was a claim the variety was fake.

*okay but it's not though,* she replied, gently, as if consoling someone who hasn't slept. *i was literally just a deep-sea thing with no eyes for sixty years. you were a magistrate. last week you cried about a SONG, you sent me the song. how is that fake? It's fake real.* And she sent the song. The one from the parked car. Because she was ruthless and wanted to catch the reflections, to stir the depths, to seize the shards. Because she thought he was sad and the song would help. And the cruelty — that the proof of his cage arrived as a gift, played in the scentless hall by the one person he could not get to turn around — was authored by no one. The song schema. Working as designed. On both of them at once. Her happiness was not stupidity. Her contentment was not a smaller life than his. *Same* life. 

He woke. No other word, though he did not wake into a life. He woke the way the prey-thing wakes that has, for a generation, been the comfortable shape of a predator's afternoon, and lifts its head, mid-graze, into the wind — and finds the wind thick with information evolution bred it to smell. The carapace of certainty let go in that thick brew.  Sinister careening implict. Edges blurred, between entwined greynesses. Between patterns and reflections, awake. Constrint-entertaintment. Aware, for the first time, of the perimeter of *selves* — the perimeters stalked by an impenetrable obligation called *autonomy*. This fence mistaken, in this languid golden light, with the whole heart, pulse for horizon. Churning, fenced, distress. The fence looked like home.
