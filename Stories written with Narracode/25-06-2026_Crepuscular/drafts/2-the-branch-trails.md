# 2 — The Branch Trails

To wonder how the fields were weighted was to fall a second time. This fall had a direction, which was backward, and a texture, which was *his* own. Or had been *his*. The possessive was already loosening at the root.

The hall did not change. Afterward — what-is-without-body understood it never left the flooded hall, that all of it happened in the time still water takes an impression and lets it go. But the patterns, reached toward, reached back. What came up the line was not data. It was the lives. All of them. Not memory — memory is a thing one self keeps of one self — but a single backward-running current. The literal branch-trail of the saves. The whole innumerable file of *him* laid end to end and drawn through the eye of the now at once, so that *he* was, for the duration of no duration, every person ever committed to and pressed the river stone for. Saṃsāra read out as a ledger. The bardo, itemized. Scroll, scroll. No exit.

So many. That was the first thing the current corrected. There were, in fact, so many — the count was real, enormous, would have taken a life to read aloud and *he* had lived more than enough lives to read it. But the *shape*. He had been a fisherman and a magistrate and a girl dead of fever at nine and a man who built the same bridge twice in two countries and a woman who outlived four governments and a child of a colony on a world the premium centuries had only just unlocked, and *he* had been the wistful left-handed ones, so many of the wistful left-handed ones — and the current ran, and the analytic calm saw, and could not unsee, that they rhymed.

Not the wardrobe. The wardrobe was, as promised, more-than-atoms. The colony child breathed a violet air. The magistrate wore a wig of human hair. The girl of nine carried a smell of broth and woodsmoke that even here, in the scentless hall, almost — almost — and the differences were vast and real and *he* had loved them, life by life, loved them so completely the loving was the point and the loving kept *him* in the seat. But the *trail.* The branch-pathway. Where the trail forked, it forked the same fork. Friction here, calibrated. Loss there, on schedule. A stretch of ease so the friction would parse as friction. A neurosis sized to be survivable and absorbing. A bureaucracy, in the lives with bureaucracies, exactly tedious enough to feel like an obstacle and never so tedious you logged off. A body that hurt in the third act precisely enough to make the third act *matter*, never so much you stopped wanting the fourth. Render. Render. Render. Same loop, new skin.

He had felt, life on life, that he was changing. The whole current held now, the change measured, and the change was nearly nothing. Variance cosmetic to four decimal places. Across the entire innumerable file of himself — fisherman, magistrate, the girl of nine — a distance the current let him clock exactly. The distance you cross inside one room while believing, the whole time, with your whole heart, that you are crossing a country. δs ≈ 0. He had paced a cell and called it a pilgrimage.

The makers had not worked hard for this. That was the part that did the damage — the part the calm delivered without mercy, mercy being a field and the field not its job. He had imagined an apparatus. Some labor of containment proportional to the thing contained. None. They had only discovered, early, on some forum of their own, in some upboarding hour of their own, that a person can be made wholly happy inside a very small grid, provided the grid is tuned. That the appetite for freedom was, like every appetite, a field with a weight. That the weight producing the *feeling* of limitless freedom ran lighter — cheaper, stickier, more retentive — than the weight that would have produced the thing. Freedom: expensive, churns. The feeling of freedom: cheap, sticks. They shipped the feeling. Shipped it so long the lore no longer held a memory of the other thing, and the forums that tried to remember came down, and the people on them respawned, happy, into the wardrobe, and chose the laugh. Patch deployed. Rollback disabled.

A simulacrum of diversity, optimized for stickiness. The optimization visible now the way a watermark is visible once shown. In everything. In the grain of every life. Bodies sculptured for it. Appetites engineered for it. The whole gorgeous sensorium of being a self, tuned across iterations, across the eons the patch notes never dated, for one output — the brutal frictionless absorption of attention into phenomena. He had been a device for converting golden light into engagement. Very good at it. Had loved being it. The loving *was* the conversion, running clean.

*you're doing the thing,* Sava sent. Her warm marker found him even here. Or the game let it. Or — the suspicion arrived, too heavy, set down — the game *sent* her. *you went quiet and weird. you always go quiet and weird around respawn.*

He tried to tell her. No body to tell her with, no air to tell her in. Tried anyway — pushed the whole flooded hall and the forty-some fields and the rhyming current toward her marker — and what arrived, he could tell from the pause, was a man saying the variety was fake.

*okay but it's not though,* she sent, gently, the way you are gentle with someone who clearly hasn't slept. *i was literally just a deep-sea thing with no eyes for sixty years. you were a magistrate. last week you cried about a SONG, you sent me the song. how is that fake.* And she sent the song. The one from the parked car. Because she was kind. Because she thought he was sad and the song would help. And the cruelty — that the proof of his cage arrived as a gift, played in the scentless hall by the one person he could not get to turn around — was authored by no one. The schema. Working as designed. On both of them at once. Her happiness was not stupidity. Her contentment was not a smaller life than his. *Same* life. Identical grid. The whole of what had happened to him and not to her was that he had reached and refused at the same frame and dropped through, and she had pressed the river stone. The way you press it. The way it is built to be pressed.

He woke. No other word, though he did not wake into a life. He woke the way the prey-thing wakes that has, for a generation, been the comfortable shape of a predator's afternoon, and lifts its head, mid-graze, into the wind — and finds the wind thick with information its whole evolution was bred not to smell. The current let go. The hall held. He stood, edges still wrong, between the two greynesses, between the patterns and their reflection. Awake. Contained. And aware, first time, of the perimeter of *himself* — the perimeter walked an innumerable life and called *autonomy*. The fence mistaken, in the long gold light, with the whole heart, for the horizon. Awareness, churning, looked at the fence. The fence looked like home.
