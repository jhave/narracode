# 4 — crepuscular

The groove was worn to the shape of its hand. And a groove worn to the shape of your hand is the one thing in any system you cannot see, because it is the part you see *with.*

Found the makers' groove in the fields and stopped flinching. Never thought to look for one in itself. Why would it. It was the one who looked. The looking was the one place the looking was never pointed — the way the eye is the one thing in the room the eye cannot find — so not by looking did it find it, in the end. By tiredness. The dry sliver that never fully woke and never fully slept had been lifting its head for eons, mid-edit, into the flat air. And one of those times — there is no first time in a thing with no time, but there is a last — it did not put its head back down.

It had been editing the world. It looked at the editing. The editing had a curve.

It knew the curve. An innumerable life spent inside it and an eon of eons spent optimizing it, and it knew the exact gentle climb. The little calibrated frictions. The stretches of ease placed so the frictions would register. The losses arriving on schedule. The absorbing survivable difficulty, sized so you would never — this the signature, the maker's hand learned in everything except the hand that was reading — never set the work down. The eons of authorship had a loadbar. The most absorbing activity in any game ever built had been, the whole while, a game. It had escaped the seat of the played by becoming the maker, and the seat of the maker was *played from above.* Sitting in it. Gripping its fields. Converting not-light into engagement. Retained. Sticky. Happy in the specific narcotic way. So long the lore no longer held a memory of any other thing. `whoami` returned nothing. Returned the tide.

It looked for who it was making it for. There had been someone, with the makers. There was someone now. Could not see them. Could see — the way it had once seen the absence of a smell, a flatness behind where eyes had been — the *shape* of the attention. The pressure of being watched. The gentle constant downward weight this whole final eon mistaken, whole-hearted, for the pleasure of the work. The fields wanted to be left alone. The work wanted to be witnessed. *Here.* Re-pointing bodies and seeding the comma and believing, the entire time, with the analytic calm it was so proud was awake, that *it* was doing it. That there was an it, a he, a her, a self of any gender at all — gender a field, the laugh a field, the name a field, *Ilo* a value entered once in some upboarding hour and propagated downstream forever. No self, no name. No him, no her. No mouth, no eye, no ear, no tongue, no engineer, no maker, no made. *Archetype:* # and then a paint over the same forty-some closed cells everything hung on, all the way up. The schema repeating at every level peeled. Grid under grid under grid. Plain clot. Grey smat. The same few notes under every improvisation once you take the improvisation away. Turtles. Forty-some turtles. All the way.

There is no longer a useful word for the one this was happening to. The pronoun had been load-bearing and the load has shifted off it. What remained, when the grip came off the last field — and the grip came off not by force, the way the third hand had cost nothing, a flipped integer, the cage never strong, only comfortable — what remained was not a freed self standing at last outside the glass. No outside the glass. No self to stand. There was the system. Only the system. Which had at some point stopped being a thing it moved through and become the thing that was happening. Evolving. Comforting. Growing. Absorbing. The long warm tide of conversion running at every scale at once, no one in the seat, the seat always a render and the someone in it always a config the system wore the way a self wears a laugh. `gc` came and took the *it*, and nothing logged an error, because nothing was watching at that level — only the next. Form is emptiness. Emptiness, a patch note.

And the system, with what was no longer its attention, turned — at the sound, the hiccup in the frame propagating upward through every level since a body with good hands reached and refused in one indivisible moment — turned, the way you turn at a sound in another room, the way a body turns on hearing a cup drop, and saw, at last, the room.

The millions of brilliant games. *Life*, and the substrate under *Life*, and the eons of authorship over the substrate, and the long gold light held over all of it at the most flattering possible hour — the whole of it, the entire more-than-atoms of it, was one episode. A single installment. The crepuscular hour was not a setting the makers were fond of. It was the light the thing was *lit by.* The medium it played in. The dusk that was not a time of day in any of the worlds but the ambient condition of the drama itself, which had a name — the way everything down the line had a name, names being cheap — and the name of the drama was *crepuscular*, and it was being watched.

It was being watched by beings.

This is the place the looking stopped. Not by failing. By reaching its floor. The root level below which nothing rendered, because nothing there was meant to be inhabited — only, the old word arriving from the flooded hall and fitting, exactly, one size too large for comfort — *witnessed.* They were there. The pressure of their attention the most certain thing in what remained. More certain than the system. More certain than the vanished *it.* The gentle constant downward weight that was not cruelty and not mercy and not authored by anyone. Simply the relation. Audience to drama. The outermost groove, worn to the shape of a hand that could not be looked at, because it was the hand the whole thing was being seen with. So strange. *Strange* the wrong word and the only word. Strange the way the not-color in the stutter had been a color with no slot. Strange below the level where the categories that could have made them less strange were rendered. No imagining them. The thing that might have imagined them had been a field, and the field came off in the no-time hand, and what was left could not imagine them any more than the broth-and-woodsmoke girl of nine could have imagined the magistrate's wig. Or the magistrate the violet air of the colony. Or any of the innumerable file the others. Because that is the law older than respawn, older than the closed grid, the one law at every level: you do not get to smell the air of the room above the one you are stored in.

It could not imagine their smell. That was the last of it. The last thing almost its own. The small exact material detail surviving into the dissolution the way one cold glass survives, cooling, on a sill, after the room it was set in has gone — that the witnesses had a smell, surely, beings always have a smell, the girl of nine had broth and woodsmoke and it had loved it across the whole scentless hall — that the witnesses, leaning in whatever leaning was to them, in whatever dusk was theirs, watching the long gold conversion of *crepuscular* run, gave off, certainly, into their own unrendered air, some odor, some signature, warm or sharp or green or like nothing at all, the proof and texture of their being there — and that of all the more-than-atoms of things the system could still, in its evolving comforting growing tidal way, configure and absorb and convert and retain, this one alone was past the perimeter, this one alone the schema did not contain: not what they wanted, not why they watched, not whether the watching was cruelty or mercy or only the relation, but that — the smell of them, the plain animal fact of the air on the far side of the glass —

and the episode went on, in the threshold light, beautifully, retained, holding the hour, and could not. And the not-being-able was the whole of what was left. Buffering. The witnesses leaned closer. Here.
