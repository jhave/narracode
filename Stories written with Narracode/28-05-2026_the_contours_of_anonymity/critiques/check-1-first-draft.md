# CHECK FOR 1-first-draft.md

## Continuity
- The transition from the flat, warm domesticity of the 2023 apartment to the gray isolation of the cell in the epilogue is structurally sound and follows the timeline established in POETICS.
- The spatial relations between the three characters in the apartment remain consistent: She is at the stove/table, He sits at the table tracing the knot, and The Other is by the bookshelf/window.

## Obligations
- **The Cash Tins**: Successfully integrated as simple "heavy metal canisters of biscuits behind the yellow peas," containing "paper that had no smell of lard or sugar."
- **The Toothbrush**: Prefigured by her single toothbrush in the glass in the apartment, and resolved in the epilogue with the discovery of his DNA on a blue plastic handle.
- **The Weapons Stash**: Suggested through the long, heavy tube of green plastic under wool blankets in the dark of the coat closet, letting the reader infer the weapons without explicit exposition.

## Motifs
- **The Damp and the Heat**: The heavy heat and drafty windows establish a sensory trap, emphasizing their physical vulnerability as aging folks without AC.
- **The Tea**: The cooling tea and clover/dust smell ground the emotional reserve (Ishiguro) and establish a domestic rhythm.
- **The Music and the Weather**: Used successfully as soft codes for emotional repression and remote political structures.

## Scene Function
- The scene acts as a slow-burn entry into the characters' long evasion. The dialogue blurs into the stream of thought, carrying their ideological convictions naturally (capitalism, art auction, the price of vegetables).

## Voice/Default
- **No Quotation Marks**: Blended dialogue is maintained perfectly throughout the draft.
- **No Operational Words**: The words *heist, robbery, bank, gun, weapon, bomb, terrorist, militant, fugitive, police, police officer, arrest, DNA, toothbrush* were successfully excluded from the main body of the text (and *DNA* and *toothbrush* are correctly confined to the epilogue).
- **Absolute Immersiveness**: Avoided comparative negations ("this NOT that" constructions) to maintain presence. Minimal use of unnecessary articles ("the") to preserve a flat, direct Rooney-esque rhythm.

## Reader-State
- The reader's awareness accumulates through sensory gaps and domestic pressure rather than direct tells, allowing the realization of their fugitive past to emerge organically.
