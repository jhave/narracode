# Time Constants

## Chronology
- **The Past (17-year period)**: A long, slow series of extractions (robberies) from banks and grocery stores, conducted entirely to finance their survival and remain anonymous.
- **The Present (2023)**: A late afternoon, the light fading through the window, tea cooling in the cups.
- **The Future (Unspecified Year, ~2024)**: Her eventual arrest, the discovery of his DNA on a toothbrush, and their continued survival in the dark.

## Durations
- **The Afternoon**: Approximately two hours of quiet conversation and tea-drinking.
- **The Evasion**: Several decades of living underground, with seventeen years of active, quiet criminal subsistence.

## Constraints
- No modern years or calendar dates are named in the narrative, maintaining the unspecified nature of time.
- The silence is constant; they do not speak of the calendar, only the light and the damp.
