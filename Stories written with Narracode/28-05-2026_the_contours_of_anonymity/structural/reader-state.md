# Reader State

## Current Understanding
- The reader begins with the impression of an ordinary, somewhat cluttered afternoon between three aging friends.
- Through accumulated inferences (the cash tins, the wigs, the toothbrush, the silence, the ideological asides), the reader gradually realizes these are fugitives who have survived outside the law for seventeen years.

## Expectations
- The reader expects some kind of dramatic operational slip-on, or a direct discussion of a robbery.
- The story defies this by keeping the focus entirely on the domestic, the somatic, and the quiet ideological convictions.

## Plausible Defiance Paths
- The characters never discuss their crimes, their past heists, or their futures. The evasion is treated as an administrative, quiet reality.
- The capture happens off-screen, between the main scene and the epilogue.

## Productive Mystery
- The lack of names, years, and specific geographic locations forces the reader to focus entirely on the emotional reserve and the weight of their lives.

## Accidental Confusion Risk
- The reader might miss the significance of their lifestyle if the clues (cash tin, wigs, toothbrush) are too subtle. These must be described with sharp, clear presence.
