# Graph

## Characters and Entities
- **She**: The central figure, managing the apartment, carrying the long-term history of their escape.
- **He**: Her former lover, still linked to her by an old, unspoken intimacy.
- **The Other**: The third member of the trio, whom she tolerates but finds prickly, bound by professional necessity.
- **The Apparatus**: The invisible state that searches for them, a constant background shadow.

## Relations
- **She <-> He**: Former lovers, deep somatic understanding, unspoken tenderness.
- **She <-> The Other**: Tolerated collaborator, professional distance, slight irritation.
- **He <-> The Other**: Colleagues in silence, shared survival history.
- **The Trio <-> The Apparatus**: Absolute evasion, quiet resistance, inevitable eventual friction.

## Relation Changes
- **She -> The State**: Reconciled to the eventual collapse of their evasion, already captured in spirit by the weight of her choice.
