# Scene Ledger

## Scenes
- **Scene 1: The Kitchen and the Tea**: Setting the cluttered space, the innocuous old friend chatting, the cold afternoon light. The dialogue blends into their movements around the kettle and the table.
- **Scene 2: The Conversation on the Market**: A gradual shift to a weary, relaxed discussion of capitalism, patriarchy, and the local vegetable sellers, highlighting their ideological convictions.
- **Scene 3: The Toothbrush and the Draft**: The physical details of the bathroom, the draft under the door, the silent somatic connections between her and the former lover, the cold toleration of the third.
- **Epilogue: The Cell and the Bed**: Her thoughts in prison under interrogation, her total insulation from their futures, and the peaceful retirement of the two men elsewhere.

## Open Scene-Level Questions
- How to keep the transition between the tea conversation and the ideological discussion entirely natural and casual.
- Balancing the sensory stream of consciousness (Woolf) with the flat, direct dialogue (Rooney).
