# Character Interiority

## Characters
- **She**: A sense of being already captured, an acceptance of her age and the inevitable end of their run. She enjoys the warmth of the room and the somatic presence of her former lover, but maintains a rigid, professional distance from the details of their survival. Her ideological convictions remain absolute but are lived rather than shouted.
- **He**: The former lover, gentle, feeling the weight of the years in his bones, carrying the silent memory of their old love. He is a professional of silence.
- **The Other**: Detached, slightly cold, surviving through a pure mechanical adherence to the code. He is a presence that she tolerates, a reminder of the practical necessity that has governed their lives.

## Arc Potentials
- **She**: Moving from the peaceful domestic afternoon of 2023 to the total isolation of the cell, where her silence becomes her final act of love and solidarity.
- **The Collaborators**: Moving from the shared kitchen to two separate, anonymous beds, settling into their quiet old age under different skies.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points
- Her realization that she does not know, and will never know, where they have gone—and that this ignorance is their greatest safety and their final connection.
