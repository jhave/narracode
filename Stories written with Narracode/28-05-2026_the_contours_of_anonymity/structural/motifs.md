# Motifs

## Active
- **The Teacup / The Kettle**: A quiet, repetitive focus on domestic warmth. The steam rising, the metal rim, the cooling of the porcelain.
- **The Window / The Street**: The bridge to the world that seeks them, the gray light coming through, the occasional glance outside.
- **The Cash Tin**: A simple, unremarked storage device that represents decades of risk, treated as mundane domestic clutter.
- **The Toothbrush**: A domestic utensil of somatic hygiene, carrying the trace of their eventual betrayal.
- **The Draft**: The cold air under the door, the physical sensation of their exposure to the elements.

## Transformations
- **The Toothbrush**: From a simple household item in a glass to the legal, scientific fact that seals their evasion.

## Avoid Overuse
- The cash tins should not be opened or touched repeatedly; they should just be part of the furniture.
- The glances out the window must remain casual, not paranoid.
