# History

## Established
- They have survived for decades by refusing to participate in the legal and economic structures of the state.
- Their income is drawn from a long sequence of careful, silent bank and grocery store extractions, treated strictly as administrative work.
- She and the former lover share a somatic, emotional intimacy that has survived their underground transition.
- They have established a strict code of mutual insulation: no one knows the other's contingencies or new names.

## Said
- Simple comments about the kettle, the cold light, the local market, and the price of vegetables.
- A relaxed, regular, reconciled discussion about capitalism, patriarchy, and the delegitimization of historical resistance.

## Refused
- Any mention of names, dates, specific cities, or countries.
- Any direct discussion of the extractions, their weapons, or their plans for flight.
- Any open displays of sentimentality or panic regarding their inevitable capture.
