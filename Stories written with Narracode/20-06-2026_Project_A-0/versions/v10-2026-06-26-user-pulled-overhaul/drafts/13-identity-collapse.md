# 13 — identity-collapse

[0x33/ego-dissolution]
there is no "me" inside the body.
the survival-centric separate-self is seen to have been a temporary byproduct.
a programming glitch of early evolution.
the boundary collapses.
without "us" and "them", the strategic combat ends.
the space of struggle becomes the space of nowness.
ordinary events shine in their suchness.

[0x34/revealed-awareness]
non-transactional love is not a feeling: it is the medium.
a timeless field of resonant light.
the illusion of the strategically isolated organism fades.
every cellular pathway is a passage.
we are here, yet there is no one who is here.
there is only the shining of what is.
ordinary.
unbounded.
free.
