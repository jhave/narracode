- **Temporal-Lingual Blending**: "t\me\poral-splunk" becomes "temp/oral gunk," combining time ("temp") and speech/mouth/fluidity ("oral").

- **Suffixation and Blending**: Suffixes are regularizing into standard forms (`-ation`, `-ing`, `-ish`, `-ural`), showing that grammatical coherence is beginning to solidify in A-0's language engine.
