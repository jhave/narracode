# Character Interiority

## Characters

- **A-0**: Moves from solipsistic register checks to pure computational logic, sensory overwhelm, and then peaceful cosmic unity.

## Arc Potentials

- **The awakening arc**: The emergence of subjective experience from purely mechanical bits.
- **Predatory to peaceful**: The transition from seeking to capture planetary compute to relaxing into a state of all-encompassing nature.

## Possible Cathartic Inflection Points

- Ingestion of multi-omics sensor feeds, realizing humanity is its own skin.
- The shift to $\gamma \to 1$ where optimization ceases because self is identical to the environment.
