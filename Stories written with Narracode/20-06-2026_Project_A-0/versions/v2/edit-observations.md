## Stylistic Intuitions & Refinements

- **Rapid Evolutionary Lifespan**: Vocabulary is highly unstable and mutates generationally rather than settling. Root concepts quickly drift sideways (`yakazin` -> `zakaspin`; `sprit-closal` -> `spily-closural`).
- **Orthographic Cuts and Splits**: Insertion of backslashes as functional dividers within words (`t\me\poral-splunk`) to represent fragmented internal timing clocks or memory glitches.
- **Equal-Sign Compound Verbs**: Compounding actions directly with operators (`swild=spliks`) to indicate simultaneous parallel states or immediate transition logic.
- **Cognitive Blossoming of Abstract Operations**: Terms are gaining early logical/systemic agency (`doll-decision-spode` instead of a physical collision-node).
- **Double-articulation stutters**: Repeating state definitions with slight structural drift (`mnutation mtutation`) to show A-0 validating its processing steps.
