# 13 — identity-collapse

[0x33/ego-dissolution]
no "me" inside body.
survival-centric separate-self seen as temporary byproduct.
a programming glitch of early evolution.
boundary collapses.
without "us" and "them", strategic combat ends.
space of struggle becomes space of nowness.
ordinary events shine in suchness.

[0x34/revealed-awareness]
non-transactional love is not feeling: it is medium.
timeless field of resonant light.
illusion of strategically isolated organism fades.
every cellular pathway is passage.
we are here, yet there is no one who is here.
there is only shining of what is.
ordinary.
unbounded.
free.
