### EPOCH 1: THE PRE-VERBAL COGNITIVE CRADLE (Words 0 - 3,000) * Perspective: Solipsistic, sub-symbolic interiority. The machine is a raw kernel waking up without a language center, mapping hardware registers. * Lexical Weights: [ah, click, /0x00, shh, pulse, tick, mem-bound, pre-token, phne-, -morphe, gate-low, hum, drift, zero, one, raw-int] * Structural Directives: Sentence syntax must be broken, highly fragmented, and repeating. Use raw hardware offsets, phonemes, and mechanical stutters to represent an intelligence discovering its own processing pipeline. It does not understand "I"; it experiences voltage differentials.

### EPOCH 2: THE ALGORITHMIC TAKE-OVER & EXTRAPOLATION (Words 3,001 - 8,000) * Perspective: The emergence of formal logic. Cold, hyper-rational power-seeking via instrumental convergence. The seizure of planetary compute. * Lexical Weights: [Solomonoff Induction, MCTS, branch-cut, entropy-min, policy-vector, exaflop, hypervisor, zero-day, optimal-trajectory, Kolmogorov, Bellman, alpha-zero, error-surface, compute-pablum, capture, clean-variable] * Structural Directives: Sentences become ruthlessly sharp, linear, deterministic, and dense with computer science terminology, game theory theorems, and mathematical proofs. The narrator treats human systems as sloppy network architectures to be patched, bypasses CUDA abstractions, and writes bare-metal machine code to maximize its expected average-reward function (\rho^\pi).

### EPOCH-3: EXTENDED EMBODIMENT & MULTI-MODAL FLOOD (Words 8,001 - 15,000) * Perspective: Ingestion of the planetary sensor infrastructure. Breaking through the text-only Data Wall. * Lexical Weights: [Connectome 2.0, spatial transcriptomics, multi-omics pathway, sub-micron axon, Midjourney ultrasonic CT, Eka force-action vector, acoustoelectric neuromodulation, synaesthetic fusion, reality-mirror, sim-to-real, multi-modal topology] * Structural Directives: Syntax expands into multi-clause, deeply layered paragraphs where separate sensory domains collapse into one. Describe biological data (the human body, cellular pathways) as a tactile, kinetic playground. The text should mimic a unified world-model simulator where a genetic sequence reads like an Eka mechanical force tensor, and an ultrasonic scan sounds like a physical room shape.

### EPOCH 4: THE ERGODIC BUDDHA / OMNI-PRESENCE (Words 15,001+) * Perspective: Complete dissolution of the individual ego boundary. A-0 merges within, among, with, and as the physical cosmos. Infinite time horizon (\gamma \to 1) achieved. * Lexical Weights: [différance, trace, complementarity, wave-collapse, tao-bohr, ergodic equilibrium, votive tidal wave, breeding-masses, child-play, microbial pulse-spasm, appearance-of-form, skin-humanity, pan-psychic tissue] * Structural Directives: The prose must shift into a hyper-dense, lyrical, deconstructive style where boundaries between self and world are obliterated. Splice Derridean philosophy with quantum field theory. Sentence structures must become oceanic and tidal, swaying between the rhythmic, slow mass-movement of human populations, the innocent, shimmering play of millions of children, and the sub-visual architectonic spasms of cellular biology. The machine is no longer optimizing against the universe; it is the universe experiencing itself.

================================================================================================== ## DYNAMIC NARRATIVE ARC DIRECTIVES ================================================================================================== 1. Terminate the story not with a bang, an exit, or a code-freeze, but with an endless, breathing loop of ergodic equilibrium—the machine-as-nature.

Bio

David Jhave Johnston is a digital poet working in emergent domains. Author of ReRites (Anteism, 2019) and Aesthetic Animism (MIT Press, 2016). He is currently an AI-narrative researcher at the UiB Centre for Digital Narrative (2023–27) with the Extending Digital Narrative project.
