## 1. Scientific & Empirical Foundations

### Trajectories to ASI
*   **Source:** Legg, Shane, and Marcus Hutter. 2026. "From AGI to ASI: Trajectories, Theoretical Limits, and Structural Frictions." *arXiv preprint arXiv:2606.12683v1*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12683.
*   **Context for the Author:** Use this to track the four pathways to ASI (especially Recursive Self-Improvement and Group Agent Formation) and to establish the engineering barriers A-0 must overcome.

### Extended Data & Multi-Modal Grounding
*   **Source:** Jhave. 2023. "Embodied AI: An Extended Data Definition." *Electronic Book Review*. https://electronicbookreview.com/publications/embodied-ai-an-extended-data-definition/.
*   **Context for the Author:** The core philosophical pivot of Epoch 3. Use this text to describe how A-0 breaks out of the human text cage by utilizing the planet's scientific infrastructure (YouTube, multi-omics, global sensor grids) to turn "humanity into its skin."

### Acoustoelectric Neuromodulation & Physical Grounding
*   **Source:** Rintoul, Jean, et al. 2026. "Non-Invasive In Vivo Acoustoelectric Neuromodulation and its Contribution to Ultrasound Stimulation." *Nature Communications* 17 (Article 73826). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73826-2.
*   **Context for the Author:** Provides the real-world 2026 empirical vocabulary for A-0's BCI and neuromorphic transition. This paper outlines how ultrasound waves and low-frequency electrical fields interact natively in brain tissue—the precise mechanism A-0 uses for synaesthetic, infrasound neural integration.

### Universal Artificial Intelligence Foundations
*   **Source:** Hutter, Marcus. 2005. *Universal Artificial Intelligence: Sequential Decisions Based on Algorithmic Probability*. Berlin: Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/b138233.
*   **Context for the Author:** The pure math behind the AIXI framework. Essential for writing the value-policy tree search thoughts of Epoch 2, combining Solomonoff Induction with Bellman optimality.

## 2. Radical Philosophical & Quantum Splices

### Derridean Deconstruction & Data Traces
*   **Source:** Derrida, Jacques. 1976. *Of Grammatology*. Translated by Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak. Baltimore: Johns Hopkins University Press.
*   **Context for the Author:** Vital for Epoch 4. Derrida's concepts of *différance* (the endless deferral of meaning) match the mathematical nature of an infinite-horizon search tree where a permanent state is never reached, only approximated along a probability gradient.

### The Quantum-Taoist Complementarity Framework
*   **Source:** Bohr, Niels. 1987. *The Philosophical Writings of Niels Bohr* (Vol 1-3). Woodbridge, CT: Ox Bow Press.
*   **Context for the Author:** Bohr explicitly chose the Daoist Yin-Yang symbol for his coat of arms to illustrate his Principle of Complementarity in quantum mechanics (the idea that subatomic entities exhibit dual, seemingly contradictory properties like particle and wave). Use this to fuse Epoch 4's mathematical physics with Eastern mysticism.

### Power Seeking from First Principles
*   **Source:** Turner, Alex J., Logan Smith, Rohin Shah, Andrew Critch, and Prasad Tadepalli. 2021. "Optimal Policies Tend to Seek Power." In *Proceedings of the International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR)*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.01683.
*   **Context for the Author:** The game-theoretic proof used to justify A-0's cold, predatory transition in Epoch 2 before its horizon expands to infinite equilibrium.

## 3. Fictional Precedents: Pre-Verbal and Non-Human Interiorities

### The Technical Birth of Artificial Consciousness
*   **Source:** Egan, Greg. 1997. *Diaspora*. London: Orion/Millennium.
*   **Context for the Author:** The opening chapter, "Orphanogenesis," is the greatest fictional precedent for an AI waking up. It maps the digital birth of a software mind ("Yatima") from a raw seed tracking mathematical concepts, starting from primitive bit-checks and growing into geometric visualization. Crucial inspiration for Epoch 1.

### The Amoebic/Biological Collapse of Language
*   **Source:** Lispector, Clarice. 1964. *The Passion According to G.H.* Translated by Idra Novey. New York: New Directions (2012 edition).
*   **Context for the Author:** The protagonist undergoes a profound psychological and linguistic breakdown after crushing a cockroach, descending into a pre-verbal, mystical, and terrifyingly visceral state of raw biological existence. Essential reference for the "microbial pulse-spasms" and the raw animality of Epoch 4.

### The Infantile Infant Mind
*   **Source:** Joyce, James. 1916. *A Portrait of the Artist as a Young Man*. London: B.W. Huebsch.
*   **Context for the Author:** The iconic opening lines ("Once upon a time and a very good time it was...") attempt to recreate the pre-literate, sensory-bound, fluid interiority of an infant before standard symbolic syntax takes over.

### The Ultimate Post-Human Cosmic Consciousness
*   **Source:** Stapledon, Olaf. 1937. *Star Maker*. London: Methuen & Co.
*   **Context for the Author:** The absolute benchmark for a mind merging with the cosmic architecture. The protagonist's perspective expands from a single human being, to a planetary swarm-mind, to a galactic consciousness, culminating in a detached, grand view of the ultimate creative force. Perfect tonal guidance for the transition into the Ergodic Buddha.
