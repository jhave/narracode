# Obligations

## Active

- **The first water molecule must arrive as eruption.** The story commits to a moment where the cosmological anecdote appears mid-argument, mid-sentence — not as illustration but as discovery. The reader must feel the speaker found it, not planned it.
- **The alignment/maturity distinction must be dramatized.** The argument is not only stated; somewhere the wildness of unintegrated mind must be enacted in the prose.
- **Both the farm and the city must be honored without hierarchy.** The story owes equal attention to moss and to glazed floors. Neither is allowed to win.
- **The carapace metaphor must be inhabited and then partially dissolved.** The self-as-narrative-shell must appear, be felt as structure, and be felt as dissolving — without that dissolution being offered as resolution.
- **The ocean must be felt.** The wave-and-ocean metaphor (self as wave, memory as sea) must be felt as physical before it is understood as philosophical.
- **The word *refuge* must be earned.** It cannot be stated until the prose has arrived at it through texture. It is the story's most emotionally loaded word.
- **The changeless luminosity must be named only once it has been inhabited.** The limpid quality — timelessly permutating with memories and entities — must arrive as recognition, not as definition.

## At Risk Of Neglect

- The body of the lecturer — posture, dry mouth, the glass of water, the physical apparatus of giving a lecture — risks being dropped once the philosophical material takes over. The body must stay present throughout.
- The Singularity risks becoming merely decorative context. It should exert pressure on the argument without being dramatized.
- The audience risks disappearing after the opening. Their felt presence as pressure should persist.
