# Time Constants

## Chronology

- **~12 billion years ago**: The first water molecule arises in deep space.
- **Lecturer's childhood/adolescence**: The farm. Seasons, moss, oak, fern. Duration unspecified.
- **Lecturer's young adulthood**: Entry into the city; the mall; the commodified ecosystem.
- **Recent past**: Graduate research; the miraculous appointment to the research chair.
- **Present**: The lecture. A specific day, time of day TBD, location TBD.

## Durations

- The lecture itself: a single sitting. Duration unspecified but felt as one continuous event.
- The academic job market devastation: ongoing; began with the promulgation of expert knowledge through chatbots; the job market thinning is presented as background fact, not recent event.
- The Singularity approach: ongoing, near-fact. Not dated precisely.

## Constraints

- The lecture is not a series of separate days. All of the story's present-tense action occurs within a single lecture.
- The cosmological timeline (12 billion years) and the personal timeline (one life) must coexist without one miniaturizing the other.
- The Singularity is background and cannot be given a precise date without over-specifying the near-future setting.
