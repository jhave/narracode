# Edit observations — AI-signature catalogue (pre-revision, 2026-05-29)

This loop is a model-driven revision (not prompter manual micro-edits). Catalogue of machine-prose signatures found in the v3 drafts and targeted for removal:

1. **Anaphoric definition-stacks / tricolons of abstract nouns.** `Maturity is …` ×5 in 3-thesis; `it implies force; it implies domination; … repression`; `Alignment requires a police force. Alignment requires prisons.`; `The system obeys. The system is observed obeying. The observation produces the obedience.` The sermon cadence of generated definition. POETICS already bans these; enforce it.

2. **Every paragraph resolving into a polished epigram.** Evenly spaced, same wattage. Real extempore thought does not peak every 80 words. Let paragraphs end flat/unresolved; reserve the aphorism for detonation.

3. **"a kind of" hedge tic** (×4 in lecture-opening) + "in some sense" (×2 in thesis) + "in a way that meant several things at once." Gestures at depth instead of committing. Cut ~70%.

4. **The untranslatable-word trope, unrisked.** Grandmother's Yoruba word for *mature* is invoked ("does not mean ripe… does not mean finished") but never named. Withholding the word while insisting on untranslatability is a hedge. Name it; let him say it aloud; then fail at the English.

5. **The self-narrating meta-move.** "I have just used the phrase *first molecules*. I want you to notice that I did…" Text performing its own spontaneous emergence — the literary cousin of an LLM narrating its process. Cut the frame; let the eruption erupt.

6. **Verbless portent fragments as recurring rhythm.** "At the centre of this architecture, a held breath." / "Field report. Earth, mid-21st century. Findings: —" / "Values as tissue. Ethics as cellular fact." Fine once; a tic when repeated.

## Target register
Bayo Akomolafe (margin-as-vantage, animist-ecological lyricism holding technical rigour; the analyst is part of what is analysed) + Joan Didion (cool exact first-person, concrete nouns carrying the affect, refusal of consolation, the sentence that withholds the moral). Rawer, lumpier, more contemporaneous. Subtract craft selectively rather than add it.
