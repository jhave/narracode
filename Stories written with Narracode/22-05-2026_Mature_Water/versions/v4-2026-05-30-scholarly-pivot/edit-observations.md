# Edit observations — v4

Reading the prompter's hand-edits to the second-attempt 2-opening.md against the original, to extract the operative logic rather than just the surface changes.

## 1. Designation line
- **Before:** "The programme lists him as Research Communicator. Dayo Lim. He read this on the train and thought about it for a stop and then looked out the window."
- **After:** "'Research Communicator. Dayo Lim'. He read this on the train, looked out the window."
- **Implication:** Compression. The doubled beat ("thought about it for a stop and then") is the kind of pacing-filler that reads as narration of an interior the text can't see. Quoting the card makes it an object he is holding, not a fact being explained. Action only: read, looked.

## 2. The glass
- **Cut:** "a glass of water on the podium that no one has drunk from yet. Someone placed it there for him. He noticed it when he walked in, the way you notice something that has been prepared for a need you haven't developed yet."
- **Kept:** "The room holds about three hundred people."
- **Implication:** The whole apparatus of significance around the glass is omniscient-narrator reflection. A just-placed glass is obviously untouched; obviously someone placed it. "a need you haven't developed yet" is the narrator assigning meaning the scene has not earned. The water motif is better planted by the object's bare presence (see prelude) than by commentary.

## 3. Biographical paragraph deleted wholesale
- **Cut:** "He grew up in more than one place. This is relevant, he has found. ... estuary ... He will tell you this is relevant. He is about to."
- **Implication:** Two compounding faults removed at once: (a) the narrator announcing relevance ("this is relevant," twice), and (b) the estuary metaphor glossed in-line. Backstory is not front-loaded as exposition; it surfaces, if at all, inside the argument. The man is not explained before he speaks.

## 4. Implausible generalizations
- **Cut:** "His father wore good suits into an office every weekday for thirty years in a city that never stopped being new to him." / "which is how he makes most decisions."
- **Implication:** "good suits" (unspecified), "an office" (unspecified), "every weekday" (true of nearly all office work, therefore empty), "thirty years" (suspiciously round), "never stopped being new to him" (a sentiment asserted, not sensed). Generalization is the enemy of perception. Either name the specific or cut.

## 5. Flashbacks → pure image
- **Fern, cut:** "He understood nothing about the fern and he also understood everything — the uncurling was a fact so complete it didn't need him there. He stayed anyway. It took a while." and the meta-line "This memory surfaces now without being summoned. He is not sure why now."
- **Implication:** The flashback keeps only what the senses register. No interpretation of the image, no narration of the memory's arrival. Person shifts to first ("I was eight") — the memory is owned and spoken, not reported from outside. Present tense ("fronds are uncurling") for immediacy.

## 6. Vague abstraction → precise term
- **Before:** "It takes a kind of suffering the system has to move through."
- **After:** "It requires reflective processing, moving through without inhibitory constraint, without the short-circuits of traumatic obedience."
- **Implication:** "a kind of" is a hedge that gestures at a concept without holding it. Replaced with the actual mechanism. The lecture trusts precise, even clinical, vocabulary.

## 7. Defensive self-interruption removed
- **Cut:** "I know this sounds like I'm saying we should remove the constraints. I'm not."
- **Implication:** Pre-empting the audience's objection is a TED reflex. The argument asserts and lets the scaffold metaphor carry the qualification ("Some constraints operate as scaffold").

## 8. Mall thesis stated → enacted
- **Before:** "It was the permission the fragrance gave them to want something. The wanting was already there. The scent was the occasion."
- **After:** "he offered scents appropriate to each; tentative recognitions of their complexity."
- **Implication:** The meaning is performed in the action, not delivered as a conclusion. "the only male there" added — concrete situating detail doing more than the abstraction it replaced.

## Carry-forward rule
If it cannot be sensed, don't say it. Name the specific or cut. The narrator describes; it does not interpret, announce, or reassure.
