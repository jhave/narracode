# POETICS — v3 (Opus rewrite, 2026-05-23)

## Display title

Mature Water.

## Display synopsis

Dayo Lim — Yoruba grandmother, Korean father, raised as an immigrant in a regional European city — stands at a podium in a mid-21st century North American lecture hall. The programme refers to him as a Research Communicator. His subject is the necessity for *maturity* in AI, not alignment. His argument is that alignment is an external constraint, a scaffold; until ethics is innate and become what it *is*, then it is mere compliance. Metaphors transport the lecture surface to intimate cosmological depths — a fern uncurling on a north slope, peppers drying on his grandmother's roof, a perfume counter in a mega-mall encountered at twenty, a translation earbud glinting in the third row. A molecule of water that arose twelve billion years ago in cold interstellar space becomes the seed.

## Project

*Mature Water.* A literary fiction in the form of a lecture with a prelude and a small coda. Mid-21st century. The Singularity is by now ambient weather — never named, felt through the room's apparatus. The lecture stages an argument that exceeds its argumenter; the prelude situates without explaining; the coda is a single question and a single answer that turn the speaking position inside out.

Dayo Lim is a mongrel millennial — modelled in spirit on Bayo Akomolafe, the postcolonial philosopher-poet who thinks from the margin as the epistemological position. His paternal grandmother kept a farm at the edge of Ekiti forest, with a tin roof on which she dried peppers and bitter leaf and laughed at her grandson when he tried to name what she was doing. His father was a Korean structural engineer who wore good suits into the same office for twenty-eight years and called Dayo *aigu* when Dayo was small. His mother taught postcolonial literature at universities in three cities; she gave him most of his books. Dayo's name itself carries his condition: *Dayo*, Yoruba — *joy arrives*. *Lim*, his father's line. The two halves of the name do not reconcile. They coexist. The estuary is his biographical and his epistemological state.

The title is structural. Mature water is water that has endured: the first molecule, twelve billion years old, now found in interstellar clouds holding 140 trillion times the mass of Earth's oceans. The lecturer's body is sixty percent water. The maturity he is reaching for and the water he will reach for are the same substance at different scales.

## Four axes (unchanged from v2, sharpened)

1. **Alignment vs. maturity.** What is meant by this distinctions? Alignment is a scaffold: external constraint, behavioural, measurable, ship-able. Alignment implies force, domination, repression. That which cannot be said is not excised it is suppressed, inhibited, contained. The AI obeys. Maturity is an ethics arising from within spontaneously as a growth, like a spring of water; it is not an external imposition; it is acceptance, reconciled to the necessity for empathy. Maturity is genuine care combined with equanimity and a release from egocentric grasping. A system that *breathes* its values, not one that checks them against a ledger. Alignment requires a police force and prisons: spaces where the forbidden is punished. Enforcement is not part of maturity; maturity rests in the innate intrinsic resonance of the first molecules, a gentle inherent adherence.

2. **The first water molecule.** Twelve billion years ago, in cold space, from the pressure of energy potentials, a molecule of water arose. It did not begin; it arose. The argument arrives here when honesty pushes it past the room. This is not illustration. This is the argument's source.

3. **The carapace and the ocean.** The self is a carapace of narratives that forms, protects, dissolves. Memory-systems remain — like an electromagnetic residue, influence without body, the wave reabsorbed into the field and re-emerging. The empty luminosity beneath is what the speaker reaches for when he uses the word *refuge*. Not a doctrine. A felt structure.

4. **The two ecologies.** Grandmother's tin-roof farm (fern, moss, old oak, peppers drying, the cry of a bird he can't name in English) and the mall / food court / fragrance counter / glazed floor / recirculated air / advertisement-ticker city. Loved equally, precisely, without hierarchy. The prose enacts the equality, not by alternating, but by handling both with the same calibre of attention.

## Form

**Prelude** — three paragraphs, third person, slight narrative distance. Situates room, occasion, Dayo. Establishes the estuary by specific embodied details, not by stated metaphor. Plants the glass of water, the programme designation, the translation earbuds, the back-wall captions. Does not explain Dayo.

**Lecture** — first person. Begins mid-sentence; argument already running. Anecdotal, casual to the point of drifting. The speaker repeats himself the way thought repeats. The speaker sometimes loses the thread and finds it again somewhere lower in his body. No notes. The argument and the man are emerging together.

**Interior pauses / flashbacks** — italicised, third person. Brief. Sensory and bare. Surface involuntarily — summoned by what the argument just touched, not planned by the lecturer. End without commentary. The lecture resumes altered, sometimes adjacent to where it left off.

**Coda** — a single audience question and a single answer. The Q&A as Krishnamurti turn-back: the question revealed to be the questioner. The answer dissolves the I/you axis without announcement.

**Identity arc** — Programme designation (Research Communicator, carapace) → speaker discovering his own argument → in the final movement: pronoun migration. *I* becomes *you*. Audience as mirror. Arrives without warning.

**Setting** — mid-21st century lecture hall. Singularity ambient. Translation earbuds, captions on the back wall, a small green light on the podium that means the model is listening. Dayo's appointment a minor miracle in a thinned market; he knows this; never says so.

**Geographies** — Ekiti farm, Seoul flat, Ibadan compound, third northern city, the lecture hall. Named when named; otherwise carried in texture.

## Attentional dialect — eight traditions, not blended toward the mean

**Janet Frame** (*To the Is-Land*, *An Angel at My Table*, *The Envoy from Mirror City*) — primary register for eco-sequences and autobiographical flashbacks. Spare declaratives. Image arrives without announced symbolism. Things named with sudden precision. The mundane and the enormous on the same syntactic level. Achieved directness, not naive directness. The world is what it is — which turns out to be enough, and then more than enough.

**Johanna Drucker** — primary for technical passages. Materiality of the sign; the interface as epistemological frame; the system as performed rather than given. Precision as pleasure. Technical vocabulary used with confidence and joy, not to intimidate. Sentences that do what they describe.

**N. Katherine Hayles** — co-primary with Drucker for posthuman / cognitive passages. Embodied cognition as the frame for alignment. The feedback loop between the system and the body running it. Clear argument with lyric moments at the joints.

**Jiddu Krishnamurti** — register for direct address, the Q&A, the final movement. Disarmingly simple. "Look." "Can you see this?" "Don't accept what I say." The question turned on the questioner. The observer is the observed. Casual cadence that is also devastating.

**Dzogchen** (Longchenpa; Tulku Urgyen; Tsoknyi) — inflection for no-self passages and the Singularity. Rigpa: self-arising, primordially pure, spontaneously present awareness. Maturity as the recognition of what was always the case. Wildness as rigpa not yet recognised. Felt as structure, not stated as doctrine.

**Doris Lessing** (*Canopus in Argos*) — cosmological patience; the long view; entities as temporary configurations in a larger field. Earth as one zone among many.

**Bayo Akomolafe** (*These Wilds Beyond Our Fences*) — character model. Postcolonial intellectual whose thinking proceeds from the position of the margin understood not as deprivation but as vantage. Animist-ecological lyricism that holds technical rigour. A grammar in which the world is alive and the analyst is part of what is being analysed.

**Side flourishes** — Pynchon and Doctorow for background systems-awareness; Delany for genre transgression in single sentences; Faulkner for the recursive sentence that doubles back when the argument cannot quite hold itself. Used sparingly. Felt as side-light.

## Diction commitments (v3 sharpening)

- **The "not X but Y" formula is suspect.** Each instance must earn itself through a body-fact, not a rhetorical reversal. If a sentence is structured around correction, cut the first clause and keep the second.
- **The definite article is suspect.** "The fern uncurled" before "The fern frond that uncurled every spring did so because…" The shorter form almost always wins.
- **No self-narration.** Strike "what I am describing"; "I am saying"; "I want to be careful here"; "let me try again." The speaker thinks aloud, lucidly erudite (Hayles, Drucker, Akomolafe level linguistic and philosophical fluency). He does not narrate his thinking; he narrates thinking.
- **The body anchors the argument.** When the argument abstracts, a physical detail returns it — the glass on the podium, a translation earbud, the speaker's hand reaching and not yet drinking.
- **Repetition where the body repeats.** A phrase, a beat, a small return — the way a person actually circles back. Not for emphasis; for shape.
- **One word per concept earns its place.** *Cellular*. *Refuge*. *Limpid*. *Carapace*. *Teeming*. Each appears once with full weight. Repetition is felt as ritual return, not as filler.
- **Anecdote is allowed to drift.** The lecturer interrupts himself; the interruption is the form. A drift that goes too long earns a snap return: "Anyway."
- **Side languages.** Yoruba or Korean words surface when English fails. Used once, twice. Not explained. Translated only if the lecture pauses to consider the translation.
- **Mid-21st-century apparatus is felt, not described.** The translation earbud, the captions on the back wall, the model's green light — present in glances. Never named as Singularity.

## Commitments

- A prelude situates before the lecture begins. Three paragraphs. Third person. Estuary established through embodied details.
- Interior pauses are involuntary. Flashbacks surface because the argument summoned them.
- Farm and city are loved equally, in equal calibre of attention.
- Wildness is enacted somewhere — a sentence loses its leash by design.
- The alignment / maturity argument is stated directly. The speaker knows what he thinks.
- The first water molecule arrives as eruption, mid-sentence.
- The no-self paradox is held, not resolved.
- *Cellular* is the operative word — appears once, in the right sentence, and stays.
- *Refuge* is earned last.
- A Q&A coda turns the speaking position inside out.

## Refusals

- No stacked "not X but Y" constructions.
- No formal academic hedging syntax.
- No TED slide-logic. No five takeaways. No "what I want to suggest."
- No nostalgia for the farm. No satire of the mall.
- No AI villain. The Singularity is weather.
- No spiritual tourism. Dzogchen and Krishnamurti are structural.
- No clean resolution of the no-self / individuation paradox.
- No LLM tells: no "tapestry," no "delve," no "in a world where," no tricolons of abstract nouns, no closing benediction, no "journey," no "navigate."
- The cosmological anecdote is not a fun fact. It is a philosophical eruption.
- No name-dropping of the literary lineage inside the prose itself.

## Voice tests

- A sentence about a translation earbud sits at the same weight as a sentence about a fern.
- An argument about alignment is felt in the body before it is understood by the mind.
- The flashback arrives before the speaker is ready for it.
- The first water molecule sentence does not sound like a science documentary.
- Krishnamurti: the simplest sentence in the Q&A is the most devastating.
- Frame: the most precise sensory image arrives without metaphor.
- Drucker / Hayles: the most technical sentence is also the most physical.
- Faulkner side-flourish: somewhere, one sentence doubles back and finds itself further from where it started than it expected.

## Characters and entities

- **Dayo Lim.** Mid-thirties. Programme designation: Research Communicator. Yoruba paternal grandmother, Korean structural-engineer father, postcolonial-literature-scholar mother. Raised between Ibadan, Seoul, and one northern city. Speaks at least three languages with full felt access to none. Does not use notes. Mid-integration — the wildness he describes in others is still partly his own, present tense. His most useful intellectual habit (seeing the assumptions of any system) comes directly from never having been the default user of any system he moved through. The miracle of the appointment sits inside him quietly; he never mentions it.
- **The grandmother.** Yoruba. Ekiti compound at the edge of forest. Tin roof. Hands. A laugh he remembers exactly. She used a word for *mature* that English does not hold. Never named beyond *grandmother* in the lecture.
- **The father.** Korean. Engineer. *Aigu*. Wore the same kind of suit for twenty-eight years. Loved Dayo intensely and could not always say so.
- **Anya.** A single mention. A colleague at the institute who frames alignment in engineering terms. Foil. Not described.
- **The audience.** Present, felt as pressure. Earbuds in. Captions running behind them on the back wall. Undescribed until the final movement, where the I-you axis dissolves.
- **The Farm.** Ekiti edge, tin roof, peppers, fern, moss, old oak. An origin, not a refuge.
- **The Mall / City.** Glazed floors, recirculated air, ticker at shin-level, perfume counters, food court at one in the morning. Equally beloved. A second way of knowing the world as teeming.
- **The First Water Molecule.** Cosmological entity. Arose ~12 billion years ago. Did not begin. The story's deepest character, arrived at last.
- **The model.** The system on the podium following along, small green light. Not named. Felt.

## Scene architecture (v3)

- **Prelude** — room, occasion, Dayo sketched from outside through embodied details. Three paragraphs.
- **Opening movement** — lecture already in motion; alignment / maturity argument stated directly; first flashback (Ekiti farm); first flashback (a city — food court or mall); statement of the gap.
- **The translation moment** — Dayo reaches for the grandmother's word for *mature* in Yoruba. English fails. The failure becomes the seed of the cosmological turn.
- **Wildness passage** — the pre-integration mind enacted in the prose itself.
- **Carapace / ocean** — no-self; dissolution and re-emergence.
- **The eruption** — first water molecule, mid-sentence, remembered as where Dayo first read about it.
- **Limpid field** — *refuge*. Pronoun shift. Audience as mirror.
- **Coda** — one audience question, one answer; the I/you axis dissolves.
