'Research Communicator. Dayo Lim'. He read this on the train, looked out the window.

Mid-21st century. A lecture hall in an institute that prints its name in lower-case across the building. Translation earbuds in a third of the audience, glinting in the house light. On the back wall, twelve scripts run in live caption — Devanagari, Hangul, Mandarin, Arabic, an Igbo phrase he can read just well enough to know it is Igbo, and seven others he cannot. On the podium a small green light, steady. Beside it, a glass of water.
