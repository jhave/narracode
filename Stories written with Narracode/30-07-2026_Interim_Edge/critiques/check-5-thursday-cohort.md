# Check — 5 thursday cohort

- **Continuity.** Thursday, four o'clock, back at 11:10. Bag unopened four days as of §6. Chlorine established as childhood memory *and* as present smell — deliberately doubled so neither explains the other.
- **Obligations.** Opened: Basil, the counter, the surrendered notebook; the sentence she starts and abandons. Held open: where she goes, what is in the bag, chapter four.
- **Motifs.** Balloon touched in passing — *hello you* — the only affection anyone shows it. Loyalty cards: new figure, and the story's clearest statement of Lara's thesis without a word of theory.
- **Scene function.** POV displacement. She is not a delivery system for the theme; she gets the section, and the reader learns things Ahmani will never learn.
- **Voice.** Faster, funnier, less shame than his sections; sorrow drops in without transition and lifts the same way. The swimming-pool memory risks reading as the answer to the chlorine — mitigated by the present-tense stairwell smell arriving *before* the reader can settle it.
- **Reader-state.** Reader will decide she is leaving, or pregnant, or in a group. The story refuses all three, and the refusal must not read as coyness — the section's warmth is what keeps it honest.
