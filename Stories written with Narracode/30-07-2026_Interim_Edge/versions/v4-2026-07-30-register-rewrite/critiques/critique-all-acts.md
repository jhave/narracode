# Critique — all acts, against POETICS.md

*Reflexive pass, Claude Opus 5, 2026-07-30. Recommends; does not revise.*

## What works

**The seed is load-bearing, not a prologue.** Every one of its images returns transformed and none returns explained: balloon → landing object → fuller → gone, leaving a paler grey; toddlers → four hundred heads swivelling at a woman who asked a question → children in a courtyard who do not turn → a kid's hand flat on crèche glass; the race → the corridor, the stairwell, the lift that stops at four; *there was no race* → a slogan read off a wall at six-forty in the morning. The dream is never interpreted. That discipline holds across all six sections.

**Atrocity as procedure.** §4 is the piece's real risk and it comes off: a row, a decimal, a dropdown whose third value is *Permit with cushion*, four minutes SLA, ninety-second average, a green tile. The one place a machine has a spine is the hard withhold on under-minimum faces. Nobody raises a voice. The narration issues no verdict, and the refusal of verdict is what makes the section land.

**Lara has her own section and her own weather.** §5's POV displacement earns itself: the loyalty cards carry her entire thesis without a word of theory, and the reader ends the section knowing things Ahmani will never know. The obligation not to let her be a delivery system for the theme is met.

**The definition field.** §6's one act of authorship — *people who stop to tie their shoe* typed into a field that has never held anything, saved, tick, timestamp, initials, then empty by morning with the audit column clean — is the strongest single beat and the correct terminus. Not defeat, not protest, not epiphany.

## Where it defaults

1. **Residual editorialising.** Three clauses name their own mechanism and should be cut or compressed to a fragment:
   - §1 *and felt clever, and felt the cleverness curdle in about four steps*
   - §2 *this is the bravest person I have met, and also, immediately, on the same track: I don't know her at all*
   - §3 *and this was love, conducted through logistics, and it worked*
   These are precisely the constructions `master_phrases_to_avoid.md` catalogues. The material carries them already.

2. **CONTINUITY runs two beats long.** The growth-math paragraph in §4 explains the retention mechanism after the VP has already said the thing that matters. Compress; let the satire stay sub-audible.

3. **The taxonomy-italic device is under-used, then abandoned.** Three appearances across §1–§3, none after. Either it is a habit of his perception, in which case it should surface once more late — ideally against something that resists it — or it should be pulled back to two so it reads as intrusion rather than as feature.

4. **The fourteen-months figure is the weakest hook.** It arrives unmotivated in §1 and ticks to eleven in §6, which risks reading as a countdown the story owes an answer to. It is stronger if it stays a symptom of his arithmetic rather than a clock.

5. **`the` audit.** Article density is at ordinary novelistic level throughout. The chosen condensation register requires localized pruning under somatic and clinical pressure — the queue rows, the stairwell, the kettle, the persona library — and restoration in the reflective clauses. This has not been done yet.
