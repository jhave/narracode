# Motifs

## Active
- **The limp balloon** (seed) — palpable edges, folds of plastic, sedated bobbing. Becomes a physical object on the fourth-floor landing, then a figure for the body, the economy, a mouth.
- **The race that was not a race** (seed) — corridors, curved wall, the lead surrendered to a shoelace. Returns as the building, the tram, the queue, the escalator, the stairwell.
- **The toddlers swivelling** (seed) — a dozen faces turning at adult anger. Returns as an open-plan floor turning at a raised voice; as a row of licensed child faces in the persona library.
- **The white-haired woman** — neutral, gentle, no judgement, just awareness. The one gaze in the story that does not score him.
- **Swivel** — chairs, heads, the away-turn of shame. The story's characteristic motion.
- **Category** — the label arriving before the person. Isolated italic lines of taxonomy-speak intruding on perception.
- **Smudge / smear** (seed: *the dream smeared beneath his hand*) — the gesture of wiping something away and spreading it.
- **Ultra-processed** — snacks with hazard ratings, a vending wall, a mouth that keeps working after the food is gone.
- **Chlorine** — Lara's Thursdays.

## Transformations
- Balloon: dream-image → landing object → fuller → (open).
- Toddlers: dream faces → persona-library faces → real children at the tram stop who do not turn.
- Race: dream corridor → office corridor → the stairwell he climbs while the lift stops at four → the last section, where nobody is running.
- Swivel: shame-motion → the floor turning → at the end, the chair turning with nobody in it, or with him in it, unstated.

## Avoid Overuse
- *Swivel* is potent and cheap. Cap at roughly one per section, load-bearing each time.
- Taxonomy-italics: one or two per section maximum. More and the device becomes the story.
- Do not let *the queue refills* or river/trust figures in — sibling-project property. One glancing echo at most, differently phrased.
- The balloon must not be allowed to become an explained symbol. It is an object on a landing.
