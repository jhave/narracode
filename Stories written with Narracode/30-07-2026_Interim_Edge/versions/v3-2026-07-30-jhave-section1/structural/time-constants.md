# Time Constants

## Chronology
- Year: **2031**. Late summer into early autumn. Heat with a civic rating.
- Section 0 (seed): dawn, a weekday, at home, before work.
- Sections 1–5 move forward across roughly six weeks.
- Section 6: dawn again, later in the season. Rain has come once.

## Durations
- Kettle: under three minutes.
- Tram: eleven minutes when the line runs, twenty-two when rerouted around the curfew hours.
- Adjacency clearance SLA: four minutes per item, measured.
- RS-09: sunset three times across fourteen months; re-forms within nine to eleven days.
- The balloon: on the landing since before they moved in (March). Does not finish.
- Lara's Thursdays: leaves at four, back after eleven.

## Constraints
- Curfew hours are municipal, announced weekly, never the same hours twice; nobody calls them curfew.
- The flat has no lift above the fourth floor.
- Lara's viva is not scheduled and this is not discussed.
- Ahmani has never seen the inside of the room where adjacency decisions are audited. It exists as a name on a form.
- The dream (seed) must not be given an interpretation by the narration.
