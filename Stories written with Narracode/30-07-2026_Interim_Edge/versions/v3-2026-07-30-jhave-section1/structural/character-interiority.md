# Character Interiority

## Characters

**Ahmani.** Desire: to be looked at without being scored. Shame: that he is good at the work, fast, and that speed feels like grace in the body. Avoidance: he will not calculate what his adjacency clearances sum to; he has the number available and does not open it. Contradiction: he thinks in categories about Lara while begging privately to be uncategorized. Hidden knowledge: he has queried RS-09 against his own device identifier and closed the window before it returned. Changing self-understanding: the dream's verdict — *there was no race* — arrives as consolation on waking and as an insult by lunch, and he cannot get it to hold one shape.

**Lara Cooper.** Desire: to write a sentence her supervisor cannot metabolize. Shame: that she finds the field beautiful, that the material she is supposed to critique gives her pleasure. Avoidance: her parents' emails, answered in under six words after four days. Contradiction: she theorizes the fetish and keeps one — a used coat, a lemon, a specific plastic. Hidden knowledge: chapter four; the Thursdays; a decision she has already made and has not yet found the phrase for. Her interiority must run at least once without Ahmani's anxiety filtering it.

**Ferouz and Sabir (his parents).** Concern as a full-time occupation, competently executed. They have a system. Sabir prices things aloud. Ferouz sends what cannot arrive.

**Corinne and Ellis Cooper.** Distance conducted with impeccable manners. They are not cruel; they are punctual.

**Delph.** Believes the deflection is the job, and it is.

**Miko.** Has already stopped noticing and is neither proud nor sorry about it.

## Arc Potentials
- Ahmani moves from *I process categories* to *I am processed* without a scene that states it.
- Lara moves from performance of scathing brightness to one unperformed sorrow, then back, unrepaired.
- The couple's tenderness intensifies while the ground under it thins. Both true at once; do not resolve toward either.
