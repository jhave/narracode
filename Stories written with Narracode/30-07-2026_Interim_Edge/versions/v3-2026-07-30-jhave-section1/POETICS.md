## Display title

Interim Edge

## Display synopsis

Ahmani, first job out of the design-math academy, works Category Integrity at the Interim Edge Advertising Corporation: he maintains the taxonomy of forty-one thousand audience segments, merges them, splits them, sunsets them, and clears which categories may run adjacent to authorized-action footage. His lover Lara Cooper is finishing a doctorate on *Fetish Process in Algorithmic Societies of Control* and speaks in rapid stripped phrases followed by cascading laughter or sudden sorrow. 2031. Heat, curfew hours, patriotic feeds, branded snacks with a hazard rating. A segment that has been sunset three times keeps re-forming with no label in its definition field. A balloon on the fourth-floor landing does not finish deflating. A white-haired woman from a dream turns up as a licensed face. Nothing closes.

## Form

- **Vignettes braided with interiority.** Scene, then extended interior burst — daydream, thought-process, dream, reverie, mild hallucination — often running simultaneously, the outer scene continuing underneath. Section 0 is the prompter's seed, verbatim, untouchable.
- **Section headers are audience-taxonomy labels** (`lapsed enthusiast`, `high-intent, low-trust`, `caregiver, adjacent`…), the occupational language applied to a life. Final section carries no label.
- **The intrusive second register.** Isolated italic lines of category-speak surfacing inside perception — Ahmani's trade deforming how he sees Lara, his parents, himself. Sparse: one or two per section. Never explained as a device.
- **Open loops.** Every planted signal stays planted: the unlabelled segment and its behaviour signature, the balloon, the white-haired woman, Lara's Thursdays and the chapter she withholds, his mother's delivered-but-never-arrived package, Peter Ganz still on the calendar, the untied lace. Hooks propel; they do not close.

## Voice

- Third person, close on Ahmani, past tense. Anxious, ironic, detailed, idiomatic. Contractions and profanity where the mind actually goes there.
- **Merge, not blend** (per harness): Lynch — uncanny recurrence, sound before sense, the stalled domestic moment, an object that behaves wrongly and is never discussed. Missouri Williams — baroque clause-piling on the society, savage comedy, decay rendered with relish. Knausgaard — exhaustive banal specificity (the kettle, the filter cone, the shirt), shame reported without cushioning.
- Lara's dialect: rapid phrases without prepositions, possessives, or context, followed by cascading shimmers of laughter or sudden sorrow. She is never made to explain herself.
- Vary long clause-piles against short flat declarations. Copular avoidance (master poetics §2.C): active tactile verbs, elliptical structures.

## Linguistic pressure

- Raise the temperature. Prune articles, prepositions, possessives — sparse and localized, under clinical or somatic pressure only (§2.A). Never mechanical.
- Lexical diet (§2.E): audit `thing`, `spent`, `wage`, `held/holding`, `bent`, `small` on sight.
- No self-reflexive commentary, no analytical clauses, no editorializing (per `master_phrases_to_avoid.md`). No paradox-aphorism or chiasmus openers. No corrective-superiority stance. No `which is only X`.
- No value dichotomies — no better/worse, right/wrong resolution offered. Dissonance is a phase, not an error.

## Refused

- Redemption arc. Ahmani does not quit, blow the whistle, or achieve clarity.
- Explanation of the unlabelled segment, the balloon, the white-haired woman, the withheld chapter.
- Satire that congratulates the reader. The advertising material is procedural, dull, and load-bearing — atrocity arrives as a dropdown, not as spectacle.
- Naming a real conflict. Authorized-action footage stays unlocated and systemic.
- Reuse of sibling-project figures (`the queue refills`, `nobody audits the river`) — one glancing echo at most.

## Amendment — loop 1 (`on_new_direction`, 2026-07-30)

Prompter direction: *grow a nuanced character-story-context arc for each, then condense every line into a subtle idiom style; generate the final index.html.*

- **Arc pass.** Each of the three principals carries a movement across the six sections, none of it stated: Ahmani — *fast at it* → *the number he won't open* → *the field he writes in* → *the slogan said badly at dawn.* Lara — performed brightness → one unperformed sorrow → a section in her own head that he never gets → a sentence she starts and abandons. The society — heat rating, snack hazard class, the hours nobody calls curfew, CONTINUITY shipping in October — moves from backdrop to the thing everyone is inside.
- **Condensation register (chosen).** Compress toward idiomatic fragment, per the register of *The Chute* and jhave's edit-pass on *Glistening*: prune articles, prepositions, possessives; drop subjects where the mind drops them; cut connective tissue and stage direction; let a long accumulative sentence survive only where it is doing work no fragment can do. Idiom before grammar — *musta*, *meh*, *where the fuck are the chips* — the cadence of a mind talking to itself, not of prose being written.
- **Figures.** Caption-only, per *Open Loops*: `Fig N.N —` lines carrying image-content as text. No image assets this loop.
- **Publication.** Story folder only. Root `index.html` and `metadata.md` stay hand-maintained.
