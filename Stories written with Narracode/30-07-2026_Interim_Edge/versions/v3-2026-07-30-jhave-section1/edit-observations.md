# Edit observations — jhave on §1 (PR #9)

*Seamless Edit Method. Diff of `drafts/7-condensed-final.md` §1, paragraphs 1–2, plus one paragraph added.*

Three paragraphs of human edit carry more register information than the whole condensation pass did. Thirteen operations, extracted below as first principles for §2–§6.

---

## A. Perception before object

> Water going from nothing to a hiss → **Listening to** the water transition from silence to hiss

The act of sensing is foregrounded; the sensed thing arrives second. Consciousness is the subject, not the kettle. Same move in the added paragraph: *Heard it enter the filter.*

## B. Somatic anchoring — locate on the body, not in the room

> Counter. → **Counter where hips meet.**

Also *His breath caught*, *Heart skewed sideways*, *thumb on wet paper*. Objects are known by where they press. An unanchored noun is a weak noun.

## C. Live self-correction, parenthetical

> that last rolling panic → rolling boil froth **(panic? no, calm.)**

He kept my word and demoted it to a *rejected candidate*. The narration proposes a figure and refuses it in-line. This is the anti-tell engine: a confident metaphor reads as authored; a metaphor caught being wrong reads as thought.

## D. Kill the extended conceit — keep one word

> He had not consented to fourteen months. Didn't know where the figure came from. There it was anyway. Itemized. Signed for while asleep.
> → **Itemized.**

Four sentences of ironic elaboration gone; one noun survives. Same execution on the coffee bag: *in the font of a document that has been to court and won* — deleted entirely. **A conceit that continues past its landing is the tell.** The wittier the sentence, the more likely it goes.

## E. Noun-collision — delete the simile marker and the prepositions

> Slept **like** freight **in the** heat, wrapped tight → **Sleep freight heat wrapped her tight.**

Article/preposition pruning taken past where I took it: nouns collide directly, no *like*, no *in the*. Confirms tell-class 11 from the other direction — he does not repair a simile, he removes the machinery that makes it a simile.

## F. Single-word full-stopped fragments as rhythm

> then coffee. Chicory and a legume. → **Coffee. Chicory. Broccoli.**

Also *Pain.* *Sad.* *Sighing.* The list loses its conjunctions and becomes a beat.

## G. Subject-dropped verb chains for procedure

> **Emptied** the drains into the compost. **Rinsed** the holder. **Put in** a fresh filter. **Poured** the water.

No *he*. The body does the sequence; the pronoun is redundant weight.

## H. Participial fragment as sentence

> **Being as quiet as possible.** … **Dripping into the cup.** … **Sighing.**

Ongoing action, no subject, no finite verb.

## I. The absurd concrete over the accurate abstract

> a legume → **Broccoli**

*Legume* is correct about chicory-blend fillers and dull. *Broccoli* is wrong and alive. He takes the funnier wrong noun every time.

## J. The world's own language, quoted and unglossed

> Bag saying COFFEE in the font of a document… → Crumpled waxed-paper bag saying **"Get Yours Here"**

My version explained the satire. His quotes real ad-speak and says nothing about it. Let the world supply its own idiocy.

## K. Colon-apposition

> **His own hands: chocolate.**

Noun, colon, attribute. No verb. And note what this does: Ahmani's skin arrives as pure colour against *a wedge of pale*, with zero exposition. **Now canon** — he is dark-skinned, Lara is not, and neither fact will ever be stated in a sentence with a verb in it.

## L. Emotion as itemized clinical series

> Loved her so much for a second his chest did something medical.
> → **Heart skewed sideways, love, a medical condition. Pain.**

Body-event, name of feeling, diagnostic category, symptom — four items, no verb of feeling. He removed the *narration* of the emotion and left the chart.

## M. Hedge the certainty

> she would leave him → **Perhaps** she would leave him

## What this means for §2–§6

The rewrite applies A–M. Concretely:

- Every scene gets its body-position and its procedure. Duration is not skipped.
- The four remaining `X, the way Y` similes die (tell-class 11), replaced by noun-collision or by nothing.
- Every joke that runs a beat past its landing gets cut to its one surviving noun. Casualties include the melon whistle, the *marble is marble* return, the balloon-asking line, and the persona-library river.
- Parenthetical self-correction becomes available as a recurring device — used sparingly, and once against something that matters.
- Lara's §5 gets the same treatment from inside *her* body, not his.
- Skin colour returns once, at most, and never with a verb.

## What is **not** in the diff, and is therefore held

He did not touch: the taxonomy-italic lines, the section headers, the Fig captions, the dialogue punctuation, or anything structural. Those stay exactly as they are.
