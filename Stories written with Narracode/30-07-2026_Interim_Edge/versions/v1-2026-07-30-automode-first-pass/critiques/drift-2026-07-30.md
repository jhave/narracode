# Drift — 2026-07-30

*What is the piece becoming, against what POETICS declared?*

**Declared:** vignettes braided with interiority; Lynch/Williams/Knausgaard merged rather than blended; a decaying oligarchic 2031; hooks that propel and never close.

**Become:** a workplace novel in miniature with one displaced section. The Knausgaard coordinate dominates — kettle, filter cone, stairwell, the exact price of a melon — and the Williams coordinate has thinned to the vending wall, the arbitration that meets quarterly, and the bins. Lynch survives almost entirely in objects: the balloon, the chained door, the wiped field, the name that is nearly his.

**Is the drift fertile?** Yes, on balance. The Williams register — baroque relish in decay — would have made the society legible as satire, and the piece is stronger for the society being *unremarkable* to the people inside it. The heat has a rating and nobody discusses the rating. That is closer to what the seed actually does: the political horror is in the first paragraph, delivered as a thought the character has already had many times, and then he closes the tabs.

**Where the drift costs something.** The absurd and the malevolent were both named in the direction, and the piece has kept the absurd (the raffle, the hamper, *let's take that offline*, the marble) while letting the malevolent become purely procedural. Nothing in the story wants anything bad to happen. There is no antagonist, not even a diffuse one — only a schema that does not permit empty and a job that runs at four. That is a defensible reading of an oligarchic anthropocene and it is also, quietly, a gentler book than the seed promised.

**Recommendation, not instruction:** if a later loop wants the malevolence back, it belongs in §4 and it should not be a person. The entity whose name is seven letters and a numeral, holding a defunct library's model releases behind a login Ahmani has no credentials for, is the available door. It should stay shut, but it could be made to feel locked rather than merely closed.

**Voice drift:** none against the prompter's cadence brief. Anxious, ironic, detailed, idiomatic, holds across all six. The one register the piece has *added* without being asked is tenderness that does not decay to match the world — the parents, the neck of a sleeping woman, a wave to a child through glass. Keep it. It is what makes the refusal of closure read as a form rather than as a trick.
