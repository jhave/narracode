## Where the piece drifted (regressions, both prompter-corrected)

**The doubled *let this stand*.** *They all knew she would not have a sentence for it, and they all let this stand* echoed the same construction from the closing of draft 1 (*and they each, in different ways, declined to name it*). Once: motif. Twice: formula. The second instance was the prose reaching for a refrain that had become a tic. The corrected version replaces the second clause with phenomenologically specific embodiment — *A small flurry of tiny gestures went through them all as they looked elsewhere or strove to evade the implication of that fact* — which is what the moment actually contains: the body-language acknowledgement, the evasion of implication. Rooney move proper: precise phenomenology of social pressure, not formula.

**"The city went on. The bus came, the bus left."** The motif of continuance is correct; the abstraction *the city went on* and the definite article *the bus* slid toward middlebrow fluency. The fix — drop the abstraction, indefinite the bus — restores particularity. *Outside, somewhere, a bus came, a bus left* is the same length and rhythm but does the work the abstraction was substituting for. Knausgård would build this sentence; Lessing would approve. The lesson is in the article: *a* makes the bus a particular event observed; *the* turns it into a category.
