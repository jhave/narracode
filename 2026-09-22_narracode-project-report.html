What the project has achieved

Narracode has produced a sustained practice of writing, editing, documenting, illustrating, and preserving literary work. The most defensible success claim is concrete: 32 works now have discoverable reading pages on main, and four additional projects survive on repository branches. Those projects encompass satire, speculative fiction, intimate encounters, a sitcom, a manifesto, and longer works. The record makes many of the human decisions and model revisions inspectable.

The audit also exposes a gap between making a work and making it findable. Before this repair, the root index listed 31 of the 32 story directories on main. Interim Edge had a complete reading page but no library card. Five other story directories existed only on branches: four distinct projects and an earlier Machine Liberation attempt. The repository retained them; the library did not reveal them.

That is evidence of output and preservation. It is not yet evidence that the harness produces better literature than the same author and model working from a strong prompt. The corpus changes model, prompt, editorial involvement, genre, length, and procedure at once. Two public entries explicitly describe composition without the Narracode procedure: The Green Interregnum and The Appointment · You.inc. The former's older attribution file still records a harness request, so even the method labels need care. No causal “harness success percentage” follows from these totals.

Project starts are kept separately for statistics. This preserves the meaningful later edition dates already displayed for Adjunct: Our Internal (July 26), The Green Interregnum (July 21), and I LOVE SMORKY (June 3), while identifying their original starts as July 8, July 20, and May 29.

The visible mistakes were straightforward: Upboarding Hour, dated June 25, appeared above July stories; the July 26 Adjunct appeared below July 25's The Chute; and July 21's The Green Interregnum appeared below July 20's Open Loops. Interim Edge is now placed at July 30. The old three-month card's “90 days” label is corrected to 92, the elapsed May 9–August 9 interval; its historical report is preserved as written.

The older generator had a second failure waiting to recur: its metadata omitted The Warmest Cold, Babbies, Machine Liberation, and Interim Edge, and its template did not preserve the report cards. Rebuilding the index could erase editorial additions. The root build now uses a curated manifest and a preserved template, validates local destinations and explicit dates, and retains report styling. The metadata omissions are also reconciled. New folders do not automatically become public cards.

The library's architectural introduction has been updated too. It previously described background refinement of eight mandatory structural files and an “autocorrecting multi-agent system.” That no longer accurately describes the September 12 protocol.

Exactly what the current harness does

The active narracode.md is a Markdown operating protocol interpreted by an AI agent inside a host such as Codex or Antigravity. It is not, by itself, an executable scheduler, a trained model, or a guarantee that every declared hook runs. Its roles name separated operations; they do not require different models or simultaneous agents. File state makes the work recoverable across passes. It does not update the model's weights.

The four state tiers matter because they distinguish authority: story facts are established; author decisions are explicit directions; model interpretations remain revisable; unresolved possibilities are options and open obligations. A plausible psychological explanation should not quietly become canon. Published stories and their archived versions are preserved rather than retrofitted into the latest method.

The protocol still contains implementation rough edges. Its snapshot instructions mix descriptive paths such as versions/vN-date-description/ with shorter versions/vN/ paths for notes. Its claim that single-shot writing necessarily produces median prose is a motivating hypothesis, not an established result of this audit. The declared hooks depend on an agent following instructions; I found no reason to present them as enforced software events.

The Warmest Cold offers one documented use of the compact state model: its attribution records a five-act September 17 AUTO_MODE run, marked completed that day, with no human prose edits. Its own review notes still describe per-scene checks and strict zero-tell constraints, and STATE.md places “Tone Achieved” under Author Decisions. That is evidence of incomplete separation between model evaluation and author instruction. It shows why a new protocol file and a clean implementation of its principles are different achievements. What success can—and cannot—be claimed

Production and recovery are demonstrable. The project has maintained 32 readable works, four more distinct projects, and a substantial record of revisions. Repairing discovery raises main's story-card coverage from 31/32 (96.9%) to 32/32 (100%). Across the 36-project census, 32/36 (88.9%) have main reading pages. That last figure measures availability, not completion or artistic merit.

The evidence has corrected the method. The August Step 2 record reports that four lexical/cadence measures detected movement in only 9% of 689 paired edits with text on both sides. Later lexical work increased reported coverage but did not establish a unique human signature. The project's own correction withdrew the rarity-as-human interpretation. Those internal results justify caution about optimizing a proxy for voice; I have not rerun the experiments or converted their diagnostic statistics into quality scores. Step 2 · Correction.

Comparative literary effectiveness remains open. The September 7 blind-reading pilot is explicitly paused: twelve baseline passages exist, but the 36 proposed variants and a completed reading study do not. The September 12 comparison between brief-only, compact protocol, and earlier harness is a proposal. Neither supplies reader preference results. There is no complete time, token, monetary-cost, or revision-hour record from which to calculate efficiency gains. Pilot checkpoint.

My assessment is that Narracode's strongest development is the preservation of authorial choices combined with a willingness to retire procedural excess. The least settled part is whether additional criticism improves a given story. A richer ledger can preserve a long work; it can also encourage a model to invent obligations. A tell scan can expose an explanatory tail; it can also flatten intentional repetition. The September protocol makes these interventions optional and bounded, which is a defensible response to the evidence, not proof of superior outcomes.

The immediate editorial priorities are to read Vetch's current movements in sequence, compare Chauffé Éclairé's rewritten fourth chapter with the first three, and decide whether Alpha-Gaia's diegetic figure instructions are intentional finished presentation. Mature Water needs a separate continuation-or-archive decision. After those decisions, a modest blind comparison could test the compact harness on fresh briefs while recording actual resource use. Further claims should follow that evidence.

Audit scope and reproducibility

I refreshed the remote references and inspected main at 490c4c9, the local branches, fetched remote branch tips, all reachable story-path history, and the workspace's sibling copies. The initial May 9 commit and the earlier report establish the launch date; root setup files also record preparatory work on May 8. “Since launch” here consistently means since May 9.

The census deduplicates alternate worktrees, story versions, chapters, renamed folders, and peripheral documents. It includes works explicitly composed without the harness because the task concerns the Narracode project library. It does not include the separate pre-Narracode works in the footer. It cannot recover uncommitted text on other machines, deleted unreachable remote history, or private chats absent from this workspace. First path-commit dates are evidence, not assumed composition or publication dates.

The August 9 report counted 25 works; today's main contains those 25 plus Interim Edge and six September projects. Thus the public count rises by seven, but one is recovered July work. The former report's claim that every item was finished and that none was written from prompt alone is not reused as a current finding. Its prose and counts remain a historical snapshot.

The index is maintained by library.json, library-template.html, and library_index.py. The dated audit's machine-readable outputs accompany this report. tools/audit_library.py reconstructs the census and daily table; its branch evidence is pinned to the inspected commits.
