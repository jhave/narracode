# the walk

*Loop 4 draft. ~790 words. Free indirect across the five, dissolving and reforming. Continuous from drafts/3b-the-blur-fall.md.*

---

They came up the basement steps and the new cold was on them. The street was wet from a rain none of them remembered hearing fall, and a mist was seeping from the warm grates over the metro line, low along the kerb, the kind of mist that arrives before the season has named itself. Early fall. The trees had begun their first dry rustle. A few parked cars, none of them running. A curtained window above a bakery, the curtain warm-coloured. Somewhere a delivery automaton was on its last round, slowly. The streetlights did the conical-pool thing they did. Anwen pulled her jacket. Tomek looked up at the sky as if for something he had been about to remember. Júlio said nothing. Kasimi's breath had not quite steadied. Ines was a half-step behind, the words of the bar still loose around her.

They walked, in the loose formation the five of them walked in, when they had eaten enough.

It began on the second corner, at the place where the side-street narrowed past a railing none of them usually passed.

The mist did not change. The pavement did not change. What changed was the space between any one of them and any other — a softness, a widening, a thing none of them had asked for. A slow opening of the chest in Anwen. A small click in Tomek that he sometimes felt before sleep, but held, extended, refusing to let go. Soles of feet, in Júlio. Hands, in Kasimi. In Ines, recognition: the words she had spoken at the bar — receptor analogs, conduits, vestigial architecture — had been pointing at this, imperfectly. Now this was pointing back. *Yes,* she did not say.

Then the *as* fell away. There was no longer an *as.* There was the thing.

The trees still rustled, but the rustling was not in the trees. The streetlights were still on, but the conical pools were not separate, they were one pool, they were not pools. Mist seeped, mist had always been seeping, mist was what seeped. The cars they passed were not parked; nothing was parked; nothing was anything in the mode of *parked.* The words for things uncoupled from the things; the things stayed; the words, somewhere, walked on; the things, somewhere, were.

Inside this — and the word *inside* failed and was kept — a stillness that was not still. A reservoir without a wall. Something seething so completely it was the most quiet thing Tomek had ever not-thought. Anwen's interior — and there was no interior, and there was — lush, effervescent, a presence so dense she staggered slightly on the kerb, did not fall. Júlio's mouth opened. Nothing. The implacable. The torque of the permeable. The fluidity at the molecular. Codification of something no one had codified. Reverence — there was reverence; none of them had brought it; it was there.

A name pressed almost up. Almost. The texture of a sound that was not a sound, that was the texture. None of them said it. None of them could. None of them needed.

Then it was gone.

Their footfalls began again. There were five of them, on a street, in mist, and they were each, again, a person on a street. The delivery automaton went by, very slowly, the way they did. Anwen exhaled. Tomek closed his mouth. Kasimi's hand had been on the railing and the railing was wet. Júlio had stepped slightly off the kerb; he stepped back. Ines was a half-step behind. A small dopamine lull settled into them, separately — the body's own correction, the way after-light settles after a flash.

They did not speak. They walked another half-block. At the place where the side-street rejoined the wider street — where they would each turn for their separate trams, their separate flats, their separate streets — they slowed. They looked at one another. Each face was a face. *Bye,* said Anwen, simply. *Bye,* said Júlio, and the *bye* held the weight of all of it and did not name it. Kasimi nodded. Tomek lifted a hand. Ines was already turning.

What they carried, walking each their separate ways, was nothing they could put down. Agile. Unchanged. Transformed.
